## 阅读边界

太初有一道深渊，北方的寒霜遇到南方的热气，化出巨人尤弥尔。后来奥丁和他的兄弟维利、维杀死了尤弥尔，用他的身体造出世界：肉成了大地，血成了海洋和湖泊，骨头成了山石，头骨成了天空。

In the beginning there is a great void where rime from the north meets heat from the south, and from it the giant Ymir arises. Later Odin and his brothers Vili and Vé kill Ymir and build the world from his body: his flesh becomes the earth, his blood the sea and lakes, his bones the rocks, his skull the sky.

### 01 · 冰与火之间 / Between ice and fire

世界还不存在时，只有一道叫金伦加的深渊。北方的寒霜与南方的热气在这里相遇，融化的水滴里生出了巨人尤弥尔。

Before the world exists there is only the void of Ginnungagap. Where rime from the north meets heat from the south, the thawing drops bring forth the giant Ymir.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第5章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.ymir_origin`

### 02 · 奥丁兄弟杀死尤弥尔 / Odin and his brothers kill Ymir

布尔的儿子们——奥丁、维利和维——杀死了巨人尤弥尔。

The sons of Borr — Odin, Vili and Vé — kill the giant Ymir.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第7章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.odin_kills_ymir`

### 03 · 用身体造世界 / A world made from a body

他们用尤弥尔的身体造出世界：肉变成大地，血变成海洋和湖泊，骨头变成山岩，头骨被举起来成为天空。

From Ymir's body they make the world: his flesh becomes the earth, his blood the sea and lakes, his bones the rocks, and his skull is raised up as the sky.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第8章。
