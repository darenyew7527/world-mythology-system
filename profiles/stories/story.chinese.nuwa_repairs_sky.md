## 阅读边界

《淮南子·览冥训》与《列子·汤问》都记载女娲炼五色石、断鳌足立四极；前者另有黑龙与芦灰，后者把补天放在天地“物有不足”的问答里，并在其后才引出共工。两份见证分开阅读，不合成后世“共工撞山导致女娲补天”的版本。

Huainanzi “Lan Ming” and Liezi “Tang Wen” both have Nüwa refine five-colored stones and cut a turtle's legs to set up the four poles; the former adds the black dragon and reed ash, while the latter frames the repair as a remedy for deficient things and introduces Gonggong only afterwards. The two witnesses are read separately and are not merged into the later version in which Gonggong's collision causes the repair.

### 01 · 四极废，九州裂 / The four poles collapse

《淮南子·览冥训》以远古为背景：支撑天地的四极崩坏，九州开裂，天不能全覆、地不能全载，大火与洪水都无法止息，猛兽鸷鸟危害百姓。本段只按这一见证的顺序概述，不补入其他文献给出的起因。

Huainanzi “Lan Ming” sets the scene in remote antiquity: the four poles holding up the world have failed and the nine provinces have split; heaven no longer covers everything and earth no longer bears everything, fires and floods cannot be stopped, and wild beasts and birds of prey attack the people. This section follows only this witness and adds no cause taken from other texts.

### 02 · 炼五色石，断鳌足 / Five-colored stones and the turtle's legs

于是女娲炼五色石来修补苍天，又截断巨鳌的足，用来重新立起四极。

Nüwa then refines five-colored stones to patch the azure sky and cuts off the legs of a great turtle to set the four poles upright again.

### 03 · 杀黑龙，积芦灰 / The black dragon and the reed ash

这一见证还写到女娲杀死黑龙以救济冀州，并堆积芦苇灰来堵住泛滥的洪水；随后天地重新安定，百姓得以存活。

This witness adds that Nüwa kills a black dragon to relieve Jizhou and piles up reed ash to stop the flood waters; afterwards heaven and earth are set right and the people survive.

> 证据说明：同段黑龙、冀州与芦灰句组。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.nuwa.kills_black_dragon`
> 未知／边界：冀州在此是文本中的九州之一，不映射为现代行政区或坐标；《列子·汤问》对应段落未陈述这两项。

### 04 · 这一段没有写共工 / No Gonggong in this passage

《览冥训》的女娲段落没有把天地崩坏归因于共工。《淮南子》确实在《天文训》另记共工怒触不周山，但那是另一章中独立的天地倾斜起源，本站不把两章拼接成因果链。

The Lan Ming Nüwa passage does not attribute the collapse to Gonggong. The Huainanzi does narrate Gonggong striking Mount Buzhou, but in a separate passage in the “Tian Wen” chapter that explains the tilt of heaven and earth; the site does not splice the two chapters into a causal chain.

> 证据说明：《览冥训》女娲段与《天文训》共工段分别定位。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.nuwa.lanming_gonggong_boundary`
> 未知／边界：把共工触山写成女娲补天起因的说法见于较晚文献，已作为独立接受层排队。

### 01 · 天地也是“物” / Heaven and earth are also things

《列子·汤问》把女娲补天放在殷汤与夏革关于天地有无极限的问答中：天地也是物，物都有不足之处，所以古时女娲修补了它的缺口。

Liezi “Tang Wen” places the repair inside a dialogue between Tang of Yin and Xia Ge on whether heaven and earth have limits: heaven and earth are also things, things have deficiencies, and so in antiquity Nüwa mended the gap.

> 证据说明：《列子·汤问》殷汤与夏革问答段。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.nuwa.liezi_deficiency_frame`
> 未知／边界：《列子》托名列御寇，传世本成书年代有争议；本站不据此把它排在《淮南子》之前。

### 02 · 五色石与鳌足 / Five-colored stones and the turtle's legs

这一见证同样写女娲炼五色石补其缺口，并截断鳌足以立四极；它没有写黑龙、冀州或芦灰。

This witness likewise has Nüwa refine five-colored stones to fill the gap and cut the turtle's legs to set up the four poles; it does not mention the black dragon, Jizhou or reed ash.

### 03 · “其后”才是共工 / Gonggong comes “afterwards”

紧接补天之后，《汤问》用“其后”引出共工氏与颛顼争为帝、怒触不周之山。在这一见证里，共工事件发生在女娲补天之后，而不是它的起因。

Right after the repair, “Tang Wen” uses “afterwards” to introduce Gonggong contending with Zhuanxu for rulership and striking Mount Buzhou in anger. In this witness the Gonggong episode follows Nüwa's repair; it is not its cause.

> 证据说明：《列子·汤问》“其后共工氏”句。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.gonggong.liezi_after_nuwa`
> 未知／边界：后世“共工触山导致女娲补天”的因果顺序不回写进本见证。

### 04 · 天倾西北，水归东南 / Heaven tilts northwest, waters gather southeast

共工撞山折断天柱、断绝地维，于是天向西北倾斜，日月星辰向那里移动；地在东南不满，众水因此流归东南。

Gonggong's blow breaks the pillar of heaven and snaps the cords of the earth, so heaven tilts toward the northwest, where the sun, moon and stars move, and the earth falls short in the southeast, where the rivers and flood waters gather.

> 证据说明：《列子·汤问》天倾西北句组。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.gonggong.tilt_etiology`
> 未知／边界：这是文本中的天地形势起源解释，不作为地理或天文史实。
