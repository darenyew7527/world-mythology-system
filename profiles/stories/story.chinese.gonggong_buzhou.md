# 共工触不周山：天倾西北的两处见证 / Gonggong strikes Mount Buzhou — two early witnesses

## 阅读边界

《淮南子·天文训》把共工与颛顼争帝、怒触不周山写成独立的天地倾斜起源；《列子·汤问》则用“其后”把同一事件接在女娲补天之后。两处见证都不把它写成女娲补天的起因。

Huainanzi “Tian Wen” narrates Gonggong contending with Zhuanxu and striking Mount Buzhou as a standalone explanation of why heaven and earth tilt; Liezi “Tang Wen” attaches the same event after Nüwa's repair with “afterwards”. Neither witness makes it the cause of Nüwa's repair.

### 01 · 共工与颛顼争为帝 / Gonggong contends with Zhuanxu

《淮南子·天文训》说，从前共工与颛顼争夺帝位。

Huainanzi “Tian Wen” says that long ago Gonggong contended with Zhuanxu to become ruler.

> 证据说明：《淮南子·天文训》“昔者共工”段。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.gonggong.contends_zhuanxu`
> 未知／边界：颛顼在此作为传说中的君主登记，不作历史真实性判断。

### 02 · 怒触不周之山 / Striking Mount Buzhou in anger

共工发怒撞向不周山，支撑天的柱子折断，系住大地的绳索断绝。

In anger Gonggong strikes Mount Buzhou; the pillar holding up heaven breaks and the cords binding the earth snap.

> 证据说明：同段不周山、天柱与地维句组。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.gonggong.strikes_buzhou`
> 未知／边界：不周山不映射为现代山名或坐标。

### 03 · 一段独立的起源解释 / A standalone etiology

结果天向西北倾斜，日月星辰随之移动；地在东南不满，积水与尘埃都归向那里。这一段没有提到女娲，是《天文训》中独立的天地形势解释。

As a result heaven tilts to the northwest and the sun, moon and stars shift with it; the earth falls short in the southeast, where waters and dust collect. The passage does not mention Nüwa and stands as an independent explanation of cosmic orientation in “Tian Wen”.

> 证据说明：《淮南子·天文训》共工段结尾。
> Claim: `claim.v0300.gonggong.tianwen_standalone`
> 未知／边界：同书《览冥训》的女娲段与此段分属不同章节，不合成单一故事。

### 03 · 天倾西北，百川东归 / Heaven tilts northwest, rivers flow southeast

天向西北倾斜，日月星辰向那里靠拢；地在东南不满，于是百川与积水都流归东南。与《天文训》相比，措辞略有不同。

Heaven tilts to the northwest and the sun, moon and stars draw toward it; the earth falls short in the southeast, so all rivers and flood waters flow there. The wording differs slightly from “Tian Wen”.
