## 阅读边界

《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷讲述图拉的大法师羽蛇神。巫师们来到他身边，其中一位叫蒂特拉卡瓦，也就是特斯卡特利波卡；他们带来的种种征兆让羽蛇神的荣光走向终结。羽蛇神离开时，随行的人在两座雪山之间冻死。

Florentine Codex Book 3 tells of Quetzalcoatl, a great wizard at Tollan. Sorcerers come to him — among them Titlacauan, also called Tezcatlipoca — and their portents bring his glory to an end. As he departs, his followers freeze to death between two snow-capped volcanoes.

### 01 · 被当作神的蒂特拉卡瓦 / Titlacauan, held to be a god

第三卷第2章讲人们怎样把一位叫蒂特拉卡瓦、也叫特斯卡特利波卡的人物当作神来敬奉。

Chapter 2 of Book 3 tells how one called Titlacauan, or Tezcatlipoca, came to be regarded as a god.

### 03 · 雪山之间 / Between the snow mountains

第14章记载，羽蛇神离去时，追随他的人经过伊斯塔克特佩特尔与波波卡特佩特尔两座雪山之间，被冻死在冰雪里。

Chapter 14 records that as Quetzalcoatl departs, his vassals freeze to death in the ice as they pass between Iztactepetl and Popocatepetl.
