## 阅读边界

《女巫预言》与《欺骗古鲁菲》都记下最初人类阿斯克和恩布拉，却给出不同的神祇组合；两版分开阅读。

Voluspa and Gylfaginning both name Ask and Embla while presenting different divine triads; the two witnesses are read separately.
