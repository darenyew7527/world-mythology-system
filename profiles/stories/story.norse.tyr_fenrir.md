## 阅读边界

提尔是众神中最勇敢的一位。众神要捆住越长越大的狼芬里尔，狼要求有人把手放进它嘴里作担保。只有提尔伸出了右手；当狼发现自己挣脱不开，就咬断了他的手。所以提尔只有一只手。

Týr is the boldest of the gods. When they set out to bind the growing wolf Fenrir, the wolf demands a hand in its mouth as a pledge. Only Týr offers his right hand; when the wolf finds it cannot break free, it bites the hand off — and so Týr is one-handed.

### 01 · 最勇敢的神 / The boldest god

斯诺里说，提尔是众神中最勇敢、最无畏的一位，打仗时人们向他祈求胜利。他只有一只手。

Snorri says Týr is the boldest and most valiant of the gods, invoked for victory in battle — and he has only one hand.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第25章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.tyr_brave`

### 02 · 一只手作担保 / A hand as a pledge

众神想用一条魔法绳索捆住狼芬里尔。狼起了疑心，要求有人把手放进它嘴里，保证这不是诡计。众神面面相觑，只有提尔伸出了右手。

The gods mean to bind the wolf Fenrir with a magic fetter. Suspicious, the wolf demands that someone put a hand in its mouth as a pledge of good faith. Only Týr holds out his right hand.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第34章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.tyr_fenrir`

### 03 · 狼咬断了手 / The wolf bites

绳索越挣越紧，狼发现自己被骗了，便咬断了提尔的手。众神都笑了，只有提尔没有笑。

The more the wolf struggles, the tighter the fetter grows. Realizing it has been tricked, Fenrir bites off Týr's hand — and all the gods laugh, except Týr.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第34章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.fenrir_bound`
