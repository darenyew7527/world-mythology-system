## 阅读边界

万奈莫宁、伊尔马里宁和勒明凯宁乘着一艘渴望出征的战船前往北方，去夺回为波赫约拉锻造的三宝。船在急流中卡在一条巨大的梭鱼背上，万奈莫宁杀死梭鱼，用它的颚骨做成了第一把康特勒琴。他的琴声让天上地下所有的生灵都来倾听，连他自己也流下了眼泪，泪水落进海里，变成了蓝色的珍珠。到了波赫约拉，洛希不肯分享三宝，万奈莫宁就用琴声把整个北方唱入梦乡。英雄们打开铜山的门锁，用牛犁断三宝的根，把它搬上船逃走。勒明凯宁忍不住在船上唱歌，一只鹤被惊醒，叫醒了波赫约拉。洛希派来浓雾、海怪和风暴，康特勒琴落进了大海；最后她化作一只巨鹰，带着上百名武士扑向战船。万奈莫宁用船舵打断了她的爪子，三宝跌进海里摔得粉碎：大块沉入海底，成了大海的财富，小块漂到卡勒瓦拉的岸边，带来了丰收；洛希只抢回了三宝的盖子，波赫约拉从此贫穷下去。

Väinämöinen, Ilmarinen and Lemminkäinen sail north in a war-boat that longs for battle, to win back the Sampo forged for Pohjola. In the rapids the boat sticks on the back of a giant pike; Väinämöinen kills it and from its jawbone makes the first kantele, whose music draws every creature of earth and sky to listen; he weeps himself, and his tears fall into the sea as blue pearls. In Pohjola Louhi will not share the Sampo, so Väinämöinen plays all the north to sleep; the heroes open the copper mountain, plough out the Sampo's roots with an ox, and carry it off in their boat. Lemminkäinen cannot help singing, a crane wakes and rouses Pohjola, and Louhi sends fog, a sea monster and a storm that sweeps the kantele overboard; at last she becomes a giant eagle carrying a hundred warriors. Väinämöinen smashes her talons with the rudder; the Sampo falls into the sea and shatters: the large pieces become the riches of the sea, the small ones drift to the shores of Kalevala and bring plenty, and Louhi takes home only the lid, so that Pohjola grows poor.

### 01 · 宝剑与哭泣的船 / The sword and the weeping boat

伊尔马里宁失去了妻子，悲伤了很久。万奈莫宁对他说：我们到北方的波赫约拉去吧，去把三宝夺回来。你为他们锻造了三宝，他们却只给我们带来不幸；三宝在那里磨出粮食、磨出财富，让北方富足，我们却一无所有。伊尔马里宁说：三宝锁在波赫约拉的石山里，有九道锁，根扎进地下九寻深，很难拿到。可他还是答应了，并且为万奈莫宁打造了一把宝剑：剑刃上有月亮和太阳，剑柄上有星星，剑身上有一匹马在嘶鸣，剑柄的圆头上有一只猫在叫，剑鞘上有一只狗在吠。两位英雄骑马出发，走到海边，听见岸边有一艘船在哭泣。那是一艘战船，它哭着说：别的船都去打仗，带回金银财宝，只有我被丢在这里，在木屑堆里一年年地腐烂。万奈莫宁说：不要哭，我会带你去打仗的。他唱起歌来，船自己滑进了水里，两位英雄登上船，向北方驶去。

Ilmarinen grieved long for his lost wife. Väinämöinen said to him: 'Let us go north to Pohjola and win back the Sampo. You forged it for them, yet they bring us only misfortune; there it grinds grain and wealth and makes the north rich, while we have nothing.' Ilmarinen said: 'The Sampo is locked in the stone mountain of Pohjola behind nine locks, its roots nine fathoms deep; it will be hard to take.' But he agreed, and forged Väinämöinen a sword: on its blade the moon and the sun, on its hilt the stars, on its side a horse neighing, on the knob of the hilt a cat mewing, on the scabbard a dog barking. The two heroes rode to the sea and heard a boat weeping on the shore. It was a war-boat, and it wept: 'The other boats go to war and bring home gold and silver; only I am left here, rotting year after year among the wood-chips.' 'Do not weep,' said Väinämöinen, 'I will take you to war.' He sang, the boat slid into the water of itself, and the heroes boarded it and sailed north.

### 02 · 勒明凯宁加入 / Lemminkäinen joins them

船经过一座岬角时，岸上有人在喊：是谁在海上航行？原来是勒明凯宁。他一个人住在海边，正因为没有了打仗的机会而闷闷不乐，看见两位英雄的船驶过，就大声问他们要到哪里去。万奈莫宁说：我们要到北方的波赫约拉去，去夺回三宝。勒明凯宁说：让我也一起去吧，我会是第三个英雄，我的剑也很锋利，我能在打仗的时候帮上你们的忙。万奈莫宁答应了。勒明凯宁拿着一块木板跳上了船，船身沉了一沉，又平稳地向前驶去。万奈莫宁坐在船尾掌舵，伊尔马里宁和勒明凯宁划桨，船儿像飞鸟一样在海浪上前进。三位卡勒瓦拉最有名的英雄——歌者、铁匠和战士——第一次一同出发，去完成一件谁也没有做到过的事。这位战士在前面的诗章里曾在波赫约拉闯下大祸，不得不逃到海岛上躲了三年，如今他又一次站到了冒险的船上。

As the boat passed a headland, someone on the shore called out: 'Who is sailing on the sea?' It was Lemminkäinen. Living alone by the sea and sullen for lack of a war to fight, he saw the heroes' boat go by and shouted to ask where they were going. Väinämöinen said: 'We are going north to Pohjola, to win back the Sampo.' Lemminkäinen said: 'Let me come too. I will be the third hero; my sword is sharp too, and I can help you in the fighting.' Väinämöinen agreed. Lemminkäinen leapt aboard with a board under his arm; the boat dipped and then sailed steadily on. Väinämöinen sat at the stern steering, Ilmarinen and Lemminkäinen rowed, and the boat sped over the waves like a bird. The three most famous heroes of Kalevala — the singer, the smith and the warrior — set out together for the first time, to do what no one had ever done. In earlier runes this warrior had brought disaster on himself in Pohjola and had to hide on an island for three years; now he stood once more aboard a boat bound for adventure.

### 03 · 大梭鱼 / The great pike

船驶进一段湍急的河道，在急流和瀑布之间穿行。万奈莫宁念着咒语，请急流的女神和水中的精灵让船平安通过。可就在急流中央，船忽然停住了，任凭怎么划桨也纹丝不动，好像被什么东西紧紧托住。勒明凯宁探身往水里一看，说：船下面不是礁石，也不是树干，而是一条巨大的梭鱼，我们的船正卡在它的背上。他拔剑向水里砍去，可是用力太猛，自己差点掉进河里，剑也没能伤到梭鱼。伊尔马里宁抓住他的头发，把他拉了回来。伊尔马里宁也拔剑去砍，剑却折断了，梭鱼仍然一动不动。最后，万奈莫宁拔出自己的宝剑，猛地刺进水里，从梭鱼的身下一挑，把这条巨大的鱼刺穿，挑出水面，劈成了几段。他们把梭鱼拖上船，在岸边煮熟，三位英雄美美地吃了一顿，只剩下一堆鱼骨。

The boat ran into a stretch of wild river, threading between rapids and falls. Väinämöinen chanted charms to the goddess of the rapids and the spirits of the water to let them pass safely. But in the middle of the rapids the boat suddenly stopped, and however they rowed it would not move, as if something held it fast. Lemminkäinen leaned over and looked: 'It is no rock and no tree-trunk beneath us, but a giant pike; our boat is stuck on its back.' He drew his sword and struck into the water, but struck so hard that he nearly fell in, and did the pike no harm; Ilmarinen caught him by the hair and pulled him back. Ilmarinen struck too, and his sword broke, and the pike did not stir. At last Väinämöinen drew his own sword, thrust it into the water and up under the fish, pierced the great pike, lifted it out and cut it in pieces. They hauled it aboard, cooked it on the shore, and the three heroes ate their fill, leaving only a heap of bones.

### 04 · 第一把康特勒琴 / The first kantele

万奈莫宁看着那堆鱼骨，心想：这些骨头要是做成一件东西，会是什么样子？他拿起梭鱼的颚骨，说：如果有一位巧手的工匠，就能用它做成一件乐器。可是谁也不会做，万奈莫宁就自己动手。他用梭鱼的颚骨做成琴身，用梭鱼的牙齿做成调弦的琴钉，又从恶灵希西那匹骟马的身上取来马鬃，做成了琴弦。这样，世界上第一把康特勒琴就造成了。伊尔马里宁和勒明凯宁都想试一试，他们拨动琴弦，发出的却是刺耳难听的声音。他们把琴带到波赫约拉，那里的人们也争着去弹，有年轻人，有老人，有少女，可没有一个人能弹出好听的曲子，琴声粗哑，人们捂着耳朵，说这琴毫无用处，还是扔进水里算了。可是琴自己哭了起来，说它不愿被扔进水里，它只等着一位真正懂得它的人来弹奏。

Looking at the heap of bones, Väinämöinen wondered what they might become. He took up the pike's jawbone and said: 'A skilled craftsman could make an instrument of this.' No one knew how, so he set to it himself. From the jawbone he made the body of the harp, from the pike's teeth the pegs, and for strings he took hair from the gelding of the evil spirit Hiisi. So the first kantele in the world was made. Ilmarinen and Lemminkäinen both tried it, but when they plucked the strings the sound was harsh and ugly. In Pohjola people crowded to play it — young men and old, maidens too — but no one could draw a fair tune from it; the sound was rough, people covered their ears, and said the harp was useless and had better be thrown into the water. But the kantele wept, saying it would not be thrown away; it was waiting for one who truly understood it.

### 05 · 万物倾听 / All creatures listen

万奈莫宁洗净双手，坐在一块歌唱的石头上，把康特勒琴放在膝上，用手指拨动琴弦。第一声响起，四周就安静了下来；琴声越来越美，传遍了山林和湖海。森林里的松鼠从这根树枝跳到那根树枝，跑来倾听；白鼬、麋鹿、山猫都来了，狼从沼泽里走出来，熊爬上了篱笆。森林之王塔皮奥和他的家人穿着盛装来到山岗上。天上的鸟儿也飞来了：鹰离开了巢，鹞子从高空落下，天鹅从湖上飞来，就连小小的燕雀也聚在他的肩头。水里的鱼儿都游到岸边，梭鱼、鲑鱼和白鲑把头伸出水面。海王阿赫托也从海底升起，坐在一朵睡莲上倾听。天上的少女们——月亮的女儿和太阳的女儿——正在纺金线、织银布，听见琴声，手里的纺锤都掉了下来。万奈莫宁一连弹了许多天，所有的生灵都被琴声迷住了。

Väinämöinen washed his hands, sat down on a stone of singing, set the kantele on his knee and plucked the strings with his fingers. At the first note all around fell silent; the music grew ever lovelier and spread through hills, woods, lakes and seas. The squirrels of the forest leapt from branch to branch to listen; ermine, elk and lynx came, the wolf came out of the swamp and the bear climbed onto the fence. Tapio, king of the forest, came with his household in festive dress to the hilltop. The birds of the sky came too: the eagle left its nest, the hawk dropped from the heights, the swans flew in from the lakes, and even the little finches gathered on his shoulders. The fishes swam to the shore, pike, salmon and whitefish lifting their heads from the water. Ahto, king of the sea, rose from the depths and sat on a water-lily to listen. The maidens of the sky — the daughters of the moon and of the sun, spinning golden thread and weaving silver cloth — let their spindles fall at the sound. Väinämöinen played for many days, and every creature was enchanted.

### 06 · 泪珠 / The tears that became pearls

所有听见琴声的人都哭了：年轻的人哭了，老人哭了，男人和女人都哭了，连小孩子也哭了。最后，万奈莫宁自己也被琴声打动，流下了眼泪。他的泪珠比越橘还大，比豌豆还圆，比鸡蛋还要光亮，一颗颗从他的眼里落下，落到脸颊上，从脸颊落到胸前，从胸前落到膝上，从膝上落到脚下，又从脚下滚进了大海，沉到了海底的黑泥里。万奈莫宁说：谁能把我的眼泪从海底捞上来？乌鸦来试，没有成功。一只蓝色的野鸭飞来，潜进海里，把那些泪珠一颗颗衔了上来。泪珠已经变了样：它们变成了蓝色的珍珠，美丽得可以戴在国王的头上，成为英雄们永远的装饰。这是《卡勒瓦拉》里最安静的时刻之一：在夺取三宝的长途中，歌者停下来，让音乐和眼泪说话。那只野鸭，在原诗里是一只蓝色的鸭子。

Everyone who heard the music wept: the young and the old, men and women, even the little children. At last Väinämöinen himself was moved by his playing and began to weep. His tears were larger than cranberries, rounder than peas, brighter than eggs; they fell from his eyes onto his cheeks, from his cheeks onto his breast, from his breast to his knees, from his knees to his feet, and from his feet rolled into the sea and sank into the dark mud of the bottom. 'Who will bring my tears up from the sea?' said Väinämöinen. The raven tried and failed. A blue duck came, dived into the sea and brought the tears up one by one. They had changed: they had become blue pearls, beautiful enough to crown a king, an ornament for heroes for ever. It is one of the quietest moments in the Kalevala: on the long road to the Sampo the singer pauses, and lets music and tears speak. In the poem the duck is a blue duck.

### 07 · 唱睡波赫约拉 / Singing Pohjola to sleep

船终于到了波赫约拉。英雄们走进洛希的大厅，洛希问他们来做什么。万奈莫宁说：我们来商量三宝的事。三宝在你们这里磨出了粮食和财富，我们也想分享一份。如果你不肯分给我们，我们就把它整个拿走。洛希说：一只松鼠不能分给三个人，一个三宝也不能分给两家。三宝留在波赫约拉，在这里转动，在这里磨面。万奈莫宁说：既然如此，我们就把它全部带回卡勒瓦拉。洛希大怒，召集了波赫约拉的全部武士，手持刀剑围了上来。万奈莫宁不慌不忙，拿起康特勒琴弹奏起来。琴声一响，波赫约拉的人们都停下了脚步，一个个闭上眼睛，倒在地上沉沉睡去。万奈莫宁又从口袋里掏出“睡眠之针”，把他们的睫毛缝在一起，让他们久久不能醒来。整个北方都陷入了沉睡。在这部史诗里，琴声和歌声比刀剑更有力量。

At last the boat reached Pohjola. The heroes went into Louhi's hall, and she asked their business. Väinämöinen said: 'We have come to speak of the Sampo. Here it grinds grain and wealth for you; we want a share. If you will not share it, we will take the whole.' Louhi said: 'A squirrel cannot be shared among three, and one Sampo cannot be shared between two households. The Sampo stays in Pohjola, to turn here and grind here.' 'Then we will take it all to Kalevala,' said Väinämöinen. Furious, Louhi summoned all the warriors of Pohjola, who closed in with swords. Unhurried, Väinämöinen took up his kantele and played. At the first notes the people of Pohjola stood still, closed their eyes one by one, and fell to the ground in deep sleep. Then he took his needles of sleep from his pouch and stitched their eyelashes together so that they would not wake for a long time. All the north lay asleep. In this epic the harp and the song are mightier than the sword.

### 08 · 挖出三宝 / Digging out the Sampo

英雄们来到波赫约拉的铜山前，三宝就锁在山里的九道门后。万奈莫宁轻声唱起开锁的歌，门锁一道道松开了；伊尔马里宁在门轴上抹了黄油，让沉重的铜门打开时不会发出吱呀的响声，免得惊醒睡着的人们。门开了，三宝就在里面，它的根深深地扎进了山岩和地下。勒明凯宁说：让我来，我力气最大。他用脚抵着地，双手抱住三宝，用尽全力往上拔，三宝却纹丝不动，它的根扎得太深了。于是他们牵来波赫约拉最大的一头公牛，套上犁，用犁把三宝的根一根根犁断。三宝终于松动了，英雄们把它从山里抬出来，搬上了船，藏在船舱里。他们解开缆绳，悄悄地离开了波赫约拉的海岸，划着船，向南方的家乡驶去。原诗在第十章里说过，三宝的根有三条：一条扎进大地，一条扎进水边，一条扎进家门前的山丘；如今这三条根都被犁断了，三宝离开了它扎根多年的地方。

The heroes came to the copper mountain of Pohjola, where the Sampo was locked behind nine doors. Väinämöinen softly sang the song that opens locks, and the locks loosened one by one; Ilmarinen smeared butter on the hinges so that the heavy copper doors would open without creaking and wake the sleepers. The doors opened, and there was the Sampo, its roots sunk deep in the rock and the earth. 'Let me,' said Lemminkäinen, 'I am strongest.' He braced his feet, clasped the Sampo in his arms and heaved with all his strength, but it did not move; its roots went too deep. So they brought the greatest ox of Pohjola, yoked it to a plough, and ploughed through the Sampo's roots one by one. At last the Sampo came loose; the heroes carried it out of the mountain, loaded it into the boat and hid it in the hold. They cast off and slipped quietly away from the shores of Pohjola, rowing south towards home. Rune X had told of the Sampo's three roots, one in the earth, one at the water's edge, one in the hill by the house; now all three were ploughed through, and the Sampo left the place where it had been rooted so long.

> 证据说明：《卡勒瓦拉》克劳福德译本第四十二章。

### 09 · 鹤的叫声 / The crane's cry

船在海上平稳地航行，三宝藏在船舱里，三位英雄心里都很高兴。勒明凯宁按捺不住，说：我们夺得了三宝，这样的喜事，怎么能不唱歌呢？万奈莫宁劝他说：现在还不是唱歌的时候。唱歌会被人听见，会把波赫约拉的人吵醒，等我们回到家乡再唱吧。勒明凯宁忍了一天、两天，到了第三天，他再也忍不住了，张开嘴就唱了起来。可他的嗓子粗哑难听，歌声在海面上远远传开。一只鹤正站在沼泽边的草丛里，被这难听的歌声吓了一跳，扑腾着翅膀飞了起来，一路尖叫着飞向北方。它的叫声越过了山岭和海湾，飞进了波赫约拉，把那些被缝住睫毛、沉睡已久的人们全都吵醒了。洛希醒来，跑到铜山一看，三宝不见了。一次不合时宜的歌唱，就这样毁掉了万奈莫宁小心翼翼的计划，追兵很快就要来了。

The boat sailed smoothly over the sea with the Sampo hidden in the hold, and the three heroes were glad. Lemminkäinen could not contain himself: 'We have won the Sampo — how can we not sing at such joy?' Väinämöinen warned him: 'This is no time for singing. Song will be heard and will wake the people of Pohjola. Wait until we are home.' Lemminkäinen held back one day, two days, but on the third he could hold back no longer and began to sing. His voice was harsh and ugly, and the song carried far over the water. A crane standing in the reeds by a swamp was startled by the dreadful singing, flapped up, and flew screaming north. Its cry crossed hills and bays and reached Pohjola, waking all the people who had slept so long with their eyelashes sewn. Louhi woke, ran to the copper mountain, and found the Sampo gone. One song at the wrong moment undid all Väinämöinen's careful plans, and the pursuit would soon be upon them.

> 证据说明：《卡勒瓦拉》克劳福德译本第四十二章。
> Claim: `claim.v0350.kal_4.09`

### 10 · 雾、海怪与风暴 / Fog, the sea monster and the storm

洛希大怒，施展魔法追赶英雄们。她先派来浓雾的少女，让大雾笼罩了海面，英雄们的船在雾中迷失了方向，一连三天三夜停在原地。万奈莫宁拔出宝剑，向大雾劈去，劈开了一条路，雾气四散，船又能前进了。接着，洛希唤起了海怪伊库-图尔索。海怪从海底升起，掀起巨浪，要把船掀翻。万奈莫宁伸手抓住海怪的耳朵，把它提出水面，问它：伊库-图尔索，你为什么从海里升起来，挡在我们的路上？他命令海怪沉回海底，永远不许再在人们面前出现。海怪沉了下去。最后，洛希向乌戈求来一场狂风暴雨，海浪滔天，船身摇晃，万奈莫宁心爱的康特勒琴被一阵大浪卷下船去，沉进了海底。万奈莫宁看着大海，伤心地说：我的快乐已经沉进了海里，再也找不回来了。这把琴后来再也没有找回来，万奈莫宁不得不在后面的诗章里重新做了一把。

Louhi in her fury worked magic to pursue the heroes. First she sent the maiden of the mist, and a thick fog covered the sea; the heroes' boat lost its way and lay still for three days and nights. Väinämöinen drew his sword and struck at the fog, cleaving a path through it; the mist scattered and the boat went on. Next Louhi called up the sea monster Iku-Turso, which rose from the deep and raised great waves to overturn the boat. Väinämöinen seized the monster by the ears, lifted it out of the water and asked: 'Iku-Turso, why have you risen from the sea to bar our way?' He commanded it to sink back to the bottom and never again show itself to men, and it sank. Last, Louhi begged Ukko for a raging storm; the waves rose sky-high, the boat pitched and rolled, and a great wave swept Väinämöinen's beloved kantele overboard into the depths. Gazing at the sea he said sorrowfully: 'My joy has sunk into the sea, and I shall never find it again.' That harp was never found again; in a later rune Väinämöinen has to make another.

> 证据说明：《卡勒瓦拉》克劳福德译本第四十二章。
> Claim: `claim.v0350.kal_4.10`

### 11 · 鹰形的洛希 / Louhi as an eagle

洛希造了一艘巨大的战船，让上百名武士带着刀剑和弓箭上船，亲自率领他们追赶。眼看就要追上了，她又变出了一个可怕的样子：她把镰刀装在脚上做爪子，把船身变成翅膀，变成一只巨大的鹰，一边翅膀上载着一百个武士，尾巴上载着一千个弓箭手，向英雄们的船扑来。万奈莫宁向乌戈祈祷，求乌戈保护他们。巨鹰落在船的桅杆上，船几乎被压翻。伊尔马里宁和勒明凯宁拔剑去砍，勒明凯宁砍下了鹰的几根羽毛。万奈莫宁从水里拔起船舵，用船舵狠狠地向巨鹰打去，打断了它的爪子，鹰爪纷纷掉进海里，只剩下一个小小的爪尖。洛希的武士们也纷纷从鹰的翅膀上跌落，掉进海里，就像秋天的树叶从树上落下来一样。这是《卡勒瓦拉》里最壮观的一场战斗：一边是变成巨鹰的北方女主人和她的大军，一边是一艘小船上的三位英雄。

Louhi built a great war-ship, put a hundred warriors aboard with swords, bows and arrows, and led the pursuit herself. When she was nearly upon them she took a terrible shape: she fixed scythes to her feet for talons, turned the ship into wings and became a giant eagle, carrying a hundred warriors on one wing and a thousand bowmen on her tail, and swooped down on the heroes' boat. Väinämöinen prayed to Ukko for protection. The giant eagle settled on the mast and nearly overturned the boat. Ilmarinen and Lemminkäinen struck at it with their swords; Lemminkäinen cut away a few of its feathers. Väinämöinen pulled the rudder from the water and struck the eagle hard with it, smashing its talons, which fell into the sea until only one small claw was left. Louhi's warriors fell from the eagle's wings into the sea like autumn leaves falling from a tree. It is the grandest battle in the Kalevala: on one side the mistress of the north turned giant eagle with her host, on the other three heroes in one small boat.

> 证据说明：《卡勒瓦拉》克劳福德译本第四十三章。
> Claim: `claim.v0350.kal_4.11`

### 12 · 三宝的碎片 / The fragments of the Sampo

洛希虽然失去了爪子，却不肯罢休。她用仅剩的那根小小的爪尖——诗中说是她的小指——勾住了三宝，想把它从船上拖走。三宝从船上滑落，跌进了大海，在海中摔成了碎片。大块的碎片沉到了海底，从此大海有了取之不尽的财富，海里的鱼和宝藏都由此而来。小块的碎片随着波浪漂流，漂到了卡勒瓦拉的海岸上。万奈莫宁在岸边一块块地拾起这些碎片，把它们种进土里，祈求它们长出黑麦和大麦，带来丰收和富足。洛希只抢回了三宝那五彩的盖子，带回了波赫约拉，从此北方日渐贫穷。洛希在空中恶狠狠地发誓：我要给你们送去瘟疫，送去冰霜，送去猛兽，我还要把太阳和月亮偷走！万奈莫宁回答说：我不怕你的诅咒。他向乌戈祈祷，请乌戈保护卡勒瓦拉的人们，保护他们的田地和庄稼，让三宝的碎片永远给这片土地带来福祉。

Though she had lost her talons, Louhi would not give up. With the one small claw she had left — her little finger, the poem says — she hooked the Sampo and tried to drag it from the boat. The Sampo slipped from the boat, fell into the sea, and was shattered. The large pieces sank to the bottom, and from them the sea has its endless riches, its fish and its treasures. The small pieces drifted on the waves to the shores of Kalevala. Väinämöinen gathered them one by one on the beach, planted them in the earth, and prayed that they would bring forth rye and barley, plenty and prosperity. Louhi took home to Pohjola only the Sampo's many-coloured lid, and from then on the north grew poorer. From the air Louhi swore fiercely: 'I will send you plague and frost and wild beasts; I will steal the sun and the moon!' Väinämöinen answered that he did not fear her curses, and prayed to Ukko to protect the people of Kalevala, their fields and their crops, and to let the fragments of the Sampo bless the land for ever.

> 证据说明：《卡勒瓦拉》克劳福德译本第四十三章。
> Claim: `claim.v0350.kal_4.12`
