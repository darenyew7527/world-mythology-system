# 阿蒙的女儿：哈特谢普苏特的神圣诞生 / Daughter of Amun: the divine birth of Hatshepsut

- 文明／传统: 古埃及

## 阅读边界

在底比斯西岸代尔拜赫里的哈特谢普苏特神庙里，一组浮雕讲述了这位女法老的神圣出身。众神之王阿蒙-拉在众神面前宣布，他要生下一位未来的统治者；托特向他推荐了图特摩斯一世的王后雅赫摩斯，又引他来到王后的寝宫。阿蒙化作国王的模样，王后被神的香气唤醒，看见了神的真身，神为将要出生的女儿取名“与阿蒙结合的哈特谢普苏特”。阿蒙吩咐陶工之神克努姆在陶轮上塑造孩子和她的卡，蛙头女神赫凯特把生命之符递到她们面前。托特向王后报喜，克努姆和赫凯特领她走进产房，孩子诞生了。哈托尔把婴儿献给阿蒙，阿蒙称她为心爱的女儿和两地之王，众女神和神牛哺育她，托特把孩子和她的卡呈给阿蒙，众神为她定下了千百万年的寿命。

In Hatshepsut's temple at Deir el-Bahari on the west bank of Thebes, a cycle of reliefs tells the divine origin of this woman king. Amun-Re, king of the gods, announces before the gods that he will beget a future ruler; Thoth commends to him Ahmose, queen of Thutmose I, and leads him to her chamber. Amun takes the king's form; the queen wakes at the god's fragrance and sees him in his true form, and the god names the coming daughter Khenemet-Amun Hatshepsut. Amun bids the potter god Khnum shape the child and her ka on his wheel, while the frog goddess Heqet holds out the sign of life. Thoth announces the birth to the queen, Khnum and Heqet lead her to the birth chamber, and the child is born. Hathor presents the infant to Amun, who greets her as his beloved daughter and king of the Two Lands; goddesses and divine cows nurse her; Thoth presents the child and her ka to Amun, and the gods fix her lifetime at millions of years.

### 01 · 众神的会议 / The council of the gods

浮雕的第一幕画的是众神之王阿蒙-拉坐在宝座上，面前是底比斯和赫利奥波利斯的众神。阿蒙-拉向众神宣布他的打算：他要生下一位未来的统治者，一位将统治两地、受众神护佑的国王。在古埃及的王权观念里，每一位法老都被看作太阳神的儿子，“拉之子”是国王王衔中的固定称号。可是哈特谢普苏特的情况很特别：她是一位女性，原本是图特摩斯二世的王后，后来以年幼的图特摩斯三世摄政的身份开始执政，最后自己加冕为王。为了说明她有资格坐上王位，她在自己的神庙里刻下了这组浮雕，宣称她不仅是图特摩斯一世的女儿，更是阿蒙-拉亲生的女儿。这组浮雕位于神庙中层柱廊的北侧，与南侧描绘远征蓬特的浮雕相对。神的会议这一幕，就像一出戏剧的开场：在凡人的故事开始以前，天上的众神已经为这个孩子的诞生做好了安排。

The first scene shows Amun-Re, king of the gods, enthroned before the gods of Thebes and Heliopolis. He announces his purpose to them: he will beget a future ruler, a king who will rule the Two Lands under the gods' protection. In Egyptian kingship every pharaoh was a son of the sun god, and 'Son of Re' was a fixed title in the royal names. Hatshepsut's case was unusual: a woman, once queen of Thutmose II, she began to govern as regent for the young Thutmose III and at last was crowned king herself. To show that she was fit for the throne, she carved this cycle in her temple, claiming to be not only the daughter of Thutmose I but the daughter of Amun-Re himself. The reliefs stand on the north side of the middle colonnade, facing the reliefs of the expedition to Punt on the south. The council of the gods opens the cycle like the first scene of a drama: before the mortal story begins, the gods in heaven have already arranged the child's birth.

### 02 · 阿蒙与托特 / Amun and Thoth

下一幕里，阿蒙与智慧之神托特交谈。托特是众神的书记和使者，以朱鹭头的形象出现。他向阿蒙谈起了一位凡间的女子：她就是图特摩斯一世的王后雅赫摩斯，比全国任何女子都美丽。在浮雕的叙述中，托特像一位宫廷中的谋臣，为神物色一位配得上神子的母亲。选择王后雅赫摩斯，本身就有深意：她是在位国王的正妻，她所生的孩子有着无可争议的王室血统。这样，哈特谢普苏特既是凡间国王的女儿，又是天上之神的女儿，两重身份互不矛盾，反而互相加强。托特在这组浮雕中扮演着重要的角色：他提出人选，引路，报喜，最后把孩子呈给阿蒙。古埃及人相信托特掌管文字、计算和知识，他也是神与神之间、神与人之间的信使。由他来安排这桩神圣的婚姻，说明一切都合乎众神的秩序。

In the next scene Amun speaks with Thoth, god of wisdom, scribe and messenger of the gods, shown with the head of an ibis. Thoth tells Amun of a mortal woman: Ahmose, queen of Thutmose I, more beautiful than any woman in the land. In the reliefs Thoth acts like a counsellor at court, finding a mother worthy of the god's child. The choice of Queen Ahmose is itself meaningful: she is the reigning king's chief wife, and her child's royal blood cannot be questioned. So Hatshepsut is both daughter of an earthly king and daughter of a god in heaven, and the two claims do not conflict but strengthen each other. Thoth plays a large part throughout the cycle: he proposes the mother, leads the way, announces the birth and finally presents the child to Amun. The Egyptians held that Thoth ruled writing, reckoning and knowledge and carried messages between gods and between gods and men; that he arranges this sacred union shows that all is in keeping with the gods' order.

### 03 · 托特引路 / Thoth leads the way

第三幕画的是托特在前面领路，阿蒙跟在后面，一起前往王后雅赫摩斯所在的王宫。这一幕没有多少文字，画面本身却传达出一种庄严的气氛：两位神一前一后，像是要去完成一件隆重的仪式。在这组浮雕里，神与人之间的界限不断被跨越：众神之王离开天上的宝座，亲自来到凡间的王宫。古埃及的神庙浮雕往往像连环画一样，一幕接一幕地展开，观者沿着柱廊走过，就能依次读完整个故事。在哈特谢普苏特的时代，这些浮雕原本色彩鲜艳，有些地方至今还保留着蓝色和红色的颜料痕迹。浮雕中阿蒙的形象后来在阿赫那吞推行单一崇拜阿吞的时候遭到破坏，许多阿蒙的名字和形象被凿去，后来又被修复过；而哈特谢普苏特本人的形象，则在她去世以后被图特摩斯三世下令凿毁。今天的观者看到的，是一组经历过两次破坏的画面，这也让这个神圣诞生的故事带上了一层历史的伤痕。

The third scene shows Thoth leading the way and Amun following, going together to the palace where Queen Ahmose is. There is little text, but the image conveys solemnity: two gods, one before the other, as if going to perform a great rite. In this cycle the boundary between gods and mortals is crossed again and again: the king of the gods leaves his throne in heaven and comes in person to an earthly palace. Egyptian temple reliefs often unfold like a picture-strip, scene after scene, so that one walking along the colonnade reads the story in order. In Hatshepsut's day the reliefs were brightly painted, and traces of blue and red survive in places. Amun's figures were later damaged when Akhenaten promoted the sole worship of the Aten, many of Amun's names and images being hacked out and afterwards restored; and Hatshepsut's own figures were defaced after her death on the orders of Thutmose III. Today's viewer sees scenes that have suffered twice, and the tale of the sacred birth carries the scars of history.

### 04 · 王后的寝宫 / In the queen's chamber

阿蒙化作王后的丈夫图特摩斯一世的模样，来到雅赫摩斯的寝宫。王后正在宫中最美的房间里安睡，神的香气使她醒了过来，她对着神的威严微笑。神立刻走到她身边，让她看见了他作为神的真身。她看见神的美貌，心中欢喜，神的爱流进了她的四肢，整个宫殿都弥漫着神的芬芳，那香气全是来自蓬特的香料。浮雕中，阿蒙和王后面对面坐在一张床上，床由两位女神塞尔凯特和奈特托着，阿蒙把象征生命的符号递到王后面前。王后对神说：您的威名是多么伟大，看见您的面容是多么美好。阿蒙对她说：“与阿蒙结合的哈特谢普苏特”将是我放在你身体里的这个女儿的名字，她将在这整片土地上行使美好的王权。这一幕是整个浮雕的核心，可是它的表现方式极为含蓄：画面上没有任何露骨的描绘，只有香气、光芒和生命之符，神的结合被表现为一种神圣的给予。

Amun came to Ahmose's chamber in the form of her husband, Thutmose I. The queen was sleeping in the loveliest room of the palace; the fragrance of the god woke her, and she smiled at his majesty. At once he went to her and let her see him in his form as a god. She rejoiced at the sight of his beauty, his love passed into her limbs, and the palace was flooded with the god's fragrance — all its scents were those of Punt. In the relief Amun and the queen sit facing each other on a couch borne by the goddesses Selket and Neith, and Amun holds the sign of life to her. The queen says to the god: how great is your fame, how fine it is to see your face. Amun says to her: Khenemet-Amun Hatshepsut shall be the name of this daughter whom I have placed in your body; she shall exercise the excellent kingship in this whole land. This scene is the heart of the cycle, yet it is shown with great restraint: nothing explicit, only fragrance, radiance and the sign of life — the god's union is shown as a sacred gift.

### 05 · 阿蒙的吩咐 / Amun's command

阿蒙离开王后以后，来到陶工之神克努姆面前，吩咐他塑造将要出生的孩子。在浮雕中，阿蒙对克努姆说：去为我塑造她，我的女儿，还有她的卡，用这些高贵的四肢；我要让她成为一位比所有神都伟大的人，她将成为两地之王。克努姆回答说，他会照阿蒙的吩咐去做：他要把她塑造得比所有的神都美好，她的形象将在众神之上，为了上下埃及之王的尊严。在古埃及人的观念里，“卡”是人的生命力和精神上的分身，它与人同时诞生，伴随人的一生，人死后，卡依然存在，需要供品的供养。克努姆不仅要塑造孩子的身体，还要塑造她的卡，这意味着这个孩子从一开始就被赋予了完整的、神圣的生命。在前面的故事里，克努姆曾在拉-哈拉赫提的命令下为巴塔造了一个妻子；在这里，他又在阿蒙的命令下为一位未来的国王塑造身体。两次都是由一位更高的神发出命令，克努姆则用他的陶轮来完成。

Leaving the queen, Amun came to Khnum, the potter god, and commanded him to fashion the child to be born. In the relief Amun says to Khnum: go and fashion her for me, my daughter, and her ka, with these noble limbs; I will make her greater than all the gods; she shall be king of the Two Lands. Khnum answers that he will do as Amun commands: he will make her better than all the gods, her form exalted above theirs, for the dignity of the King of Upper and Lower Egypt. To the Egyptians the ka was a person's life-force and spiritual double, born with him, accompanying him through life, and surviving after death, when it needed offerings. Khnum is to shape not only the child's body but her ka, which means she is given a whole and sacred life from the start. In another tale Khnum made a wife for Bata at Pre-Harakhti's command; here he shapes the body of a future king at Amun's command. Both times a higher god gives the order, and Khnum carries it out on his wheel.

### 06 · 陶轮上的孩子 / The child on the potter's wheel

这一幕是整组浮雕中最著名的画面之一：羊头的克努姆坐在陶轮前，陶轮上站着两个一模一样的小孩，一个是哈特谢普苏特的身体，一个是她的卡。在克努姆对面，蛙头的生育女神赫凯特跪着，把象征生命的符号伸向两个孩子，赋予她们生命。题记中，克努姆对孩子说：我用阿蒙的这些四肢塑造了你，我来塑造你，要让你比所有的神都美好；我赐给你一切生命和满足、一切稳固、一切心中的喜悦；我赐给你一切健康、一切土地、一切国度和一切人民；我赐给你在荷鲁斯的宝座上作为两地之王出现，像拉一样，直到永远。在古埃及艺术中，陶轮上塑造婴儿的画面后来成了“神圣诞生”主题的标准图像，在卢克索神庙阿蒙霍特普三世的浮雕和后来托勒密时代神庙的“诞生之屋”里都能看到。克努姆用泥土造人的观念非常古老，这一形象说明：每一个生命都由神亲手塑造，而国王的生命则格外完美。

This is one of the most famous scenes of the cycle: ram-headed Khnum sits at his potter's wheel, on which stand two identical small children — Hatshepsut's body and her ka. Facing Khnum kneels the frog-headed birth goddess Heqet, holding the sign of life toward the two children to give them life. In the caption Khnum says to the child: I have formed you of these limbs of Amun; I have come to fashion you better than all the gods; I give you all life and satisfaction, all stability, all joy of heart; I give you all health, all lands, all countries and all people; I give you to appear upon the throne of Horus as king of the Two Lands, like Re, for ever. In Egyptian art the infant shaped on the wheel became the standard image of the divine birth, seen again in Amenhotep III's reliefs at Luxor temple and in the 'birth houses' of Ptolemaic temples. The idea of Khnum making people from clay is very old; the image says that every life is shaped by a god's hand, and the king's life is shaped to perfection.

### 07 · 托特的报喜 / Thoth's announcement

孩子在陶轮上塑造完成以后，托特又一次出场。这一次，他来到王后雅赫摩斯面前，向她报喜。在浮雕中，托特举起手，对王后说出了一长串称号：她是上下埃及之王的伟大的妻子，是众神喜爱的人，是两地的女主人，阿蒙对她的高贵地位感到满意。托特的这番话，等于正式宣布王后将成为神子的母亲。这一幕的构图很像后来许多文化中的“报喜”图像：一位神的使者来到一位将要怀上神子的女子面前，宣告她的荣耀。当然，古埃及的这组浮雕比那些后来的作品早了一千多年，它们之间是否有联系，学者们看法不一，本篇不作推测。对古埃及人来说，这一幕的意义在于确认：王后雅赫摩斯怀的孩子，是在众神的见证下、按照众神的意旨而来的。托特作为书记之神出现在这里，也像是在为这件事做一份神圣的记录。

When the child had been shaped on the wheel, Thoth appeared again — this time before Queen Ahmose, to announce the birth to her. In the relief Thoth raises his hand and addresses the queen with a long string of titles: great wife of the King of Upper and Lower Egypt, beloved of the gods, mistress of the Two Lands; Amun is satisfied with her great dignity. Thoth's words amount to a formal proclamation that the queen will be mother of the god's child. The composition resembles the 'annunciation' images of many later cultures: a divine messenger comes to a woman who will bear a god's child and declares her glory. These Egyptian reliefs are more than a thousand years older than those later works, and scholars disagree whether there is any link; this retelling makes no conjecture. For the Egyptians the meaning of the scene was to confirm that the child Queen Ahmose carried came by the gods' will and before their witness. Thoth, god of scribes, appears here as if making a sacred record of the event.

### 08 · 走向产房 / To the birth chamber

接下来，克努姆和赫凯特一左一右，牵着王后雅赫摩斯的手，领她走向产房。浮雕中的王后腹部微微隆起，神态安详，身后跟着一列神祇。这一幕把天上的创造和人间的生产连接在一起：在陶轮上塑造孩子的克努姆和赋予她生命的赫凯特，现在亲自陪伴母亲走向分娩的地方。古埃及的妇女分娩时，往往在一个专门的地方进行，有时是家中的一个小房间，有时是屋外搭起的凉棚，人们会在那里供奉保护产妇的神灵，如赫凯特、塔维瑞特和贝斯。在王室的诞生浮雕中，这些民间的习俗被升华成了神圣的仪式：护送王后的不是凡间的接生婆，而是创造生命的神本身。这一幕的题记中，赫凯特和克努姆向王后说着祝福的话，许诺她平安生产。从众神的会议到这里，整个故事一步一步地从天上走到了人间，下一幕，孩子就要诞生了。

Next Khnum and Heqet, one on each side, take Queen Ahmose by the hands and lead her to the birth chamber. In the relief the queen is gently swollen with child and serene, followed by a line of gods. The scene joins heavenly creation to earthly birth: Khnum, who shaped the child on the wheel, and Heqet, who gave it life, now accompany the mother in person to the place of birth. Egyptian women gave birth in a special place — sometimes a small room of the house, sometimes a bower set up outside — where they honoured the gods who protected women in labour, such as Heqet, Taweret and Bes. In the royal birth reliefs these everyday customs are raised into sacred ritual: the queen is escorted not by mortal midwives but by the very gods who make life. In the captions Heqet and Khnum speak blessings over the queen and promise her a safe delivery. From the council of the gods to here, the story has come step by step from heaven to earth; in the next scene the child is born.

### 09 · 诞生 / The birth

诞生的一幕画得庄严而拥挤：王后雅赫摩斯坐在宝座上，抱着刚出生的婴儿，四周围绕着许多神灵和精灵，它们分成几排，有的跪着，有的举着手表示欢呼。其中有掌管出生和命运的女神梅斯赫奈特，她坐在产砖之上——古埃及妇女分娩时蹲坐在砖上，这些砖本身也被神化了。梅斯赫奈特对孩子说出了祝福的话，许诺她将像拉一样永远出现在荷鲁斯的宝座上。浮雕中还有象征上下埃及的女神，有人认为她们在为新生的统治者守护两地。这一幕的许多细节已经残缺，学者们根据不同的线描和照片，对其中一些神灵的身份有不同的看法，本篇只讲能够辨认的部分。可以确定的是，浮雕的设计者想要表现的，是整个神界都在为这个孩子的诞生而欢庆。对一位要以女性身份称王的统治者来说，这样隆重的诞生场面，正是她最有力的宣言：她的王位不是偶然得来的，而是从出生那一刻起就被众神认定了的。

The birth scene is solemn and crowded: Queen Ahmose sits enthroned holding the newborn child, surrounded by many gods and spirits in rows, some kneeling, some raising their hands in acclaim. Among them is Meskhenet, goddess of birth and destiny, seated on the birth-brick — Egyptian women gave birth crouching on bricks, and the bricks themselves were deified. Meskhenet speaks blessings over the child, promising that she will appear on the throne of Horus like Re for ever. There are also goddesses of Upper and Lower Egypt, thought by some to guard the Two Lands for the new ruler. Many details of the scene are damaged, and scholars differ on the identity of some figures depending on the drawings and photographs they use; this retelling tells only what can be made out. What is certain is that the designers meant to show the whole divine world rejoicing at the child's birth. For a ruler who was to reign as king though a woman, so solemn a birth was her strongest declaration: her throne did not come by chance but was confirmed by the gods from the moment she was born.

### 10 · 献给阿蒙 / Presented to Amun

孩子出生以后，女神哈托尔把她抱到阿蒙面前。阿蒙伸出双手接过孩子，对她说话。题记中，阿蒙称她为自己的女儿：来吧，平安地来吧，我亲生的女儿，我心爱的玛阿特卡拉，你是占有两地、坐在荷鲁斯宝座上的国王，直到永远。玛阿特卡拉是哈特谢普苏特称王以后的王名，意思是“玛阿特是拉的卡”。在这里，阿蒙用她作为国王的名字来称呼一个刚出生的婴儿，说明这组浮雕是在她称王以后、按照王权的需要来设计的：她从出生起就被叫作国王。这一幕和故事的第一幕前后呼应：在开头，阿蒙在众神面前宣布他要生下一位统治者；在这里，他亲手接过这位统治者，承认她是自己的女儿，并且当着众神的面宣布她的王权。哈托尔在这组浮雕中扮演着母亲与保护者的角色：她是天空和爱的女神，也常被描绘成一头神牛，下一幕里，她还要哺育这个孩子。

After the birth the goddess Hathor carried the child to Amun. Amun stretched out his hands, took the child and spoke to her. In the caption Amun calls her his daughter: come, come in peace, daughter of my body, my beloved Maatkare; you are the king who takes possession of the Two Lands upon the throne of Horus for ever. Maatkare was the throne name Hatshepsut took as king, meaning 'Maat is the ka of Re'. Here Amun calls a newborn infant by her royal name, which shows that the cycle was designed after she became king and to serve her kingship: she is called king from her birth. The scene answers the first: at the start Amun announced to the gods that he would beget a ruler; here he takes that ruler in his own hands, acknowledges her as his daughter, and proclaims her kingship before the gods. Hathor plays mother and protector in the cycle: goddess of the sky and of love, often shown as a divine cow, she will also nurse the child in the next scene.

### 11 · 神的哺育 / The divine nursing

接下来，阿蒙吩咐众女神哺育这个孩子。浮雕中，哈托尔和其他乳母女神坐着，给婴儿和她的卡喂奶；在下面，还画着两头神牛，婴儿和她的卡跪在牛的身下吮吸乳汁。在古埃及的宗教中，哈托尔常以母牛的形象出现，她的乳汁被认为能赐予神圣的力量。法老被描绘成吮吸女神之乳的形象，在许多神庙和雕像中都能看到，这象征着国王从神那里获得了生命和权力。在这组浮雕里，哺育的场面也同时出现了孩子的卡，说明她的身体和她的精神分身都得到了神的滋养。尼罗河的神和其他赐予丰饶的神灵也出现在这一部分，为孩子带来生命所需的一切。这一幕温柔而庄严：一个刚出生的孩子，被天上的母亲们围绕着，喝着神的乳汁长大。对哈特谢普苏特来说，这一幕的意义在于强调她与众神的亲密关系——她不仅由神所生、由神塑造，还由神亲自养育。

Next Amun commands the goddesses to nurse the child. In the relief Hathor and other nurse goddesses sit suckling the infant and her ka; below, two divine cows are shown, with the infant and her ka kneeling to drink beneath them. In Egyptian religion Hathor often takes the form of a cow, and her milk was believed to give divine power. Pharaohs shown drinking from a goddess's breast appear in many temples and statues, symbolising that the king receives life and power from the gods. Here the nursing scene includes the child's ka as well, showing that both her body and her spiritual double are nourished by the gods. Nile gods and other bringers of plenty also appear in this part, bringing the child all that life needs. The scene is tender and solemn: a newborn surrounded by heavenly mothers, growing on the milk of the gods. For Hatshepsut its meaning is her closeness to the gods — not only born of a god and shaped by a god, but nursed by the gods themselves.

### 12 · 托特呈子与神定寿命 / Thoth presents the child, and the gods fix her lifetime

在诞生浮雕的最后几幕，托特把孩子和她的卡一起呈到阿蒙面前（学者布鲁纳编为第十四幕）。接着，众神为这个孩子定下了她的寿命（第十五幕）：画面中，托特和书写女神塞莎特等神灵记录下她将统治的年数——千百万年，象征着永恒的王权。在古埃及的观念里，神灵在王的诞生或加冕时为他定下在位的年数，并把它记录在圣树的叶子或年历上，这是确认王权的重要仪式。至此，整个故事画上了句号：从众神的会议开始，经过神与王后的结合、陶轮上的塑造、报喜、诞生、献子和哺育，最后以众神确定她的寿命结束。这组浮雕是古埃及“神圣诞生”主题最早的完整实例之一，后来阿蒙霍特普三世在卢克索神庙也刻下了类似的诞生浮雕。哈特谢普苏特用这个故事向世人宣告：她是阿蒙-拉亲生的女儿，她的王位来自天上。讽刺的是，在她死后，她的名字和形象被从许多纪念物上抹去，正是这些残存的神庙浮雕，让她的故事流传至今。

In the last scenes of the cycle Thoth presents the child and her ka together to Amun (Brunner's scene 14). Then the gods fix the child's lifetime (scene 15): Thoth, the writing goddess Seshat and other gods record the years she will reign — millions of years, a sign of eternal kingship. In Egyptian thought the gods set a king's years of rule at his birth or coronation and recorded them on the leaves of the sacred tree or on annals, an important rite confirming kingship. So the whole story closes: it began with the council of the gods and ran through the union of the god and the queen, the shaping on the wheel, the announcement, the birth, the presentation and the nursing, and ends with the gods fixing her lifetime. The cycle is one of the earliest complete examples of the Egyptian 'divine birth' theme; later Amenhotep III carved a similar birth cycle at Luxor temple. With this story Hatshepsut declared that she was the true daughter of Amun-Re and that her throne came from heaven. Ironically, after her death her name and images were erased from many monuments, and it is these surviving temple reliefs that have carried her story to us.
