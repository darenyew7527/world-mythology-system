## 阅读边界

三国吴人徐整的《三五历纪》已经失传，只在后世类书中留有引文。《艺文类聚》卷一引它说：天地未分时混沌如鸡蛋，盘古生在其中；一万八千年后天地开辟，清者为天、浊者为地；天每天高一丈，地每天厚一丈，盘古每天长一丈，又过一万八千年，天极高、地极深、盘古极长。

Xu Zheng's Sanwu liji is lost, surviving only in later encyclopedias. Yiwen leiju juan 1 quotes it: before heaven and earth parted, all was chaos like a hen's egg, and Pangu was born inside; after 18,000 years they opened, clear becoming heaven and murky becoming earth; each day heaven rose a zhang, earth thickened a zhang and Pangu grew a zhang, until after another 18,000 years heaven was highest, earth deepest and Pangu tallest.

### 01 · 混沌如鸡蛋 / Chaos like an egg

很久以前，天和地还没有分开，到处是一团混沌，样子像一个鸡蛋。盘古就生在这团混沌之中。

Long ago heaven and earth were not yet apart; everything was one chaos shaped like a hen's egg, and Pangu was born inside it.

> 证据说明：《艺文类聚》卷一引《三五历纪》；《太平御览》卷一引文字句略异。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.china.pangu_egg`
> 未知／边界：原书已佚，只能依靠类书引文。

### 02 · 清者为天，浊者为地 / Clear above, murky below

过了一万八千年，天地开辟：轻而清的阳气上升成为天，重而浊的阴气下沉成为地。盘古就在天地之间，一天之中变化九次，比天更神，比地更圣。

After 18,000 years heaven and earth opened: the light, clear yang rose to become heaven, the heavy, murky yin sank to become earth. Pangu stood between them, changing nine times a day, more divine than heaven and wiser than earth.

> 证据说明：同一引文“阳清为天，阴浊为地”。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.china.pangu_separation`

### 03 · 一天长高一丈 / A zhang a day

从此天每天升高一丈，地每天加厚一丈，盘古也每天长高一丈。这样又过了一万八千年，天高到了极点，地深到了极点，盘古也长到了极点。在这之后，才有了三皇。

From then on heaven rose a zhang each day, earth grew a zhang thicker, and Pangu grew a zhang taller. After another 18,000 years heaven was as high as it could be, earth as deep, and Pangu as tall. Only after that came the Three Sovereigns.

> 证据说明：同一引文“天日高一丈……盘古日长一丈”。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.china.pangu_growth`
> 未知／边界：“盘古垂死化身为日月山川”出自另一部书，本故事不合并。
