## 阅读边界

梅芙用女儿、财宝和诗人的讽刺逼迫库丘林的结拜兄弟费迪亚德出战。两个曾在斯卡塔赫门下一同学艺的朋友在浅滩上苦战四天，白天拼杀，夜里互赠草药和食物；第四天，库丘林用可怕的迦耶·博尔加杀死了费迪亚德，抱着他的尸体痛哭。库丘林的父亲苏阿尔塔姆到艾温·马哈呼喊求援，阿尔斯特人终于从衰弱中醒来。在加雷赫的大战中，弗格斯遵守诺言在库丘林面前退让，四省大军溃散，库丘林饶了梅芙的性命。可是褐色公牛已经被带到了康诺特，它与白角公牛在一整夜里跑遍了爱尔兰，把对手撕成碎片，最后回到故乡，心碎而死。

With her daughter, her treasures and the threat of poets' satire, Medb forces Cú Chulainn's foster-brother Fer Diad to fight him. The two friends who learned arms together under Scáthach fight four days at the ford, sending each other herbs and food each night; on the fourth day Cú Chulainn kills Fer Diad with the dreadful Gáe Bulga and weeps over him. His father Sualtam's cry at Emain Macha rouses the Ulstermen from their debility. In the great battle at Gárech, Fergus keeps his promise and yields to Cú Chulainn, the host of the four provinces breaks, and Cú Chulainn spares Medb's life. But the Brown Bull has been driven to Connacht; through a whole night it fights the White-horned across Ireland, tears it to pieces, returns home, and dies when its heart breaks.

### 01 · 梅芙的诱惑 / Medb's offer

勇士们一个接一个死在浅滩上，梅芙和艾利尔商量：派谁去才能打败库丘林？他们一致认为，只有费迪亚德能做到。费迪亚德是库丘林的结拜兄弟，两人曾一起在阿尔巴的女武士斯卡塔赫门下学习武艺，他们的本领几乎一样，只是库丘林学会了迦耶·博尔加的用法，费迪亚德没有学；而费迪亚德有一身像角一样坚硬的皮肤，任何刀剑都刺不穿。可是费迪亚德不肯和自己的兄弟作战，拒绝了梅芙的使者。梅芙就派去了德鲁伊、讽刺诗人和咒术师，威胁他说，如果他不来，他们就要用讽刺诗羞辱他，让他的脸上长满三个水泡：耻辱、污点和羞愧，让他在死前就失去名誉。费迪亚德为了自己的名誉，只好来了。梅芙热情地款待他，等他喝醉了，许诺给他：一辆价值四个七个女奴的马车，十二个人的武装，从平原上分给他一块土地，免除他和他子孙的一切赋税，还把她的女儿芬达维尔嫁给他，外加她自己的友谊。费迪亚德要求六位担保人立誓保证这些承诺，梅芙一一满足了。第二天，他答应出战。弗格斯听说了，赶去警告库丘林。

As champion after champion fell, Medb and Ailill agreed that only Fer Diad could overcome Cú Chulainn: his foster-brother, who had learned arms with him under the woman-warrior Scáthach in Alba, equal to him in every feat except the Gáe Bulga, which only Cú Chulainn had learned; and Fer Diad had a skin of horn that no weapon could pierce. He refused to fight his brother, so Medb sent druids, satirists and spell-makers to threaten him with satire that would raise three blisters on his face — shame, blemish and disgrace — so that he would lose his honour before he died. For his honour's sake he came. Medb feasted him, and when he was drunk promised him a chariot worth four times seven bondmaids, equipment for twelve men, land on the plain, freedom from tribute for him and his descendants, her daughter Finnabair as wife, and her own friendship. He demanded six sureties for these, and she gave them, and next day he agreed to fight. Fergus heard and went to warn Cú Chulainn.

### 02 · 第一天：投枪 / The first day: casting-weapons

第二天清晨，费迪亚德来到浅滩，在浅滩北岸等候，库丘林从南岸来了。两人隔着河水对望，费迪亚德说：你怎么敢来和我决斗？当年我们在斯卡塔赫那里，你不过是我的侍从，给我准备武器和床铺。库丘林说：那时我年纪小，确实是你的侍从，可是今天，世上没有一个勇士是我打不过的。我们曾经一同学艺，一同出生入死，同睡一张床，是心心相印的兄弟，你不该为了一个女人来和我作对。两人争论了很久，终于决定开战。库丘林让费迪亚德先选武器，费迪亚德说：我们先用投掷的兵器吧。他们拿起了八面有边的盾牌、八支标枪、八把象牙柄的短剑和八支短矛，像蜜蜂在晴天里飞来飞去一样，把兵器向对方投去，从清晨一直投到中午，没有一件兵器没有击中对方的盾牌，可是谁也没有伤到谁。中午，他们又换上了磨光的轻矛，一直投到黄昏，两人都受了伤，流了血。天黑了，他们停战，走到对方身边，互相拥抱，亲吻了三次。那一夜，他们的马在同一片草地上吃草，他们的车夫在同一堆火边取暖。库丘林把阿尔斯特的医生用的草药分一半送给费迪亚德，费迪亚德把梅芙送来的美食和酒分一半送给库丘林。

At dawn Fer Diad waited on the north side of the ford and Cú Chulainn came to the south. Fer Diad taunted him that at Scáthach's he had been only his servant, readying his weapons and his bed; Cú Chulainn answered that he had been young then, but now there was no warrior he could not overcome, and reproached Fer Diad for coming against his foster-brother, with whom he had shared training, dangers and a bed, for a woman's sake. At last they fought. Cú Chulainn gave Fer Diad the choice of weapons, and he chose casting-weapons: shields with rims, eight darts, eight ivory-hilted swords and eight spears, which flew between them like bees on a fine day from morning till noon, every one striking a shield and none wounding; from noon to evening they cast polished javelins, and both were wounded. At nightfall they went to each other, embraced and kissed three times. That night their horses grazed in one paddock and their charioteers warmed themselves at one fire; Cú Chulainn sent Fer Diad half of every healing herb the Ulster physicians brought him, and Fer Diad sent Cú Chulainn half of the food and drink Medb sent.

### 03 · 第二天与第三天 / The second and third days

第二天，轮到库丘林选择兵器，他说：今天我们用宽刃的重矛吧，坐在战车上作战，离得更近一些，好让我们早点分出胜负。他们驾着战车，从清晨战到黄昏，用重矛互相刺击，两人的身上都布满了伤口，鸟儿可以从他们的伤口里飞进飞出。天黑后，他们又像前一天一样，互相拥抱，亲吻了三次，把马放在一起吃草，把车夫留在同一堆火边，库丘林又把草药送给费迪亚德，费迪亚德又把食物和酒送给库丘林。第三天清晨，库丘林看见费迪亚德走来，觉得他的脸色阴沉，和往日不同。库丘林说：你今天脸色这么难看，费迪亚德，你是来送死的。费迪亚德说：我被梅芙拖进了这场决斗，我知道，我们两人之中，今天必有一个要倒下。这一天，费迪亚德选择了沉重的、能劈开一切的大剑，两人从清晨砍杀到黄昏，从肩膀到大腿砍下了一块块的血肉。天黑后，他们分开了，可是这一夜，两人没有再拥抱，他们的马不再在一起吃草，他们的车夫也不再在同一堆火边取暖。两个朋友心里都明白，明天将是最后一天。

On the second day Cú Chulainn chose broad heavy spears, fighting from their chariots so that they might come to a decision sooner, and from morning to evening they pierced each other until birds could have flown in and out of their wounds. At nightfall they embraced and kissed three times as before, their horses grazed together and their charioteers shared a fire, and they exchanged herbs and food. On the third morning Fer Diad's face was gloomy and changed; Cú Chulainn told him he looked like a man come to his death, and Fer Diad said Medb had dragged him into the fight and one of them must fall that day. He chose heavy, hard-smiting swords, and from morning till evening they hewed great pieces of flesh from each other's shoulders and thighs. That night they parted without embracing; their horses did not graze together nor their charioteers share a fire. Both knew the next day would be the last.

### 04 · 迦耶·博尔加 / The Gáe Bulga

第四天清晨，费迪亚德知道这一天将决定一切，他穿上了最坚固的战甲，在腹部绑上一块大石头，以防备那可怕的迦耶·博尔加。库丘林说：今天我们用什么兵器？费迪亚德说：由你来选。库丘林说：那就用“浅滩的技艺”吧。费迪亚德听了，心中一沉，他知道库丘林在浅滩上从未被打败过。两人在浅滩中激战，库丘林几次跳上费迪亚德的盾牌边缘，想从上面刺他的头，都被费迪亚德用盾牌震落到水里。库丘林的身体开始发生可怕的变形，他像一个巨人一样高大起来，费迪亚德的剑刺进了他的胸膛，鲜血染红了河水。这时，库丘林向他的车夫莱格喊道：把迦耶·博尔加给我！莱格把那支矛顺着河水推了过去，库丘林用脚趾夹住，向费迪亚德掷去。那支矛刺穿了费迪亚德的铁甲和石头，进入他的身体后，矛头上的三十个倒钩张开，充满了他的全身。费迪亚德倒了下去，说：你用得不公平，我的死是你造成的。他倒在库丘林的怀里，死去了。

On the fourth morning Fer Diad, knowing this day would decide everything, put on his strongest armour and bound a great flat stone over his belly against the Gáe Bulga. Cú Chulainn asked what weapons they would use; 'Your choice,' said Fer Diad, and Cú Chulainn chose the feat of the ford, at which Fer Diad's heart sank, for Cú Chulainn had never been beaten at a ford. Fighting in the water, Cú Chulainn leapt again and again onto the rim of Fer Diad's shield to strike at his head, and each time Fer Diad shook him off into the ford. Then Cú Chulainn's distortion came on him and he swelled like a giant, while Fer Diad's sword pierced his breast and his blood reddened the stream. 'The Gáe Bulga!' he called to Láeg, who sent it down the stream; Cú Chulainn caught it between his toes and cast it at Fer Diad. It went through the iron and the stone, and inside him its thirty barbs opened and filled his body. Fer Diad fell, saying it was unfairly done and his death was Cú Chulainn's doing, and died in his arms.

### 05 · 库丘林的哀歌 / Cú Chulainn's lament

库丘林抱起费迪亚德，带着他的武器和铠甲，把他抬过浅滩，放在北岸。然后，库丘林倒在他的身边，昏了过去。莱格喊醒他，说：快起来，库丘林，康诺特的大军要来了，现在不是躺着的时候。库丘林说：我为什么要起来？我亲手杀死了他。他看着费迪亚德的脸，唱起了哀歌：那是游戏，那是欢乐，直到我遇见了费迪亚德；我们一起学艺，一起在斯卡塔赫的身边，我们发誓永远不互相为敌。他说：在我遇见费迪亚德之前，所有的战斗都只是游戏，如今他倒下了，我的心也像一块破碎的石头。他回忆起两人在阿尔巴的日子，回忆起斯卡塔赫的教导，回忆起他们并肩作战的往事，一遍又一遍地呼唤着他的名字。最后，他对莱格说：剖开费迪亚德的身体，把迦耶·博尔加取出来吧，我不能没有我的武器。莱格取出了那支矛，库丘林看着满身鲜血的兄弟，说：直到最后的审判之日，我也会记着你。从这一刻起，库丘林自己也伤重得无法站立，有好些天，他只能躺在那里养伤。

Cú Chulainn lifted Fer Diad and carried him with his arms and armour across the ford to the north side; then he fainted beside him. Láeg roused him: the host of Connacht was coming, and this was no time to lie down. Why should he rise, said Cú Chulainn, when he had killed this man with his own hand? Looking on Fer Diad's face, he sang his lament: all was play and pleasure until he met Fer Diad; they had learned together at Scáthach's side and sworn never to be enemies; every fight before had been a game, and now Fer Diad had fallen and his heart was like a broken stone. Again and again he called his name, recalling their days in Alba. At last he had Láeg cut the Gáe Bulga out of the body, for he could not be without his weapon, and looking on his brother's blood he said he would remember him to the day of doom. From then he was so badly wounded that for days he could not stand.

### 06 · 苏阿尔塔姆的呼喊 / Sualtam's cry

库丘林的父亲苏阿尔塔姆听说儿子伤重，赶来看他。库丘林对父亲说：你到艾温·马哈去，告诉阿尔斯特人起来作战，我已经无力再守护他们了，他们的牛群被抢走了，他们的妇女被掳走了。苏阿尔塔姆骑上库丘林的灰马，飞驰到艾温·马哈，高声呼喊：阿尔斯特的男人们被杀了，女人们被掳走了，牛群被赶走了！可是阿尔斯特人还在衰弱中，没有人回答他。按照阿尔斯特的规矩，在国王开口之前，谁也不能说话，而国王只能在德鲁伊们开口之后说话。他喊了三次，德鲁伊卡斯巴德终于说：是谁在这里杀人、抢劫、掳掠？苏阿尔塔姆又重复了一遍，没有人理睬他，他愤怒地掉转马头要离开。马猛地一转身，苏阿尔塔姆自己的盾牌的锋利边缘竟割断了他的脖子，他的头落进了盾牌里。灰马驮着他的尸体又回到了王宫，盾牌里的头仍在喊着同样的话：阿尔斯特的男人们被杀了，女人们被掳走了，牛群被赶走了！国王康胡巴尔说：这喊声太响了，天还在上面，大地还在下面，大海还在四周，除非天塌下来，海水淹没大地，我一定会把每一头牛送回它的牛棚，把每一个女人送回她的家。阿尔斯特人终于从衰弱中醒来了。

Cú Chulainn's father Sualtam came to his wounded son, who sent him to Emain Macha to rouse the Ulstermen: their men were being slain, their women carried off, their cattle driven away. Sualtam rode Cú Chulainn's Grey of Macha to Emain and cried it aloud, but the Ulstermen lay in their debility and none answered, for by Ulster's custom no one might speak before the king, and the king not before the druids. After his third cry the druid Cathbad asked who was killing and plundering; Sualtam repeated it, was ignored, and turned his horse in anger to leave. As the horse wheeled, the sharp rim of his own shield cut off his head, which fell into the shield; the horse carried the body back into the palace, and the head in the shield still cried the same words. 'That cry is too loud,' said King Conchobar: 'the sky is above us, the earth beneath us, the sea around us; unless the sky falls with its stars, or the sea overwhelms the earth, I will bring every cow back to its byre and every woman to her home.' The Ulstermen rose from their debility.

### 07 · 阿尔斯特集结 / The muster of Ulster

康胡巴尔派出使者，召集阿尔斯特各地的勇士们。从北到南，从东到西，阿尔斯特人纷纷从衰弱中站起来，拿起武器，向国王身边集结。他们在斯兰内会合，然后向梅芙大军所在的加雷赫平原进发。梅芙的信使马克·罗斯被派去察看敌情，他站在高处，看见了阿尔斯特人的大军：一队又一队勇士从山丘上走下来，平原上升起了浓雾，雾中闪着光亮，像天上的星星，那是勇士们的眼睛；雷声隆隆，那是战车的车轮；还有一阵狂风，那是勇士们的呼吸。马克·罗斯一队一队地描述来者的模样：有的勇士披着紫色的斗篷，佩着金色的胸针，有的头发金黄，有的盾牌上镶着白银。流亡的弗格斯站在梅芙身边，听马克·罗斯描述一队，就说出那一队的首领是谁：这是康胡巴尔国王本人，这是他的儿子，这是某某英雄，他们个个都是勇猛无比的战士。梅芙和艾利尔听着，心中越来越不安。弗格斯说：今天，阿尔斯特人会让你们明白，他们是什么样的人。

Conchobar sent messengers to summon the champions of Ulster, and from north and south, east and west the Ulstermen rose from their weakness, took up arms and gathered to the king at Slemain, then marched to the plain of Gárech where Medb's host lay. Medb's messenger Mac Roth went to look and saw them come: troop after troop descending the hills; a mist on the plain lit by sparks like stars, which were the warriors' eyes; thunder, which was the chariot wheels; a great wind, which was the breath of heroes. He described each company — purple cloaks with golden brooches, golden hair, shields rimmed with silver — and the exiled Fergus beside Medb named each leader: this is King Conchobar himself, this his son, this such a hero, each a mighty warrior. Medb and Ailill grew more and more uneasy. 'Today,' said Fergus, 'the Ulstermen will show you what they are.'

### 08 · 三座山丘 / The three hills

加雷赫和伊尔加雷赫的大战开始了。两军相遇，喊杀声震天。梅芙的大军三次冲破阿尔斯特人的阵线，每一次都杀到了阿尔斯特人的后方。弗格斯手握他的宝剑卡拉德博尔格——这把剑举起来像一道彩虹——在阿尔斯特人中间左右砍杀，他要找到国王康胡巴尔。弗格斯找到了康胡巴尔，用卡拉德博尔格砍向他的盾牌，盾牌发出了巨大的呻吟声。全阿尔斯特人的盾牌都跟着呻吟起来。这时，有一位勇士赶来抓住了弗格斯的手臂——各个本子里此人的名字说法不一，有的说是康胡巴尔的儿子科马克·孔德·隆加斯——对他说：不要砍你的国王，不要毁灭你的族人，你的愤怒应该发泄在别处。把你的剑转向山丘吧，砍向那些山顶，这样你就不会杀死你的同胞。弗格斯听从了他的劝告，转过身去，举起卡拉德博尔格，向三座山丘砍去，削平了三座山头。直到今天，米斯平原上还有三座被削平山顶的小山，人们称它们为“米斯的三座秃丘”。就在这时，躺在床上养伤的库丘林听见了卡拉德博尔格砍击康胡巴尔盾牌的呻吟声。

The battle of Gárech and Irgárech began. Three times Medb's host broke through the Ulster lines into their rear. Fergus, wielding his sword Caladbolg, which when raised was like a rainbow, hewed through the Ulstermen seeking King Conchobar, and when he found him struck his shield so that it groaned aloud, and all the shields of Ulster groaned with it. Then a warrior seized his arm — the versions differ on who, some naming Conchobar's son Cormac Cond Longas — saying: let him not strike his king nor destroy his people, but turn his wrath elsewhere — strike the hills. Fergus turned and with Caladbolg struck off the tops of three hills, which stand to this day as the three Maela of Meath, the three bald hills. And at that moment Cú Chulainn, lying wounded, heard the groan of Conchobar's shield under Caladbolg's stroke.

> 证据说明：《夺牛长征记》（邓恩译本）·加雷赫之战。

### 09 · 弗格斯退让 / Fergus gives way

库丘林听见了盾牌的呻吟声，问：是谁敢砍我的养父康胡巴尔的盾牌？人们告诉他，那是弗格斯。库丘林伤得很重，阿尔斯特人用铁箍和木桩把他固定在床上，免得他的伤口裂开。可是他一听到这个声音，就猛地挣脱了所有的束缚，那些铁箍和柱子都被崩飞了，他的伤口也全部裂开，鲜血涌了出来。他跳上战车，冲向战场，找到了弗格斯，对他说：转过身来，弗格斯，否则你会后悔的。弗格斯问：是谁这么傲慢地对我说话？库丘林说：是你的养子库丘林，你答应过我，有一天会在我面前退让，现在就是那一天。弗格斯说：我确实答应过。他转过身去，向后退了三大步。他一退，他所率领的阿尔斯特流亡者也跟着撤退了；伦斯特人和芒斯特人看见弗格斯撤退，也跟着撤离了战场，只剩下梅芙和艾利尔率领的康诺特人，还有他们的子孙们。正午时分，库丘林冲进了战场。

Cú Chulainn heard the groan and asked who dared strike the shield of his foster-father Conchobar; it was Fergus, he was told. He was so badly wounded that the Ulstermen had bound him to his bed with hoops and stakes lest his wounds open; but he burst every bond, the hoops and stakes flew off and his wounds broke open. He sprang into his chariot, found Fergus and told him to turn back or suffer for it. Who spoke so proudly? asked Fergus. His foster-son Cú Chulainn, who had his promise to give way before him one day — and this was the day. 'I promised it,' said Fergus, and turned and went back three great steps. As he withdrew, the Ulster exiles under him withdrew too, and the men of Leinster and Munster, seeing Fergus go, left the field, so that only Medb and Ailill with the men of Connacht and their sons remained. At midday Cú Chulainn came into the battle.

> 证据说明：《夺牛长征记》（邓恩译本）·弗格斯退让。
> Claim: `claim.v0340.tain_2.09`

### 10 · 梅芙被饶 / Medb spared

到了傍晚，梅芙的大军已经溃不成军，只剩下一些残兵护送着她向西撤退。梅芙把褐色公牛交给了一队人，让他们抄近路赶回康诺特，无论如何，她终于得到了那头牛。在撤退的路上，梅芙不得不停下来，躲到一旁，正在这时，库丘林找到了她。梅芙对他说：我请求你的保护，饶了我吧。库丘林说：如果我在你背后杀死你，那也是你应得的。可是他没有下手，因为他不杀女人。他让梅芙离开，保护着她撤退，直到康诺特的大军全部越过了阿特隆的浅滩，回到了自己的国土上。弗格斯看着溃败的大军，对梅芙说：今天我们这些人遭遇的事，真是一场羞辱。可是一群由母马领头的马队，走错了路、被毁掉了，这本来就是常有的事。他的意思是，这场灾难都是因为梅芙的领导。库丘林在撤退的大军后面，用他的剑砍断了三座山丘的山顶，作为对弗格斯的那三座山丘的回应。

By evening Medb's host was routed, a remnant guarding her retreat westward. She had sent the Brown Bull ahead by a short way to Connacht with a company of men — she had the bull at least. On the way back Medb had to stop and step aside, and there Cú Chulainn came upon her. She asked his protection and his mercy. He said it would be no more than her due if he killed her from behind; but he did not, for he would not kill a woman. He let her go and guarded her retreat until the host of Connacht had crossed the ford at Áth Luain into their own land. Looking on the routed army, Fergus said to Medb that it was a shameful day for them all, but it was usual for a herd led by a mare to stray and be destroyed — meaning the disaster came from her leadership. Behind the retreating host, Cú Chulainn struck the tops from three hills with his sword in answer to Fergus's three hills.

> 证据说明：《夺牛长征记》（邓恩译本）·梅芙被饶。

### 11 · 两头公牛的决斗 / The fight of the bulls

褐色公牛被赶到了康诺特，它看见了一片陌生的、美丽的土地，发出了三声震耳欲聋的吼叫。艾利尔牛群里的白角公牛芬本纳赫听见了，没有一头公牛敢在它的地盘上这样吼叫，它昂起头，向克鲁亨跑来。所有的人都聚集在一起，要看两头公牛决斗。人们推举了刚刚结束战斗、还在养伤的布里克留来做裁判，因为他对两头牛都没有偏心。两头公牛相遇了，它们用脚刨地，把泥土抛过头顶，眼睛像燃烧的火球，鼻孔喷着气，像铁匠的风箱，两头牛的角撞在一起，发出雷鸣般的巨响。它们打斗着，把站在旁边的裁判布里克留踩在脚下，踩死了。夜幕降临，人们只能听见它们的吼叫和撞击声。在这一整夜里，两头公牛打遍了整个爱尔兰，它们所到之处，大地被踩出了深深的痕迹。第二天早晨，人们看见褐色公牛向西经过了克鲁亨，它的角上挑着白角公牛的残骸。

Driven into Connacht, the Brown Bull saw the fair strange land and bellowed three times. Ailill's White-horned Bull, Finnbennach, heard — no bull dared bellow so in its pastures — and came toward Cruachan with its head raised. All gathered to watch the fight, choosing as judge Bricriu, who was recovering from his wounds, since he favoured neither. The bulls met, pawing the earth and flinging it over their backs, their eyes like balls of fire, their nostrils blowing like smiths' bellows, their horns clashing like thunder. They trampled Bricriu to death beneath their hooves. Night fell and only their bellowing and clashing could be heard; through that night they fought across all Ireland, their hooves marking the ground wherever they went. In the morning the Brown Bull was seen passing west of Cruachan with the remains of the White-horned on its horns.

> 证据说明：《夺牛长征记》（邓恩译本）·公牛之战。

### 12 · 褐色公牛之死 / The death of the Brown Bull

褐色公牛带着白角公牛的残骸一路向东奔跑，把对手的肢体一块块抛撒在爱尔兰各地。它在阿特隆的浅滩上抛下了白角公牛的腰，从此那里被叫作“阿特·卢安”，意思是“腰的浅滩”；它在特雷姆抛下了肝脏，在别处又抛下了其他的部分，每一个地方都因此得到了名字。褐色公牛回到了家乡库利，它狂怒地冲向那里的人群，杀死了许多人。最后，它来到了一座山脊上，它的心终于在胸中碎裂了，它倒在地上死去了。那座山脊从此被叫作“德鲁姆·塔尔布”，意思是“公牛的山脊”。这场为了一头牛而发动的战争，就这样结束了：两头公牛都死了，无数的勇士倒在了浅滩上和平原上。艾利尔和梅芙与阿尔斯特人、与库丘林讲和，此后七年间，阿尔斯特和康诺特之间没有再发生战争。芬达维尔留在了库丘林那里，康诺特人回到了自己的土地上，阿尔斯特人也带着荣誉回到了艾温·马哈。故事在这里结束，抄写者在书的末尾写道：愿所有忠实地记住这个故事、不再添加别的东西的人得到祝福。

The Brown Bull ran east with the White-horned's remains, scattering its limbs across Ireland: at the ford of Athlone it dropped the loins, so the place was called Áth Luain, 'ford of the loins', and at Trim the liver, and other parts elsewhere, each place taking its name from them. It came home to Cúailnge and raged among the people there, killing many, until on a ridge its heart broke in its breast and it fell dead; the ridge was called Druim Tairb, 'the ridge of the bull'. So the war fought for a bull ended, with both bulls dead and countless warriors fallen at fords and on plains. Ailill and Medb made peace with Ulster and with Cú Chulainn, and for seven years there was no war between Ulster and Connacht; Finnabair stayed with Cú Chulainn, the men of Connacht returned home, and the Ulstermen went back to Emain Macha in glory. At the end of the tale the scribe wrote a blessing on all who would faithfully remember it and add nothing to it.

> 证据说明：《夺牛长征记》（邓恩译本）·结局。
