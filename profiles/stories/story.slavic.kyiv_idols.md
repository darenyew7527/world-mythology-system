## 阅读边界

《往年纪事》980 年条：弗拉基米尔在基辅宫殿外的山岗上立起神像——银头金须的木雕佩伦，还有霍尔斯、达日博格、斯特里博格、西马尔格尔和莫科什，人们向它们献祭。988 年他受洗后下令推倒神像，佩伦被绑在马尾上拖下山，丢进第聂伯河。

The Primary Chronicle's annal for 980: Volodimer sets up idols on the hill outside his Kyiv palace — a wooden Perun with a silver head and golden moustache, then Khors, Dazhbog, Stribog, Simargl and Mokosh — and people make offerings to them. After his baptism in 988 he has them cast down; Perun is tied to a horse's tail, dragged down the hill and thrown into the Dnieper.

### 01 · 山岗上的神像 / Idols on the hill

《往年纪事》980 年条记载：弗拉基米尔开始在基辅独自为王，就在宫殿庭院外的山岗上立起一排神像。

The Primary Chronicle's annal for 980 records that when Volodimer began to reign alone in Kyiv, he set up a row of idols on the hill outside the palace courtyard.

> 证据说明：《往年纪事》980 年条。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.slavic.pvl_980_witness`

### 02 · 银头金须的佩伦 / Perun with a silver head

最前面的是佩伦：木头雕成，头是银的，胡须是金的。

First stood Perun, carved of wood, with a silver head and a golden moustache.

> 证据说明：《往年纪事》980 年条。
> Claim: `claim.v060.slavic.perun_980_image`

### 03 · 名单最后的莫科什 / Mokosh at the end of the list

接下来是霍尔斯、达日博格、斯特里博格、西马尔格尔，名单的最后一位是莫科什。人们向这些神像献上祭品。编年史只记下了莫科什的名字，没有说她掌管什么。

Then came Khors, Dazhbog, Stribog and Simargl, and last on the list, Mokosh. People brought offerings to the idols. The chronicle records only Mokosh's name, not what she governed.

> 证据说明：《往年纪事》980 年条神名名单。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.slavic.mokosh_980`
> 未知／边界：关于莫科什职能的说法来自其他材料与现代研究，本故事不补写。

### 04 · 988 年：神像倒下 / 988: the idols fall

八年后，弗拉基米尔受洗，下令推倒这些神像：有的砍碎，有的烧掉。佩伦被绑在马尾上拖下山，有人用棍子抽打，最后被扔进第聂伯河。

Eight years later Volodimer was baptized and ordered the idols overthrown — some cut to pieces, others burned. Perun was tied to a horse's tail and dragged down the hill, beaten with sticks, and thrown into the Dnieper.

> 证据说明：《往年纪事》988 年条。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.slavic.perun_cast_down`
> 未知／边界：这是皈依一方的记载，不是对斯拉夫宗教的中立描述。
