# 一座金字塔上的两位神：大神庙 / Two gods on one pyramid — the Templo Mayor

## 阅读边界

特诺奇蒂特兰的大神庙顶上并立着两座神殿：南边供奉太阳战神、墨西加人的守护神维齐洛波奇特利，北边供奉与农业相连的雨神特拉洛克。墨西哥国家人类学与历史研究所说，神庙始建于1325年，此后多次重建与扩建。

Atop the Templo Mayor of Tenochtitlan stood two shrines: on the south, Huitzilopochtli, solar god of war and patron of the Mexica; on the north, Tlaloc, god of rain linked to agriculture. INAH says the first small temple was built at the city's founding in 1325 and was rebuilt and enlarged many times.

### 01 · 南边的战神 / The war god on the south

大神庙南侧的神殿属于维齐洛波奇特利——墨西加人的太阳战神，也是他们的守护神。

The southern chapel of the Templo Mayor belongs to Huitzilopochtli, the Mexica's solar god of war and their patron.

> 证据说明：INAH 大神庙遗址博物馆说明。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.mexica.huitz_templo`

### 02 · 北边的雨神 / The rain god on the north

北侧的神殿属于特拉洛克——掌管雨水、与农业紧密相连的神。战争与农业，两位神并立在同一座金字塔的顶端。

The northern temple belongs to Tlaloc, god of rain and closely tied to agriculture. War and farming stand side by side at the top of one pyramid.

> 证据说明：INAH 大神庙遗址博物馆说明。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.mexica.tlaloc_templo`

### 03 · 一层层长高的神庙 / A temple that grew in layers

据 INAH 介绍，墨西加人在1325年建立特诺奇蒂特兰时修了一座小神庙，此后经过多次重建和扩建，越修越高。

According to INAH, the Mexica built a small temple when they founded Tenochtitlan in 1325, and it was rebuilt and enlarged many times over.

> 证据说明：INAH 大神庙遗址博物馆说明。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.mexica.templo_layers`
