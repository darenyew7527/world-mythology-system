# 太阳神乌图与吉尔伽美什的远征 / Utu helps Gilgameš set out against Ḫuwawa

## 阅读边界

吉尔伽美什决定进山建立名声。伙伴恩奇都提醒他：山是太阳神乌图的事，得先告诉乌图。吉尔伽美什向乌图祈求，乌图接受了他的眼泪作为礼物，赐给他七位勇士做向导，去面对山中的守护者胡瓦瓦。

Gilgameš resolves to make his name in the mountains. His companion Enkidu warns that the mountains are Utu's concern, so the sun god must be told. Gilgameš prays, and Utu accepts his tears as a gift and grants him seven warriors as guides to face Ḫuwawa, the guardian of the mountains.
