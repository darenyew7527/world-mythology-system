## 阅读边界

The Acropolis Museum and Greek official heritage record preserve the public route layer from Athens along the Sacred Way to the Telesterion at Eleusis; the internal mysteries are not reconstructed.

### 01 · 雅典的公开起点 / Public beginning in Athens

博物馆档案说宗教进程从雅典开始，并把雅典厄琉西尼翁出土材料纳入路线展览。

The museum record says the religious procession began in Athens and includes material from the Athenian Eleusinion in the route exhibition.

### 02 · 沿圣道前往厄琉息斯 / Along the Sacred Way to Eleusis

公开进程由雅典沿圣道通向厄琉息斯；达夫尼阿佛洛狄忒圣所等地点属于道路考古层，不是虚构的冒险节点。

The public procession moved from Athens along the Sacred Way toward Eleusis; sites such as the Aphrodite sanctuary at Daphne belong to the road’s archaeology, not an invented adventure sequence.

> 证据说明：卫城博物馆路线说明与既有圣道档案。
> Claim: `claim.v080.eleusinia_sacredway`
> 未知／边界：每一停驻点与日期尚未在当前来源中完整列出。

### 03 · 进入厄琉息斯遗址 / Entering the Eleusinian sanctuary

希腊官方遗产资料说明，圣道在厄琉息斯延续为进程道路，并通向遗址内部。

Greek official heritage information describes the Sacred Way continuing as the Processional Road inside the Eleusinian site.

> 证据说明：希腊官方遗产页的遗址路线说明。
> Claim: `claim.v080.sacredway_eleusis`
> 未知／边界：这里只处理可见道路与遗址关系。

### 04 · 在仪式厅结束的公开路线 / Public route culminating at the Telesterion

博物馆档案把公开进程的终点置于厄琉息斯圣所的特勒斯特里翁；这不授权重建保密入会仪式。

The museum record places the public procession’s culmination at the Telesterion in the Eleusinian sanctuary; this does not authorize reconstruction of secret initiation rites.
