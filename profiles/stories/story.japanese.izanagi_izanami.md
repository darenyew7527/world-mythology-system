## 阅读边界

天神命伊邪那岐与伊邪那美巩固漂浮的大地。两神站在天浮桥上，用天沼矛搅动海水，滴下的盐水堆成淤能碁吕岛。伊邪那美生下火神后被烧伤而死；伊邪那岐追到黄泉，她却说已经吃了黄泉的食物。伊邪那岐回来后洗净身体，最后从左眼、右眼和鼻子生出天照大神、月读和须佐之男。

The heavenly deities command Izanagi and Izanami to consolidate the drifting land. From the Floating Bridge of Heaven they stir the brine with a jewelled spear, and the drops become Onogoro Island. Izanami dies after bearing the fire deity; Izanagi follows her to Yomi, but she has already eaten of its hearth. Returning, he purifies himself, and from his left eye, right eye and nose are born Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo.

### 01 · 天地初开 / When heaven and earth began

《古事记》的正文从天地开辟写起。它说：天地刚刚分开的时候，在高天原上出现了三位神：天之御中主神、高御产巢日神和神产巢日神。张伯伦把后两个名字分别译作“高贵而能生成万物的奇妙之神”和“神圣而能生成万物的奇妙之神”。这三位神都是独自出现的，出现以后就隐去了身形，书中没有讲他们的样子，也没有讲他们做了什么。那时候，下面的国土还很年轻，像漂在水上的油脂一样，像水母一样随波漂流，没有一处是固定的。就在这时，有一样东西像芦苇的嫩芽一样萌发出来，从中又出现了两位神：宇摩志阿斯诃备比古迟神和天之常立神。这五位神被称为“别天神”，也就是与后来诸神不同的、特别的天神，他们同样是独自出现、随即隐去身形的神。其中的高御产巢日神和神产巢日神，在后面的故事里还会一次次出现：一位主持高天原上的商议，一位把死去的大国主救活。故事就从这样一个漂浮不定、还没有固定下来的世界开始。

The Kojiki proper opens at the time when heaven and earth first began. In the Plain of High Heaven there came into being three deities: Ame-no-minaka-nushi, Takami-musubi and Kami-musubi, the last two of whom Chamberlain renders the High-August-Producing-Wondrous Deity and the Divine-Producing-Wondrous Deity. All three came into being alone and hid their persons; the text says nothing of how they looked or what they did. The land below was still young, like floating oil, drifting about like a jellyfish, with nothing yet fixed. Then, from something that sprouted up like a reed-shoot, two more deities came into being, Umashi-ashi-kabi-hiko-ji and Ame-no-toko-tachi, who likewise came alone and hid their persons. These five are set apart as the separate Heavenly Deities. Takami-musubi and Kami-musubi will return again and again: the one presiding over the councils of High Heaven, the other bringing the dead Ōkuninushi back to life. The story begins in a world still afloat and not yet made firm.

### 02 · 神世七代 / The seven divine generations

接着出现的是国之常立神和丰云野神，他们也是独自出现、隐去身形的神。此后诸神开始成对出现：宇比地迩神与须比智迩神，角杙神与活杙神，意富斗能地神与大斗乃辨神，於母陀流神与阿夜诃志古泥神，最后是伊邪那岐神与伊邪那美神。张伯伦把这最后两个名字译作“邀请之男神”和“邀请之女神”：名字里的“伊邪那”有邀请、招呼的意思，“岐”和“美”分别表示男和女。从国之常立神到伊邪那岐、伊邪那美，被称为“神世七代”：前面两位独自出现的神各算一代，后面每一对男女神合算一代。这些名字的意思并不都清楚，有的像在说泥土和沙，有的像在说木桩，有的像在说宽大的门户，有的像在说完满的面貌和敬畏的言辞，像是一步步描写大地成形、万物有了模样的过程，但书中并没有讲他们做了什么。真正开始动手造国的，是最后出现的这一对兄妹神。

Next came into being Kuni-no-toko-tachi and Toyo-kumo-nu, who also came alone and hid their persons. After them the deities came in pairs: U-hiji-ni and Su-hiji-ni, Tsunu-guhi and Iku-guhi, Oho-to-no-ji and Oho-to-no-be, Omo-daru and Aya-kashiko-ne, and last of all Izanagi and Izanami, whom Chamberlain renders the Male-Who-Invites and the Female-Who-Invites: izana- carries the sense of inviting, and -ki and -mi mark male and female. From Kuni-no-toko-tachi to Izanami are called the Seven Divine Generations: the two single deities count a generation each, and each pair counts as one. The meanings of the names are not all clear; some seem to speak of mud and sand, of posts, of great doorways, of a perfect face and of awe, as if the land were slowly taking shape, but the text does not say what these deities did. The work of making the land falls to the last pair.

### 03 · 淤能碁吕岛 / The island of Onogoro

天上的众神一齐命令伊邪那岐和伊邪那美：去把这片漂浮的国土整治好，使它坚固，把它生出来。众神的命令里有三件事——整治、巩固、生出——后面的故事，正是一步步完成这三件事。众神赐给他们一支装饰着美玉的长矛，叫作天之沼矛。两位神站在天之浮桥上，把长矛伸进下面的海水里，“咕噜咕噜”地搅动起来——这是书中模仿搅动海水的声音。他们把长矛提起来，从矛尖滴下的海水层层堆积，变成了一座岛，这就是淤能碁吕岛。张伯伦把这个名字译作“自凝岛”，意思是“自己凝结而成的岛”。这是天地间第一块坚固的土地。淤能碁吕岛究竟在哪里，书中没有说，后世有许多说法，都只是推测。两位神从天之浮桥上走下来，降落到这座岛上。造国的工作，就要从这里开始了。

All the heavenly deities commanded Izanagi and Izanami to make, consolidate and give birth to the drifting land — three tasks that the rest of the story works through one by one — and granted them a jewelled spear, the Ame-no-nuhoko. Standing on the Floating Bridge of Heaven, the two pushed the spear down and stirred the brine until it went curdle-curdle, the text imitating the sound. When they drew the spear up, the brine that dripped from its point piled up and became an island: Onogoro, which Chamberlain renders Self-Curdling Island. It was the first firm ground. Where Onogoro lies the text does not say; later identifications are only conjecture. The two deities descended from the Floating Bridge onto the island, and from there the making of the land began.

### 04 · 天之御柱与水蛭子 / The heavenly pillar and the leech-child

两位神在岛上竖起了一根天之御柱，又建起一座八寻殿——“寻”是两臂张开的长度，八寻就是极言其宽大。伊邪那岐问伊邪那美：你的身体是怎样长成的？伊邪那美说：我的身体长成了，只有一处没有长合。伊邪那岐说：我的身体也长成了，只有一处长得多余。我想用多余的地方填补你没有长合的地方，生出国土，你看怎样？伊邪那美说：好。伊邪那岐说：那么我们绕着天之御柱走，相遇以后结为夫妻。你从右边绕，我从左边绕。两人绕柱相遇时，伊邪那美先开口说：啊，多么好的男子！伊邪那岐接着说：啊，多么好的少女！说完他又说：女子先开口是不吉利的。可他们还是结合了，生下的第一个孩子是水蛭子。书中没有描写他的样子，只说他们把这个孩子放进芦苇编的小船里，任它漂走了。接着又生了淡岛，也不算在他们的孩子之中。

On the island they set up a heavenly pillar and built a hall eight fathoms wide. Izanagi asked Izanami how her body was formed. She answered that it was fully formed except for one place that had not grown together; he said his was fully formed except for one place that had grown to excess, and proposed to fill her place with his and so give birth to lands. She agreed. 'Then let us walk round this heavenly pillar,' he said, 'and meet and be joined. You go round from the right, I from the left.' When they met, Izanami spoke first: 'Ah, what a fair and lovely youth!' Then Izanagi: 'Ah, what a fair and lovely maiden!' Afterwards he said it was unlucky for the woman to speak first. Yet they came together, and their first child was a leech-child, whom they put into a boat of reeds and let float away. Next they bore the island of Aha, which also was not reckoned among their children.

### 05 · 重新绕柱，生下大八岛 / The Great Eight Islands

两位神商量说：我们生的孩子不好，应该去问问天上的众神。他们一起回到天上，请求众神的指示。天上的众神用太占——一种烧鹿肩骨看裂纹的占卜——占了一卦，说：因为女子先开口，所以不好。回去重新说一遍吧。两人回到岛上，像以前一样绕着天之御柱走，这一次伊邪那岐先说：啊，多么好的少女！伊邪那美再说：啊，多么好的男子！这样结合以后，他们生下的是一个个岛屿：先是淡路岛；接着是伊予之二名岛，它一个身体有四张脸，每张脸都有名字：伊予国叫爱比卖，赞岐国叫饭依比古，粟国叫大宜都比卖，土左国叫建依别；然后是隐岐岛；筑紫岛也是一个身体四张脸，分别是筑紫国、丰国、肥国和熊曾国；再往后是壹岐岛、对马岛、佐渡岛，最后是大倭丰秋津岛，也就是本州。因为这八个岛最先生出，所以这片土地被称为“大八岛国”。之后他们又生下了六个小岛。

The two took counsel: 'The children we have borne are not good. We should report this before the heavenly deities.' They went up together, and the heavenly deities divined by grand divination — reading the cracks in a burned deer's shoulder-blade — and said: 'It was not good because the woman spoke first. Go down again and say it anew.' So they walked round the pillar as before, and this time Izanagi spoke first. Then they gave birth to islands: Awaji first; then Iyo-no-futana, one body with four faces, each named — Iyo called Ehime, Sanuki called Iiyori-hiko, Aha called Ōgetsu-hime and Tosa called Takeyori-wake; then Oki; then Tsukushi, likewise one body with four faces, the lands of Tsukushi, Toyo, Hi and Kumaso; then Iki, Tsushima, Sado, and last Ō-yamato-toyo-aki-tsu-shima. Because these eight islands were born first, the country is called the Land of the Eight Great Islands. After them they bore six smaller islands.

### 06 · 众神诞生 / The birth of the deities

生完了国土，伊邪那岐和伊邪那美又接着生神。书中把这些神的名字一个个记下来：大事忍男神，石土毘古神，石巢比卖神，大户日别神，天之吹男神，大屋毘古神，风木津别之忍男神；然后是海神大绵津见神，河口之神速秋津日子神和速秋津比卖神——这一对河口之神又生下了沫那艺、沫那美、颊那艺、颊那美等掌管水沫与水面的神；接着是风神志那都比古神，树木之神久久能智神，山神大山津见神，原野之神鹿屋野比卖神，又名野椎神，大山津见神和野椎神又生下了掌管土地、雾气、山谷的许多神。他们还生下了一位叫作“鸟之石楠船”的神，又叫天鸟船，是诸神乘坐的船；又生下了掌管食物的大宜都比卖。有的名字一长串，读起来就像是在点数天地间的万物：石头、门户、屋顶、海浪、水沫、雾气、山谷……世界就这样一样样有了自己的神灵。可是，在这一连串的诞生之后，等待着他们的却是一场灾祸。

Having borne the lands, Izanagi and Izanami went on to bear deities, and the text records each name: Oho-koto-oshi-wo, Iha-tsuchi-biko, Iha-su-hime, Oho-to-hi-wake, Ame-no-fuki-wo, Oho-ya-biko, Kaza-mo-tsu-wake-no-oshi-wo; the great sea deity Oho-wata-tsu-mi; the river-mouth deities Haya-aki-tsu-hiko and Haya-aki-tsu-hime, who in turn bore deities of foam and of the water's surface; the wind deity Shina-tsu-hiko, the tree deity Kuku-no-chi, the great mountain deity Oho-yama-tsu-mi and the moor goddess Kaya-nu-hime, also called Nu-dzu-chi, who together bore deities of earth, mist and valleys. They bore the Bird's Rock-Camphor-Boat, also called the Heavenly Bird-Boat, and the food goddess Oho-ge-tsu-hime. Some names are very long, so that the list reads like a counting of everything in heaven and earth: rocks, doorways, roofs, waves, foam, mist, valleys. So, thing by thing, the world received its deities. But at the end of this long run of births a calamity was waiting.

### 07 · 火神之火 / The fire deity

最后，伊邪那美生下了火之迦具土神，也叫火之夜艺速男神、火之炫毘古神。因为生下了火神，她的阴部被烧伤，从此一病不起。她在病中呕吐，从呕吐物中生出了金山毘古神和金山毘卖神，也就是金属之神；从粪便中生出了波迩夜须毘古神和波迩夜须毘卖神，也就是黏土之神；从尿中生出了水神弥都波能卖神，以及和久产巢日神，和久产巢日神的女儿就是掌管食物的丰宇气毘卖神。即使在病痛之中，她的身体也还在不停地生出新的神来：金属、黏土、水和五谷，都是从这位垂死的母神身上来的。可是终于，伊邪那美因为生下火神而离开了人世——《古事记》用的说法是“神避”，也就是神的隐去。书中总结说，伊邪那岐与伊邪那美一起生下的岛屿共十四个，神共三十五位。创造了国土和万物之神的母亲，就这样因为生下火而死去，这是《古事记》里第一次出现死亡。

Last of all Izanami bore the fire deity Kagutsuchi, also called Hi-no-yagi-haya-wo and Hi-no-kaga-biko. In bearing this child her private parts were burned, and she lay down sick. From her vomit were born Kana-yama-biko and Kana-yama-bime, deities of metal; from her dung, Hani-yasu-biko and Hani-yasu-bime, deities of clay; from her urine, the water goddess Mitsu-ha-no-me and Waku-musubi, whose daughter is Toyo-uke-bime, goddess of food. Even in sickness her body went on bringing forth deities: metal, clay, water and grain all came from the dying mother. But at last, because she had borne the fire deity, Izanami divinely retired — the Kojiki's word for her passing away. The text sums up that together the pair had borne fourteen islands and thirty-five deities. So the mother of the lands and their deities dies from giving birth to fire; it is the first death in the Kojiki.

### 08 · 哀哭与斩杀火神 / Mourning and the slaying of the fire deity

伊邪那岐悲痛地说：我亲爱的妹妹啊，我怎么能用你换来这一个孩子！他匍匐在伊邪那美的枕边和脚边痛哭，从他的眼泪里生出了一位女神，叫作泣泽女神，住在香山下的畝尾木本。伊邪那岐把伊邪那美安葬在出云国与伯伎国交界的比婆山上。接着，他拔出佩带的十拳剑，砍下了儿子迦具土神的头。剑尖上溅出的血落在岩石上，生出了石拆神、根拆神和石筒之男神；剑锷附近的血溅出来，生出了甕速日神、樋速日神和建御雷之男神，也就是后来降服出云的建御雷神；从剑柄上滴下、从指缝间漏出的血，又生出了暗淤加美神和暗御津羽神两位山谷之水的神。从迦具土神被砍杀的身体各处——头、胸、腹、阴部、左右手、左右脚——又生出了八位山神。伊邪那岐用来斩杀火神的这把剑，名叫天之尾羽张，又名伊都之尾羽张。

Izanagi cried: 'Oh my beloved younger sister! That I should have exchanged you for this single child!' He crept weeping round her pillow and her feet, and from his tears was born the goddess Naki-sawa-me, who dwells at the foot of the trees on the hill of Unewo by Mount Kagu. He buried Izanami on Mount Hiba on the border of Izumo and Hahaki. Then he drew the ten-grasp sword at his side and cut off the head of his son Kagutsuchi. The blood on the point of the sword splashed on the rocks and became Iha-saku, Ne-saku and Iha-tsutsu-no-wo; the blood near the guard became Mika-haya-bi, Hi-haya-bi and Take-mika-dzu-chi-no-wo, the Takemikazuchi who will later bring Izumo to submission; the blood dripping from the hilt through his fingers became Kura-okami and Kura-mitsu-ha, deities of the valley waters. From Kagutsuchi's head, chest, belly, loins, hands and feet eight mountain deities were born. The sword was named Ame-no-wo-ha-bari, also Itsu-no-wo-ha-bari.

### 09 · 黄泉：不要看我 / Yomi: do not look at me

伊邪那岐思念妻子，追到了黄泉国。伊邪那美从宫殿紧闭的门里出来迎接他，伊邪那岐就隔着门对她说：我亲爱的妹妹啊，我和你一起造的国土还没有完成，请你回来吧！伊邪那美说：可惜你没有早一点来。我已经吃了黄泉灶火煮的食物——这句话的意思是，她已经属于黄泉了。不过，既然我亲爱的哥哥特意来了，我想回去。我要去和黄泉的神商量一下。在这期间，请你千万不要看我。她说完回到殿里，过了很久都没有出来。伊邪那岐等不下去了，他折下插在左边发髻上的多齿梳子的一根边齿，点起火来，走进殿里察看。只见伊邪那美的身上爬满了蛆虫，八位雷神盘踞在她身上：头上是大雷，胸口是火雷，腹部是黑雷，阴部是拆雷，左手是若雷，右手是土雷，左脚是鸣雷，右脚是伏雷。这就是后来故事里所说的“黄泉八雷神”。

Wishing to see his wife again, Izanagi followed her to the Land of Yomi. She came out to meet him from the closed door of the palace. 'My beloved younger sister,' he said, 'the lands that you and I were making are not yet finished. Come back!' She answered: 'Alas that you did not come sooner! I have eaten of the food of the furnace of Yomi. Yet since my beloved elder brother has come, I wish to return; I will discuss it with the deities of Yomi. Do not look at me.' She went back in and stayed so long that he could not wait. He broke off an end tooth of the comb in the left bunch of his hair, lit it, and went in. Maggots were swarming over her, and eight thunder deities had been born on her body: Great-Thunder on her head, Fire-Thunder on her breast, Black-Thunder on her belly, Crack-Thunder below, Young-Thunder on her left hand, Earth-Thunder on her right, Sounding-Thunder on her left foot and Reclining-Thunder on her right.

> 证据说明：《古事记》张伯伦译本第九节。

### 10 · 黄泉丑女与三个桃子 / The Ugly Females of Yomi and the three peaches

伊邪那岐看见这副情景，吓得转身就逃。伊邪那美说：你让我蒙受了羞辱！她派黄泉丑女去追赶他。伊邪那岐取下头上的黑色发饰扔在身后，发饰立刻变成了葡萄，丑女们停下来捡葡萄吃；吃完了又追上来，他又折下右边发髻上的梳子扔出去，梳子变成了竹笋，丑女们又停下来拔竹笋吃。伊邪那美又派八位雷神，率领一千五百名黄泉的军队来追。伊邪那岐拔出十拳剑，一边在身后挥舞，一边逃跑。追到黄泉比良坂——黄泉与人间交界的山坡——的坡脚下时，他摘下长在那里的三个桃子，向追兵投去，追兵全都逃回去了。伊邪那岐对桃子说：你们帮助了我，以后苇原中国的人们遇到苦难的时候，你们也要像这样帮助他们。他给桃子取名意富加牟豆美命。

Terrified at the sight, Izanagi turned and fled. 'You have put me to shame!' cried Izanami, and sent the Ugly Females of Yomi after him. He threw down his black head-dress, which turned into grapes, and while they stopped to eat he fled on; when they came after him again he broke the comb from the right bunch of his hair and threw it, and it turned into bamboo shoots, which they pulled up and ate. Then she sent the eight thunder deities with fifteen hundred warriors of Yomi. He drew his ten-grasp sword and fled brandishing it behind him. At the foot of the Even Pass of Yomi, the slope between Yomi and the living world, he took three peaches growing there and smote the pursuers with them, and they all fled back. He said to the peaches: 'As you have helped me, so help the people of the Central Land of Reed-Plains when they are in trouble,' and named them Oho-kamu-dzu-mi.

> 证据说明：《古事记》张伯伦译本第九节。

### 11 · 千引石前的诀别 / Farewell at the thousand-draught rock

最后，伊邪那美亲自追来了。伊邪那岐搬来一块要一千个人才拉得动的大石头——千引石，堵住了黄泉比良坂。两位神隔着石头面对面站着，说出了诀别的话，书中称之为“度事户”，一般理解为宣告断绝夫妻关系的话。伊邪那美说：我亲爱的哥哥，既然你这样做，我就每天把你国家的人勒死一千个。伊邪那岐回答：我亲爱的妹妹，如果你那样做，我就每天建起一千五百座产房。所以，世上每天必定有一千人死去，也必定有一千五百人出生。从此生与死被这块石头隔开，而活着的人总是比死去的多。伊邪那美从此被称为黄泉津大神，又因为她追上了伊邪那岐，也被叫作道敷大神。堵住黄泉坡的那块石头，被称为道反之大神，也叫塞坐黄泉户大神。书中说，所谓黄泉比良坂，就是现在出云国的伊赋夜坂。

Last of all Izanami herself came in pursuit. Izanagi pulled up a rock that would take a thousand men to lift and blocked the Even Pass of Yomi with it. Standing face to face on either side of the rock, they spoke the words of parting, which the text calls the exchange of the door of words, usually understood as a declaration ending the marriage. Izanami said: 'My beloved elder brother, if you do this, I will strangle a thousand of the people of your land every day.' Izanagi answered: 'My beloved younger sister, if you do that, I will set up fifteen hundred birth-houses every day.' That is why a thousand people surely die every day and fifteen hundred are surely born: the rock divides life from death, and the living always outnumber the dead. Izanami was thenceforth called the Great Deity of Yomi, and also the Road-Reaching Great Deity because she had overtaken him. The rock is called the Great Deity of the Road-Turning-Back. The Even Pass of Yomi, the text says, is the pass of Ifuya in Izumo.

> 证据说明：《古事记》张伯伦译本第九节。
> Claim: `claim.v0350.kojiki_izanagi.11`

### 12 · 禊祓与三贵子 / Purification and the three noble children

伊邪那岐说：我去了一个丑恶污秽的国家，必须洗净我的身体。他来到筑紫日向的橘之小门的阿波岐原，在河口举行禊祓。他把手杖、腰带、袋子、衣服、裤子、帽子和左右手的手镯一件件扔掉，每扔下一件，就生出一位神，从手杖生出的是冲立船户神，一共生出了十二位神。接着他说：上游水太急，下游水太缓。就走进中游的水里洗身，先生出了由黄泉污秽而来的八十祸津日神和大祸津日神，又生出了纠正灾祸的神直毘神、大直毘神和伊豆能卖神，然后在水底、水中、水面洗涤，生出了三位绵津见神和三位筒之男神。书中说，三位绵津见神是阿昙连一族祭祀的祖神，三位筒之男神是墨江的三位大神。最后，他洗左眼时生出了天照大神，洗右眼时生出了月读命，洗鼻子时生出了建速须佐之男命。故事从这里接到下一部：三位尊贵的孩子即将各自领受父亲的命令。

'I have been to a hideous and polluted land,' said Izanagi, 'so I must purify my body.' He came to the plain of Ahagi by the river-mouth of Tachibana in Himuka in Tsukushi and performed purification. As he threw down his staff, girdle, bag, garment, trousers, hat and the bracelets of each arm, a deity was born of each — from the staff, Tsuki-tatsu-funa-do — twelve in all. Then, saying that the upper waters were too swift and the lower too sluggish, he went down into the middle reach to wash. First were born the two deities of calamity, Ya-so-maga-tsu-bi and Oho-maga-tsu-bi, from the pollution of Yomi; then Kamu-nahobi, Oho-nahobi and Idzu-no-me to set the calamity right; then, as he washed at the bottom, in the middle and on the surface of the water, three Wata-tsu-mi deities and three Tsutsu-no-wo deities. The text adds that the three Wata-tsu-mi are the ancestral deities worshipped by the Adzumi clan, and the three Tsutsu-no-wo the three great deities of Suminoe. Last, as he washed his left eye, Amaterasu was born; his right eye, Tsukuyomi; his nose, Take-haya-Susanoo. The story passes here to the next part, where the three noble children receive their father's charge.

> 证据说明：《古事记》张伯伦译本第十节。

### 01 · 天浮桥上的长矛 / A spear from the Floating Bridge

天上的众神命令伊邪那岐和伊邪那美去巩固还在漂浮的大地，并交给他们一支镶玉的天沼矛。两神站在天浮桥上，把矛伸进海里搅动；提起矛时，矛尖滴下的盐水层层堆积，成了淤能碁吕岛。

The heavenly deities command Izanagi and Izanami to make firm the drifting land and give them a jewelled spear. Standing on the Floating Bridge of Heaven, they stir the brine; when they lift the spear, the drops from its tip pile up into Onogoro Island.

> 证据说明：张伯伦译本第三节（kj010）。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.japan.onogoro_result`
> 未知／边界：《日本书纪》说两神是自己决定动手，本版不合并。

### 02 · 火神出生 / The birth of the fire deity

两神结合，生出许多岛屿和神灵。可是伊邪那美生下火神时被烧伤，病倒在床，最终“神去”——也就是死去。

Joined together, the pair bring forth islands and deities. But when Izanami gives birth to the Deity-of-Fire she is burned, falls sick, and at length 'divinely retires' — she dies.

> 证据说明：张伯伦译本第七节（kj014）。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.japan.izanami_death`

### 03 · 追到黄泉 / Following her to Yomi

伊邪那岐想再见到妻子，便追到黄泉，请她回去：“我们造的国土还没有完成。”伊邪那美回答：你来得太晚了，我已经吃了黄泉灶火煮的东西——不过她答应去和黄泉的神商量。

Longing to see her, Izanagi follows Izanami to Yomi and asks her to come back, for the lands they made are not yet finished. She answers that he has come too late: she has already eaten from the hearth of Yomi — yet she agrees to consult the deities there.

> 证据说明：张伯伦译本第九节（kj016）。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.japan.izanagi_yomi`
> 未知／边界：之后的逃离情节可参看本库“黄泉中的八雷神”故事。

### 04 · 洗出三位贵神 / Three deities born of washing

伊邪那岐回来后在水中洗净自己，身上不断化生出神。最后，他洗左眼时生出天照大神，洗右眼时生出月读，洗鼻子时生出须佐之男。

Back from Yomi, Izanagi bathes to purify himself, and deities are born as he washes. Last of all, washing his left eye brings forth Amaterasu, his right eye Tsukuyomi, and his nose Susanoo.
