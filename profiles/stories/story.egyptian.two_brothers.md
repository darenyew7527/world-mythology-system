## 阅读边界

阿努比斯和巴塔是一对亲兄弟，哥哥有家有妻，弟弟像儿子一样替他耕田放牛，连牛都会告诉他哪里的草最好。嫂子诱惑巴塔遭到拒绝，反诬他施暴，哥哥拿着长矛躲在牛棚门后要杀他，母牛们警告了巴塔。巴塔逃跑时向拉-哈拉赫提祈祷，太阳神在兄弟之间造出一条满是鳄鱼的河。天亮后巴塔说出真相，以自残明志，前往松树谷，把心放在松树的花上；哥哥回家杀了不忠的妻子。九柱神在松树谷遇见巴塔，拉-哈拉赫提命克努姆为他造了一个妻子，七位哈托尔却预言她将死于刀下。大海卷走她的一缕香发送到法老那里，法老派人把她接来，她泄露了巴塔的秘密，松树被砍倒，巴塔死去。哥哥看见啤酒起沫，找了三年才找到那颗心，把弟弟救活。巴塔先后化作公牛和两棵鳄梨树，最后借一片木屑重生为法老的儿子，登上王位，审判了妻子，让哥哥做了王储。

Anubis and Bata are brothers; the elder has house and wife, and the younger works his fields and herds his cattle like a son — the cattle even tell him where the grass is best. The elder's wife tries to seduce Bata, is refused, and accuses him falsely; Anubis waits behind the byre door with a spear, and the cows warn Bata. Fleeing, Bata prays to Pre-Harakhti, who sets a water full of crocodiles between the brothers. At dawn Bata tells the truth, maims himself to prove it, and goes to the Valley of the Pine, placing his heart on the pine's flower; Anubis goes home and kills his faithless wife. The Ennead meets Bata in the valley, and Pre-Harakhti has Khnum fashion him a wife, though the Seven Hathors foretell she will die by the knife. The sea carries a scented lock of her hair to Pharaoh, who has her brought to him; she betrays Bata's secret, the pine is cut, and Bata dies. Anubis, seeing his beer froth, searches three years for the heart and revives his brother. Bata becomes a bull, then two persea trees, and at last, through a splinter, is reborn as Pharaoh's son, takes the throne, judges his wife and makes his brother crown prince.

### 01 · 两兄弟 / The two brothers

纸草一开头说：从前有两个兄弟，同一个母亲、同一个父亲所生。哥哥叫阿努比斯，弟弟叫巴塔。阿努比斯有房子，有妻子，弟弟和他住在一起，就像他的儿子一样。是弟弟为他做衣服，是弟弟跟着他的牛群到田野里去，是弟弟犁地，是弟弟为他收割，田里的所有活计都是弟弟替他做的。他的弟弟是一个出色的人，在整个国家里没有人能和他相比，因为神的力量就在他身上。这个开头描绘了一幅古埃及农村的家庭图景：哥哥是一家之主，弟弟没有成家，在哥哥家里干活，像儿子侍奉父亲一样侍奉哥哥。纸草特意说明两兄弟是同父同母所生，又说巴塔身上有“神的力量”，为后面的奇迹埋下了伏笔。这份纸草是第十九王朝的书吏恩纳纳抄写的，字迹工整，保存得几乎完整，是古埃及流传下来最著名的故事之一。故事里的兄弟俩都用了神的名字，可在纸草中，他们首先是一对普通的农家兄弟。

The papyrus begins: once there were two brothers of one mother and one father. The elder was called Anubis and the younger Bata. Anubis had a house and a wife, and his younger brother lived with him like a son. It was the younger who made clothes for him, who went behind his cattle to the fields, who ploughed and who harvested for him; he did all the work of the fields. His younger brother was an excellent man; there was none like him in the whole land, for the strength of a god was in him. The opening paints a family of the Egyptian countryside: the elder brother is head of the house, the younger unmarried, working in his brother's house and serving him as a son serves a father. The papyrus stresses that they had the same father and mother, and says Bata had 'the strength of a god', preparing the wonders to come. The papyrus was copied by the Nineteenth-Dynasty scribe Ennana in a careful hand and is almost complete — one of the most famous tales to survive from ancient Egypt. Both brothers bear the names of gods, but in the papyrus they are first of all two farming brothers.

### 02 · 会说话的牛 / The cattle that spoke

每天早晨，巴塔赶着牛群出去，晚上再把它们赶回来，背上背着田里的各种蔬菜、牛奶、木柴和一切好东西，放在哥哥面前，哥哥和嫂子吃喝完毕，他才回到牛棚里，和牛群睡在一起。天亮以后，他做好饭，放在哥哥面前，哥哥给他带上去田里吃的面包，他就赶着牛群到田野里去。在路上，牛群会对他说：某某地方的草很好。他听了牛的话，就把牛赶到它们想去的那片好草地上。于是，他照看的牛长得非常好，生下的小牛也非常多。这一段带着童话般的色彩：牛会说话，巴塔能听懂牛的语言，人和动物之间有一种亲密的默契。古埃及人非常珍视他们的牛，在墓葬壁画里，常常能看到牧人赶着牛群过河、给母牛挤奶的场面。纸草让牛群开口说话，不仅表现了巴塔的善良与勤劳，也为后面的情节做了准备：正是这些被他细心照料的牛，在危急关头救了他的命。

Every morning Bata drove the cattle out and every evening brought them back, laden with all the vegetables of the field, with milk, with wood and with every good thing of the field, and laid them before his elder brother; when Anubis and his wife had eaten and drunk, Bata went to the stable and slept among his cattle. When the land grew light he prepared food and set it before his brother, who gave him bread for the field, and he drove the cattle out to the pastures. As he walked behind them they would say to him: 'The grass is good in such and such a place.' He listened to them and took them to the good grass they wanted. So the cattle in his care grew very fine and their calves were very many. The passage has a fairy-tale colour: cattle speak, Bata understands them, and man and beast share a close understanding. The Egyptians prized their cattle; tomb paintings often show herdsmen driving cattle through water and milking cows. Making the cattle speak shows Bata's goodness and diligence and also prepares the plot: these very cattle, tended with such care, will save his life.

### 03 · 嫂子的诱惑 / The temptation

到了耕种的季节，兄弟俩一起到田里去犁地。哥哥让弟弟回村里去取种子。巴塔回到家里，看见嫂子正坐着梳头，就对她说：起来，给我种子，我好赶回田里去，哥哥在等我。她说：自己去仓库拿吧，别打扰我梳头。巴塔走进仓库，拿了一个大罐子，装了许多种子——三袋大麦和两袋小麦——扛在肩上走了出来。嫂子看见他扛着这么重的东西，惊叹他的力气，心中起了邪念，便站起来拉住他，提出要他与她同床，还答应给他做漂亮的衣服。巴塔一听，就像上埃及的豹子一样大怒，对她说：你对我来说就像母亲，你的丈夫对我来说就像父亲，是他把我养大的。你对我说的这是多么大的罪过！不要再对我说这样的话，我不会告诉任何人，也不会让它从我嘴里传出去。说完，他扛起种子，回到了田里。纸草对这一段只用了几句话，重点在巴塔的拒绝：他把嫂子看作母亲，把哥哥看作父亲，坚守着家庭的伦常。

At ploughing time the brothers went together to plough. The elder sent the younger back to the village to fetch seed. Bata came home and found his brother's wife sitting and braiding her hair. He said: 'Get up and give me seed, so I can hurry back to the field; my brother is waiting.' She said: 'Go, open the storeroom and take what you want; don't make me leave my hair unfinished.' He went into the storeroom, took a large jar and loaded himself with a great deal of seed — three sacks of barley and two of emmer — and came out carrying it. Seeing him with so great a load, she marvelled at his strength, desire came into her heart, and she rose, took hold of him and asked him to lie with her, promising to make him fine clothes. At this Bata became like an Upper Egyptian leopard in fury and said: 'You are like a mother to me, and your husband like a father; he raised me. What is this great wrong you have said to me? Do not say it to me again, and I will tell no one; I will not let it come out of my mouth.' He took up his load and went to the field. The papyrus gives this only a few lines, its weight on Bata's refusal: he holds his brother's wife as a mother and his brother as a father, keeping the order of the family.

### 04 · 谎言 / The lie

巴塔走后，嫂子害怕自己说的话被传出去。她拿来油脂和布条，把自己弄成一副被人殴打过的样子，躺在床上，装作病了。傍晚，哥哥照常回到家里，弟弟赶着牛群跟在后面。哥哥走进屋里，发现妻子躺着，像是被人打了，她没有像往常那样往他手上倒水，也没有点灯，屋子里一片漆黑，她躺在那里呕吐。丈夫问她：谁和你说过话了？她说：除了你的弟弟，没有人和我说过话。他回来拿种子的时候，看见我一个人坐着，就对我说：来，我们一起躺一会儿，你把头发松开。我没有听他的，我说：我不是你的母亲吗？你的哥哥不就像你的父亲一样吗？他害怕了，就打了我，不让我告诉你。如果你让他活着，我就死给你看。等他晚上回来的时候，你就杀了他，因为他做了这样的坏事。这段谎言把巴塔说过的话原样反了过来：巴塔对她说的是“你就像我的母亲”，她却把这句话安在自己嘴上，把罪名推给了巴塔。

When Bata had gone, his brother's wife was afraid of what she had said. She took fat and grease and made herself look like one who had been beaten, and lay down as if ill. In the evening her husband came home as usual, his younger brother following with the cattle. Anubis entered the house and found his wife lying as though beaten; she did not pour water on his hands as usual, nor had she lit the lamp; the house was in darkness, and she lay vomiting. Her husband asked: 'Who has spoken with you?' She said: 'No one but your younger brother. When he came to fetch seed he found me sitting alone and said: Come, let us lie together for an hour; loosen your hair. I would not listen to him. I said: Am I not your mother, and is not your elder brother like a father to you? He was afraid and beat me so that I would not tell you. If you let him live, I shall die. When he comes home this evening, kill him, for he has done this evil thing.' Her lie turns Bata's words inside out: he had told her 'you are like a mother to me'; she puts those words in her own mouth and lays the guilt on him.

### 05 · 母牛的警告 / The cows' warning

哥哥一听，就像上埃及的豹子一样暴怒。他磨快了长矛，拿在手里，躲到牛棚的门后，要在弟弟晚上赶牛回来时杀死他。太阳落山了，巴塔照常背着田里的各种蔬菜回来。走在最前面的母牛一进牛棚就对它的牧人说：你的哥哥拿着长矛站在门后，要杀你，快从他面前逃走吧。巴塔听懂了领头母牛的话。第二头母牛进来，也说了同样的话。他从牛棚门下往里看，看见了哥哥的脚，哥哥正拿着长矛站在门后。巴塔放下背上的东西，拔腿就跑，哥哥拿着长矛在后面紧追。在这里，第二章里那些会说话的牛终于派上了用场：巴塔平日对它们的照顾，换来了它们在关键时刻的警告。纸草让两头母牛先后说出同样的话，这种重复是古埃及故事常用的手法，使情节显得庄重，也让读者感受到危险的迫近。从这一刻起，原本像父子一样亲密的兄弟，变成了追杀者和逃亡者。

Hearing this, the elder brother became like an Upper Egyptian leopard. He sharpened his spear, took it in his hand and stood behind the door of the stable to kill his younger brother when he came home in the evening with the cattle. When the sun set, Bata came home as usual, laden with every vegetable of the field. The lead cow entered the stable and said to her herdsman: 'Your elder brother is standing before you with his spear to kill you; run away from him.' He understood what his lead cow said. The next cow entered and said the same. He looked under the door of the stable and saw his brother's feet as he stood behind the door, spear in hand. He set down his load and fled, and his elder brother went after him with his spear. Here the speaking cattle of the second chapter come into their own: Bata's daily care for them is repaid by their warning at the crucial moment. The papyrus has two cows say the same words in turn — a repetition common in Egyptian tales, giving the scene weight and making the danger feel close. From this moment brothers once close as father and son become pursuer and fugitive.

### 06 · 鳄鱼之河 / The water full of crocodiles

巴塔一边逃跑，一边向太阳神拉-哈拉赫提祈祷，说：我的好主人，您是分辨恶人和义人的神。拉听见了他所有的祈求。拉在他和他哥哥之间造出了一大片水，水里满是鳄鱼，一个在这边，一个在那边。哥哥两次拍打自己的手，因为他没能杀死弟弟。弟弟在对岸叫他说：你在那边等到天亮吧，太阳升起的时候，我会当着太阳的面和你辩明是非，因为它会把恶人交给义人。从今以后，我再也不和你住在一起了，我要到松树谷去。纸草称太阳神为“拉-哈拉赫提”，意思是“拉，地平线上的荷鲁斯”，是升起和落下的太阳的合称。在古埃及人看来，太阳神每天巡视世界，看见一切，所以他是最公正的法官。巴塔向他祈求的，不是力量，而是公正的裁判；太阳神的回答，不是替他惩罚哥哥，而是在两人之间隔开一道河，让真相有机会在天亮时被说出来。在这个故事里，神总是在关键时刻出现，却从不代替人去行动。

As he fled, Bata prayed to Pre-Harakhti, saying: 'My good lord, it is you who judge the wrongdoer from the just.' And Re heard all his plea. Re made a great water appear between him and his elder brother, full of crocodiles, one of them on one side and the other on the other. The elder brother struck his hand twice because he had not killed him. The younger called to him from the other side: 'Wait there until dawn. When the sun disk rises I shall be judged with you before him, and he will deliver the wrongdoer to the just. I shall not be with you any more; I shall go to the Valley of the Pine.' The papyrus names the sun god Pre-Harakhti, 'Re, Horus of the Horizon', the rising and setting sun in one. To the Egyptians the sun god sees all as he crosses the world each day, and so is the most just of judges. Bata asks not for strength but for a fair judgement; and the god does not punish the brother for him but sets a river between them, so that truth may be spoken at dawn. In this tale the gods appear at the decisive moment yet never act in place of men.

### 07 · 黎明时的真相 / The truth at dawn

天亮了，太阳升起来，两兄弟隔着河相望。巴塔对哥哥说：你为什么在背后追我，要不分青红皂白地杀我？你连我的话都不听。我是你的弟弟，你像我的父亲，你的妻子像我的母亲，不是吗？你派我回去拿种子的时候，是你的妻子对我说：来，我们一起躺一会儿。可是你看，她却把事情颠倒过来告诉了你。他把真相一五一十地告诉了哥哥，然后以太阳神的名义起誓。为了证明自己的清白，他拿起一把芦苇刀，割下了自己身上的一部分，扔进水里，被一条鲶鱼吞了下去。他变得虚弱无力。哥哥听明白了，悲痛欲绝，放声大哭，可是因为水里满是鳄鱼，他无法渡河到弟弟身边。纸草在这里只是平静地叙述，没有渲染。巴塔用一种极端的方式表明了自己的清白：他宁愿伤害自己，也要证明他对嫂子没有任何邪念。哥哥终于明白了真相，可是兄弟之间已经隔着一条无法跨越的河。

When the land grew light and the sun rose, the brothers looked at each other across the water. Bata said to his elder brother: 'Why have you come after me to kill me wrongfully, without hearing what I had to say? I am your younger brother; you are like a father to me, and your wife like a mother, are you not? When you sent me to fetch seed, it was your wife who said to me: Come, let us lie together for an hour. But see, she has turned it around for you.' He told his brother everything that had happened and swore by Pre-Harakhti. To prove his innocence he took a reed knife, cut away part of his own body and threw it into the water, where a catfish swallowed it. He grew faint and weak. His elder brother understood and grieved greatly, weeping aloud, but he could not cross to his brother because of the crocodiles. The papyrus tells this calmly, without dwelling on it. Bata proves his innocence by an extreme act: he would rather harm himself than leave any doubt that he had no desire for his brother's wife. The elder brother at last knows the truth, but now an uncrossable river lies between them.

### 08 · 松树谷的心 / The heart in the Valley of the Pine

巴塔对哥哥说：你只记得坏事，就不能记得一件好事，或者我为你做过的一件事吗？现在你回家去照看你的牛吧，我不会再待在你待的地方了，我要到松树谷去。你要为我做的事是这样的：我要把我的心取出来，放在松树的花顶上。如果松树被砍倒，我的心掉在地上，你就要来找它，哪怕找上七年，你也不要灰心。找到以后，你把它放进一碗清凉的水里，我就会活过来，为那个伤害我的人报仇。你会知道我出了事的：当有人把一罐啤酒递到你手里，啤酒起了泡沫，那就是我出事的征兆，你不要耽搁。说完，他就前往松树谷，哥哥则回到家里，手按在头上，身上涂满尘土。他回到家，杀死了妻子，把她扔给狗吃，然后坐下来为弟弟哀悼。这一章是整个故事的关键：巴塔把自己的心放在了身体之外，他的生命从此系在一棵树上，也系在哥哥的忠诚上。古埃及人认为心是生命和智慧的所在，把心放在别处，是一种神奇的保命之法，也是一个巨大的弱点。

Bata said to his elder brother: 'You remember one bad thing; can you not remember one good thing, or a thing I did for you? Now go home and care for your cattle, for I will not stay where you are; I am going to the Valley of the Pine. This is what you shall do for me: I shall take out my heart and place it on top of the flower of the pine. If the pine is cut down and my heart falls to the ground, you shall come to seek it, even if you spend seven years seeking it; do not let your heart be weary. When you find it, put it in a bowl of cool water, and I shall live to take revenge on him who wronged me. You will know that something has happened to me when a jug of beer is given into your hand and it ferments; do not delay.' He went away to the Valley of the Pine, and his elder brother went home, his hand on his head, smeared with dust. He reached his house, killed his wife, cast her to the dogs and sat mourning his younger brother. This chapter is the hinge of the tale: Bata places his heart outside his body, binding his life to a tree and to his brother's loyalty. The Egyptians held the heart to be the seat of life and mind; to place it elsewhere is a marvellous safeguard and also a great weakness.

> 证据说明：多比尼纸草（大英博物馆 EA 10183）松树谷的心。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.two_brothers.08`

### 09 · 九神与克努姆 / The Ennead and Khnum

巴塔在松树谷里独自生活，白天打猎，晚上回到松树下睡觉，他的心就放在松树的花顶上。后来，他为自己建了一座住宅，里面摆满了各种好东西。有一天，他走出去，遇见了九柱神，他们正在巡视整个国家。九柱神彼此商量，然后对他说：巴塔，九柱神的公牛，你为什么一个人在这里？你因为哥哥阿努比斯的妻子离开了家乡，现在哥哥已经杀了她，你已经为所受的一切冤屈报了仇。他们非常同情他。拉-哈拉赫提对克努姆说：为巴塔造一个妻子吧，不要让他一个人生活。克努姆就为他造了一个伴侣，她的身体比全国任何女子都美丽，所有神的精华都在她的身上。七位哈托尔来看她，异口同声地说：她将死于刀下。克努姆是古埃及人心目中用陶轮造人的神，他把人的身体像陶器一样塑造出来。在这里，正是拉-哈拉赫提——那位在河边回应了巴塔祈祷的太阳神——再一次关照他，命令克努姆为他造一个妻子。七位哈托尔则是古埃及人相信会在孩子出生时预言命运的女神，她们的预言为故事后来的结局埋下了伏笔。

Bata lived alone in the Valley of the Pine, hunting desert game by day and coming back at evening to sleep under the pine on whose flower his heart lay. After some days he built himself a mansion and filled it with every good thing. One day he went out and met the Ennead, walking about administering the whole land. The Ennead conferred and said to him: 'Bata, bull of the Ennead, are you here alone? You left your town because of the wife of your elder brother Anubis; now he has killed his wife, and you are avenged for all that was done to you.' They felt very sorry for him. Pre-Harakhti said to Khnum: 'Fashion a wife for Bata, that he not live alone.' Khnum made him a companion more beautiful in body than any woman in the whole land, for the fluid of every god was in her. The Seven Hathors came to see her and said with one voice: 'She will die by the knife.' Khnum, to the Egyptians, was the god who shaped people on the potter's wheel as a potter shapes vessels. Here it is Pre-Harakhti — the sun god who answered Bata's prayer by the river — who cares for him again and commands Khnum to make him a wife. The Seven Hathors were the goddesses believed to foretell a child's fate at birth, and their words foreshadow the ending.

### 10 · 大海与一缕头发 / The sea and a lock of hair

巴塔深深地爱着她。她住在他的家里，他整天在野外打猎，把猎物带回来放在她面前。他对她说：不要到外面去，免得大海把你抢走，我没法从它那里把你救回来，因为我和你一样是个女人——这句话指他已失去了男子的身体。他把心的秘密告诉了她：我的心放在松树的花顶上，如果有人找到它，我就得和他搏斗。有一天，巴塔出去打猎，她出门到松树下散步，大海看见了她，涌起波浪追赶她。她赶紧跑回家里，大海对松树说：替我抓住她！松树就抓下了她的一缕头发。大海把这缕头发带到了埃及，放在法老的洗衣工们洗衣服的地方。这缕头发的香气染在了法老的衣服上，法老责怪洗衣工，说他的衣服上有油膏的气味。洗衣工头每天都挨骂，不知道该怎么办，他走到河边，看见水里有一缕头发，就让人把它捞上来，发现它香气扑鼻，便把它带给了法老。在古埃及人的想象里，大海常常是陌生而危险的力量，在这里，它就像一个贪婪的追求者，从远方伸手夺走了女神造出的美人。

Bata loved her very much. She stayed in his house while he spent the day hunting desert game and brought it to lay before her. He said to her: 'Do not go outside, lest the Sea carry you off; I could not rescue you from it, for I am a woman like you' — meaning he had lost his manhood. And he told her his secret: 'My heart is placed on top of the flower of the pine; if someone finds it, I shall have to fight him.' One day, when Bata had gone hunting, she went out to stroll under the pine. The Sea saw her and surged after her. She ran back into the house, and the Sea called to the pine: 'Seize her for me!' and the pine took a lock of her hair. The Sea carried it to Egypt and laid it down at the place of Pharaoh's launderers. The scent of the lock got into Pharaoh's clothes, and he quarrelled with the launderers, saying his clothes smelled of ointment. The chief launderer, scolded every day, did not know what to do; going down to the shore, he saw the lock of hair in the water, had it brought up, found its scent very sweet, and took it to Pharaoh. In the Egyptian imagination the sea was often a strange and dangerous power; here it is like a greedy suitor reaching from afar to snatch the beauty the gods had made.

> 证据说明：多比尼纸草（大英博物馆 EA 10183）大海与一缕头发。

### 11 · 法老的使者 / Pharaoh's envoys

法老召来了精通学问的书吏，他们对法老说：这缕头发属于拉-哈拉赫提的一个女儿，所有神的精华都在她身上，这是从另一个国度给您的礼物。让人到所有的外国去寻找她吧，至于那缕头发被送来的松树谷，就派许多人去，把她带回来。法老说：你们说得很好。于是派出了使者。许多天以后，派到各国去的人都回来向法老报告，只有派到松树谷去的人没有回来，因为巴塔把他们都杀了，只留下一个人回来报信。法老于是派了许多士兵和战车去把她带回来，还派了一个女人，手里拿着女子喜欢的各种美丽首饰。那个女子跟着她回到了埃及，全国上下都为她欢呼。法老非常爱她，封她为“伟大的贵妇”。在这里，克努姆为巴塔造的妻子被书吏们称为“拉-哈拉赫提的女儿”——她本来就是在太阳神的命令下诞生的。可是她没有忠于巴塔，而是被首饰和王宫的荣华所吸引，自愿来到了法老身边。这一转折让人想起故事开头嫂子的背叛：巴塔又一次被身边的女人出卖了。

Pharaoh summoned the learned scribes, who said to him: 'This lock of hair belongs to a daughter of Pre-Harakhti, in whom is the fluid of every god; it is a greeting to you from another land. Let envoys go to every foreign land to seek her; and to the Valley of the Pine, from which the lock came, send many men to fetch her.' Pharaoh said: 'What you have said is very good,' and envoys were sent. After many days the men sent to the foreign lands came back to report, but those sent to the Valley of the Pine did not, for Bata had killed them, letting only one return to tell Pharaoh. Then Pharaoh sent many soldiers and charioteers to bring her back, and with them a woman bearing every kind of fine lady's jewellery. The woman came back to Egypt with her, and the whole land rejoiced over her. Pharaoh loved her very much and named her Great Noble Lady. Here the wife Khnum fashioned for Bata is called by the scribes a daughter of Pre-Harakhti — she was born at the sun god's command. Yet she is not faithful to Bata; drawn by jewels and the splendour of the palace, she goes willingly to Pharaoh. The turn recalls the sister-in-law's betrayal at the start: once again Bata is betrayed by the woman beside him.

> 证据说明：多比尼纸草（大英博物馆 EA 10183）法老的使者。

### 12 · 砍倒松树 / The pine is cut down

法老问她关于她丈夫的事，她对法老说：让人去砍倒那棵松树，把它砍成碎片。法老就派了士兵带着工具去砍松树。他们来到松树前，砍下了放着巴塔之心的那朵花。就在那一刻，巴塔倒下死去了。纸草对巴塔之死只写了这样一句话，却显得格外沉重：他曾在河边以太阳神的名义证明自己的清白，曾在松树谷里独自打猎、建起住宅，曾把自己最大的秘密告诉了最亲近的人，而正是这个秘密，让他失去了生命。这一章也应验了七位哈托尔的预言的一部分：这个女子的命运将与刀联系在一起，而在应验之前，她先让巴塔死于斧下。古埃及的故事中常常出现这样的情节：英雄的生命寄托在某一件外物上——一棵树、一件东西——只要这件东西完好，英雄就不会死。这类主题在世界许多民族的民间故事中都能看到，人们称之为“体外之心”或“外在的灵魂”。现在，巴塔的心落在了地上，他唯一的希望，就是哥哥会遵守诺言，来寻找他的心。

Pharaoh asked her about her husband, and she said to Pharaoh: 'Let the pine be felled and cut to pieces.' So Pharaoh sent soldiers with their tools to fell the pine. They came to the pine and cut the flower on which Bata's heart lay, and he fell dead at that moment. The papyrus gives Bata's death a single sentence, and it weighs heavily: he had proved his innocence by the river in the sun god's name, hunted alone and built a house in the Valley of the Pine, and told his greatest secret to the one closest to him — and it was that secret that cost him his life. The chapter fulfils part of the Seven Hathors' word: this woman's fate is bound up with the blade, but before it comes upon her she brings Bata down by the axe. Egyptian tales often place a hero's life in some outside thing — a tree, an object — so that while it is whole he cannot die. The motif appears in folktales of many peoples and is known as the 'external heart' or 'external soul'. Now Bata's heart lies on the ground, and his one hope is that his brother will keep his word and come to seek it.

> 证据说明：多比尼纸草（大英博物馆 EA 10183）砍倒松树。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.two_brothers.12`

### 13 · 起沫的啤酒 / The frothing beer

就在松树被砍倒的第二天，巴塔的哥哥阿努比斯走进家里，坐下洗手，有人递给他一罐啤酒，啤酒起了泡沫；又递给他一罐葡萄酒，葡萄酒也变浑浊了。他立刻拿起手杖和凉鞋，带上衣服和武器，出发前往松树谷。他走进弟弟的住宅，发现弟弟躺在床上，已经死了。他看着弟弟的尸体痛哭，然后去寻找弟弟的心。他在松树底下寻找，从傍晚一直找到天亮。他就这样找了三年，没有找到。到了第四年，他的心想要回到埃及去，他说：明天我就走。天亮以后，他又在松树下走了一整天，傍晚往回走的时候，他又回头看了一眼，找到了一颗果实——那正是弟弟的心。他拿来一碗清凉的水，把心放进去，然后像往常一样坐下来。夜里，心吸饱了水，巴塔的四肢颤动起来，他睁开眼睛看着哥哥。哥哥把那碗水端到他嘴边，他喝了下去，心回到了原来的位置，巴塔又变回了从前的样子。兄弟俩紧紧拥抱，互相诉说。哥哥没有忘记诺言，在第一章里像父亲一样的哥哥，在这里终于以父亲般的坚持把弟弟救了回来。

On the day after the pine was felled, Anubis, Bata's elder brother, entered his house and sat down to wash his hands; he was given a jug of beer, and it fermented; he was given another of wine, and it turned bad. He took his staff and sandals, his clothes and his weapons, and set out for the Valley of the Pine. He entered his younger brother's mansion and found him lying dead on his bed. He wept over him, and then went to seek his heart under the pine, searching from evening to dawn. He spent three years seeking it without finding it. In the fourth year his heart longed to return to Egypt, and he said: 'I will go tomorrow.' At dawn he walked under the pine once more all day, and in the evening, turning back for a last look, he found a fruit — it was his brother's heart. He brought a bowl of cool water, placed the heart in it and sat down as usual. When night came the heart absorbed the water; Bata's limbs trembled, and he looked at his elder brother. Anubis held the bowl of water to his lips, he drank, his heart stood in its place, and he became as he had been. The brothers embraced and talked together. The elder brother kept his word; the one who was like a father in the first chapter at last brings his brother back with a father's persistence.

> 证据说明：多比尼纸草（大英博物馆 EA 10183）起沫的啤酒与寻找心。

### 14 · 公牛 / The bull

巴塔对哥哥说：看，我要变成一头公牛，身上有各种美丽的颜色，没有人知道它的来历。你坐在我的背上，等太阳升起时，我们就到我妻子所在的地方去，我要为自己报仇。你把我带到法老那里去，法老会为我给你银子和金子作为报酬，因为你给他带来了一个大奇迹，人们会在全国为我欢呼，然后你就回你的村子去。天亮以后，巴塔变成了他所说的那头公牛，哥哥坐在他的背上，天亮时到了法老所在的地方。法老看见这头公牛非常高兴，为它举行了盛大的祭祀，说：这是一个大奇迹。全国都为它欢呼。法老给了哥哥银子和金子，哥哥回到了自己的村子。有一天，公牛走进厨房，站在那位贵妇的身边，对她说：看，我还活着。她问：你是谁？公牛说：我是巴塔。你让法老砍倒了那棵松树，你知道那是为了不让我活下去。可是看，我还活着，我是一头公牛。贵妇非常害怕。她陪法老饮酒作乐时，让法老发誓答应她的一个要求，然后说：让我吃这头公牛的肝吧。法老很为难，可是只好照办。

Bata said to his elder brother: 'See, I shall become a great bull of every beautiful colour, whose nature no one will know. Sit on my back, and when the sun rises we shall be where my wife is, and I shall avenge myself. Take me to the place where Pharaoh is; he will reward you with silver and gold for bringing him a great wonder, and the whole land will rejoice over me; then you will go back to your village.' When it was dawn Bata became the bull he had spoken of, and his elder brother sat on his back; at dawn he reached the place where Pharaoh was. Pharaoh was very glad of the bull and made a great offering for it, saying: 'It is a great wonder.' The whole land rejoiced over it. Pharaoh gave the elder brother silver and gold, and he went home to his village. One day the bull entered the kitchen and stood beside the Lady and said to her: 'See, I am still alive.' She said: 'Who are you?' He said: 'I am Bata. You knew when you had Pharaoh fell the pine that it was to keep me from living. But see, I am alive; I am a bull.' The Lady was very frightened. When she was drinking with Pharaoh she made him swear to grant her request, and said: 'Let me eat the liver of this bull.' Pharaoh was troubled, but had to do it.

> 证据说明：多比尼纸草（大英博物馆 EA 10183）公牛。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.two_brothers.14`

### 15 · 两棵鳄梨树与新国王 / The persea trees and the new king

公牛被宰杀时，它的两滴血落在法老宫殿门口的两根门柱旁，长成了两棵高大的鳄梨树，每一棵都美极了。人们告诉法老，法老为它们举行了祭祀，全国都为它们欢呼。后来，法老和贵妇一起坐在树下，一棵树对贵妇说：你这个骗子，我是巴塔，我还活着。你让法老砍倒了松树，又让他杀了我变成的公牛。贵妇又让法老发誓，然后说：让人把这两棵鳄梨树砍倒，做成漂亮的家具吧。法老派了工匠去砍树，贵妇站在一旁看，一片木屑飞进了她的嘴里，她咽了下去，就怀了孕。日子满了，她生下一个儿子。法老非常喜爱这个孩子，先让他做库什的王子，后来又立他为全国的王储。过了许多年，法老升天了，王储继承了王位。新国王说：把陛下的大臣们召来，我要让他们知道我经历的一切。人们把他的妻子带来，他当着大臣们的面审判了她，大臣们都同意他的判决。他又把哥哥召来，立他为全国的王储。巴塔做了三十年的埃及国王，他去世后，他的哥哥继承了王位。七位哈托尔的预言应验了，而两兄弟最终以国王和继承人的身份重聚，故事在和解与公正中结束。

When the bull was slaughtered, two drops of its blood fell beside the two doorposts of Pharaoh's palace and grew into two great persea trees, each of them very beautiful. Pharaoh was told, made offerings to them, and the whole land rejoiced. Later, as Pharaoh sat beneath them with the Lady, one tree said to her: 'You liar! I am Bata, and I am still alive. You had Pharaoh fell the pine and kill the bull I became.' Again the Lady made Pharaoh swear, and said: 'Let these two persea trees be felled and made into fine furniture.' Pharaoh sent craftsmen to fell the trees, and the Lady stood watching; a splinter flew into her mouth, she swallowed it, and she conceived. In due time she bore a son. Pharaoh loved the child greatly, made him King's Son of Kush and later Crown Prince of the whole land. After many years Pharaoh flew up to heaven, and the Crown Prince became king. The new king said: 'Let His Majesty's great officials be brought, that I may tell them all that has happened to me.' His wife was brought, he judged her before them, and they agreed with his judgement. He summoned his elder brother and made him Crown Prince of the whole land. Bata reigned as king of Egypt for thirty years, and when he died his elder brother succeeded him. The Seven Hathors' word is fulfilled, and the brothers meet again as king and heir; the tale ends in reconciliation and justice.

> 证据说明：多比尼纸草（大英博物馆 EA 10183）鳄梨树与巴塔为王。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.two_brothers.15`

## 编辑说明

依据大英博物馆藏多比尼纸草（EA 10183），按纸草顺序讲述。故事中的哥哥与神同名，叫阿努比斯，巴塔也是一位古老的神名，学者认为这个故事与上埃及某地关于这两位神的传说有关，但在纸草中他们是凡人兄弟。诱惑、自残与杀妻等情节只讲结果，不渲染细节。
