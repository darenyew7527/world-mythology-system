# 普塔用心与舌创造世界：孟菲斯神学 / Ptah creates by heart and tongue: the Memphite Theology

## 阅读边界

公元前八世纪，库施王朝的法老沙巴卡在孟菲斯的普塔神庙里发现一卷被虫蛀坏的古书，命人把它重新刻在一块石板上。石上的文字先讲大地之神盖布怎样裁决荷鲁斯与塞特之争：起初把埃及一分为二，后来把全部遗产都给了荷鲁斯，荷鲁斯在孟菲斯统一了两地，而孟菲斯的主神普塔被称为“塔特恩”，统一两地之王。接着是著名的“神学”部分：一切神都以普塔的形式出现，阿图姆是在普塔的心中和舌上成形的；心想出一切，舌说出一切，众神、城市、食物和一切技艺都由普塔的话语而生。普塔造完万物后安息了，众神进入用木、石、泥做成的身体里。最后讲奥西里斯在水中淹溺，伊西斯和奈芙蒂斯把他救上孟菲斯的岸边，他进入大地，荷鲁斯在父亲的怀抱中成为上下埃及之王。

In the eighth century BCE the Kushite pharaoh Shabaka found in the temple of Ptah at Memphis an old book eaten by worms and had it carved anew on a stone slab. The text first tells how Geb, god of the earth, judged between Horus and Seth: at first he divided Egypt between them, then gave the whole inheritance to Horus, who united the Two Lands at Memphis — and Ptah, god of Memphis, is called Ta-tenen, the king who unites the land. Then comes the famous 'theology': all the gods appear as forms of Ptah, and Atum took shape in Ptah's heart and on his tongue; the heart conceives all and the tongue speaks all, and gods, towns, food and every craft come from Ptah's word. When he had made all things Ptah rested, and the gods entered bodies of wood, stone and clay. Last, Osiris is drowned in the water, Isis and Nephthys bring him ashore at Memphis, he enters the earth, and Horus becomes king of Upper and Lower Egypt in his father's arms.

### 01 · 被虫蛀的古书 / The worm-eaten book

石板最上面的两行横写的文字，是沙巴卡国王的王衔和一段说明：上下埃及之王沙巴卡，受“城墙之南的普塔”喜爱的人，愿他像拉一样永远活着。陛下在他父亲“城墙之南的普塔”的神庙里，把这篇文字重新抄写了一遍。陛下发现它是祖先留下的一部作品，已经被虫蛀坏了，从头到尾都读不懂。于是陛下重新抄写了它，让它比原来更好，好让他的名字和他的纪念物在他父亲普塔的神庙里永远长存，这是上下埃及之王为他的父亲普塔所做的事，愿他永远拥有生命。这段话很有意思：一位法老说自己在神庙里找到了一卷被虫蛀坏的古书，并下令把它刻在石头上，好让它永不毁坏。沙巴卡是来自南方库施的国王，他统治埃及时，特别注重恢复古老的宗教传统，把这篇文字刻在石头上，也是在宣示自己是古老秩序的继承者。讽刺的是，这块为了“永存”而刻的石头，后来却被人当作磨盘，中间的许多文字都被磨掉了。

The two horizontal lines at the top of the slab give King Shabaka's titles and a statement: the King of Upper and Lower Egypt Shabaka, beloved of Ptah-South-of-his-Wall, living like Re for ever. His Majesty copied this text anew in the house of his father Ptah-South-of-his-Wall. His Majesty had found it as a work of the ancestors that was worm-eaten, so that it could not be understood from beginning to end. His Majesty copied it anew so that it became better than it had been before, in order that his name might endure and his monument last in the house of his father Ptah — what the King of Upper and Lower Egypt did for his father Ptah, may he be given life for ever. The statement is striking: a pharaoh says he found a worm-eaten book in the temple and had it carved in stone so that it would never perish. Shabaka was a king from Kush in the south who, ruling Egypt, set great store on restoring ancient religious tradition; carving this text in stone also declared him heir to the old order. Ironically the stone carved to last for ever was later used as a millstone, and much of its text was ground away.

### 02 · 塔特恩 / Ta-tenen

序言之后，碑文称颂普塔：上下埃及之王就是这个伟大的名字——塔特恩，城墙之南，永恒之主。“塔特恩”的意思是“升起的土地”，指的是在创世之初从原始之水中升起的第一块土丘。在孟菲斯的神学里，普塔与塔特恩合而为一，他既是创造万物的神，也是那第一块从混沌之水中露出的大地。“城墙之南”指普塔在孟菲斯的神庙位于“白城墙”——孟菲斯古老的城墙——的南边。碑文接着说，他就是统一这片土地的人。在古埃及人看来，孟菲斯是上下埃及交界的地方，传说中第一位统一埃及的国王就在这里建都，所以孟菲斯被称为“两地的天平”。普塔作为孟菲斯的主神，被看作这种统一的神圣保证者。这一段为后面的两个故事做了铺垫：盖布把埃及给了荷鲁斯，荷鲁斯在孟菲斯统一了两地；而普塔，则是这一切背后的创造者。在其他城市的神学里，创世之神是阿图姆或别的神，孟菲斯的祭司们则通过这篇文字，宣称普塔才是最古老、最伟大的神。

After the preface the text praises Ptah: the King of Upper and Lower Egypt is this Great Name, Ta-tenen, South-of-his-Wall, Lord of Eternity. Ta-tenen means 'the risen land', the first mound that rose from the primeval waters at the beginning of creation. In the theology of Memphis Ptah and Ta-tenen are one: he is both the god who made all things and the first earth that emerged from the waters of chaos. 'South-of-his-Wall' refers to Ptah's temple standing south of the White Wall, the ancient wall of Memphis. The text goes on: he is the one who united this land. To the Egyptians Memphis lay where Upper and Lower Egypt met; the first king to unite Egypt was said to have founded his capital there, so Memphis was called 'the Balance of the Two Lands'. Ptah, god of Memphis, was held to be the divine guarantor of that unity. This prepares the two stories that follow: Geb gives Egypt to Horus, and Horus unites the Two Lands at Memphis; and Ptah stands behind it all as creator. In the theologies of other cities the creator was Atum or another god; through this text the priests of Memphis claimed that Ptah was the oldest and greatest of the gods.

### 03 · 盖布的第一次裁决 / Geb's first judgement

碑文接着讲了一个关于王权的故事。众神之主盖布下令，让九柱神聚集到他的身边。他在荷鲁斯和塞特之间进行裁判，结束了他们的争吵。他让塞特在上埃及的土地上做上埃及之王，一直到塞特出生的地方苏；又让荷鲁斯在下埃及的土地上做下埃及之王，一直到他父亲淹死的地方，那个地方叫做“两地的分界”。这样，荷鲁斯管辖一个地区，塞特管辖一个地区，他们在阿延为两地订立了和约。这就是两地的划分。盖布是大地之神，是奥西里斯和塞特的父亲，荷鲁斯的祖父，在众神的谱系中，他是王权的传承者。荷鲁斯和塞特之争是古埃及最著名的神话之一：塞特杀死了他的哥哥奥西里斯，奥西里斯的儿子荷鲁斯要为父报仇、夺回王位。在其他版本里，这场争斗持续了八十年，在众神的法庭上反复辩论。在孟菲斯神学里，裁决却显得简洁而有秩序：盖布先把国家一分为二，让两位神各治一方，以和平的方式结束争端。

Next the text tells a story of kingship. Geb, lord of the gods, commanded that the Nine Gods gather to him. He judged between Horus and Seth and ended their quarrel. He made Seth king of Upper Egypt in the land of Upper Egypt, up to the place where he was born, which is Su; and Geb made Horus king of Lower Egypt in the land of Lower Egypt, up to the place where his father was drowned, which is 'Division-of-the-Two-Lands'. So Horus stood over one region and Seth over another, and they made peace over the Two Lands at Ayan. That was the division of the Two Lands. Geb, god of the earth, father of Osiris and Seth and grandfather of Horus, is the one through whom kingship passes in the genealogy of the gods. The contest of Horus and Seth is one of the most famous Egyptian myths: Seth killed his brother Osiris, and Osiris's son Horus sought to avenge him and win back the throne. In other versions the dispute lasts eighty years and is argued over and over before the gods' tribunal. In the Memphite Theology the judgement is brief and orderly: Geb first divides the land, letting each god rule one part and ending the quarrel in peace.

### 04 · 全部遗产归于荷鲁斯 / The whole inheritance to Horus

可是后来，盖布觉得荷鲁斯的份额和塞特的份额一样，这样不对。于是盖布把他全部的遗产都给了荷鲁斯，因为荷鲁斯是他儿子的儿子，是他的长子之子。盖布对九柱神说：我已经选定了你，荷鲁斯，你是长子。盖布说：我把遗产给这位继承人，给我儿子的儿子，给上埃及的豺狼，给开路者，给我所生的那一位的儿子。于是，荷鲁斯站在这片土地之上，成为这片土地的统一者，被宣称为伟大的名字：塔特恩，城墙之南，永恒之主。两位伟大的魔法女神——也就是上埃及和下埃及的王冠——长在了他的头上。他就是荷鲁斯，作为上下埃及之王出现，在“城墙之州”统一了两地，那正是两地统一的地方。这一段把荷鲁斯的王权与孟菲斯紧紧地联系在一起：统一埃及的地方就是孟菲斯，而荷鲁斯在这里得到的称号，正是普塔的称号“塔特恩”。这样，人间的王权、荷鲁斯的神话和孟菲斯的主神普塔，在这篇文字里融为一体。

But then it seemed wrong to Geb that the portion of Horus was like the portion of Seth. So Geb gave his entire inheritance to Horus, for he was the son of his son, his firstborn. Geb said to the Nine Gods: 'I have chosen you, Horus, the firstborn.' Geb said: 'I give the inheritance to this heir, to the son of my son, the Upper Egyptian jackal, the opener of the ways, the son born of my son.' Then Horus stood over the land, the uniter of this land, proclaimed in the great name Ta-tenen, South-of-his-Wall, Lord of Eternity. The Two Great Magicians — the crowns of Upper and Lower Egypt — grew upon his head. He is Horus who arose as King of Upper and Lower Egypt, who united the Two Lands in the Wall Nome, the place where the Two Lands were united. This passage binds Horus's kingship tightly to Memphis: the place where Egypt was united is Memphis, and the title Horus receives there is Ptah's own title, Ta-tenen. So earthly kingship, the myth of Horus and Memphis's god Ptah merge into one in this text.

### 05 · 芦苇与纸草 / Reed and papyrus

碑文接着说：芦苇和纸草被放在普塔神庙的双扇大门上。芦苇是上埃及的象征植物，纸草是下埃及的象征植物，把它们一起放在普塔神庙的门口，象征着两地在普塔的庇护下统一。这意味着荷鲁斯和塞特得到了平息、联合了起来，他们像兄弟一样相处，不再争吵，在他们所在的任何地方，都在普塔的神庙——两地的天平——里结为一体，在那里，上埃及和下埃及被衡量。在这篇文字里，荷鲁斯和塞特的故事没有以一方的毁灭告终，而是以和解告终：他们在孟菲斯和好了。这与其他一些版本不同，在那些版本里，塞特被打败、受到惩罚，甚至被描绘成邪恶的化身。孟菲斯的祭司们更看重统一与和平，他们把普塔的神庙描绘成两地和解的地方。这一段之后，石板上有一大段文字因为被当作磨盘使用而几乎完全磨损了，学者们只能辨认出零星的词句，有人认为那里原本是一段戏剧性的对话。本篇不补写这段残缺。

The text goes on: reed and papyrus were placed on the double door of the house of Ptah. The reed was the emblem of Upper Egypt and the papyrus of Lower Egypt; set together at the door of Ptah's temple, they signify the union of the Two Lands under Ptah's protection. That means Horus and Seth were pacified and united; they fraternised and ceased to quarrel wherever they might be, being united in the house of Ptah, the Balance of the Two Lands, in which Upper and Lower Egypt are weighed. Here the tale of Horus and Seth ends not with one side destroyed but in reconciliation: they make peace at Memphis. This differs from other versions in which Seth is defeated and punished, even portrayed as the embodiment of evil. The priests of Memphis valued unity and peace and made Ptah's temple the place where the Two Lands were reconciled. After this passage a long stretch of the slab was almost entirely worn away by its use as a millstone; scholars can read only scattered words, and some think it held a dramatic dialogue. This retelling does not fill in the gap.

### 06 · 普塔的诸形 / The forms of Ptah

在磨损的部分之后，碑文进入了最著名的“神学”部分。它首先列出以普塔的形式出现的众神：在伟大宝座上的普塔；普塔-努恩，生出阿图姆的父亲；普塔-瑙内特，生下阿图姆的母亲；伟大的普塔，九柱神的心与舌；还有其他几种形式，其中一些名字已经残缺。努恩和瑙内特是原始之水的男性和女性化身，在赫利奥波利斯的创世神话里，阿图姆从努恩之中出现，自己创造了世界。孟菲斯的祭司们在这里做了一个巧妙的安排：他们并不否认阿图姆的创世之功，而是说，阿图姆本身是由普塔生出来的——普塔以原始之水的形式，成为阿图姆的父亲和母亲。这样，普塔就站在了一切神的源头，比阿图姆更古老、更根本。这种把其他城市的主神解释为本城主神的某种形式或产物的做法，在古埃及宗教中很常见，学者们称之为“神学的整合”。普塔在这里不再只是孟菲斯的工匠之神，而是成了万物之始。

After the worn section the text enters its most famous part, the 'theology'. It first lists the gods who came into being as Ptah: Ptah upon the Great Throne; Ptah-Nun, the father who made Atum; Ptah-Naunet, the mother who bore Atum; Ptah the Great, heart and tongue of the Nine Gods; and several other forms, some of whose names are damaged. Nun and Naunet are the male and female forms of the primeval waters; in the creation myth of Heliopolis Atum emerged from Nun and made the world by himself. The priests of Memphis make a clever move here: they do not deny Atum's work of creation but say that Atum himself was born of Ptah — that Ptah, as the primeval waters, was Atum's father and mother. So Ptah stands at the source of all the gods, older and more fundamental than Atum. Interpreting the chief god of another city as a form or product of one's own was common in Egyptian religion; scholars call it theological integration. Ptah is no longer only the craftsman god of Memphis but the beginning of all things.

### 07 · 心与舌 / Heart and tongue

碑文接着说：在心中成形的，在舌上成形的，是阿图姆的形象。因为那位极其伟大的神是普塔，他通过这颗心和这条舌头，把生命赐给了所有的神和他们的卡；荷鲁斯以普塔的形式在这颗心中成形，托特以普塔的形式在这条舌头上成形。于是，心和舌支配着所有的肢体，因为心存在于每一个身体之中，舌存在于每一张嘴里——所有的神、所有的人、所有的牲畜、所有爬行的东西、一切有生命的东西，心想它想要的，舌命令它想要的。在古埃及人的观念里，心是思想、意志和情感的所在，舌则把心中所想的说出来、变成命令。孟菲斯神学把这两者放在创世的中心：普塔先在心中构想，再用舌头说出，世界就这样诞生了。荷鲁斯与心相连，托特与舌相连——托特是智慧和文字之神，常被称为“普塔的舌头”。这种通过思想和话语创造世界的观念，在古代近东的宗教文献中极为罕见，许多学者把它与后来的“道”或“逻各斯”的观念相比较，但本篇不推测它们之间的联系。

The text goes on: there took shape in the heart, there took shape on the tongue the form of Atum. For the very great one is Ptah, who gave life to all the gods and their kas through this heart and through this tongue, in which Horus had taken shape as Ptah, in which Thoth had taken shape as Ptah. Thus heart and tongue rule over all the limbs, for the heart is in every body and the tongue in every mouth — of all gods, all men, all cattle, all creeping things, all that lives — the heart thinking whatever it wishes and the tongue commanding whatever it wishes. To the Egyptians the heart was the seat of thought, will and feeling, and the tongue spoke what the heart conceived and turned it into command. The Memphite Theology places the two at the centre of creation: Ptah first conceives in his heart, then speaks with his tongue, and so the world is born. Horus is joined to the heart and Thoth to the tongue — Thoth, god of wisdom and writing, often called the tongue of Ptah. Creation through thought and word is very rare in ancient Near Eastern religious texts, and many scholars compare it with later ideas of the Word or Logos; this retelling makes no claim about any link.

> 证据说明：沙巴卡石（大英博物馆 EA 498）心与舌的神学。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.memphite.07`

### 08 · 牙齿与嘴唇 / Teeth and lips

碑文接着把普塔的九柱神和阿图姆的九柱神作了对比：普塔的九柱神在他面前，是心与舌、牙齿与嘴唇。阿图姆的九柱神是通过他的精液和手指诞生的；而普塔的九柱神，则是这张嘴里的牙齿和嘴唇，这张嘴说出了一切事物的名字，舒和泰芙努特就是从这张嘴里出来的，这张嘴生出了九柱神。在赫利奥波利斯的创世神话里，阿图姆独自一人，用身体的方式——他的精液和手——生出了第一对神舒和泰芙努特；有的版本则说他打喷嚏生出舒，吐口水生出泰芙努特。孟菲斯的祭司们在这里并没有否定那个古老的神话，而是重新解释了它：所谓“精液和手指”，在普塔这里就是“牙齿和嘴唇”；舒和泰芙努特不是从身体里生出来的，而是被说出来的。这是对创世方式的一次深刻改写：从身体的生殖，变成了语言的命名。说出一样东西的名字，就是创造了它。在古埃及人看来，名字有着神奇的力量，知道一个神的真名，就能掌握他的力量；在这里，命名本身就成了创造。

The text then compares Ptah's Ennead with Atum's: his Ennead is before him as heart and tongue, teeth and lips. Atum's Ennead came into being through his semen and his fingers; but Ptah's Ennead is the teeth and lips in this mouth which pronounced the name of every thing, from which Shu and Tefnut came forth, and which gave birth to the Ennead. In the creation myth of Heliopolis Atum, alone, brought forth the first pair, Shu and Tefnut, from his body — from his semen and hand; in some versions he sneezed out Shu and spat out Tefnut. The priests of Memphis do not deny the old myth but reinterpret it: what was 'semen and fingers' for Atum is 'teeth and lips' for Ptah; Shu and Tefnut are not born of a body but spoken. It is a profound rewriting of creation, from bodily begetting to the naming of words. To speak the name of a thing is to create it. To the Egyptians names had magical power — to know a god's true name was to hold his power — and here naming itself becomes creation.

### 09 · 心想与舌说 / What the heart devises and the tongue commands

碑文接着解释这种创造是怎样发生的：眼睛的看见，耳朵的听见，鼻子的呼吸，都报告给心。是心使一切认识产生，是舌说出心所想的。就这样，所有的神都诞生了，他的九柱神也完成了。因为神的每一句话，都是通过心所构想、舌所命令而产生的。就这样，卡被造了出来，赫姆苏特也被指定了——它们供给一切食物和一切养料——都是通过这句话。正义被给予做被爱之事的人，惩罚被给予做被恨之事的人。就这样，生命被给予和平的人，死亡被给予犯罪的人。就这样，一切劳动、一切技艺都被造了出来，手臂的动作，腿的移动，所有肢体的运动，都依照这个由心构想、由舌说出、使万物得以成就的命令。在这一段里，普塔的创造不只是造出了众神和世界，还造出了道德与社会的秩序：正义与惩罚、生与死、劳动与技艺，都来自神的话语。普塔原本就是工匠和技艺之神，孟菲斯的工匠们崇拜他；在这里，一切手艺被说成是普塔的心与舌的延伸。

The text then explains how this creation works: the sight of the eyes, the hearing of the ears, the breathing of the nose report to the heart. It is the heart that makes every understanding come forth, and the tongue that announces what the heart thinks. Thus all the gods were born and his Ennead was completed. For every word of the god came about through what the heart devised and the tongue commanded. Thus the kas were made and the hemsut appointed — they that make all food and all nourishment — through this word. Justice is given to him who does what is loved, and punishment to him who does what is hated. Thus life is given to the peaceful and death to the criminal. Thus all labour and all crafts are made, the action of the arms, the motion of the legs, the movement of all the limbs, according to this command devised by the heart and spoken by the tongue, which makes all things happen. In this passage Ptah's creation brings forth not only gods and world but moral and social order: justice and punishment, life and death, labour and craft all come from the god's word. Ptah was god of craftsmen and the arts, worshipped by the craftsmen of Memphis; here every craft is described as an extension of his heart and tongue.

> 证据说明：沙巴卡石（大英博物馆 EA 498）心想舌说而生万物。

### 10 · 普塔歇息 / Ptah rests

碑文说：所以人们说普塔是“创造一切、生出众神的那一位”。他就是塔特恩，生出众神的神，一切都从他那里出来——食物、供给、神的祭品和一切美好的事物。于是人们认识到、明白了，他是众神中最强大的一位。于是，在他造完万物和一切神圣的话语之后，普塔满足了，安息了。“安息”在埃及语里用的词是“霍特普”，同时有“满足”“平静”和“安息”的意思，普塔的一个常见称号“奈弗尔-霍尔”与之相关，在孟菲斯，人们常说普塔是“满足的神”。学者们常常把这一句与其他创世神话中“造物之后休息”的说法相比较，但两者的语境不同，本篇不作牵连。在这一段里，孟菲斯神学达到了它的高潮：它明确宣称普塔是“众神中最强大的一位”，一切美好的事物——包括神庙中献给众神的祭品——都来源于他。这正是这篇文字的写作目的之一：为孟菲斯的神庙和祭司们确立崇高的地位，说明他们侍奉的神，是一切神的创造者。

The text says: so it is said of Ptah, 'He who made all and created the gods.' He is Ta-tenen, who gave birth to the gods, and from whom every thing came forth — foods, provisions, divine offerings, all good things. Thus it is recognised and understood that he is the mightiest of the gods. And so Ptah was satisfied, or rested, after he had made all things and all divine words. The Egyptian word is hotep, meaning at once 'satisfied', 'at peace' and 'at rest'; at Memphis Ptah was often called a god at peace. Scholars often compare this line with other creation myths in which the creator rests after making the world, but the contexts differ, and this retelling draws no connection. Here the Memphite Theology reaches its climax: it states plainly that Ptah is the mightiest of the gods, and that all good things — even the offerings given to the gods in temples — come from him. That was one purpose of the text: to raise the temple and priests of Memphis to the highest rank, showing that the god they served was the maker of all gods.

> 证据说明：沙巴卡石（大英博物馆 EA 498）普塔造物后安息。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.memphite.10`

### 11 · 众神进入他们的身体 / The gods enter their bodies

碑文接着说：他生出了众神，他建造了城市，他设立了各州，他把众神安置在他们的神龛里，他确定了他们的祭品，他建立了他们的神殿，他按照他们的心愿做出了他们的身体。于是众神进入了他们的身体，这些身体用各种木头、各种石头、各种泥土，以及在他身上生长的一切东西做成，众神就在其中显现。于是，所有的神和他们的卡都聚集到他的身边，心满意足，与两地之主结合在一起。这一段非常特别：它把神像的制作也归于普塔的创造。在古埃及，神像是用木头、石头、金属等材料做成的，人们相信神灵会进入这些神像，住在神庙之中，接受供奉。普塔作为工匠之神，正是一切神像制作者的保护神。在这里，众神的“身体”——也就是神庙里的神像——被说成是普塔按照众神的心愿亲手造出来的，而那些木头和石头，又都“生长在他身上”，因为他就是大地塔特恩。这样，从天上的众神到神庙中的神像，从城市和州的设立到祭品的安排，一切宗教生活的秩序都来自普塔。

The text goes on: he gave birth to the gods, he made the towns, he established the nomes, he placed the gods in their shrines, he settled their offerings, he established their shrines, he made their bodies according to their wishes. Thus the gods entered into their bodies, of every kind of wood, every kind of stone, every kind of clay, every thing that grows upon him, in which they came to be. Thus were gathered to him all the gods and their kas, content, united with the Lord of the Two Lands. The passage is remarkable: it credits the making of divine images to Ptah's creation too. In Egypt cult statues were made of wood, stone and metal, and the gods were believed to enter them, dwell in the temples and receive offerings. Ptah, as god of craftsmen, was patron of all who made such images. Here the gods' 'bodies' — the statues in the temples — are said to be made by Ptah himself according to the gods' wishes, and the wood and stone 'grow upon him', for he is the earth, Ta-tenen. So from the gods in heaven to the images in the temples, from the founding of towns and nomes to the ordering of offerings, the whole order of religious life comes from Ptah.

> 证据说明：沙巴卡石（大英博物馆 EA 498）众神进入他们的身体。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.memphite.11`

### 12 · 孟菲斯的奥西里斯 / Osiris at Memphis

碑文的最后一部分回到了奥西里斯的故事。它先说，在普塔神庙里那使众神心中欢喜的伟大宝座，就是塔特恩的粮仓，一切生命的女主人，两地的给养由此而来。接着说：奥西里斯淹没在他的水中。伊西斯和奈芙蒂斯向外张望，看见了他，就去照料他。荷鲁斯急忙命令伊西斯和奈芙蒂斯抓住奥西里斯，不让他沉没。她们及时听从了命令，把他带到了岸上。他走进了神秘的门户，带着永恒之主们的荣耀。就这样，奥西里斯在王家要塞进入了大地，那是在他来到的那片土地的北边。他的儿子荷鲁斯在他父亲奥西里斯的怀抱中，作为上下埃及之王出现，众神在他的前面，也在他的后面。在这个版本里，奥西里斯之死的地点被放在了孟菲斯附近：他在水中淹溺，被救上孟菲斯的岸边，在那里进入大地。孟菲斯的祭司们由此把奥西里斯的墓、荷鲁斯的王权与普塔的城市联系在一起。整篇文字从王权开始，经过创世，又以王权结束：荷鲁斯在父亲的怀中成为国王，这正是每一位法老继承王位的神话原型。

The last part of the text returns to Osiris. It says first that the Great Throne in the house of Ptah, which gives joy to the hearts of the gods, is the granary of Ta-tenen, mistress of all life, through which the sustenance of the Two Lands is provided. Then: Osiris drowned in his water. Isis and Nephthys looked out, beheld him and attended to him. Horus quickly commanded Isis and Nephthys to grasp Osiris and prevent his drowning. They heeded in time and brought him to land. He entered the mysterious portals in the glory of the lords of eternity. Thus Osiris came into the earth at the Royal Fortress, to the north of the land to which he had come. And his son Horus arose as King of Upper and Lower Egypt in the arms of his father Osiris, in the presence of the gods who were before him and behind him. In this version Osiris's death is placed near Memphis: he drowns, is brought ashore at Memphis, and there enters the earth. Through it the priests of Memphis linked Osiris's tomb and Horus's kingship to Ptah's city. The whole text begins with kingship, passes through creation and ends with kingship again: Horus becoming king in his father's arms — the mythic model for every pharaoh's succession.

> 证据说明：沙巴卡石（大英博物馆 EA 498）奥西里斯在孟菲斯被救上岸。
> Claim: `claim.v0360.memphite.12`
