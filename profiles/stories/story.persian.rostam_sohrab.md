## 阅读边界

鲁斯塔姆在边境打猎时，拉赫什被人偷走，他一路追到萨曼甘国。夜里，公主塔米娜来到他的房中，说她仰慕他已久，愿嫁给他，为他生一个儿子。两人成婚，鲁斯塔姆临别时留下一枚臂环，说生了儿子就戴在他的手臂上。孩子苏赫拉布长得飞快，十岁就无人能敌。他从母亲那里得知父亲是鲁斯塔姆，就决心率军攻入伊朗，推翻凯·卡乌斯，让父亲做国王。图兰王阿夫拉西亚布暗中命令将领们不让父子相认，盼着两人自相残杀。苏赫拉布攻下白堡，被女战士戈尔达法丽德用计脱身；凯·卡乌斯急召鲁斯塔姆，又因他迟到而发怒。两军对阵，苏赫拉布一再问起鲁斯塔姆，伊朗的俘虏却骗他说鲁斯塔姆不在军中，鲁斯塔姆自己也隐瞒了名字。父子二人斗了三天：第一天不分胜负，第二天苏赫拉布摔倒了父亲却被骗放过了他，第三天鲁斯塔姆向神求回力气，把苏赫拉布摔倒，一剑刺进了他的胸膛。苏赫拉布临死说出父亲的名字，鲁斯塔姆看见了臂环，悲痛欲绝。他派人向凯·卡乌斯求取长生药，国王却迟迟不给，苏赫拉布死在了父亲的怀里。

Hunting on the border, Rostam loses Rakhsh to thieves and tracks him to Samangān. At night the princess Tahmineh comes to his chamber, saying she has long admired him and wishes to marry him and bear his son. They marry, and when he leaves he gives her an armlet: if a son is born, let him wear it on his arm. The boy, Sohrāb, grows wondrously fast and at ten has no equal. Learning from his mother that his father is Rostam, he resolves to invade Iran, overthrow Kay Kāvus and make his father king. Afrāsiyāb of Tūrān secretly orders his generals to keep father and son apart, hoping one will kill the other. Sohrāb takes the White Castle, from which the warrior maiden Gordāfarid escapes him by a ruse; Kay Kāvus summons Rostam and rages at his delay. When the armies meet, Sohrāb asks again and again for Rostam, but an Iranian captive tells him Rostam is not there, and Rostam himself hides his name. Father and son fight for three days: the first undecided; on the second Sohrāb throws his father but is tricked into sparing him; on the third Rostam, his strength restored by prayer, throws Sohrāb and drives a dagger into his breast. Dying, Sohrāb names his father; Rostam sees the armlet and is broken with grief. He sends to Kay Kāvus for the elixir of life, but the king delays, and Sohrāb dies in his father's arms.

### 01 · 丢失的拉赫什 / Rakhsh is lost

有一天，鲁斯塔姆心中烦闷，就带上弓箭和套索，骑着拉赫什到伊朗与图兰交界的地方去打猎。他在一片原野上射死了一头野驴，用树枝生起火来，烤了野驴，吃饱以后，摘下拉赫什的辔头让它去吃草，自己躺在草地上睡着了。这时，一群图兰的骑手路过，看见这匹骏马在原野上独自吃草，就抛出套索把它套住，牵回城里去了。鲁斯塔姆醒来，四处寻找，不见拉赫什的踪影，心中又急又恼：没有了拉赫什，他穿着沉重的铠甲，带着铁杵，只能徒步行走，图兰人会怎样嘲笑他？他顺着马蹄印一路追去，一直追到了萨曼甘国的城门口。萨曼甘的国王和贵族们听说鲁斯塔姆来了，都出城迎接，国王对他说：英雄啊，在我的国土上，没有人敢伤害你，你的马一定能找回来，今晚请到我的宫中做客吧。

One day, restless in heart, Rostam took his bow and lasso and rode Rakhsh to hunt on the border between Iran and Tūrān. On a plain he shot a wild ass, kindled a fire of branches, roasted it and ate his fill, then took off Rakhsh's bridle to let him graze and lay down on the grass to sleep. A band of Turanian riders passed, saw the splendid horse grazing alone, cast their lassos over him and led him off to their city. Rostam woke and searched everywhere, but there was no trace of Rakhsh. He was anxious and vexed: without Rakhsh he must walk in his heavy armour carrying his mace — how the Turanians would mock him! He followed the hoofprints all the way to the gate of the city of Samangān. When the king and nobles of Samangān heard that Rostam had come they rode out to meet him, and the king said: 'Hero, no one in my land would dare to harm you; your horse will surely be found. Be my guest in the palace tonight.'

### 02 · 塔米娜的夜访 / Tahmineh comes by night

萨曼甘的国王为鲁斯塔姆举行了盛大的宴会，鲁斯塔姆喝了酒，被领到一间铺着锦缎的卧室里休息。夜深了，房门忽然悄悄打开，一个侍女提着一支点燃的蜡烛走进来，身后跟着一位美丽的少女，她的脸像月亮，身段像柏树，眉毛像弯弓，头发像黑色的套索。鲁斯塔姆惊奇地问她是谁，深夜来做什么。少女说：我是萨曼甘国王的女儿，名叫塔米娜，在这片土地上没有人见过我的面容。我听过许多关于你的故事：你怎样不怕狮子、恶龙和魔鬼，你的铁杵怎样让大地颤抖。我早就倾心于你，暗暗发誓除了你谁也不嫁。如今神把你送到了我们的城里，我来是要告诉你：如果你愿意，我想做你的妻子，为你生一个像你一样勇敢的儿子。而且，我会让人把你的拉赫什找回来。鲁斯塔姆见她既美丽又聪慧，心中欢喜，第二天就请一位智慧的祭司向国王求婚。国王非常高兴，当即按照礼仪把女儿嫁给了他。

The king of Samangān held a great feast for Rostam, and when he had drunk he was led to a chamber hung with brocade to rest. Deep in the night the door opened softly; a slave-girl came in carrying a lighted candle, and behind her a beautiful maiden, her face like the moon, her figure like a cypress, her brows like bows and her hair like a dark lasso. Astonished, Rostam asked who she was and why she came at night. 'I am the daughter of the king of Samangān,' she said; 'my name is Tahmineh, and no one in this land has seen my face. I have heard many tales of you — how you fear neither lion nor dragon nor demon, how the earth trembles at your mace. I have long given you my heart and secretly vowed to marry no one but you. Now God has brought you to our city, and I have come to tell you: if you will, I wish to be your wife and bear you a son as brave as you. And I will have your Rakhsh found.' Seeing her as wise as she was beautiful, Rostam was glad, and the next day he sent a wise priest to ask the king for her hand. The king rejoiced and gave him his daughter by the proper rites.

### 03 · 留下的信物 / The token left behind

婚后不久，人们找回了拉赫什，鲁斯塔姆必须回到伊朗去了。临别时，他从自己的手臂上解下一枚臂环——上面镶着一颗宝石，是一件人人都认得的珍宝——交给塔米娜，说：好好保管它。如果神赐给你一个女儿，就把它编进她的头发里，愿它保佑她平安；如果你生了一个儿子，就把它系在他的手臂上，让他像我一样成为勇敢的人，让他凭着这件信物来找我。说完，他拥抱了塔米娜，跨上拉赫什，向伊朗的方向驰去，从此再也没有回到萨曼甘。九个月以后，塔米娜生下了一个儿子，他的脸像月亮一样明亮，笑起来像花一样灿烂，人们说他长得和鲁斯塔姆、和祖父萨姆一模一样。塔米娜给他取名为苏赫拉布，把那枚臂环系在了他的手臂上。

Soon after the marriage Rakhsh was found, and Rostam had to return to Iran. At parting he took from his arm an armlet set with a jewel, a treasure known to all, and gave it to Tahmineh: 'Keep this well. If God gives you a daughter, braid it into her hair and may it keep her safe; if you bear a son, bind it on his arm, that he may become brave as I am, and by this token let him come to find me.' He embraced Tahmineh, mounted Rakhsh and rode off towards Iran, and never came back to Samangān. Nine months later Tahmineh bore a son with a face bright as the moon and a smile like a flower, and people said he was the very image of Rostam and of his grandfather Sām. Tahmineh named him Sohrāb and bound the armlet on his arm.

### 04 · 苏赫拉布的成长 / Sohrāb grows up

苏赫拉布长得出奇地快：一个月大时就像一岁的孩子，三岁时就学会了骑马射箭，五岁时有了狮子般的勇气，到了十岁，萨曼甘没有一个人能和他比武。他胸膛宽阔，手臂粗壮，跑起来像风一样快。有一天，他来到母亲面前，问她：我为什么比别的孩子都高大强壮？我的父亲是谁？别人问起我的家世，我该怎样回答？塔米娜说：孩子，不要生气，你的父亲就是伟大的鲁斯塔姆，扎尔的儿子，萨姆的孙子，世上没有比他更勇敢的英雄。她拿出鲁斯塔姆寄来的信和三颗宝石给他看，又叮嘱他：千万不要让图兰王阿夫拉西亚布知道这件事，他是鲁斯塔姆的仇敌，会想办法害你；也不要让你父亲知道你已经长成了一个勇士，否则他会把你从我身边叫走。苏赫拉布听了，热血沸腾，说：我要率领大军进攻伊朗，推翻凯·卡乌斯，让我的父亲鲁斯塔姆坐上王位；然后再去打败阿夫拉西亚布，让天下不再有两个国王。

Sohrāb grew astonishingly fast: at one month he was like a child of a year, at three he rode and shot, at five he had a lion's courage, and at ten no one in Samangān could match him in arms. Broad-chested and strong-armed, he ran swift as the wind. One day he came to his mother and asked: 'Why am I taller and stronger than other children? Who is my father? What shall I say when people ask about my birth?' Tahmineh said: 'Do not be angry, child. Your father is the great Rostam, son of Zāl, grandson of Sām; there is no braver hero in the world.' She showed him a letter Rostam had sent and three jewels, and warned him: 'Never let Afrāsiyāb, king of Tūrān, know this; he is Rostam's enemy and would seek to harm you. Nor let your father know you have grown into a warrior, or he will call you away from me.' Sohrāb's blood was fired: 'I will lead an army against Iran, overthrow Kay Kāvus and set my father Rostam on the throne; then I will defeat Afrāsiyāb, so that the world has no longer two kings.'

### 05 · 阿夫拉西亚布的阴谋 / Afrāsiyāb's plot

苏赫拉布为自己挑选了一匹配得上他的骏马，召集了一支军队。消息传到图兰王阿夫拉西亚布那里，他心中暗喜，想出了一个阴险的计策。他派出两位将领胡曼和巴尔曼，带着一万二千名精兵和许多礼物去投奔苏赫拉布，表面上是支持他攻打伊朗。他在私下里对两位将领说：这件事一定要保守秘密，千万不能让父子二人相认。如果苏赫拉布在战场上遇见鲁斯塔姆，就要想办法让他们彼此不知道对方是谁。也许这个年轻的勇士会杀死鲁斯塔姆，那样伊朗就失去了最强的支柱；也许鲁斯塔姆会杀死这个孩子，那样鲁斯塔姆的心也会被悲伤压垮。不论哪一种，对我们图兰都有好处。胡曼和巴尔曼领了命令，带着礼物和阿夫拉西亚布的书信来到萨曼甘，苏赫拉布高兴地接待了他们，一点也不知道这背后的阴谋。

Sohrāb chose himself a horse worthy of him and gathered an army. When the news reached Afrāsiyāb, king of Tūrān, he secretly rejoiced and devised a wicked plan. He sent two generals, Hūmān and Bārmān, with twelve thousand picked men and many gifts to join Sohrāb, as though to support his attack on Iran. In private he told the two: 'Keep this secret: father and son must never know each other. If Sohrāb meets Rostam in battle, contrive that neither knows who the other is. Perhaps the young warrior will kill Rostam, and Iran will lose its strongest pillar; perhaps Rostam will kill the boy, and grief will break Rostam's heart. Either way Tūrān gains.' Hūmān and Bārmān took their orders and came to Samangān with gifts and a letter from Afrāsiyāb, and Sohrāb welcomed them gladly, knowing nothing of the plot behind them.

### 06 · 白堡的女战士 / The warrior maiden of the White Castle

苏赫拉布率领大军进入伊朗，来到边境上的白堡。守城的勇士哈吉尔出城迎战，几个回合就被苏赫拉布打下马来，成了俘虏。城中的人们惊慌失措。这时，城里有一位名叫戈尔达法丽德的少女，她是守将的女儿，勇敢而精通武艺。她把头发藏进头盔里，穿上铠甲，骑马出城，向苏赫拉布挑战。两人交战，她射出的箭像雨点一样密集，苏赫拉布用盾牌挡住，冲上前去，用长矛挑落了她的头盔，她的长发一下子披散下来，苏赫拉布这才发现对手是一个少女，心中又惊又爱。戈尔达法丽德见势不妙，就对他说：你若在两军面前和一个女子交战，会被人笑话的。不如我们讲和，城堡和城里的一切都归你，我在城中等你。苏赫拉布相信了她，跟到城门前，她却一闪身进了城，关上城门，在城墙上笑着对他说：图兰的勇士啊，你还是回去吧，等鲁斯塔姆来了，你就没有好日子过了。当天夜里，城里的人们从一条秘密的地道逃走了，第二天苏赫拉布攻进城时，城中已经空无一人。

Sohrāb led his army into Iran and came to the White Castle on the border. Its champion Hajīr rode out against him and was unhorsed and taken prisoner within a few passes. The people in the castle were in dismay. Among them was a maiden named Gordāfarid, daughter of the commander, brave and skilled in arms. She hid her hair in a helmet, put on armour, rode out and challenged Sohrāb. They fought; her arrows fell thick as rain, but Sohrāb took them on his shield, charged, and with his spear knocked off her helmet, and her long hair tumbled down. Only then did he see his foe was a maiden, and he was astonished and drawn to her. Seeing her peril, Gordāfarid said: 'You will be mocked if you fight a woman before both armies. Let us make peace: the castle and all in it are yours, and I will wait for you within.' Sohrāb believed her and followed her to the gate, but she slipped inside, shut the gate, and laughed at him from the walls: 'Warrior of Tūrān, go home; when Rostam comes, your good days are over.' That night the people of the castle escaped by a secret passage, and when Sohrāb stormed it the next day it was empty.

### 07 · 国王的召唤 / The king's summons

白堡失守的消息传到凯·卡乌斯那里，国王惊恐万分，立刻写信给鲁斯塔姆，派勇士吉夫日夜兼程送到扎布尔斯坦，请他火速前来抵挡这个年轻的敌人。鲁斯塔姆读了信，听说图兰来了一个年轻的勇士，心中奇怪：图兰怎么会有这样的英雄？我只在萨曼甘有过一个孩子，可他还小，不可能是他。他并不着急，留吉夫在家中饮酒作乐，一连几天才出发。等他们来到凯·卡乌斯的宫廷，国王勃然大怒，骂鲁斯塔姆藐视王命，下令把他和吉夫一起吊死。鲁斯塔姆怒不可遏，说：我是伊朗的守护者，不是你的奴隶！你这次自己去对付那个年轻人吧。他骑上拉赫什扬长而去。众位勇士都慌了，年老的古达尔兹赶去追他，劝他说：人们会说鲁斯塔姆是怕了那个年轻人才走的。鲁斯塔姆听了，不愿背上胆怯的名声，就回到了宫中，凯·卡乌斯也向他道了歉。

When news of the White Castle's fall reached Kay Kāvus, the king was terrified and at once wrote to Rostam, sending the champion Gīv riding day and night to Zābulistān to beg him to come with all speed against the young enemy. Reading the letter and hearing of a young champion from Tūrān, Rostam wondered: 'How could Tūrān have such a hero? I had only one child, in Samangān, and he is still small; it cannot be he.' He was in no hurry, kept Gīv feasting at his house for several days, and only then set out. When they reached the court, Kay Kāvus flew into a rage, accused Rostam of scorning the royal command, and ordered him and Gīv hanged. Rostam was furious: 'I am the guardian of Iran, not your slave! Deal with the young man yourself this time.' He mounted Rakhsh and rode off. The champions were dismayed; old Gūdarz rode after him and said: 'People will say Rostam left because he feared the young man.' Unwilling to bear the name of coward, Rostam returned to court, and Kay Kāvus begged his pardon.

### 08 · 营帐边的夜探 / Night in the enemy camp

伊朗大军来到白堡前，与图兰军队隔着平原对阵。夜里，鲁斯塔姆换上图兰人的衣服，悄悄潜入敌营，想亲眼看看这个年轻的勇士。他看见苏赫拉布坐在宴席的主位上，身材高大，像一棵挺拔的柏树，相貌英武，鲁斯塔姆心中暗暗称奇。坐在苏赫拉布身边的，是塔米娜的兄弟赞德·拉兹姆，塔米娜派他随军，就是为了在战场上把鲁斯塔姆指认给苏赫拉布。赞德·拉兹姆出帐时，在暗处撞见了陌生的鲁斯塔姆，喝问他是谁，鲁斯塔姆一拳打在他的头上，把他打死了。唯一认得鲁斯塔姆的人，就这样死去了。第二天早晨，苏赫拉布登上高处，让俘虏哈吉尔把伊朗军中每一位勇士的帐篷指给他看，他特别问到一座绿色的大帐篷，帐前竖着一面绘着龙的旗帜，旁边拴着一匹高大的骏马。哈吉尔心想：如果说出这是鲁斯塔姆，这个年轻人一定会去找他决斗，也许会把他杀死。于是他撒谎说：那是一位从中国来的盟友，鲁斯塔姆不在军中，他在扎布尔斯坦过节。

The Iranian army came before the White Castle and faced the Turanians across the plain. At night Rostam put on Turanian clothes and stole into the enemy camp to see the young champion for himself. He saw Sohrāb seated at the head of the feast, tall and straight as a cypress, noble of face, and marvelled in his heart. Beside Sohrāb sat Zende-razm, Tahmineh's brother, whom she had sent with the army to point out Rostam to Sohrāb on the field. As Zende-razm left the tent he met the stranger in the dark and challenged him; Rostam struck him on the head with his fist and killed him. So the one man who knew Rostam by sight was dead. In the morning Sohrāb climbed a height and had the captive Hajīr point out the tent of each Iranian champion; he asked especially about a great green tent with a banner painted with a dragon before it and a tall horse tethered beside it. Hajīr thought: if I say this is Rostam's, the young man will seek him out and may kill him. So he lied: 'That is an ally from China; Rostam is not with the army; he is in Zābulistān for a festival.'

### 09 · 第一天 / The first day

苏赫拉布骑马冲到伊朗军前，大声挑战，又冲进营中，砍倒了凯·卡乌斯帐前的旗帜，伊朗的勇士们没有一个敢出来应战。凯·卡乌斯只好请鲁斯塔姆出战。鲁斯塔姆穿上虎皮战袍，骑着拉赫什来到两军之间。他看着眼前这个年轻人，心中涌起一种说不出的感情，对他说：年轻人，你还这样年轻，何必来送死？我见过许多战场，打败过无数魔鬼和勇士，我可怜你，不忍心杀你。苏赫拉布望着这位老英雄，心中也忽然生出一种奇怪的亲切感，问道：请你告诉我实话，你是不是鲁斯塔姆，扎尔的儿子？鲁斯塔姆为了不让对方知道自己的身份，回答说：我不是鲁斯塔姆，我只是他的一个手下，他是一位大英雄，而我连马厩都没有。苏赫拉布失望极了。两人用长矛、刀剑和铁杵交战，长矛断了，刀剑卷了刃，铁杵弯了，两人都汗流浃背，筋疲力尽，直到黄昏也分不出胜负，只好约定第二天再战。

Sohrāb rode up before the Iranian host and shouted his challenge, then charged into the camp and cut down the banner before Kay Kāvus's tent, and not one Iranian champion dared come out. Kay Kāvus had to ask Rostam to fight. Rostam put on his tiger-skin coat and rode Rakhsh out between the armies. Looking at the young man, he felt a stirring he could not name, and said: 'Young man, you are still so young; why come to your death? I have seen many battlefields and overcome countless demons and champions; I pity you and would not kill you.' Gazing at the old hero, Sohrāb too felt a strange kinship and asked: 'Tell me truly, are you Rostam, son of Zāl?' To keep his name hidden, Rostam answered: 'I am not Rostam, only one of his men; he is a great hero, and I have not even a stable of my own.' Sohrāb's hope fell. They fought with spears, swords and maces until the spears broke, the swords were notched and the maces bent; both ran with sweat and were spent, and when evening came neither had won, and they agreed to fight again the next day.

### 10 · 第二天的计谋 / The ruse of the second day

第二天，苏赫拉布一见到鲁斯塔姆，就笑着迎上去说：昨夜你睡得好吗？我们为什么要打仗呢？我心里对你有说不出的亲近，我们放下武器，坐下来一起喝酒吧。告诉我，你真的不是鲁斯塔姆吗？鲁斯塔姆说：我们昨天约好了要摔跤，不要再说这些话了。两人下马摔跤，从早晨一直扭打到中午，苏赫拉布像一头狮子扑向猎物一样，把鲁斯塔姆摔倒在地，骑在他的胸口上，拔出匕首就要砍下他的头。鲁斯塔姆急中生智，说：年轻人，在我们伊朗有一个规矩：两个勇士摔跤，第一次把对手摔倒的人不能杀他，要第二次摔倒对手才能取他的性命。苏赫拉布年轻而骄傲，相信了这番话，就放开了他。回到营中，胡曼听说了这件事，责备苏赫拉布说：你放走了一头已经落进网里的狮子，你会为此后悔的。苏赫拉布却不以为意。

On the second day, as soon as Sohrāb saw Rostam he came to meet him smiling: 'Did you sleep well? Why should we fight? My heart feels a kinship with you I cannot explain; let us lay down our arms and sit and drink together. Tell me — are you truly not Rostam?' Rostam said: 'We agreed yesterday to wrestle; say no more of this.' They dismounted and wrestled from morning till noon, and Sohrāb, like a lion falling on its prey, threw Rostam to the ground, sat on his chest and drew his dagger to cut off his head. Quick-witted in his peril, Rostam said: 'Young man, among us in Iran there is a custom: when two champions wrestle, the one who first throws his foe may not kill him; only when he throws him a second time may he take his life.' Young and proud, Sohrāb believed it and let him go. Back in camp Hūmān heard of it and rebuked him: 'You have let go a lion already in the net; you will regret it.' But Sohrāb thought little of it.

### 11 · 第三天 / The third day

鲁斯塔姆脱身以后，来到一条溪边，洗净了身体，跪在地上向造物主祈祷。据说他年轻的时候力气大得惊人，走在岩石上，双脚都会陷进石头里，他曾经请求神收回一部分力量，好让他能像常人一样行走。现在，他祈求神把那份力量还给他。神应允了他的祈祷。第三天，两人又在战场上相遇，又一次摔起跤来。这一次，鲁斯塔姆恢复了全部的力量，他抓住苏赫拉布的头和腰，像拔起一棵树一样把他举起来，重重地摔在地上。他知道这个年轻人不会被压在地上太久，就迅速拔出匕首，刺进了苏赫拉布的胸膛。苏赫拉布在血泊中翻过身来，叹息着说：这是我自作自受，命运把我的钥匙交到了你的手里。可是你要知道，无论你逃到哪里，就算你变成水里的鱼、天上的星，我的父亲也会找到你，为我报仇。我的父亲就是鲁斯塔姆。我来这里，只是为了找他。

Having escaped, Rostam went to a stream, washed himself, knelt and prayed to the Creator. It is told that in his youth his strength had been so great that when he walked on rock his feet sank into the stone, and he had asked God to take back part of it so that he could walk like other men. Now he prayed God to give that strength back, and God granted his prayer. On the third day they met again and wrestled once more. This time, with all his strength restored, Rostam seized Sohrāb by the head and waist, lifted him like an uprooted tree and dashed him to the ground. Knowing the young man would not stay down long, he drew his dagger and drove it into Sohrāb's breast. Sohrāb turned over in his blood and sighed: 'I have brought this on myself; fate gave the key of my life into your hand. But know this: wherever you flee, though you become a fish in the sea or a star in the sky, my father will find you and avenge me. My father is Rostam. I came here only to find him.'

### 12 · 长生药与哀悼 / The elixir and the mourning

鲁斯塔姆听见这句话，眼前一黑，昏倒在地。醒来后，他颤抖着问：你有什么能证明你是鲁斯塔姆的儿子？我就是鲁斯塔姆！苏赫拉布说：解开我的铠甲，看看我的手臂。鲁斯塔姆解开铠甲，看见了那枚自己当年留给塔米娜的臂环。他撕扯着自己的衣服和头发，痛哭失声，说：我亲手杀死了我的儿子。苏赫拉布说：哭也没有用了，这是命中注定的。鲁斯塔姆派古达尔兹去见凯·卡乌斯，请求国王赐给他宫中珍藏的长生药，能治好一切创伤。凯·卡乌斯却心想：如果这个年轻人活下来，父子二人联手，我的王位还能保得住吗？他迟迟不肯给药。鲁斯塔姆决定亲自去求，还没有走到国王那里，就有人追上来报告：苏赫拉布已经死了。鲁斯塔姆悲痛欲绝，想用匕首自尽，被众人拦住。他烧掉了自己的帐篷和宝座，用金色的锦缎包裹儿子的遗体，带回扎布尔斯坦安葬。消息传到萨曼甘，塔米娜撕碎了衣裳，日夜痛哭，抱着儿子的战袍和骏马，不到一年，就随着儿子离开了人世。

At these words the world went dark before Rostam's eyes and he fell senseless. When he came to, he asked trembling: 'What token have you that you are Rostam's son? I am Rostam!' Sohrāb said: 'Undo my armour and look at my arm.' Rostam undid the armour and saw the armlet he had once left with Tahmineh. He tore his clothes and his hair and cried aloud: 'I have killed my own son with my own hand.' Sohrāb said: 'Weeping is useless now; this was fated.' Rostam sent Gūdarz to Kay Kāvus to beg the elixir kept in the king's treasury that heals every wound. But Kay Kāvus thought: if this young man lives and father and son join together, will my throne be safe? and he delayed. Rostam resolved to go and beg it himself, but before he reached the king a messenger overtook him: Sohrāb was dead. In his grief Rostam would have killed himself with his dagger, but the others held him back. He burned his tent and his throne, wrapped his son's body in golden brocade and carried it home to Zābulistān for burial. When the news reached Samangān, Tahmineh tore her clothes and wept day and night, clasping her son's armour and his horse, and within a year she followed her son out of the world.
