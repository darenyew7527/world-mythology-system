## 阅读边界

大英博物馆藏《阿尼纸草》第3框画着审判大厅：天平一边放着阿尼的心脏，另一边是代表玛阿特的羽毛。胡狼头的阿努比斯扶稳天平，托特既以狒狒形象坐在天平上，又以朱鹭头书吏的形象准备记录结果。

British Museum Papyrus of Ani, frame 3, shows the Hall of Judgment: Ani's heart in one pan, a feather representing Maat in the other. Jackal-headed Anubis steadies the balance, and Thoth appears both as a baboon on top of it and as the ibis-headed scribe ready to record the result.

### 01 · 心脏与羽毛 / A heart and a feather

画面中央是一座天平。左边的秤盘里放着阿尼的心脏，右边是一根羽毛，代表真理与秩序女神玛阿特。

At the centre stands a balance. In the left pan lies Ani's heart; in the right, a feather representing Maat, goddess of truth and order.

> 证据说明：大英博物馆 EA10470,3 藏品描述。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.egypt.ani_depicts_weighing`

### 02 · 阿努比斯扶稳天平 / Anubis steadies the balance

胡狼头的阿努比斯跪在天平旁，拉着右边秤盘的绳子，扶稳天平的铅锤。博物馆的说明称他是“在防腐之所的那一位”。

Jackal-headed Anubis kneels by the balance, holding the cord of the right-hand pan and steadying its plumb bob. The museum describes him as 'he who is in the place of embalming'.

> 证据说明：同一藏品描述中关于阿努比斯的部分。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.egypt.anubis_weighing`

### 03 · 托特准备记录 / Thoth stands ready to record

托特在画中出现两次：一只小狒狒坐在天平顶端；右边站着朱鹭头的“众神书吏”，手持调色板和芦苇笔，准备记下审问的结果。

Thoth appears twice: as a small baboon sitting on top of the balance, and as the ibis-headed scribe of the gods standing to the right with palette and reed, ready to write down the result.

> 证据说明：同一藏品描述中关于托特的部分。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.egypt.thoth_weighing`
> 未知／边界：称量的最终结果和吞噬心脏的怪兽不在这条博物馆描述里，本站不补写。
