# 佩蕾的旅程：从卡希基到哈雷茂茂 / Pele's journey to Halemaʻumaʻu

- ID: `story.hawaiian.pele_journey`
- 文明／传统: 夏威夷
- 故事类型: `MYTHIC_NARRATIVE`
- 证据状态: `SOURCE_BACKED`
- 阅读时间: 4 分钟

## 阅读边界

美国国家公园管理局概述夏威夷口述传统：火山女神佩蕾生在遥远的卡希基，为寻找适合她的火与家人的居所，远航来到夏威夷群岛，一路向东南迁移，最后在基拉韦厄山顶的哈雷茂茂火山口安家，据说至今仍住在那里。按照夏威夷传统，经过她的土地要先请求许可。

The National Park Service summarizes Hawaiian oral history: the volcano deity Pele was born in distant Kahiki and voyaged to the Hawaiian Islands seeking a home for her fire and family, moving southeast until she settled in Halemaʻumaʻu crater at the summit of Kīlauea, where she is said still to live. In Hawaiian tradition one asks her permission to travel through her land.

### 01 · 生在卡希基 / Born in Kahiki

国家公园管理局介绍说，按照口述传统，佩蕾是夏威夷的火山之神，是一股自然之力，也是这些火山地貌的创造者。她出生在遥远的卡希基，为了给自己的火和家人寻找合适的家，乘船来到夏威夷群岛。

The National Park Service explains that, in oral history, Pele is the Hawaiian volcano deity, an elemental force and the creator of these volcanic landscapes. Born in the distant land of Kahiki, she voyaged to the Hawaiian Islands to find a suitable home for her fire and family.

> 证据说明：NPS“Pele”与“Holo Mai Pele”页面。

### 02 · 一路向东南 / Island by island to the southeast

她一座岛一座岛地向东南迁移，寻找能安顿下来的地方。

She moved southeast through the islands, one after another, looking for a place to settle.

### 03 · 哈雷茂茂的家 / A home in Halemaʻumaʻu

最后她来到基拉韦厄山顶的哈雷茂茂火山口，把这里定为永久的家。据说她至今仍住在那里。

At last she came to Halemaʻumaʻu crater at the summit of Kīlauea and made it her permanent home. She is said to live there still.

> 证据说明：NPS 两页介绍。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.hawaii.pele_halemaumau`

### 04 · 经过她的土地 / Passing through her land

在夏威夷传统中，经过佩蕾的土地要先请求她的许可。国家公园管理局也请访客在这片神圣的地景中保持尊重。公园还提醒：不同的 moʻolelo 版本可能差别很大。

In Hawaiian tradition it is customary to ask Pele's permission to travel through her land, and the park asks visitors to behave respectfully in this sacred landscape. It also notes that versions of the moʻolelo may differ, sometimes greatly.
