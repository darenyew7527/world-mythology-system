## 塔内 · Tāne

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.maori.creation_many](../story.maori.creation_many.md)

> Te Ara 所说的森林之神；在部分部族版本中是分开天地的重要角色。

> *Named by Te Ara as atua of the forests; in some tribal versions important in separating earth and sky.*

### 1. 从黑暗中出现 / Out of darkness

Te Ara 写道：在毛利传统中，创世的核心行动是天与地的分离。故事从黑暗与虚无开始，天父兰吉努伊与地母帕帕图阿努库从中出现。起初天地紧紧相连，他们的孩子就出生在两者之间。

*Te Ara writes that in Māori tradition the central act of creation is the separation of earth and sky. The story begins in darkness and nothingness, from which Ranginui the sky father and Papatūānuku the earth mother emerge. At first earth and sky are joined, and their children are born between them.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Māori creation traditions · Page 1 — Common threads in creation stories · Te Ara“毛利创世传统”第1页。</sub>

### 2. 孩子们分开父母 / The children part their parents

孩子们决定把父母分开。天与地一旦分离，光就照进了世界——从黑暗走向光明，正是由孩子们的这一行动完成的。

*The children decide to separate their parents. Once earth and sky are apart, light enters the world: the move from darkness to light is achieved by the children's act.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Māori creation traditions · Page 1 — Common threads in creation stories · 同一页面。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：Te Ara 说明：在部分部族版本中，塔内在分开天地时起重要作用；本站不选定某一部族的说法。</sub>

### 3. 自然的众神 / Atua of the natural world

分离之后，孩子们成为自然各部分的神（atua）：塔内是森林之神，塔加罗阿是海洋之神，还有地震之神鲁奥莫科、风与天气之神塔维里马特阿。把这些神编进同一个谱系，是毛利人解释自然世界的传统方式。

*After the separation the children become atua of different parts of nature: Tāne of the forests, Tangaroa of the sea, Rūaumoko of earthquakes, Tāwhirimātea of winds and weather. Weaving these atua into one genealogy is the traditional Māori way of explaining the natural world.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Māori creation traditions · Page 1 — Common threads in creation stories · 同一页面。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：鲁奥莫科与塔维里马特阿尚未建立独立实体。</sub>

### 4. 不是只有一个版本 / Not a single version

Te Ara 也提醒，各部族的创世故事差异很大，特别是关于至高存在的角色。这里只讲公开页面所总结的共同线索，具名部族的版本须经相应权威授权。

*Te Ara also cautions that tribal versions vary considerably, especially on the role of a supreme being. This retelling covers only the common threads the public page summarizes; named iwi versions require permission from the appropriate authority.*

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：具名 iwi／hapū 版本仍为权限阻塞。</sub>
