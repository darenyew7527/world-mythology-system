## 布里吉德 · Brigid

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.irish.second_battle_mag_tuired](../story.irish.second_battle_mag_tuired.md)

> 《第二次马格图雷德之战》中达格达之女布里格，为儿子鲁阿丹哀哭，是爱尔兰第一次听到的哀歌。

> *Bríg, daughter of the Dagda in Cath Maige Tuired, whose keening for her son Rúadán is the first heard in Ireland.*

### 1. 达格达的大锅 / The Dagda's cauldron

达努神族带来了四件宝物，其中一件是从穆里亚斯城带来的达格达的大锅：凡是围着它吃饭的人，从没有一个是不饱足地离开的。

*The Túatha Dé Danann brought four treasures. One was the Dagda's cauldron from the city of Murias: no company that ate from it ever went away unsatisfied.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Cath Maige Tuired: The Second Battle of Mag Tuired (English translation) · §§6–167 · Gray 本 §6。</sub>

### 2. 百艺之主来到塔拉 / The master of all arts at Tara

鲁格来到塔拉的大门前，守门人问他会什么手艺。他报出一门又一门，而宫里每门手艺都已有人；最后他问：有没有一个人能同时精通所有这些？于是他以“百艺之主”的身份被请了进去。

*Lug comes to the door of Tara, and the doorkeeper asks what art he brings. He names one after another, and each already has a master within — until he asks whether anyone there has all of them at once. So he is admitted as the Samildánach, master of all arts.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Cath Maige Tuired: The Second Battle of Mag Tuired (English translation) · §§6–167 · Gray 本 §§53–72。</sub>

### 3. 萨温节的河边 / By the river at Samain

萨温节前后，达格达在乌恩辛河边遇见一个女人正在洗东西，一只脚踩在南岸，一只脚踩在北岸。她就是莫莉甘。两人结合之后，她告诉达格达弗魔族将在哪里登陆，并许诺要取弗魔王因德赫的心血。

*Around Samain the Dagda meets a woman washing at the river Unshin, one foot on the southern bank and one on the northern. She is the Morrígan. After they unite she tells him where the Fomoire will land and promises to take the heart's blood of their king Indech.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Cath Maige Tuired: The Second Battle of Mag Tuired (English translation) · §§6–167 · Gray 本 §84。</sub>

### 4. 第一次哀歌 / The first keening

布里格是达格达的女儿，她和布雷斯的儿子鲁阿丹被派去刺杀铁匠戈伊布纽，却被戈伊布纽杀死。布里格赶来为儿子哀哭，先是尖声恸哭，最后低声啜泣——这是爱尔兰第一次听到哀歌与哭泣。

*Rúadán, son of Bres and of Bríg the Dagda's daughter, is sent to kill the smith Goibniu and is killed instead. Bríg comes and keens for her son, shrieking first and weeping at the end — the first time keening and weeping were heard in Ireland.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Cath Maige Tuired: The Second Battle of Mag Tuired (English translation) · §§6–167 · Gray 本 §§123–125。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：文本写作 Bríg；与数据库中“布里吉德”实体的对应依学界通行用法。</sub>

### 5. 巴洛尔的毒眼 / Balor's deadly eye

弗魔族勇士巴洛尔有一只毒眼，只在战场上睁开，要四个人用把手才能抬起眼皮。鲁格用投石一击，石头把那只眼睛从巴洛尔脑后打了出去，结果看见它的反而是他自己的军队。

*The Fomorian champion Balor has a deadly eye, opened only on the battlefield, its lid lifted by four men with a handle. Lug casts a sling stone that drives the eye out through the back of Balor's head, so that it is his own host that looks upon it.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Cath Maige Tuired: The Second Battle of Mag Tuired (English translation) · §§6–167 · Gray 本 §§133–135。</sub>

### 6. 莫莉甘宣告胜利 / The Morrígan proclaims the victory

战斗结束后，恩玛斯的女儿莫莉甘把这场大胜宣告给爱尔兰的王者高地、神丘里的众灵、各条大河与河口，然后又说出关于未来的预言。

*When the battle is won, the Morrígan, daughter of Ernmas, proclaims the great victory to the royal heights of Ireland, its síd-hosts, its chief waters and river-mouths — and then speaks prophecy.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Cath Maige Tuired: The Second Battle of Mag Tuired (English translation) · §§6–167 · Gray 本 §§166–167。</sub>
