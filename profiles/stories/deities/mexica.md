## 特拉洛克 · Tlaloc

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.mexica.templo_mayor_twin_shrines](../story.mexica.templo_mayor_twin_shrines.md)

> 与农业相关的雨神；大神庙北侧是他的神庙（墨西哥国家人类学与历史研究所）。

> *Rain god linked to agriculture whose temple occupied the north side of the Templo Mayor (INAH).*

### 1. 南边的战神 / The war god on the south

大神庙南侧的神殿属于维齐洛波奇特利——墨西加人的太阳战神，也是他们的守护神。

*The southern chapel of the Templo Mayor belongs to Huitzilopochtli, the Mexica's solar god of war and their patron.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Museo de Sitio del Templo Mayor · Museum description · INAH 大神庙遗址博物馆说明。</sub>

### 2. 北边的雨神 / The rain god on the north

北侧的神殿属于特拉洛克——掌管雨水、与农业紧密相连的神。战争与农业，两位神并立在同一座金字塔的顶端。

*The northern temple belongs to Tlaloc, god of rain and closely tied to agriculture. War and farming stand side by side at the top of one pyramid.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Museo de Sitio del Templo Mayor · Museum description · INAH 大神庙遗址博物馆说明。</sub>

### 3. 一层层长高的神庙 / A temple that grew in layers

据 INAH 介绍，墨西加人在1325年建立特诺奇蒂特兰时修了一座小神庙，此后经过多次重建和扩建，越修越高。

*According to INAH, the Mexica built a small temple when they founded Tenochtitlan in 1325, and it was rebuilt and enlarged many times over.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Museo de Sitio del Templo Mayor · Museum description · INAH 大神庙遗址博物馆说明。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：各建造阶段的具体年代仍有不确定之处。</sub>

## 特斯卡特利波卡 · Tezcatlipoca

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.mexica.quetzalcoatl_tollan](../story.mexica.quetzalcoatl_tollan.md)

> 又称蒂特拉卡瓦；《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷中令羽蛇神在图拉衰落的巫师之一。

> *Also called Titlacauan; among the sorcerers who bring Quetzalcoatl's glory at Tollan to an end (Florentine Codex Book 3).*

### 1. 被当作神的蒂特拉卡瓦 / Titlacauan, held to be a god

第三卷第2章讲人们怎样把一位叫蒂特拉卡瓦、也叫特斯卡特利波卡的人物当作神来敬奉。

*Chapter 2 of Book 3 tells how one called Titlacauan, or Tezcatlipoca, came to be regarded as a god.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Digital Florentine Codex · Book 3, chapters 2–5 and 14 · 《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷第2章。</sub>

### 2. 图拉的大法师 / The great wizard of Tollan

第3章讲羽蛇神——图拉的一位大法师。第4章说，当巫师们来到他身边，他的荣光便走到了尽头；第5章又记下蒂特拉卡瓦带来的另一个异兆。

*Chapter 3 tells of Quetzalcoatl, a great wizard at Tollan. Chapter 4 relates how his glory came to an end when the sorcerers came to him, and chapter 5 records another portent brought about by Titlacauan.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Digital Florentine Codex · Book 3, chapters 2–5 and 14 · 《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷第3–5章。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：本段依据章节标题与概要，具体情节尚未逐句核对。</sub>

### 3. 雪山之间 / Between the snow mountains

第14章记载，羽蛇神离去时，追随他的人经过伊斯塔克特佩特尔与波波卡特佩特尔两座雪山之间，被冻死在冰雪里。

*Chapter 14 records that as Quetzalcoatl departs, his vassals freeze to death in the ice as they pass between Iztactepetl and Popocatepetl.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Digital Florentine Codex · Book 3, chapters 2–5 and 14 · 《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷第14章。</sub>

## 科约尔沙乌基 · Coyolxauhqui

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.mexica.birth_huitzilopochtli](../story.mexica.birth_huitzilopochtli.md)

> *Daughter of Coatlicue who leads her brothers against their mother and is killed by Huitzilopochtli at Coatepec.*

### 1. 一团羽毛 / A ball of feathers

科阿特利库埃住在蛇山上。一天她打扫时，一团羽毛从天上飘下来。她把羽毛捡起来，塞在腰间——就这样怀孕了。

*Coatlicue lives on Coatepec. One day as she sweeps, a ball of feathers drifts down. She picks it up and tucks it at her waist — and so she becomes pregnant.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Digital Florentine Codex · Book 3, chapter 1 · 《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷第1章。</sub>

### 2. 兄妹要杀母亲 / Her children turn against her

她的女儿科约尔沙乌基对兄弟们——四百个南方人——说：母亲让我们蒙羞，我们必须杀了她。于是他们全副武装，朝蛇山进发。

*Her daughter Coyolxauhqui tells her brothers, the four hundred Centzon Huitznahua, that their mother has shamed them and must be killed. Armed, they set out for Coatepec.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Digital Florentine Codex · Book 3, chapter 1 · 《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷第1章。</sub>

### 3. 全副武装地出生 / Born fully armed

腹中的维齐洛波奇特利安慰母亲：不要怕，我知道该怎么做。敌人冲上山顶的那一刻，他出生了，手持盾牌、投枪和投枪器。

*From the womb Huitzilopochtli comforts his mother: do not be afraid, I know what to do. The moment the attackers reach the summit he is born, carrying shield, darts and dart-thrower.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Digital Florentine Codex · Book 3, chapter 1 · 《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷第1章。</sub>

### 4. 山顶的胜利 / Victory on the summit

维齐洛波奇特利砍下科约尔沙乌基的头，她的身体沿着蛇山滚落下去。四百个兄弟也被他从山顶追得四散奔逃。

*Huitzilopochtli cuts off Coyolxauhqui's head, and her body tumbles down the side of Coatepec. He drives the four hundred brothers from the summit, scattering them in every direction.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Digital Florentine Codex · Book 3, chapter 1 · 《佛罗伦萨手抄本》第三卷第1章。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：学者认为这段叙事与大神庙的布局相呼应，属于解释，不写成事实。</sub>
