## 安格拉·曼纽 · Angra Mainyu

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.zoroastrian.sixteen_lands](../story.zoroastrian.sixteen_lands.md)

> 《万迪达德》第一章中“全然是死亡”的存在，对阿胡拉·马兹达创造的每一片良地施以反创造的灾祸。

> *In Videvdad 1, 'all death', who answers each good land made by Ahura Mazda with a counter-created plague.*

### 1. 第一片良地 / The first good land

阿胡拉·马兹达说：我所创造的良地中，第一片是雅利安瓦伊贾，在善河岱提亚旁边。

*Ahura Mazda says: the first of the good lands I created was Airyana Vaejah, by the good river Daitya.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Vendidad, Fargard 1, translated by James Darmesteter · Fargard 1 · 第一章第一片良地。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：雅利安瓦伊贾不映射为现代坐标。</sub>

### 2. 全然是死亡的对手 / The one who is all death

随后，“全然是死亡”的安格拉·曼纽来了，他针对这片良地造出了河中之蛇和寒冬，那是恶魔们的作品。

*Then came Angra Mainyu, who is all death, and against it he counter-created the serpent in the river and winter, a work of the daevas.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Vendidad, Fargard 1, translated by James Darmesteter · Fargard 1 · 第一章第一片良地。</sub>

### 3. 十六地，十六灾 / Sixteen lands, sixteen plagues

名单一片接一片地继续：第二片是粟特人居住的平原，安格拉·曼纽就造出蝗虫。如此一共列出十六片良地，每一片都对应一种反创造的灾祸。

*The list goes on land by land: the second is the plain where the Sughdhas dwell, and Angra Mainyu makes the locust. In all, sixteen good lands are named, each answered by a counter-created plague.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Vendidad, Fargard 1, translated by James Darmesteter · Fargard 1 · 第一章第二片良地及以下。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：译者认为这是宗教地理，混合了神话之地与真实国家；只有部分地名可以确认。</sub>

## 密特拉 · Mithra

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.zoroastrian.mihr_yasht](../story.zoroastrian.mihr_yasht.md)

> 《密赫尔颂》中的“广阔牧场之主”：阿胡拉·马兹达所造、与其同受崇拜，守护契约，有千耳万眼。

> *Lord of wide pastures in the Mihr Yasht: created by Ahura Mazda as worthy of worship as himself, guardian of the contract, with a thousand ears and ten thousand eyes.*

### 1. 与造物主同样尊贵 / As worthy as his maker

颂歌一开头，阿胡拉·马兹达对查拉图斯特拉说：我创造广阔牧场之主密特拉时，使他与我自己一样，值得献祭、值得祈祷。

*The hymn opens with Ahura Mazda telling Zarathustra: when I created Mithra, lord of wide pastures, I made him as worthy of sacrifice and as worthy of prayer as myself.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mihr Yasht (Yasht 10), translated by James Darmesteter · Yt 10.1–13 · Yt 10.1。</sub>

### 2. 不可背弃契约 / Never break the contract

对密特拉说谎、背弃契约的恶人，会给整个国家带来死亡。所以契约绝不可背弃——不论是与信徒订立的，还是与不信者订立的。

*The wretch who lies to Mithra, breaking the contract, brings death upon the whole land. So the contract must never be broken, whether made with one of the faithful or with an unbeliever.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mihr Yasht (Yasht 10), translated by James Darmesteter · Yt 10.1–13 · Yt 10.2。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“契约”是译者对 miθra 一词的理解，学界另有讨论。</sub>

### 3. 千耳万眼 / A thousand ears, ten thousand eyes

颂歌赞美密特拉说真话，是集会中的首领；他有一千只耳朵，一万只眼睛，什么都瞒不过他。

*The hymn praises Mithra as truth-speaking, a chief in assemblies, with a thousand ears and ten thousand eyes — nothing escapes him.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mihr Yasht (Yasht 10), translated by James Darmesteter · Yt 10.1–13 · Yt 10.7。</sub>

### 4. 越过哈拉山 / Over Mount Hara

每天清晨，他是天上诸神中第一个越过哈拉山的，比不朽的、驾着快马的太阳还要早。

*Each morning he is the first of the heavenly gods to reach over Mount Hara, ahead of the undying, swift-horsed sun.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mihr Yasht (Yasht 10), translated by James Darmesteter · Yt 10.1–13 · Yt 10.13。</sub>
