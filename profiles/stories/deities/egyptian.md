## 伊西斯 · Isis

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.egyptian.heliopolitan_family](../story.egyptian.heliopolitan_family.md)

> 在 UCL 对奥西里斯故事的概述中，伊西斯收集奥西里斯的肢体并怀上荷鲁斯；大都会博物馆的哀悼像把她与奈芙蒂斯配成一对。

> *In the UCL summary of the Osiris cycle Isis gathers Osiris's limbs and conceives Horus; Met mourning figures pair her with Nephthys.*

### 1. 太阳神的第一对子女 / The creator's first pair

在埃及的创世观念里，主要的创造者是太阳神，他也被称为阿图姆。他最先生出一男一女：舒和泰芙努特。学者认为“舒”这个名字和“干燥”有关，“泰芙努特”则代表湿润。

*In Egyptian creation thought the principal creator is the sun-god, also called Atum. His first offshoots are a son and a daughter, Shu and Tefnet; scholars link Shu's name to dryness and read Tefnet as moisture.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Gods and goddesses: creation · Gods and goddesses: creation · UCL Digital Egypt 创世页面。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“干燥／湿润”是现代学者的词源解释。</sub>

### 2. 大地与天空出生 / Earth and sky are born

舒和泰芙努特又生下两个孩子：大地之神盖布和天空女神努特。干燥与湿润，变成了坚实的大地和古埃及人眼中充满水的天空。

*Shu and Tefnet in turn produce two children: Geb, god of the earth, and Nut, goddess of the sky — dryness and moisture crystallised into solid earth and a sky the Egyptians thought of as watery.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Gods and goddesses: creation · Gods and goddesses: creation · 同一页面的子女段落。</sub>

### 3. 舒把天地分开 / Shu holds earth and sky apart

盖布和努特被舒分开，天在上、地在下。零散的古代记载暗示，这种分离必须被强制维持，否则天地还会继续生出神来。

*Shu separates Geb and Nut, sky above and earth below. Scattered references suggest the separation had to be enforced, or earth and sky would keep producing divine offspring.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Gods and goddesses: creation · Gods and goddesses: creation · UCL 页面关于天地分离的说明。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：这是学者对零散材料的归纳，不是单一文本的原话。</sub>

### 4. 塞特、奥西里斯与伊西斯 / Seth, Osiris and Isis

后来，奥西里斯“无序的兄弟”塞特出于嫉妒杀害了他，把遗体分开，并取代他成为世界之王。伊西斯找回奥西里斯的肢体，让遗体恢复到足以受孕，生下了荷鲁斯。

*Later Osiris's anarchic brother Seth, out of jealousy, murders him, dismembers him and takes his place as king of the world. Isis collects the limbs and revives the body enough to conceive a child, Horus.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Gods and goddesses: creation · Gods and goddesses: creation · UCL 页面的奥西里斯故事概述。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：古代文献中这个故事有许多版本；本站不合成“标准版”。</sub>

## 哈托尔 · Hathor

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.egyptian.heavenly_cow](../story.egyptian.heavenly_cow.md)

> 在《天牛之书》中，拉的眼睛喝下红色啤酒后回归的慈爱、母性的形象。

> *In the Tale of the Heavenly Cow, the loving, maternal character to which the raging Eye of Ra returns after drinking red beer.*

### 1. 人类反叛拉 / Humankind rebels against Ra

一部新王国时期的宗教文献记载：人们起来反叛太阳神拉。拉决定惩罚他们，后来他也离开人间，退到天上。

*A New Kingdom religious composition records that people rebel against the sun-god Ra. He resolves to punish them, and in the end he withdraws to the sky.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Gods and goddesses: creation · Gods and goddesses: creation — the Tale of the Heavenly Cow · UCL 页面对《天牛之书》的概述。</sub>

### 2. 眼睛化作塞赫麦特 / The Eye becomes Sekhmet

拉把自己的眼睛从身体里派出去，成为一个独立的“女儿”。她化作塞赫麦特——意思是“强大的女神”——几乎把人类全部消灭。

*Ra sends his Eye out from his body as a separate offshoot, a 'daughter'. As Sekhmet, 'the mighty goddess', she almost annihilates humankind.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Gods and goddesses: creation · Gods and goddesses: creation — the Tale of the Heavenly Cow · 同一概述的“拉的眼睛”段落。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“强大的女神”是 UCL 给出的释义。</sub>

### 3. 一湖红色的啤酒 / A lake of red beer

众神用计：他们把一湖啤酒染成红色，看起来像人血。她喝下后醉得香甜，脾气也软化下来，重新变回慈爱、温柔的哈托尔。

*The gods play a trick: they colour a lake of beer red to look like human blood. She drinks, becomes sweetly drunk, and reverts to her loving, maternal character as Hathor.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Gods and goddesses: creation · Gods and goddesses: creation — the Tale of the Heavenly Cow · 同一概述的结尾。</sub>

在埃及的创世观念里，主要的创造者是太阳神，他也被称为阿图姆。他最先生出一男一女：舒和泰芙努特。学者认为“舒”这个名字和“干燥”有关，“泰芙努特”则代表湿润。

*In Egyptian creation thought the principal creator is the sun-god, also called Atum. His first offshoots are a son and a daughter, Shu and Tefnet; scholars link Shu's name to dryness and read Tefnet as moisture.*

### 2. 大地与天空出生 / Earth and sky are born

舒和泰芙努特又生下两个孩子：大地之神盖布和天空女神努特。干燥与湿润，变成了坚实的大地和古埃及人眼中充满水的天空。

*Shu and Tefnet in turn produce two children: Geb, god of the earth, and Nut, goddess of the sky — dryness and moisture crystallised into solid earth and a sky the Egyptians thought of as watery.*

### 3. 舒把天地分开 / Shu holds earth and sky apart

盖布和努特被舒分开，天在上、地在下。零散的古代记载暗示，这种分离必须被强制维持，否则天地还会继续生出神来。

*Shu separates Geb and Nut, sky above and earth below. Scattered references suggest the separation had to be enforced, or earth and sky would keep producing divine offspring.*
