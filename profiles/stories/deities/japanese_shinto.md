## 伊邪那岐 · Izanagi

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.japanese.izanagi_izanami](../story.japanese.izanagi_izanami.md)

> 《古事记》中的男性创世神：与伊邪那美造出淤能碁吕岛，追往黄泉，归来禊祓时生出天照大神、月读与须佐之男。

> *Male creator deity of the Kojiki: with Izanami he forms Onogoro Island, follows her to Yomi, and on returning gives birth to Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo as he purifies himself.*

### 1. 天浮桥上的长矛 / A spear from the Floating Bridge

天上的众神命令伊邪那岐和伊邪那美去巩固还在漂浮的大地，并交给他们一支镶玉的天沼矛。两神站在天浮桥上，把矛伸进海里搅动；提起矛时，矛尖滴下的盐水层层堆积，成了淤能碁吕岛。

*The heavenly deities command Izanagi and Izanami to make firm the drifting land and give them a jewelled spear. Standing on the Floating Bridge of Heaven, they stir the brine; when they lift the spear, the drops from its tip pile up into Onogoro Island.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sections III–X (kj010–kj017) · 张伯伦译本第三节（kj010）。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：《日本书纪》说两神是自己决定动手，本版不合并。</sub>

### 2. 火神出生 / The birth of the fire deity

两神结合，生出许多岛屿和神灵。可是伊邪那美生下火神时被烧伤，病倒在床，最终“神去”——也就是死去。

*Joined together, the pair bring forth islands and deities. But when Izanami gives birth to the Deity-of-Fire she is burned, falls sick, and at length 'divinely retires' — she dies.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sections III–X (kj010–kj017) · 张伯伦译本第七节（kj014）。</sub>

### 3. 追到黄泉 / Following her to Yomi

伊邪那岐想再见到妻子，便追到黄泉，请她回去：“我们造的国土还没有完成。”伊邪那美回答：你来得太晚了，我已经吃了黄泉灶火煮的东西——不过她答应去和黄泉的神商量。

*Longing to see her, Izanagi follows Izanami to Yomi and asks her to come back, for the lands they made are not yet finished. She answers that he has come too late: she has already eaten from the hearth of Yomi — yet she agrees to consult the deities there.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sections III–X (kj010–kj017) · 张伯伦译本第九节（kj016）。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：之后的逃离情节可参看本库“黄泉中的八雷神”故事。</sub>

### 4. 洗出三位贵神 / Three deities born of washing

伊邪那岐回来后在水中洗净自己，身上不断化生出神。最后，他洗左眼时生出天照大神，洗右眼时生出月读，洗鼻子时生出须佐之男。

*Back from Yomi, Izanagi bathes to purify himself, and deities are born as he washes. Last of all, washing his left eye brings forth Amaterasu, his right eye Tsukuyomi, and his nose Susanoo.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sections III–X (kj010–kj017) · 张伯伦译本第十节（kj017）及译注。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：三神的通行名称来自译者注释。</sub>

## 伏雷 · Fusuikazuchi

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.japanese.eight_thunder_yomi](../story.japanese.eight_thunder_yomi.md)

> 《古事记》黄泉段八雷神之一。

> *One of the eight thunder kami in the Kojiki Yomi episode.*

### 1. 黄泉段落 / The Yomi passage

学术神名索引把八位雷神定位到《古事记》上卷的黄泉段落，并说他们出现在伊邪那美身上。

*The scholarly divine-name index locates the eight thunder kami in the Yomi episode of the upper Kojiki scroll and places them on Izanami.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Kojiki divine-name index · Upper Scroll, Yomi episode · 《古事记》上卷黄泉段落索引。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：只采用公开学术索引范围。</sub>

### 2. 八个独立名称 / Eight distinct names

数据库为大雷、火雷、黑雷、拆雷、若雷、土雷、鸣雷与伏雷分别建档，不把八名压成一个泛称。

*The database keeps Ooikazuchi, Honoikazuchi, Kuroikazuchi, Sakuikazuchi, Wakaikazuchi, Tsuchiikazuchi, Naruikazuchi, and Fusuikazuchi as separate records rather than one generic name.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Kojiki divine-name index · Upper Scroll, Yomi episode · 八个成员Claims及神名索引。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：部分名称的精细语义仍待校勘。</sub>

### 3. 不自动等同地方神 / No automatic local identification

鸣雷、若雷等相近名称在后世神社或地方传统中可能出现，但系统不会仅凭名字相似就认定为《古事记》中的同一神。

*Similar names such as Naruikazuchi or Wakaikazuchi may occur in later shrine or local traditions, but name similarity alone does not establish identity with the Kojiki figures.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Kojiki divine-name index · Upper Scroll, Yomi episode · 成员Claim及身份冲突档案。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：身份关系保留为争议或未知。</sub>
