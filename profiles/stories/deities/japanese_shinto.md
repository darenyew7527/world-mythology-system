### 1. 天孙降临的诏命 / The command to descend

建御雷神回到天上，报告苇原中国已经平定。天照大神和高木神——也就是高御产巢日神——就命令天照大神的儿子天忍穗耳命：现在国土已经平定了，你就照原先的委托，降下去统治那片国土吧。天忍穗耳命回答说：我正在准备降临的时候，生了一个儿子，名叫天迩岐志国迩岐志天津日高日子番能迩迩艺命。应该派这个孩子降下去。这个孩子是天忍穗耳命和高木神的女儿万幡丰秋津师比卖命所生，也就是说，他同时是天照大神和高木神的孙子。于是两位大神对迩迩艺命下令：这丰苇原的瑞穗国，是交给你统治的国土。你就照着命令降下去吧。张伯伦把迩迩艺命的长名字逐字译出，大意是“天地丰饶、天日高照的稻穗丰熟之王子”。这位“天孙”，就是这一部故事的主人公之一。

*Takemikazuchi went back up and reported that the Central Land of Reed-Plains had been pacified. Amaterasu and Takagi-no-kami — Takami-musubi — then commanded Amaterasu's son Ame-no-oshi-ho-mimi: 'The land is now pacified; go down and rule it as we entrusted it to you.' He answered: 'While I was making ready to descend, a son was born to me, named Ame-nigishi-kuni-nigishi-ama-tsu-hi-daka-hiko-ho-no-ninigi. This child should be sent down.' The child was born of Ame-no-oshi-ho-mimi and Takagi-no-kami's daughter Yorozu-hata-toyo-aki-tsu-shi-hime, so that he was grandson both of Amaterasu and of Takagi. The two great deities commanded Ninigi: 'This Luxuriant Reed-Plains Land of Fresh Rice-Ears is the land you are to rule. Descend as commanded.' Chamberlain translates the long name word by word, roughly Heaven's-Plenty-Earth's-Plenty-Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty. This August Grandchild is one of the heroes of this part.*

### 2. 天之八衢的猿田毘古 / Sarutahiko at the heavenly crossroads

迩迩艺命正要降临的时候，在天之八衢——天上八条道路交会的地方——站着一位神，他身上发出的光，向上照亮了高天原，向下照亮了苇原中国。天照大神和高木神命令天宇受卖神说：你虽然是个柔弱的女子，却是一位能与任何神面对面而不退缩的神。你去问问他：天神的儿子要降临，是谁在这里挡路？天宇受卖神就是当年在天之岩户前狂舞、逗得众神大笑的那位女神。她上前去问，那位神回答说：我是国津神，名叫猿田毘古神。我听说天神的儿子要降临，所以前来迎接，愿意做天孙的向导。原来这位在路口放光的神，并不是来阻拦，而是来引路的。张伯伦把他的名字译作“猿田之王子神”。原文说天宇受卖命是“与神相对而能胜过对方”的神，所以众神派她去问话。《古事记》没有细写猿田毘古的样子；《日本书纪》的一段异文则说他鼻长七咫，背长七尺多，眼睛像八咫镜一样闪闪发亮。

*As Ninigi was about to descend, at the heavenly eight-forked crossroads stood a deity whose light shone up to the Plain of High Heaven and down to the Central Land of Reed-Plains. Amaterasu and Takagi-no-kami commanded Ame-no-Uzume: 'Though you are a delicate woman, you are a deity who can face down any deity. Go and ask him who stands in the way where our child is to descend.' She was the goddess who had once danced before the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling and set the gods roaring with laughter. When she asked, the deity answered: 'I am an earthly deity named Sarutahiko. I heard that the child of the heavenly deities was to descend, and I have come to meet him and to serve as his guide.' The shining deity at the crossroads had come not to bar the way but to lead it. Chamberlain renders his name the Deity Prince of Saruta. The text says Ame-no-Uzume was a deity who could outface any deity, which is why she was sent. The Kojiki does not describe Sarutahiko; a variant in the Nihon shoki says his nose was seven hands long, his back more than seven feet tall, and his eyes shone like an eight-hand mirror.*

### 3. 神器与五伴绪 / The regalia and the five chiefs

天照大神为迩迩艺命安排了五位随从的神，称为“五伴绪”：天儿屋命、布刀玉命、天宇受卖命、伊斯许理度卖命和玉祖命，他们都是当年在天之岩户前出过力的神。天照大神又把当年把她从岩户里引出来的八尺勾玉和镜子，以及须佐之男从大蛇尾中得到的草薙剑交给他，还派了思金神、天手力男神和天石门别神同行。天照大神说：这面镜子，你要把它当作我的御魂，像祭拜我一样祭拜它。又说：思金神，你要承担起祭祀的事务，掌管政事。书中接着说，这两位神如今在五十铃宫受到祭祀，也就是伊势的神宫。故事里的镜、玉、剑，在这里第一次被一起交到天孙手中。书中还一一记下了五位随从神的后人：天儿屋命是中臣连的祖先，布刀玉命是忌部首的祖先，天宇受卖命是猿女君的祖先，伊斯许理度卖命是作镜连的祖先，玉祖命是玉祖连的祖先；天石门别神又名栉石窗神、丰石窗神，是守护宫门的神。

*Amaterasu assigned to Ninigi five attendant deities, the five chiefs of companies: Ame-no-koyane, Futo-tama, Ame-no-Uzume, Ishi-kori-dome and Tama-no-oya, all of whom had played their part before the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling. She gave him the curved jewels and the mirror that had drawn her out of the rock-dwelling, and the Kusanagi sword that Susanoo had taken from the serpent's tail, and sent with him Omoikane, Ame-no-tajikarao and Ame-no-iwato-wake. She said: 'Regard this mirror as my august spirit, and reverence it as you would reverence me,' and: 'Omoikane, take charge of the rites and carry on the government.' The text adds that these two are worshipped at the shrine of Isuzu, the shrine at Ise. Mirror, jewels and sword are here placed together in the hands of the August Grandchild for the first time in the story. The text also records the descendants of the five chiefs: Ame-no-koyane is ancestor of the Nakatomi, Futo-tama of the Imbe, Ame-no-Uzume of the Saru-me, Ishi-kori-dome of the mirror-makers and Tama-no-oya of the jewel-makers; Ame-no-iwato-wake, also called Kushi-iwa-mado and Toyo-iwa-mado, is the deity who guards the palace gates.*

### 4. 降临高千穗 / The descent on Takachiho

迩迩艺命离开了天上的岩座，分开天上层层叠叠的八重云，威风凛凛地开出一条道路，经过天之浮桥，降落在筑紫日向的高千穗的久士布流多气峰上。天忍日命和天津久米命背着天上的石箭袋，佩着头椎大刀，拿着天上的波士弓和真鹿儿矢，走在天孙的前面开路；书中说，他们是大伴连和久米直两族的祖先。迩迩艺命站在那里说：这里正对着韩国，有一条笔直的路通到笠沙的岬角，早晨的太阳直直照来，傍晚的太阳也照得明亮。所以这里是非常好的地方。他在那里把宫柱深深地立在地底的岩石上，把屋梁高高地架到高天原，住了下来。从高天原到人间的这一段路，就这样走完了。日向的高千穗峰究竟在哪里，后世有宫崎县的高千穗和雾岛山的高千穗峰等不同说法，《古事记》本身没有给出更多线索。

*Ninigi left the heavenly rock-seat, pushed apart the eight-fold heavenly clouds, and with mighty way-dividing made his way down by the Floating Bridge of Heaven to the peak of Kuji-furu-take of Takachiho in Himuka in Tsukushi. Ame-no-oshi-hi and Ama-tsu-kume went before him carrying heavenly rock-quivers, girded with mallet-headed swords, holding heavenly bows and true-deer arrows; the text names them as ancestors of the Ōtomo and Kume clans. Ninigi said: 'This place faces Kara; a straight road runs through to the cape of Kasasa; it is a land where the morning sun shines straight and the evening sun shines bright. This is an exceedingly good place.' There he set his palace pillars deep in the bedrock, raised its cross-beams to the Plain of High Heaven, and dwelt. So the road from High Heaven to the human world was travelled. Where the peak of Takachiho in Himuka lies is disputed — later traditions point to Takachiho in Miyazaki or to the Takachiho peak of the Kirishima mountains — and the Kojiki itself gives no further clue.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XXXIII–XLIII (kj040–kj050) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第三十三至三十四节。</sub>

### 5. 猿女君与海参 / The Duchesses of Saru and the sea-slug

迩迩艺命对天宇受卖命说：你送那位为我们引路的猿田毘古神回去吧。是你把他的名字问出来的，你就承受他的名字，侍奉他吧。所以，书中说，猿女君一族的女子都带着猿田毘古这位男神的名字，被称为“猿女”。后来，猿田毘古神在阿邪诃打鱼，手被比良夫贝夹住，沉进海里淹死了：他沉到海底时，生出了底度久御魂；海水冒泡时，生出了都夫多都御魂；水泡破开时，生出了阿和佐久御魂。天宇受卖命送猿田毘古回去以后返回，把大大小小的鱼都召集起来，问它们：你们愿意侍奉天神的儿子吗？鱼们都说：愿意侍奉。只有海参一声不吭。天宇受卖命说：这张嘴就是不肯回答的嘴！就用小刀把海参的嘴割开了。所以，书中说，海参的嘴至今仍是裂开的。书中还说，此后每一代天皇在位的时候，志摩国进献的海产初物都要分一份给猿女君。

*Ninigi said to Ame-no-Uzume: 'Escort Sarutahiko, who served as our guide, back home. Since it was you who learned his name, take his name and serve him.' Therefore, says the text, the women of the Saru-me clan bear the name of the male deity Sarutahiko and are called the Duchesses of Saru. Later, while fishing at Azaka, Sarutahiko had his hand caught by a hirabu shell and sank and drowned in the sea; as he sank to the bottom, as the water bubbled, and as the bubbles burst, three deities were born. Ame-no-Uzume, coming back from escorting him, gathered all the fishes broad of fin and narrow of fin and asked: 'Will you serve the child of the heavenly deities?' All said they would — except the sea-slug, which said nothing. 'This is a mouth that gives no answer!' said Ame-no-Uzume, and slit its mouth with her small knife. That, says the text, is why the sea-slug's mouth is slit to this day. The text adds that in every reign a share of the first fruits of the sea sent from Shima is granted to the Saru-me.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XXXIII–XLIII (kj040–kj050) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第三十五至三十六节。</sub>

### 6. 木花之佐久夜毘卖与石长比卖 / Konohana-sakuya-hime and Iwanaga-hime

迩迩艺命在笠沙的岬角遇见一位美丽的少女，问她是谁的女儿。少女说：我是大山津见神的女儿，名叫神阿多都比卖，又叫木花之佐久夜毘卖——意思是“像树上的花一样灿烂盛开的公主”。迩迩艺命说：我想娶你，你看怎样？她说：我不能回答，我的父亲大山津见神会回答。迩迩艺命派人去求婚，大山津见神非常高兴，让她的姐姐石长比卖和她一同出嫁，还带上了满满一百桌的礼物。可是石长比卖长得非常丑陋，迩迩艺命一见就害怕，把她送了回去，只留下了妹妹木花之佐久夜毘卖，与她共度一夜。大山津见神因为石长比卖被送回而深感羞耻，说：我把两个女儿一起献上，是有用意的：有石长比卖在，天神子孙的寿命就会像岩石一样长久，不论风雪；有木花之佐久夜毘卖在，他们就会像树上的花一样繁荣。如今你只留下了木花之佐久夜毘卖，天神子孙的寿命就会像树上的花一样短暂。书中说，所以直到今天，历代天皇的寿命都不长久。

*At the cape of Kasasa, Ninigi met a beautiful maiden and asked whose daughter she was. She said: 'I am the daughter of the great mountain deity Ōyamatsumi; my name is Kamu-ata-tsu-hime, also Konohana-sakuya-hime' — the Princess Blossoming like the Flowers of the Trees. He said: 'I wish to marry you. What do you say?' She answered: 'I cannot say; my father Ōyamatsumi will answer.' When Ninigi sent to ask, Ōyamatsumi was delighted, and sent her elder sister Iwanaga-hime with her, together with a hundred tables of gifts. But Iwanaga-hime was very ugly; Ninigi took fright at the sight of her and sent her back, keeping only the younger sister, with whom he spent one night. Ōyamatsumi, ashamed at Iwanaga-hime's return, said: 'I sent both daughters for a purpose: with Iwanaga-hime, the lives of the heavenly deity's descendants would endure like the rocks through snow and wind; with Konohana-sakuya-hime, they would flourish like the blossoms of the trees. Since you have kept only Konohana-sakuya-hime, their lives will be as brief as the blossoms of the trees.' That, says the text, is why the lives of the Heavenly Sovereigns down to this day are not long.*

### 7. 火中生子 / Children born in fire

过了些日子，木花之佐久夜毘卖来对迩迩艺命说：我怀孕了，就要生产了。天神的孩子不能私下里生，所以来告诉你。迩迩艺命却说：只过了一夜就怀孕了？这一定不是我的孩子，一定是哪个国津神的孩子。木花之佐久夜毘卖说：如果我怀的是国津神的孩子，生产时就不会平安；如果是天神的孩子，就一定平安。她建起一座八寻宽、没有门的大殿，走进去以后用泥土把四面封死，到生产的时候，就在殿里放起火来。在熊熊大火之中，她生下了三个儿子：火势正旺时生的叫火照命，书中说他是阿多隼人的祖先；接着生的叫火须势理命；最后生的叫火远理命，又名天津日高日子穗穗手见命。张伯伦把火照命和火远理命分别译作“火之照耀”和“火之消退”。三个孩子都平安降生，母亲用这场大火证明了自己的清白。

*After some time Konohana-sakuya-hime came to Ninigi and said: 'I am with child and my time is near. The child of a heavenly deity must not be born in secret, so I have come to tell you.' But Ninigi said: 'With child after a single night? It cannot be mine. It must be the child of some earthly deity.' She answered: 'If the child I carry is an earthly deity's, the birth will not go well; if it is the heavenly deity's, it will surely go well.' She built a doorless hall eight fathoms wide, went inside, sealed it all round with clay, and when her time came set fire to the hall. In the midst of the flames she bore three sons: the first, born when the fire blazed brightest, Hoderi, whom the text names ancestor of the Hayato of Ata; next Hosuseri; and last Hoori, also called Ama-tsu-hi-daka-hiko-ho-ho-de-mi. Chamberlain renders Hoderi and Hoori as Fire-Shine and Fire-Subside. All three were born safely, and the mother proved her truth by fire.*

### 8. 海幸与山幸 / The luck of the sea and the luck of the mountains

火照命成了海幸彦，用鱼钩捕捞海里大大小小的鱼；火远理命成了山幸彦，用弓箭猎取山里大大小小的野兽。“幸”在这里指的是猎具和收获，张伯伦说英语里没有合适的词，只好译作“运气”。有一天，火远理命对哥哥说：我们交换一下猎具用吧。他请求了三次，哥哥都不答应，最后才勉强换了。火远理命拿着哥哥的鱼钩去钓鱼，一条鱼也没有钓到，还把鱼钩弄丢在海里了。哥哥火照命来要回自己的鱼钩，说：山的猎具是山的，海的猎具是海的，我们还是各自换回来吧。弟弟说：你的鱼钩我钓鱼时丢了。哥哥一定要他还。弟弟就把自己佩带的十拳剑打碎，做成五百个鱼钩来赔，哥哥不要；又做了一千个鱼钩来赔，哥哥还是不要，说：我只要我原来的那个鱼钩。三兄弟的名字都与火有关：火照、火须势理、火远理，像是记下了他们在火中出生时火势的起落。

*Hoderi became the Luck of the Sea, catching fish broad of fin and narrow of fin with his hook; Hoori became the Luck of the Mountains, taking beasts rough of hair and soft of hair with his bow. The word sachi here means both the hunting gear and its yield, and Chamberlain says English has no satisfactory equivalent, so he renders it luck. One day Hoori asked his brother to exchange their gear. Three times he asked and was refused, and at last the elder reluctantly agreed. Hoori took the fish-hook to sea, caught not a single fish, and lost the hook in the water. Hoderi came to ask for it back: 'Mountain luck to the mountain, sea luck to the sea — let us each have our own again.' The younger said he had lost it fishing, but the elder insisted. So Hoori broke up his ten-grasp sword and made five hundred hooks in recompense, but his brother would not take them; he made a thousand, and still his brother refused: 'I want my own hook.' All three brothers' names speak of fire — Fire-Shine, Fire-Climax, Fire-Subside — as if recording the rise and fall of the flames in which they were born.*

### 9. 盐椎神与海神之宫 / The salt deity and the road to the sea palace

火远理命在海边哭泣，这时盐椎神来了，问他：天津日高日子，你为什么哭？他说出了鱼钩的事。盐椎神说：我来为你想个好办法。他编了一艘缝隙紧密、不透水的竹笼小船，让火远理命坐进去，对他说：我把船推出去以后，你就顺着漂一会儿，会遇到一股好的潮流，你就跟着潮流走。前面会有一座宫殿，像鱼鳞一样一层层盖起来的，那就是绵津见神——海神——的宫殿。到了海神宫门口，旁边有一口井，井边有一棵枝叶繁茂的桂树。你就坐在那棵树上，海神的女儿看见你，会替你出主意的。火远理命照着他的话，乘着小船顺着潮流漂去，果然来到了那座鱼鳞般的宫殿前，爬上井边的桂树坐了下来。张伯伦把盐椎神译作“盐之主神”；那艘小船，原文叫“无间胜间之小船”，意思是编得严密、没有一点缝隙的竹笼船。火远理命独自坐在这样的小船里，离开陆地，顺着潮流漂向大海深处。

*As Hoori wept on the seashore, the salt deity Shio-tsuchi came and asked: 'Why do you weep, Sky's Sun-Height?' He told the story of the hook. Shio-tsuchi said: 'I will make a good plan for you.' He wove a little boat of bamboo so tightly that no water could enter, set Hoori in it and said: 'When I push the boat off, drift for a while and you will meet a pleasant current; follow it. You will come to a palace built like the scales of fishes: the palace of Watatsumi, the sea deity. By its gate there is a well, and by the well a many-branched cassia tree. Sit in that tree, and the sea deity's daughter will see you and counsel you.' Hoori did as he was told, drifted with the current, came to the palace built like fish-scales, and climbed the cassia tree by the well and sat there. Chamberlain renders Shio-tsuchi as the Deity Salt-Possessor; the boat is called in the original a small boat of closely woven bamboo without interstices. Alone in it, Hoori left the land behind and drifted with the current into the depths of the sea.*

### 10. 井边的玉与海神之女 / The jewel in the vessel and the sea deity's daughter

海神的女儿丰玉毘卖的侍女，拿着玉做的水器来井边打水，看见井水里映出了光，抬头一看，树上坐着一位英俊的男子，觉得十分奇怪。火远理命向她要水喝，侍女就打了水，盛在玉器里献给他。他却没有喝，而是解下颈上的玉，含在嘴里，吐进玉器中。那颗玉粘在器皿上，怎么也取不下来。侍女只好把粘着玉的器皿拿去交给丰玉毘卖。丰玉毘卖觉得奇怪，亲自出门去看，一见到他就被他迷住了，两人四目相对。她回去告诉父亲：我们家门口有一位很美的人。海神亲自出来一看，说：这是天津日高的儿子虚空津日高啊。他把火远理命请进宫里，铺上八层海驴皮的垫子，上面再铺八层绸缎的垫子，请他坐下，摆上一百桌礼物宴请他，把女儿丰玉毘卖嫁给了他。火远理命就在海神之宫住了三年。

*A maid of the sea deity's daughter Toyotama-bime came to the well with a jewelled vessel to draw water. She saw a light in the water, looked up, and saw a handsome man in the tree, and wondered at it. Hoori asked her for water; she drew it and offered it in the jewelled vessel. He did not drink, but loosened a jewel from his neck, took it in his mouth and spat it into the vessel. The jewel stuck fast and could not be removed, so the maid took the vessel, jewel and all, to Toyotama-bime. Wondering, the princess went out to look, was charmed at the sight of him, and they exchanged glances. She told her father: 'There is a beautiful person at our gate.' The sea deity went out himself and said: 'This is Sora-tsu-hi-daka, the son of Ama-tsu-hi-daka.' He brought Hoori in, spread eight layers of rugs of sea-asses' skins and eight layers of silk rugs over them, seated him, feasted him with a hundred tables of gifts, and gave him his daughter Toyotama-bime in marriage. Hoori lived in the sea palace for three years.*

### 11. 潮盈珠与潮干珠 / The tide-flowing and tide-ebbing jewels

三年过去，火远理命想起往事，长长地叹了一口气。丰玉毘卖告诉父亲：他住了三年从不叹气，昨夜却长叹了一声。海神问他缘故，他就讲了鱼钩的事。海神把大大小小的鱼都召集起来，问：有没有哪条鱼拿了这个鱼钩？鱼们说：最近鲷鱼说喉咙里卡了东西，吃不下饭，一定是它。一查鲷鱼的喉咙，果然找到了鱼钩。海神把鱼钩洗干净交给火远理命，教他说：你把鱼钩还给哥哥时，要说“这是个忧郁的钩、焦躁的钩、贫穷的钩、愚蠢的钩”，背过手去交给他。哥哥如果在高处种田，你就在低处种；他在低处种，你就在高处种。我掌管着水，三年之内你哥哥一定会穷下去。他要是因此怨恨来攻打你，你就拿出潮盈珠让潮水淹没他；他要是求饶，就拿出潮干珠让潮水退去救他。海神又召来众鳄，一只长一寻的鳄鱼说一天就能送他回去。火远理命骑着它回到人间，把佩带的小刀系在它的脖子上作为谢礼。回去以后，一切都如海神所说：哥哥越来越穷，凶狠地来攻打他，被潮盈珠淹得求饶，又被潮干珠救起，最后叩头说：从今以后，我日夜做你的守卫。书中说，隼人一族至今还表演溺水的动作，就是由此而来。

*After three years Hoori remembered the past and heaved a great sigh. Toyotama-bime told her father: 'In three years he never sighed, but last night he heaved a great sigh.' The sea deity asked him why, and he told of the hook. The sea deity summoned all the fishes great and small and asked whether any had taken it. They said: 'The sea-bream has lately complained of something stuck in its throat and cannot eat; surely it is that.' The hook was found in the bream's throat, washed and given to Hoori, with this counsel: 'When you give it back to your brother, say: this hook is a gloomy hook, a fretful hook, a poor hook, a silly hook — and hand it to him behind your back. If he makes high fields, make yours low; if low, make yours high. I rule the waters, and in three years he will grow poor. If he attacks you in resentment, use the tide-flowing jewel to drown him; if he begs for mercy, use the tide-ebbing jewel to save him.' Then he summoned the wani, and a one-fathom wani said it could carry Hoori home in a day; Hoori rode it home and tied his small sword to its neck in thanks. All fell out as the sea deity said: the elder grew poor, attacked fiercely, was drowned by the tide-flowing jewel until he begged, was saved by the tide-ebbing jewel, and bowed down: 'Henceforth I will be your guard day and night.' That, says the text, is why the Hayato still perform the motions of drowning.*

### 12. 鸬鹚羽的产屋 / The birth-hut thatched with cormorant feathers

后来，丰玉毘卖亲自从海里出来，对火远理命说：我怀孕了，现在就要生了。天神的孩子不能生在海里，所以我出来了。她在海边建起一座产屋，用鸬鹚的羽毛做屋顶，可是屋顶还没有盖完，她就要生了，只好走进屋里。临产前她对丈夫说：凡是别国的人生孩子的时候，都会变回自己本来的样子。我现在也要变回本来的样子生产，请你千万不要看我。火远理命觉得奇怪，偷偷往里一看，只见妻子变成了一条八寻长的大鳄，在地上爬动扭曲。他吓得转身逃走。丰玉毘卖知道他偷看了，羞愧难当，留下孩子，说：我本想永远通过海路来往，可是你偷看了我的样子，我实在羞耻。她关闭了海与陆地之间的通路，回到了海里。孩子取名为鹈葺草葺不合命，意思是“鸬鹚羽毛还没有盖完的屋顶”。丰玉毘卖虽然怨恨丈夫偷看，却忍不住思念，便让妹妹玉依毘卖来抚养孩子，托她带去一首歌：红色的玉啊，连穿玉的线都在发光；可是像白玉一样的你的模样，更加高贵。火远理命答歌说：在野鸭飞来的海岛上，与我共寝的妻子啊，我一生都不会忘记你。火远理命住在高千穗宫五百八十年。后来，鹈葺草葺不合命娶了姨母玉依毘卖，生下四个儿子，最小的儿子神倭伊波礼毘古命，就是后来的神武天皇。《古事记》的上卷到这里结束。

*Later Toyotama-bime herself came out of the sea and said to Hoori: 'I am with child and my time has come. The child of a heavenly deity must not be born in the sea, so I have come out.' On the shore she built a birth-hut thatched with cormorant feathers, but before the roof was finished her pains came and she went inside. She said to her husband: 'All people of other lands, when they give birth, return to their native form. I too shall take my native form to bear the child. I beg you not to look at me.' Wondering, he peeped in, and saw that his wife had become a wani eight fathoms long, crawling and writhing. He fled in fear. Knowing he had looked, she was overcome with shame, left the child and said: 'I meant always to come and go by the road of the sea, but you have seen my form, and I am ashamed.' She closed the boundary between sea and land and went back down into the sea. The child was named Ugaya-fuki-aezu, Cormorant-Thatch-Meeting-Incompletely. Though she resented his peeping, she could not stop longing for him, and sent her younger sister Tamayori-bime to nurse the child, with a song: red jewels — even their string shines; but you, like white jewels, are nobler still. He answered: my wife, with whom I slept on the island where the wild ducks come, I shall not forget you all my life. Hoori dwelt in the palace of Takachiho five hundred and eighty years. Ugaya-fuki-aezu later married his aunt Tamayori-bime and had four sons, the youngest of whom, Kamu-yamato-ihare-biko, is the future Emperor Jimmu. Here the first volume of the Kojiki ends.*

### 1. 大国主的五个名字 / The five names of the Master of the Great Land

《古事记》在讲完须佐之男在出云建宫、娶栉名田比卖之后，接着列出了他的子孙：须佐之男和栉名田比卖生下八岛士奴美神，此后一代代往下传，传到天之冬衣神，天之冬衣神娶了刺国大神的女儿刺国若比卖，生下的就是这一部故事的主人公——从须佐之男往下数，正好是第六代。书中说他有五个名字：大国主神、大穴牟迟神、苇原色许男神、八千矛神、宇都志国玉神。这些名字后来会在故事里一个个出现：年轻时人们叫他大穴牟迟；在根之国，须佐之男叫他苇原色许男，也就是“苇原中国的丑男子”；求婚时他叫八千矛神，也就是“拥有八千支矛的神”；最后，须佐之男在黄泉比良坂上命令他成为“大国主”和“宇都志国玉”，也就是统治人间国土的神。他还有许多兄弟，书中称为八十神。他的母亲刺国若比卖，在后面的故事里两次哭着去救他。故事开始的时候，他还只是兄弟中最受欺负的一个。

*After telling how Susanoo built his palace in Izumo and married Kushinada-hime, the Kojiki lists his descendants: their son Yashima-jinumi, and so on down the generations to Ame-no-fuyu-kinu, who married Sashikuni-waka-hime, daughter of the great deity of Sashikuni, and fathered the hero of this part — the sixth generation from Susanoo. The text gives him five names: Ōkuninushi, Ōnamuji, Ashihara-shiko-wo, Yachihoko and Utsushi-kunitama. They surface one by one in the story: as a youth he is Ōnamuji; in the Nether-Distant-Land Susanoo calls him Ashihara-shiko-wo, the Ugly Male of the Reed-Plains; as a wooer he is Yachihoko, the Deity of Eight Thousand Spears; and at the Even Pass of Yomi Susanoo charges him to become Ōkuninushi and Utsushi-kunitama, ruler of the living land. He has many brothers, the Eighty Deities, and a mother, Sashikuni-waka-hime, who twice goes weeping to save him. When the story begins he is the most put-upon of the brothers.*

### 2. 稻羽的白兔 / The white hare of Inaba

八十神都想娶稻羽的八上比卖，一起出发去求婚，让大穴牟迟背着袋子，像仆人一样跟在后面。走到气多岬，他们看见一只被剥光了皮的兔子躺在地上。八十神对兔子说：你去海里洗个澡，然后躺在高山顶上吹风。兔子照做了，海水干了以后，它的皮肤被风吹得裂开，痛得直哭。大穴牟迟最后赶到，问它为什么哭。兔子说：我本来住在淤岐岛，想渡海过来，就骗海里的和迩说，我们来比一比谁的族人多，你们排成一行，我从你们背上踩过去数一数。和迩们排好了队，我一路踩着它们的背走过来，快到岸时却说了一句：你们上当了！最后那只和迩就抓住我，剥光了我的衣服。大穴牟迟教它到河口用淡水洗身，再把蒲草的花粉铺在地上，在上面打滚。兔子照做，身体恢复如初。书中说，这就是稻羽的白兔，现在叫作兔神。兔子对他说：八十神得不到八上比卖，虽然你背着袋子，她一定会嫁给你。

*The Eighty Deities all wished to marry Princess Yakami of Inaba, and set out together, making Ōnamuji carry their bag like a servant. At Cape Keta they found a hare lying stripped of its fur. They told it to bathe in the sea and lie on a high mountain in the wind; it did, and as the salt dried its skin split, and it lay weeping with pain. Ōnamuji came last and asked why it wept. The hare said: 'I lived on the island of Oki and wanted to cross. I tricked the wani of the sea, saying, let us count whose tribe is larger; line up and I will count you as I step across your backs. They lined up and I trod across, but just before landing I said, you have been deceived! The last wani seized me and stripped off all my clothing.' Ōnamuji told it to wash in the fresh water of the river-mouth and roll in the pollen of the sedges. It did, and its body was restored. This, says the text, is the White Hare of Inaba, now called the Hare Deity. The hare said: 'The Eighty Deities will not win the princess. Though you carry the bag, she will be yours.'*

### 3. 手间山的赤猪 / The red boar of Mount Tema

八上比卖果然对八十神说：我不听你们的话，我要嫁给大穴牟迟。八十神大怒，商量要杀死大穴牟迟。他们把他带到伯伎国手间山的山脚下——伯伎国大致在今天鸟取县的西部——对他说：山上有一头红色的野猪，我们把它赶下来，你在下面等着抓住它。要是抓不住，就杀了你。他们在山上把一块像野猪那样大的石头用火烧得通红，推下山去。大穴牟迟去抓那块石头，被烧得粘在石头上死了。他的母亲哭着上天，向神产巢日神求救。神产巢日神派了两位女神下来：蚶贝比卖和蛤贝比卖，她们的名字就是赤贝和蛤蜊。蚶贝比卖刮下贝壳上的粉末，蛤贝比卖取来水，把两样东西调和成像母亲的乳汁一样的药，涂遍他的全身。大穴牟迟又活了过来，变成了一个英俊的年轻人，到处走动。这是他第一次死而复生。

*Princess Yakami told the Eighty Deities: 'I will not listen to you. I will marry Ōnamuji.' Furious, they plotted to kill him. They took him to the foot of Mount Tema in Hahaki, roughly the west of present-day Tottori, and said: 'There is a red boar on this mountain. We will drive it down; you wait below and catch it. If you fail, we will kill you.' Then they heated in fire a great stone shaped like a boar and rolled it down. Ōnamuji caught it, was burned fast to it and died. His mother went up weeping to heaven and begged Kami-musubi for help, and Kami-musubi sent down two women, Princess Cockle-Shell and Princess Clam, named for the ark-shell and the clam. Princess Cockle-Shell scraped powder from her shell and Princess Clam brought water, and they mixed them like a mother's milk and smeared it over him. He came to life again as a handsome young man and walked about. It was the first time he died and lived again.*

### 4. 大树的裂缝 / The cleft in the tree

八十神看见他又活了，再次设下圈套。他们把大穴牟迟带进山里，砍倒一棵大树，在树干的裂缝里打进一个楔子，让他站进裂缝中间，然后猛地拔掉楔子，把他夹死在树干里。母亲又一次哭着到处寻找，终于找到了他，劈开大树，把他取出来救活了。这已经是他第二次死而复生。母亲对他说：你再住在这里，终究会被八十神害死的。她把他送到木国——也就是后来的纪伊国——的大屋毘古神那里躲避，张伯伦把这位神的名字译作“大屋之王子”。可是八十神一路追到了木国，拉满了弓，向大屋毘古神要人。大屋毘古神就让大穴牟迟从一棵树的树杈之间悄悄逃走，对他说：你到须佐之男所在的根之坚洲国去吧，那位大神一定会为你出主意。大穴牟迟就这样离开了人间，去寻找自己的祖先。

*Seeing him alive again, the Eighty Deities set another trap. They led Ōnamuji into the mountains, felled a great tree, drove a wedge into the cleft of the trunk, made him stand in the cleft, and then pulled out the wedge and crushed him to death. Again his mother searched for him weeping, found him, split the tree, took him out and restored him — the second time he had died and lived again. She said: 'If you stay here, you will be destroyed by the Eighty Deities in the end,' and sent him to the deity Oho-ya-biko, Chamberlain's Great-House-Prince, in the land of Ki, later called Kii. But the Eighty Deities pursued him there, fitting arrows to their bows and demanding him. Oho-ya-biko let him slip away through the fork of a tree, telling him: 'Go to the Nether-Distant-Land where Susanoo dwells. That great deity will surely counsel you.' So Ōnamuji left the living world to seek his ancestor.*

### 5. 须势理毘卖与蛇室 / Suseri-bime and the snake room

根之坚洲国——张伯伦译作“幽远之国”——就是当年须佐之男哭着要去的母亲之国，如今他住在这里。大穴牟迟来到须佐之男的宫殿，须佐之男的女儿须势理毘卖出来迎接，两人四目相对，一见钟情，当即结为夫妻。须势理毘卖回到殿里对父亲说：来了一位非常英俊的神。须佐之男出来一看，说：这是苇原色许男啊。“色许男”字面是“丑男子”，也有人理解为强悍的男子。须佐之男把他叫进来，却让他睡在满是毒蛇的蛇室里。须势理毘卖偷偷把一条驱蛇的领巾交给丈夫——领巾是一种披在肩上的长巾——对他说：蛇要咬你的时候，你就把这条领巾挥三下，把它们赶开。大穴牟迟照她的话做了，蛇都安静下来，他安安稳稳地睡了一夜，第二天平安走了出来。这是须佐之男给他的第一次考验，此后还有三次。

*The Nether-Distant-Land, the mother's land that Susanoo had once wept to go to, was now his dwelling. When Ōnamuji came to Susanoo's palace, Susanoo's daughter Suseri-bime came out; they looked at each other and were married at once. She went in and told her father: 'A very handsome deity has come.' Susanoo went out, looked at him and said: 'This is the Ugly Male of the Reed-Plains' — shiko-wo, literally ugly male, though some read it as a fierce one. He called him in, but made him sleep in a room full of snakes. Suseri-bime secretly gave her husband a snake-scarf, a long cloth worn over the shoulders, and said: 'When the snakes are about to bite you, wave this scarf three times to drive them off.' He did as she said, the snakes grew quiet, and he slept peacefully and came out unharmed in the morning. It was the first of Susanoo's trials; three more were to follow.*

### 6. 蜈蚣与蜂之室 / The room of centipedes and wasps

第二天晚上，须佐之男又让他睡在一间满是蜈蚣和马蜂的屋子里。须势理毘卖又交给他一条领巾，书中叫作“蜈蚣与蜂之比礼”，像上一次一样教他在危急的时候挥动。大穴牟迟又平安地度过了一夜，第二天照样走了出来。须佐之男的考验并没有到此为止。这位大神当年在高天原上也曾胡作非为，被众神驱逐出天界，如今轮到他来考验来娶自己女儿的年轻人。在这位暴躁的大神眼里，这个年轻人还远远没有证明自己。而在故事里，每一次真正救了大穴牟迟的，都是须势理毘卖的智慧与爱。她站在丈夫一边，悄悄地违背父亲的意愿；在《古事记》里，这样在关键时刻出手相助的女子并不少见，后面的丰玉毘卖和弟橘比卖也是如此。一次次的考验，也让这个曾被兄长们欺负的年轻人渐渐成为能统治国土的神。

*The next night Susanoo put him in a room full of centipedes and wasps. Again Suseri-bime gave him a scarf, which the text calls the centipede-and-wasp scarf, and taught him to wave it in danger as before, and again he came out unharmed. Susanoo's trials were not over. The great deity who had once run riot in High Heaven and been driven out by the gods now tested the young man who had come for his daughter, and in his eyes the youth had proved nothing yet. In the story it is Suseri-bime's wit and love that save Ōnamuji each time: she takes her husband's side and quietly works against her father's will. Women who step in at the decisive moment are not rare in the Kojiki; Toyotama-bime and Oto-tachibana-hime will do the same later on. Trial by trial, the young man once bullied by his brothers was becoming a deity fit to rule the land.*

### 7. 火烧原野与老鼠 / The burning moor and the mouse

须佐之男又把一支鸣镝——箭头上装着中空的响器、射出去会呼啸作响的箭——射进一片大草原，命令大穴牟迟去把箭找回来。等他走进草原深处，须佐之男就从四面放火，烧起了整片原野。火从四面围上来，大穴牟迟找不到出路。这时一只老鼠出来对他说：里面空荡荡，外面窄窄窄。他明白了老鼠的意思，用脚一踩地面，就掉进了一个地洞，躲在里面，大火从头顶烧了过去。不久，老鼠又把那支鸣镝衔来交给他，箭上的羽毛已经被小老鼠们啃光了。须势理毘卖以为丈夫已经死了，哭着拿着办丧事的器具来到原野上；须佐之男也以为他死了，走出来站在烧过的原野上。大穴牟迟却拿着那支箭走了出来，把箭献给了须佐之男。老鼠的那两句话，在原文里是“内者富良富良，外者须夫须夫”，读起来就像一句咒语。在高天原上，众神曾用火与笑声把天照大神请出岩户；在这里，救下大穴牟迟的却是一只地洞里的小老鼠。

*Next Susanoo shot a whizzing arrow into a great moor and sent Ōnamuji to fetch it. When he was deep in the moor, Susanoo set fire to it all round. Ōnamuji could find no way out, until a mouse came and said: 'Inside, hollow-hollow; outside, narrow-narrow.' Understanding, he stamped on the ground, fell into a hole, and hid there while the fire passed over him. Soon the mouse brought him the whizzing arrow in its mouth; its feathers had been eaten by the mouse's children. Suseri-bime, thinking her husband dead, came weeping with the things for a funeral, and Susanoo, thinking the same, stood on the burned moor. But Ōnamuji came out with the arrow and presented it to him. The mouse's words in the original, uchi wa hora-hora, soto wa subu-subu, sound almost like a spell. In High Heaven the gods had drawn Amaterasu from her cave with fire and laughter; here it is a small mouse in a burrow that saves Ōnamuji.*

### 8. 头上的蜈蚣 / Lice that were centipedes

须佐之男把大穴牟迟带回家里，叫进一间八寻宽的大屋子，让他替自己捉头上的虱子。大穴牟迟一看，须佐之男头上爬的根本不是虱子，而是一只只蜈蚣。须势理毘卖悄悄递给丈夫一把椋树的果实和一些红土。椋树的果实是黑紫色的小果子，嚼碎了就像碾碎的虫子。大穴牟迟把椋树果放进嘴里咬碎，再和着红土一起吐出来。须佐之男以为他在把蜈蚣一只只咬碎吐掉，原文说他“心里觉得这个年轻人可爱”，就放心地睡着了。从蛇室、蜈蚣与蜂之室，到火烧原野，再到头上的“虱子”，须佐之男的考验一次比一次危险，而每一次都被他的女儿悄悄化解。这是最后一次考验，也是大穴牟迟逃走的机会。须佐之男曾经在出云斩杀八岐大蛇、救下栉名田比卖，如今他睡着时，命运却交到了这个年轻人手里。

*Susanoo took him back into the house, called him into a great room eight fathoms wide, and made him pick the lice from his head. But the creatures on Susanoo's head were centipedes. Suseri-bime secretly gave her husband berries of the muku tree, small dark fruit that look like crushed insects when chewed, and some red earth. Ōnamuji chewed the berries and spat them out with the red earth, and Susanoo, thinking he was biting the centipedes to pieces and spitting them out, felt fond of him in his heart — the text's own words — and fell asleep. From the snake room to the centipedes and wasps, the burning moor and now the 'lice', each trial was more dangerous than the last, and each was quietly undone by his daughter. It was the last trial, and Ōnamuji's chance to escape. Susanoo, who once slew the eight-forked serpent in Izumo and saved Kushinada-hime, now lay asleep with his fate in the young man's hands.*

### 9. 生太刀、生弓矢与天诏琴 / The sword of life, the bow and the speaking lute

大穴牟迟趁须佐之男睡着，把他的头发一缕缕绑在屋子的椽子上，用一块要五百个人才拉得动的大石头堵住屋门，背起妻子须势理毘卖，带上须佐之男的生太刀、生弓矢和天诏琴，逃了出去。逃跑时天诏琴碰到了树，大地都震动起来。须佐之男被惊醒，一拉头发，把整座屋子都拉倒了。等他把绑在椽子上的头发解开，两人已经逃得很远。须佐之男一直追到黄泉比良坂，远远望着他们喊道：用你拿走的生太刀和生弓矢，把你的兄弟们赶到山坡下、河滩上去！你要成为大国主神，成为宇都志国玉神，让我的女儿须势理毘卖做你的正妻，在宇迦山下把宫柱深深地立在地底的岩石上，把屋梁高高地架到高天原，住在那里吧，你这家伙！大穴牟迟照他的话赶走了八十神，开始造国。八上比卖也来到他身边，但她害怕正妻须势理毘卖，把生下的孩子夹在树杈之间就回去了。

*While Susanoo slept, Ōnamuji tied his hair to the rafters of the room, blocked the door with a rock that would take five hundred men to move, took his wife Suseri-bime on his back, and fled carrying Susanoo's great sword of life, his bow and arrows of life, and his heavenly speaking lute. The lute brushed against a tree and the earth resounded. Susanoo woke and pulled the whole house down with his hair; by the time he had untied it from the rafters they were far away. He pursued them to the Even Pass of Yomi and, gazing after them, called out: 'With the sword of life and the bow of life drive your brothers down the slopes and into the river-beds! Become Ōkuninushi, become Utsushi-kunitama, make my daughter Suseri-bime your chief wife, and at the foot of Mount Uka set your palace pillars deep in the bedrock and raise your cross-beams to the Plain of High Heaven, and dwell there, you rascal!' He did so, drove away the Eighty Deities and began to make the land. Princess Yakami came to him too, but in fear of the chief wife she left her child in the fork of a tree and went home.*

### 10. 八千矛神的求婚之歌 / The songs of the Deity of Eight Thousand Spears

以八千矛神为名的大国主，又到遥远的越国去向沼河比卖求婚。他站在她家门前唱歌，唱自己怎样听说越国有一位聪明美丽的女子，怎样远道而来，拉着门板等到天明，林中的鸟已经开始啼叫。沼河比卖隔着门回唱：请你不要着急，等到夜里，我就会是你的。第二天夜里，两人结为夫妻。正妻须势理毘卖为此十分嫉妒。大国主准备从出云上大和去，一只手搭在马鞍上，一只脚踏在马镫里，唱歌给她听：他换了三套衣服，黑色的不合适，翠鸟一样青色的也不合适，最后换上山田里的染料染成的衣服，才觉得合适；又说，你将来会像山上孤零零的芒草一样哭泣。须势理毘卖举起大酒杯走到他身边，唱道：你是男子，到处都有妻子，而我是女子，除了你没有别的丈夫。两人用酒杯立下誓约，手搭着彼此的脖子，从此和睦相处，书中说他们直到今天仍安然相守。

*As Yachihoko, the Deity of Eight Thousand Spears, he went to distant Koshi to woo Princess Nunakawa. Standing at her door he sang how he had heard of a wise and beautiful woman in Koshi, how he had come from afar and stood pulling at her door until dawn while the birds of the forest began to cry. She sang back through the door: be not impatient; when night comes I will be yours. The next night they were married. His chief wife Suseri-bime was deeply jealous. As he was about to go up from Izumo to Yamato, one hand on the saddle and one foot in the stirrup, he sang to her: of three robes he had tried, the black was not right and the kingfisher-blue was not right, but the robe dyed with the juice of the dye-plant sown in the mountain fields suited him; and she would weep like a single clump of grass on the mountain. Suseri-bime came to him lifting a great wine-cup and sang: you are a man and have wives wherever you go, but I am a woman and have no husband but you. They pledged each other with the cup, their hands on each other's necks, and the text says they are at peace together to this day.*

### 11. 少名毘古那 / Sukuna-bikona

有一天，大国主在出云的美保岬，看见一位神乘着用萝藦豆荚做成的小船，踏着浪花漂过来，身上穿着用整张剥下来的小动物的皮做的衣服——原文的字一说是鹅，一说指飞蛾。大国主问他的名字，他不回答；问随从的众神，谁也不知道。这时，一只蛤蟆说：久延毘古一定知道。大国主召来久延毘古，久延毘古说：这是神产巢日神的儿子少名毘古那神。大国主去问神产巢日神，神产巢日神说：他确实是我的孩子，是我那些孩子中从我的指缝间漏下去的那一个。你们两个结为兄弟，一起把国土造好、巩固起来吧。从此，大国主和少名毘古那一起造国。后来，少名毘古那渡海去了常世国。书中说，那位知道他名字的久延毘古，就是今天田里的稻草人，虽然不能走路，却知道天下所有的事情。

*Once, at Cape Miho in Izumo, Ōkuninushi saw a deity riding the crests of the waves in a little boat made from a seed-pod of the kagami plant, clothed in the whole stripped skin of some small creature — the character is read by some as a goose and by others as a moth. Asked his name, he did not answer, and none of the attendant deities knew it. Then a toad said: 'Kuyebiko will surely know.' Kuyebiko, called for, said: 'This is Sukuna-bikona, the son of Kami-musubi.' When Ōkuninushi asked Kami-musubi, she said: 'He is truly my child, the one of my children who slipped through between my fingers. Be brothers, and make and consolidate the land together.' So the two made the land together, until Sukuna-bikona crossed over the sea to the Eternal Land. Kuyebiko, the text says, is the scarecrow in the fields today, who cannot walk but knows everything under heaven.*

### 12. 御诸山的神 / The deity of Mount Mimoro

少名毘古那走了，大国主忧愁地说：我一个人怎么能把这片国土造好呢？要和哪一位神一起造才好？这时，海面上亮起光来，一位神照着海面过来，说：你要是好好地供奉我，我就和你一起把国土造成。不然的话，国土是造不成的。大国主问：那么，要怎样供奉你呢？那位神说：你把我供奉在大和那青翠如垣墙的群山东边的山上。书中说，这就是住在御诸山上的神。御诸山也就是今天奈良的三轮山。《古事记》在这里没有写出这位神的名字；《日本书纪》的一段异文则说，这位从海上来的神是大国主自己的“幸魂奇魂”。至此，大国主造国的故事告一段落。书中接着列出了大年神的子孙，然后，高天原上的众神开始关心下界的这片国土了。这座山在后来的故事里一直被视为神所居住的山。

*With Sukuna-bikona gone, Ōkuninushi said in sorrow: 'How can I make this land alone? With what deity should I make it?' Then a light shone over the sea, and a deity came illuminating it and said: 'If you will worship me well, I will make the land together with you. Otherwise it cannot be made.' Ōkuninushi asked how he should worship him, and the deity said: 'Worship me on the eastern mountain of the green fence of Yamato.' This, says the text, is the deity who dwells on Mount Mimoro, the mountain now called Miwa in Nara. The Kojiki gives the deity no name here; a variant in the Nihon shoki says that the deity from the sea was Ōkuninushi's own spirit of good fortune and wondrous spirit. Here the making of the land rests. The text goes on to list the children of the Great-Harvest Deity, and then the deities of the Plain of High Heaven turn their attention to the land below. The mountain remains, throughout the later stories, a mountain where a deity dwells.*

### 13. 天若日子与雉鸣女 / Ame-no-Wakahiko and the pheasant

天照大神说：丰苇原的瑞穗国应该由我的儿子天忍穗耳命来统治。天忍穗耳命站在天之浮桥上往下一看，说：下面吵闹得很！就回来报告了。高御产巢日神和天照大神在天安河边召集众神商议，思金神出主意，先派了天菩比神下去，他却去讨好大国主，三年都没有回来复命。于是又派天若日子，赐给他天之麻迦古弓和天之波波矢。天若日子一到下界，就娶了大国主的女儿下照比卖，想把这片国土据为己有，八年都没有复命。众神又派了一只名叫鸣女的雉鸡去问他。雉鸡停在天若日子门前的桂树上，传达了天神的问话。天佐具卖劝他射死这只鸟，天若日子就用天神赐的弓箭射穿了雉鸡，箭一直飞到天安河边高木神的面前。高木神看见箭羽上有血，说：如果天若日子没有违命，射的是恶神，这支箭就不要射中他；如果他有邪心，就让他死于此箭。他把箭从箭孔投回去，正射中躺在床上的天若日子的胸口，天若日子当场死去。书中说，“当心还矢”这句话就由此而来。

*Amaterasu declared that the Luxuriant Reed-Plains Land of Fresh Rice-Ears should be ruled by her son Ame-no-oshi-ho-mimi. He looked down from the Floating Bridge of Heaven, said that the land below was in a great uproar, and went back to report. Takami-musubi and Amaterasu assembled the deities by the Tranquil River of Heaven, and on Omoikane's counsel they sent Ame-no-hohi, who curried favour with Ōkuninushi and sent no report for three years. Next they sent Ame-no-Wakahiko with the heavenly deer-bow and feathered arrows. He married Ōkuninushi's daughter Shitateru-hime, schemed to take the land for himself, and sent no report for eight years. The deities sent a pheasant, Naki-me, to question him. She perched on the cassia tree at his gate and spoke the heavenly deities' words. Urged by Ama-no-sagu-me, he shot the pheasant with the heavenly bow; the arrow passed through her and flew up to Takagi-no-kami by the Tranquil River. Seeing blood on its feathers, Takagi said: 'If Ame-no-Wakahiko has not disobeyed and shot at evil deities, let this arrow not strike him; if he has an evil heart, let him die by it,' and threw it back down through the hole it had made. It struck Ame-no-Wakahiko in the chest as he lay on his bed, and he died. Hence, says the text, the saying 'beware a returning arrow'.*

### 14. 建御雷与让国 / Takemikazuchi and the yielding of the land

众神最后派出了建御雷神，由天鸟船神陪同。两位神降落到出云国伊那佐的小滨，建御雷神拔出十拳剑，剑尖朝上倒插在浪头上，盘腿坐在剑尖上，问大国主：天照大神和高木神派我们来问你：你统治的苇原中国，是天神之子应当统治的国土，你意下如何？大国主说：我不能回答，我的儿子八重事代主神会回答，可他到美保岬打鸟钓鱼去了。天鸟船神把事代主找来，事代主说：遵命，把这国土献给天神之子吧。说完踏翻了自己的船，拍出“天之逆手”，把船变成青柴垣，藏身其中。另一个儿子建御名方神用指尖托着千引石走来，要和建御雷比力气。他一抓建御雷的手，那只手就变成了冰柱，又变成了剑刃；建御雷反过来抓住他的手，像抓一根嫩芦苇一样捏碎扔开。建御名方逃到信浓的州羽海，求饶说：我决不离开这里，也不违背父亲和事代主的话。大国主终于说：这片国土就献出去吧。只请为我建一座宫殿，宫柱深深立在地底岩石上，屋梁高高架到高天原，我就隐退在弯弯曲曲的道路深处。于是人们在出云的多艺志小滨建起了宫殿，建御雷神回到天上复命。

*Last, the deities sent Takemikazuchi, with Ame-no-tori-fune. The two came down to the little shore of Inasa in Izumo. Takemikazuchi drew his ten-grasp sword, stood it upside down on the crest of a wave, sat cross-legged on its point and asked Ōkuninushi: 'Amaterasu and Takagi-no-kami have sent us to ask: the Central Land of Reed-Plains that you rule is the land the heavenly deity's child should rule. What is your mind?' Ōkuninushi said: 'I cannot answer. My son Yae-Kotoshironushi will answer, but he has gone to Cape Miho to hunt birds and fish.' Fetched, Kotoshironushi said: 'I obey; offer this land to the heavenly deity's child.' He trod on his boat to overturn it, clapped his hands in the heavenly backward clap, turned the boat into a green fence and hid within it. Then another son, Takeminakata, came carrying a thousand-draught rock on his fingertips and challenged Takemikazuchi to a trial of strength. When he grasped Takemikazuchi's hand it turned into an icicle and then into a sword-blade; Takemikazuchi grasped his in return like a young reed, crushed it and flung it away. Takeminakata fled to the sea of Suwa in Shinano and begged for his life, vowing never to leave it and not to go against his father or Kotoshironushi. At last Ōkuninushi said: 'Let this land be offered. Only build me a dwelling with pillars set deep in the bedrock and cross-beams raised to the Plain of High Heaven, and I will retire into the winding ways.' A palace was built on the shore of Tagishi in Izumo, and Takemikazuchi went back up to report.*

## 伊服岐能山之神 · Deity of Mount Ibuki

**有完整故事 / Full story**

> *Deity of Mount Ibuki who appears to the swordless Yamato Takeru as a white boar the size of an ox and, mistaken for its messenger, strikes him with a great hailstorm (Kojiki, Sect. LXXXVIII).*

**长篇故事 · 12 章**

### 1. 小碓命与兄长 / Wo-usu and his elder brother

景行天皇在缠向的日代宫统治天下，他有许多儿子，其中有一对兄弟：哥哥叫大碓命，弟弟叫小碓命，也叫倭男具那命。有一天，天皇对小碓命说：你哥哥为什么早晚都不来一起用膳？你去好好教导他。过了五天，大碓命还是没有来。天皇问小碓命：你哥哥怎么还不来？你是不是没有教导他？小碓命回答：我已经教导过了。天皇问：你是怎么教导的？小碓命说：清早他去厕所的时候，我等着把他抓住，折断了他的手脚，用草席包起来扔掉了。这段话在书中说得很直接，本篇只交代结果。天皇听了，对这个儿子凶猛暴烈的性情感到害怕。这位少年后来的名字叫倭建命，他的英雄故事，就从父亲的这份恐惧开始。书中在前面的部分还记了一件事：天皇曾派大碓命去美浓召两位少女进宫，大碓命却把她们据为己有，另找别的女子冒名献上；天皇心里明白，却一直闷闷不乐。

*The Heavenly Sovereign Keikō ruled from the palace of Hishiro at Makimuku. Among his many sons were two brothers: the elder, Oho-usu, and the younger, Wo-usu, also called Yamato-woguna. One day the sovereign said to Wo-usu: 'Why does your elder brother not come to the morning and evening meals? Go and teach him.' Five days passed and still the elder brother did not come. The sovereign asked: 'Why does your brother still not come? Have you not taught him?' Wo-usu said he had. 'How did you teach him?' Wo-usu answered that he had waited for his brother at dawn when he went to the privy, seized him, broken his limbs, wrapped him in matting and thrown him away. The text tells it bluntly; this retelling gives only the outcome. The sovereign was afraid of his son's fierce and violent heart. The boy would later be called Yamato Takeru, and his hero's story begins with his father's fear. An earlier part tells that the sovereign had sent Oho-usu to summon two maidens of Mino to court, and that Oho-usu took them for himself and sent other women under their names; the sovereign knew and was troubled.*

### 2. 少女装扮 / Disguised as a girl

天皇说：西方有两个熊曾建，他们不肯归顺，没有礼数，你去把他们杀了。就派小碓命去了西方。那时小碓命还很年轻，头发在额前束成少年的发髻。他向姑母倭比卖命讨来她的上衣和裙子，又把一把短剑藏在怀里，出发了。到了熊曾建的家，只见房子四周围着三重军士，熊曾兄弟正在新建一座石室，准备举行落成的宴会，到处都是人声。小碓命在附近转了转，等着宴会的日子。到了那一天，他把束起的头发放下来梳成少女的样子，穿上姑母的衣裙，完全变成了一个少女，混在女人们中间，走进了石室。熊曾建兄弟一眼看中了这个少女，就让她坐在两人中间，一起饮酒作乐。倭比卖命是天皇的妹妹，在伊势侍奉天照大神；熊曾大致在今天九州的南部。一个束着少年发髻的皇子，就这样穿着姑母的衣裳，独自走进了敌人的宴席。

*The sovereign said: 'In the west there are two Kumaso bravoes who will not submit and have no respect. Go and kill them,' and sent Wo-usu west. He was still young, his hair bound up at the forehead in a boy's knot. He begged from his aunt Yamato-hime her upper robe and skirt, hid a short sword in his bosom, and set out. At the Kumaso bravoes' house he found it surrounded by three rings of warriors; the brothers were building a new cave-dwelling for a housewarming feast, and the place was full of noise. Wo-usu walked about nearby, waiting for the day of the feast. When it came he let down his hair like a girl's, put on his aunt's robe and skirt, became in every way a young girl, mingled with the women and went into the dwelling. The two Kumaso bravoes were taken with the maiden at once, seated her between them, and feasted. Yamato-hime was the sovereign's sister, serving Amaterasu at Ise; Kumaso lay roughly in the south of present-day Kyushu. So a prince with a boy's hair-knot walked alone into his enemies' feast in his aunt's clothes.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十节。</sub>

### 3. 倭建的名号 / The name Yamato Takeru

宴会正热闹的时候，小碓命从怀里拔出短剑，抓住哥哥熊曾建的衣领，一剑刺穿了他的胸膛。弟弟熊曾建吓得转身就逃，小碓命追到石室的台阶下，从背后抓住他，把剑刺了进去。弟弟熊曾建说：不要动这把剑，我有话要说。小碓命就按住他，听他说。熊曾建问：您是谁？小碓命说：我是统治大八岛国、住在缠向日代宫的大带日子淤斯吕和气天皇的儿子，名叫倭男具那王。天皇听说你们熊曾建两个不肯归顺、没有礼数，派我来杀你们。熊曾建说：确实是这样。在西方，没有比我们兄弟两个更勇猛强悍的人了；可是在大倭国，却有比我们更勇猛的男子。我要献给你一个名号：从今以后，你就叫倭建御子吧。说完，小碓命就把他杀了。从此，人们称他为倭建命。“建”在这里是勇猛的意思，“倭建”就是“大和的勇士”。他一生最有名的这个名字，竟是一位敌人临死前送给他的赞许。

*When the feast was at its height, Wo-usu drew the sword from his bosom, seized the elder Kumaso bravo by the collar and thrust the sword through his chest. The younger bravo fled in terror; Wo-usu pursued him to the foot of the steps of the dwelling, caught him from behind and thrust the sword into him. The younger bravo said: 'Do not move the sword. I have something to say.' Holding him down, Wo-usu listened. 'Who is your augustness?' 'I am the son of the Heavenly Sovereign Ōtarashi-hiko-oshiro-wake, who dwells in the palace of Hishiro at Makimuku and rules the Land of the Eight Great Islands; my name is King Yamato-woguna. The sovereign heard that you two Kumaso bravoes would not submit and had no respect, and sent me to kill you.' The bravo said: 'It is true. In the west there are none so brave and strong as we two; but in great Yamato there is a man braver than we. I will offer you a name: henceforth be called the August Child Yamato Takeru.' When he had spoken, Wo-usu killed him. From then on he was called Yamato Takeru. Takeru here means brave: Yamato Takeru is the brave one of Yamato. The name he is best known by was a tribute from a dying enemy.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十节。</sub>

### 4. 出云建与木刀 / The Izumo bravo and the wooden sword

倭建命回京的路上，把山神、河神和海峡之神一一平定，然后进入出云国，想要杀死出云建。他先和出云建结为朋友，又暗中用赤梼木做了一把假刀，佩在身上。有一天，两人一起在肥河里洗澡。倭建命先从河里出来，把出云建解下的刀佩在自己身上，说：我们来交换刀吧。出云建从河里出来，就佩上了倭建命的那把木刀。倭建命说：来，我们比比刀法吧。两人各自拔刀，出云建拔不出那把木刀，倭建命就拔出真刀，把出云建杀了。他唱了一首歌，大意是：出云建佩的那把刀，刀鞘上缠着一层又一层的藤蔓，看起来很好，里面却没有刀身，真是可怜啊。他平定了西方，回到京城，向天皇复命。这两件事，一靠扮成少女，一靠交换刀剑，都是用计取胜。同样的木刀计和同一首歌，在《日本书纪》里被放进另一个故事：出云的一对兄弟之间。两部书在这里各有各的讲法。

*On his way back Yamato Takeru subdued the deities of the mountains, the rivers and the straits, and then went into Izumo intending to kill the Izumo bravo. First he made friends with him, and secretly made a false sword of ichii wood, which he wore. One day they bathed together in the river Hi. Yamato Takeru came out first, girded on the sword the Izumo bravo had taken off, and said: 'Let us exchange swords.' The Izumo bravo came out and girded on the wooden sword. 'Come,' said Yamato Takeru, 'let us cross swords.' Each drew; the Izumo bravo could not draw the wooden sword, and Yamato Takeru drew the real one and killed him. He sang a song whose sense is: the sword the Izumo bravo wore, its scabbard wound with creepers many times — fine to look at, but with no blade inside, alas. Having subdued the west, he returned to the capital and reported to the sovereign. Both deeds, the girl's disguise and the exchange of swords, were won by trickery. The same wooden-sword trick and the same song appear in the Nihon shoki in a different story, between two brothers of Izumo; the two books tell it each in their own way.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十一节。</sub>

### 5. 伊势的姑母 / The aunt at Ise

倭建命刚刚回来，天皇又命令他说：你去把东方十二道那些凶暴的神和不肯归顺的人平定了。天皇派吉备臣的祖先御锄友耳建日子跟他同行，又赐给他一支柊木做的八寻长矛。倭建命出发去东方，先到了伊势，参拜神宫，对在那里侍奉的姑母倭比卖命说：天皇是不是想让我死？他派我去打西方那些凶暴的人，我回来还没有多久，又不给我军队，就要派我去平定东方十二道的凶暴之人。这样想来，天皇一定是想让我死啊。他一边诉说，一边哭泣。临走时，倭比卖命把草薙剑交给他——这就是须佐之男从八岐大蛇尾中得到、天孙降临时带到人间的那把剑——又给了他一个袋子，说：如果遇到紧急的事情，就打开这个袋子。那支长矛，原文叫“比比罗木之八寻矛”，比比罗木就是柊树。至于袋子里装着什么，他当时并不知道。一个刚刚立下大功的皇子在姑母面前哭泣，这是《古事记》里少有的、写英雄软弱的一幕。

*No sooner had Yamato Takeru returned than the sovereign commanded him again: 'Go and subdue the savage deities and unsubmissive people of the twelve roads of the East.' He sent with him Mi-suki-tomo-mimi-take-hiko, ancestor of the Kibi lords, and gave him a holly-wood spear eight fathoms long. On his way east Yamato Takeru went first to Ise, worshipped at the shrine, and said to his aunt Yamato-hime, who served there: 'Does the sovereign wish me dead? He sent me to strike the savage people of the west, and I have hardly come back before, without giving me any troops, he sends me to subdue the savage people of the twelve roads of the East. Thinking of this, I can only believe he wishes me to die.' He wept as he spoke. When he left, Yamato-hime gave him the Kusanagi sword — the sword Susanoo took from the serpent's tail and the August Grandchild brought down from heaven — and a bag, saying: 'If anything urgent happens, open the mouth of this bag.' The spear is called in the original an eight-fathom spear of hihiragi, the holly. What the bag held he did not yet know. A prince fresh from great deeds weeping before his aunt is one of the rare scenes in the Kojiki that show a hero's weakness.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十二节。</sub>

### 6. 相武的野火 / The fire on the moor of Sagamu

倭建命到了尾张国，进了尾张国造的祖先美夜受比卖的家，本想娶她为妻，转念一想，等回来时再娶吧，就和她立下婚约，继续往东去了。他把山河间凶暴的神和不肯归顺的人一一平定。到了相武国，那里的国造骗他说：这片原野里有一个大沼泽，住在沼泽里的神非常凶暴。倭建命就走进原野去看那位神。国造在原野上四面放火。倭建命知道上了当，打开姑母给的袋子，里面是一块打火石。他先用草薙剑割开身边的草，再用打火石点起一圈逆火，把烧来的大火挡了回去，平安地走了出来。他回过头来，把那些国造全部杀死，放火烧掉，所以那个地方至今叫作烧津。《日本书纪》说这把剑因为割草而得名“草薙”，而《古事记》在更早的大蛇故事里就已经这样称呼它了。书中把这件事记在相武国，而“烧津”这个地名今天在骏河，也就是静冈县，这一点历来被注释家讨论。

*In Owari, Yamato Takeru entered the house of Miyazu-hime, ancestress of the rulers of Owari, and thought to marry her; then, deciding to marry her on his return, he pledged himself to her and went on east. He subdued the savage deities and unsubmissive people of the mountains and rivers one by one. In Sagamu the local ruler deceived him: 'In this moor is a great marsh, and the deity of the marsh is very violent.' Yamato Takeru went into the moor to see the deity, and the ruler set fire to the moor all round. Knowing he had been deceived, he opened his aunt's bag and found a fire-striker. First he mowed the grass around him with the Kusanagi sword, then lit a counter-fire with the striker and drove back the oncoming fire, and came out safe. He turned back, killed all those rulers and burned them, so that the place is still called Yaizu, Burning Ford. The Nihon shoki says the sword was named Kusanagi, Grass-Mower, from this deed; the Kojiki already calls it so in the earlier serpent story. The text places this in Sagamu, while the name Yaizu belongs today to Suruga, in Shizuoka — a discrepancy long discussed by commentators.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十三节。</sub>

### 7. 弟橘比卖投海 / Oto-tachibana-hime enters the sea

倭建命从那里出发，渡过走水海。海峡的神掀起大浪，船在原地打转，无法前进。这时，倭建命的妃子弟橘比卖命说：我代替皇子进入海中吧。皇子应当完成被派来做的事，回去向天皇复命。她要入海的时候，在浪上铺了八层菅草垫子、八层皮垫子和八层绸缎垫子，坐在上面，沉入了海中。汹涌的波浪自己平息下来，船又能前进了。弟橘比卖临走前唱了一首歌，大意是：在相武那群山耸立的小原野上，熊熊大火之中，你站在火里还在问我是否平安——啊，你啊。七天以后，她的梳子漂到了岸边。倭建命拾起那把梳子，为她修了陵墓，把梳子安放在里面。这首歌让人知道，在相武原野的大火里，她一直在丈夫的身边。走水海大致是今天东京湾口的浦贺水道一带，水流湍急，所以叫作“走水”。

*From there Yamato Takeru crossed the sea of Hashirimizu. The deity of the strait raised great waves; the boat spun round and could not go forward. Then his consort Oto-tachibana-hime said: 'I will enter the sea in the prince's stead. The prince must accomplish the task he was sent on and report to the sovereign.' When she was about to enter the sea, she spread eight layers of sedge rugs, eight layers of skin rugs and eight layers of silk rugs on the waves, sat on them, and sank into the sea. The raging waves calmed of themselves and the boat could go on. Before she went she sang a song whose sense is: on the little moor of Sagamu among the standing peaks, in the midst of the burning fire, you stood and asked after me — ah, you. Seven days later her comb drifted ashore. Yamato Takeru took up the comb, made her a tomb and laid the comb in it. Her song shows that in the fire on the moor of Sagamu she had been at her husband's side. The Hashirimizu sea is roughly the Uraga channel at the mouth of Tokyo Bay, its current so swift that it was called Running Water.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十四节。</sub>

### 8. 足柄坂的白鹿 / The white deer on the Ashigara pass

倭建命继续深入东方，把凶暴的虾夷人都平定了，又平定了山河间凶暴的神，然后踏上了回程。来到足柄坂下，他正在吃干粮，那座山坡的神变成一只白鹿，走过来站在他面前。倭建命拿起吃剩的野蒜，等白鹿走近时扔过去，正好打中了它的眼睛，把它打死了。他登上足柄坂，回望来路，想起沉入海中的弟橘比卖，长叹了三声：吾妻哉！——“啊，我的妻子！”所以，书中说，从此人们就把那片土地叫作“阿豆麻”，也就是“东国”。这一声叹息，成了一个地方的名字。倭建命一路征战，从不诉苦，只在这里流露了一次深深的悲伤。虾夷指当时东方不服从朝廷的人们；足柄坂在今天神奈川与静冈交界的山上。白鹿被野蒜打中眼睛，这样细小的一笔，让这位山神的死显得格外突然。

*Yamato Takeru went on deep into the East, subdued all the savage Emishi, pacified the savage deities of the mountains and rivers, and set out to return. At the foot of the Ashigara pass, as he was eating his dried rice, the deity of the pass changed into a white deer and came and stood before him. He took up the leftover end of the wild chive he had been eating, waited for the deer to come near, threw it, struck it in the eye and killed it. Then he climbed the pass, looked back the way he had come, remembered Oto-tachibana-hime who had sunk into the sea, and sighed three times: 'Azuma haya!' — 'Alas, my wife!' Therefore, says the text, the land has been called Azuma, the East. A sigh became the name of a land. Through all his fighting Yamato Takeru never complained; only here does he show his deep grief. The Emishi were the peoples of the East who did not submit to the court; the Ashigara pass lies in the mountains on the border of present-day Kanagawa and Shizuoka. The small detail of the wild chive striking the deer's eye makes the mountain deity's death all the more sudden.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十五节。</sub>

### 9. 酒折宫的火守老人 / The old fire-keeper at Sakaori

倭建命越过足柄坂，进入甲斐国，住在酒折宫里。有一天，他唱了一句歌：越过新治和筑波，已经睡了几夜？——意思是说，离开东方的新治与筑波以来，已经过了多少个夜晚了？身边的人没有一个能接下去。这时，一个在旁边看守火堆的老人接着唱道：数一数日子，夜是九夜，日是十日。倭建命十分赞赏这个老人，就封他为东国造。这一问一答的两句歌，后世被看作日本连歌的起源之一。在征战的长途中，这是少有的安静时刻：一位疲倦的皇子数着离家的日子，一位卑微的老人替他把日子数了出来。新治与筑波都在今天的茨城县，甲斐就是今天的山梨县，酒折宫的旧址在今天的甲府。身边的随从没有一个答得上来，答出这一句的，却是一个看火的老人。

*Crossing from the pass into Kai, Yamato Takeru stayed at the palace of Sakaori. One day he sang a line: since passing Niibari and Tsukuba, how many nights have I slept? — that is, how many nights since leaving Niibari and Tsukuba in the East? None of his followers could answer it. Then an old man who was tending the fire took it up: counting the days, of nights there have been nine, of days ten. Yamato Takeru praised the old man and made him ruler of the Eastern Land. This exchange of two lines has been seen in later times as one of the beginnings of linked verse in Japan. On the long campaign it is a rare quiet moment: a weary prince counting the days away from home, and a humble old man counting them for him. Niibari and Tsukuba are in present-day Ibaraki, Kai is present-day Yamanashi, and the site of Sakaori is in today's Kofu. None of his attendants could answer; it was the old man watching the fire who did.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十六节。</sub>

### 10. 美夜受比卖与伊吹山 / Miyazu-hime and Mount Ibuki

倭建命从甲斐越过科野，平定了科野坂的神，回到尾张，进了美夜受比卖的家，履行当初的约定。两人以歌相答，结为夫妻。随后，他把草薙剑留在美夜受比卖的身边，空着手去讨伐伊服岐能山——伊吹山——的神，说：这座山的神，我空手就能把他抓住。他登上山去，在山坡上遇见一头白色的野猪，大得像一头牛。倭建命大声说：这头变成白猪的，是山神的使者吧。现在不杀它，等我回来的时候再杀。说完就继续往上走。可是那头白猪并不是使者，正是山神本人。因为他说了这样的大话，山神降下了猛烈的冰雹，打得倭建命神志昏迷。这把一路保护他的剑，此刻已经不在他的身边。书中还记下，宴会上倭建命看见她外衣的下摆沾着月事的血迹，便以歌相问，她以歌相答，说日月流转，等他等得月亮都已升起。伊吹山在今天滋贺与岐阜的交界处。后世注释家认为，倭建命对着山神口出狂言——原文称为“言举”——正是招来灾祸的原因。

*From Kai Yamato Takeru crossed Shinano, subdued the deity of the Shinano pass, and came back to Owari, to Miyazu-hime's house, to keep his pledge. They exchanged songs and were married. Then he left the Kusanagi sword with Miyazu-hime and went bare-handed to take the deity of Mount Ibuki, saying: 'As for the deity of this mountain, I will take him with my bare hands.' Climbing the mountain, he met on its slope a white boar as big as an ox. He said aloud: 'This one that has turned into a white boar must be the messenger of the mountain deity. I will not kill it now; I will kill it on my way back,' and went on climbing. But the white boar was no messenger; it was the deity himself. Because of these boastful words the deity sent down a great storm of hail that struck Yamato Takeru senseless. The sword that had protected him all the way was no longer at his side. The text also tells that at the feast he saw the stain of her monthly blood on the hem of her robe and questioned her in song, and she answered in song that months had passed and the moon had risen while she waited for him. Mount Ibuki lies on the border of present-day Shiga and Gifu. Later commentators see his boastful speech against the deity — kotoage, speaking out — as what brought the disaster on him.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十七至八十八节。</sub>

### 11. 三重的腿 / Legs like a triple-bent rice cake

倭建命下了山，来到玉仓部的清泉边休息，神志稍稍清醒了一些，所以那眼泉被叫作居寤清水。他继续往前走，到了当艺野，说：我的心本来总想在天空中飞翔，可现在我的脚却走不动了，变得蹒跚踉跄。所以那里叫作当艺。他又拄着手杖慢慢地走，那段坡就叫杖衡坂。到了尾津岬的一棵孤松下，他发现以前在这里吃饭时忘下的一把刀，竟然还在原处。他唱道：尾津岬那棵孤松啊，我的兄长，你若是人，我就给你佩上刀，给你穿上衣服。到了三重村，他说：我的腿像三重弯曲的年糕一样，累极了。所以那里叫作三重。书中一路记下他留下的地名，像是记下了他越来越沉重的脚步。当艺在今天的岐阜一带，尾津岬在伊势的桑名附近，据说今天三重县的名字也与这里有关。那棵孤松下的刀，原来一直在等着它的主人。

*Coming down the mountain, he rested by the clear spring of Tamakurabe and his mind cleared a little, so the spring is called the Awakening Spring. He went on to the moor of Tagi and said: 'My heart always longed to fly through the sky, but now my feet will not walk; they stagger.' So the place is called Tagi. Leaning on a staff he walked slowly on, and the slope is called the Staff Slope. At the single pine on Cape Otsu he found a sword he had left there once while eating, still lying where he had left it, and he sang: single pine of Cape Otsu, my elder brother — if you were a man, I would gird you with a sword and clothe you. At the village of Mie he said: 'My legs are like a rice cake bent three times; I am utterly weary.' So the place is called Mie. The text records the place-names he left behind, as if marking his ever heavier steps. Tagi lies in present-day Gifu and Cape Otsu near Kuwana in Ise; the name of Mie Prefecture is said to come from this place. The sword under the lone pine had been waiting all along for its master.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. LXXIX–XC (kj086–kj097) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第八十九节。</sub>

### 12. 思国之歌与白鸟 / The songs of home and the white bird

到了能烦野，倭建命思念故乡，唱道：大和是国中最好的地方，层层叠叠的青山像垣墙一样环绕，大和啊，多么美好。又唱：愿那些性命完好的人，把平群山上的熊白梼叶插在头上。又唱：啊，从我家那边升起的云啊。病势沉重的时候，他唱了最后一首歌：我把那把大刀留在了少女的床边——那把刀啊！唱完就死了。使者把消息报告给京城。他的妃子和孩子们赶来，在那里修了陵墓，在周围的稻田里爬来爬去，哭着唱歌。这时，倭建命化作一只八寻长的白鸟，飞上天空，向海边飞去。妃子和孩子们在后面追，赤脚被竹子的残茬划破也不觉得痛，一边追一边唱。书中说，这四首歌都在他的葬礼上唱过，所以直到今天，天皇的大葬还唱这些歌。白鸟飞到河内的志几停了下来，人们在那里修了陵墓，叫作白鸟御陵。可是白鸟又从那里飞上天空，飞走了。

*At the moor of Nobo, longing for his home, he sang: Yamato is the best of lands; the green mountains fold round it like a fence; Yamato, how lovely. And: let those whose lives are whole put leaves of the bear-oak of Mount Heguri in their hair. And: ah, the clouds rising from the direction of my home. When his sickness grew grave he sang a last song: by the maiden's bed I left my great sword — that sword! — and when he had sung it he died. Messengers carried the news to the capital. His consorts and children came down, made a tomb there, and crawled about the rice fields around it, weeping and singing. Then Yamato Takeru turned into a great white bird eight fathoms long, rose into the sky and flew towards the shore. His consorts and children followed, their bare feet cut on bamboo stubble, feeling no pain, singing as they went. The text says these four songs were all sung at his funeral, and so they are sung at the great funerals of the Heavenly Sovereigns to this day. The white bird stopped at Shiki in Kawachi, and a tomb was made there called the Tomb of the White Bird. But from there it rose into the sky again and flew away.*

### 1. 天地初开 / When heaven and earth began

《古事记》的正文从天地开辟写起。它说：天地刚刚分开的时候，在高天原上出现了三位神：天之御中主神、高御产巢日神和神产巢日神。张伯伦把后两个名字分别译作“高贵而能生成万物的奇妙之神”和“神圣而能生成万物的奇妙之神”。这三位神都是独自出现的，出现以后就隐去了身形，书中没有讲他们的样子，也没有讲他们做了什么。那时候，下面的国土还很年轻，像漂在水上的油脂一样，像水母一样随波漂流，没有一处是固定的。就在这时，有一样东西像芦苇的嫩芽一样萌发出来，从中又出现了两位神：宇摩志阿斯诃备比古迟神和天之常立神。这五位神被称为“别天神”，也就是与后来诸神不同的、特别的天神，他们同样是独自出现、随即隐去身形的神。其中的高御产巢日神和神产巢日神，在后面的故事里还会一次次出现：一位主持高天原上的商议，一位把死去的大国主救活。故事就从这样一个漂浮不定、还没有固定下来的世界开始。

*The Kojiki proper opens at the time when heaven and earth first began. In the Plain of High Heaven there came into being three deities: Ame-no-minaka-nushi, Takami-musubi and Kami-musubi, the last two of whom Chamberlain renders the High-August-Producing-Wondrous Deity and the Divine-Producing-Wondrous Deity. All three came into being alone and hid their persons; the text says nothing of how they looked or what they did. The land below was still young, like floating oil, drifting about like a jellyfish, with nothing yet fixed. Then, from something that sprouted up like a reed-shoot, two more deities came into being, Umashi-ashi-kabi-hiko-ji and Ame-no-toko-tachi, who likewise came alone and hid their persons. These five are set apart as the separate Heavenly Deities. Takami-musubi and Kami-musubi will return again and again: the one presiding over the councils of High Heaven, the other bringing the dead Ōkuninushi back to life. The story begins in a world still afloat and not yet made firm.*

### 2. 神世七代 / The seven divine generations

接着出现的是国之常立神和丰云野神，他们也是独自出现、隐去身形的神。此后诸神开始成对出现：宇比地迩神与须比智迩神，角杙神与活杙神，意富斗能地神与大斗乃辨神，於母陀流神与阿夜诃志古泥神，最后是伊邪那岐神与伊邪那美神。张伯伦把这最后两个名字译作“邀请之男神”和“邀请之女神”：名字里的“伊邪那”有邀请、招呼的意思，“岐”和“美”分别表示男和女。从国之常立神到伊邪那岐、伊邪那美，被称为“神世七代”：前面两位独自出现的神各算一代，后面每一对男女神合算一代。这些名字的意思并不都清楚，有的像在说泥土和沙，有的像在说木桩，有的像在说宽大的门户，有的像在说完满的面貌和敬畏的言辞，像是一步步描写大地成形、万物有了模样的过程，但书中并没有讲他们做了什么。真正开始动手造国的，是最后出现的这一对兄妹神。

*Next came into being Kuni-no-toko-tachi and Toyo-kumo-nu, who also came alone and hid their persons. After them the deities came in pairs: U-hiji-ni and Su-hiji-ni, Tsunu-guhi and Iku-guhi, Oho-to-no-ji and Oho-to-no-be, Omo-daru and Aya-kashiko-ne, and last of all Izanagi and Izanami, whom Chamberlain renders the Male-Who-Invites and the Female-Who-Invites: izana- carries the sense of inviting, and -ki and -mi mark male and female. From Kuni-no-toko-tachi to Izanami are called the Seven Divine Generations: the two single deities count a generation each, and each pair counts as one. The meanings of the names are not all clear; some seem to speak of mud and sand, of posts, of great doorways, of a perfect face and of awe, as if the land were slowly taking shape, but the text does not say what these deities did. The work of making the land falls to the last pair.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第二节。</sub>

### 3. 淤能碁吕岛 / The island of Onogoro

天上的众神一齐命令伊邪那岐和伊邪那美：去把这片漂浮的国土整治好，使它坚固，把它生出来。众神的命令里有三件事——整治、巩固、生出——后面的故事，正是一步步完成这三件事。众神赐给他们一支装饰着美玉的长矛，叫作天之沼矛。两位神站在天之浮桥上，把长矛伸进下面的海水里，“咕噜咕噜”地搅动起来——这是书中模仿搅动海水的声音。他们把长矛提起来，从矛尖滴下的海水层层堆积，变成了一座岛，这就是淤能碁吕岛。张伯伦把这个名字译作“自凝岛”，意思是“自己凝结而成的岛”。这是天地间第一块坚固的土地。淤能碁吕岛究竟在哪里，书中没有说，后世有许多说法，都只是推测。两位神从天之浮桥上走下来，降落到这座岛上。造国的工作，就要从这里开始了。

*All the heavenly deities commanded Izanagi and Izanami to make, consolidate and give birth to the drifting land — three tasks that the rest of the story works through one by one — and granted them a jewelled spear, the Ame-no-nuhoko. Standing on the Floating Bridge of Heaven, the two pushed the spear down and stirred the brine until it went curdle-curdle, the text imitating the sound. When they drew the spear up, the brine that dripped from its point piled up and became an island: Onogoro, which Chamberlain renders Self-Curdling Island. It was the first firm ground. Where Onogoro lies the text does not say; later identifications are only conjecture. The two deities descended from the Floating Bridge onto the island, and from there the making of the land began.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第三节。</sub>

### 4. 天之御柱与水蛭子 / The heavenly pillar and the leech-child

两位神在岛上竖起了一根天之御柱，又建起一座八寻殿——“寻”是两臂张开的长度，八寻就是极言其宽大。伊邪那岐问伊邪那美：你的身体是怎样长成的？伊邪那美说：我的身体长成了，只有一处没有长合。伊邪那岐说：我的身体也长成了，只有一处长得多余。我想用多余的地方填补你没有长合的地方，生出国土，你看怎样？伊邪那美说：好。伊邪那岐说：那么我们绕着天之御柱走，相遇以后结为夫妻。你从右边绕，我从左边绕。两人绕柱相遇时，伊邪那美先开口说：啊，多么好的男子！伊邪那岐接着说：啊，多么好的少女！说完他又说：女子先开口是不吉利的。可他们还是结合了，生下的第一个孩子是水蛭子。书中没有描写他的样子，只说他们把这个孩子放进芦苇编的小船里，任它漂走了。接着又生了淡岛，也不算在他们的孩子之中。

*On the island they set up a heavenly pillar and built a hall eight fathoms wide. Izanagi asked Izanami how her body was formed. She answered that it was fully formed except for one place that had not grown together; he said his was fully formed except for one place that had grown to excess, and proposed to fill her place with his and so give birth to lands. She agreed. 'Then let us walk round this heavenly pillar,' he said, 'and meet and be joined. You go round from the right, I from the left.' When they met, Izanami spoke first: 'Ah, what a fair and lovely youth!' Then Izanagi: 'Ah, what a fair and lovely maiden!' Afterwards he said it was unlucky for the woman to speak first. Yet they came together, and their first child was a leech-child, whom they put into a boat of reeds and let float away. Next they bore the island of Aha, which also was not reckoned among their children.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第四节。</sub>

### 5. 重新绕柱，生下大八岛 / The Great Eight Islands

两位神商量说：我们生的孩子不好，应该去问问天上的众神。他们一起回到天上，请求众神的指示。天上的众神用太占——一种烧鹿肩骨看裂纹的占卜——占了一卦，说：因为女子先开口，所以不好。回去重新说一遍吧。两人回到岛上，像以前一样绕着天之御柱走，这一次伊邪那岐先说：啊，多么好的少女！伊邪那美再说：啊，多么好的男子！这样结合以后，他们生下的是一个个岛屿：先是淡路岛；接着是伊予之二名岛，它一个身体有四张脸，每张脸都有名字：伊予国叫爱比卖，赞岐国叫饭依比古，粟国叫大宜都比卖，土左国叫建依别；然后是隐岐岛；筑紫岛也是一个身体四张脸，分别是筑紫国、丰国、肥国和熊曾国；再往后是壹岐岛、对马岛、佐渡岛，最后是大倭丰秋津岛，也就是本州。因为这八个岛最先生出，所以这片土地被称为“大八岛国”。之后他们又生下了六个小岛。

*The two took counsel: 'The children we have borne are not good. We should report this before the heavenly deities.' They went up together, and the heavenly deities divined by grand divination — reading the cracks in a burned deer's shoulder-blade — and said: 'It was not good because the woman spoke first. Go down again and say it anew.' So they walked round the pillar as before, and this time Izanagi spoke first. Then they gave birth to islands: Awaji first; then Iyo-no-futana, one body with four faces, each named — Iyo called Ehime, Sanuki called Iiyori-hiko, Aha called Ōgetsu-hime and Tosa called Takeyori-wake; then Oki; then Tsukushi, likewise one body with four faces, the lands of Tsukushi, Toyo, Hi and Kumaso; then Iki, Tsushima, Sado, and last Ō-yamato-toyo-aki-tsu-shima. Because these eight islands were born first, the country is called the Land of the Eight Great Islands. After them they bore six smaller islands.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第五节。</sub>

### 6. 众神诞生 / The birth of the deities

生完了国土，伊邪那岐和伊邪那美又接着生神。书中把这些神的名字一个个记下来：大事忍男神，石土毘古神，石巢比卖神，大户日别神，天之吹男神，大屋毘古神，风木津别之忍男神；然后是海神大绵津见神，河口之神速秋津日子神和速秋津比卖神——这一对河口之神又生下了沫那艺、沫那美、颊那艺、颊那美等掌管水沫与水面的神；接着是风神志那都比古神，树木之神久久能智神，山神大山津见神，原野之神鹿屋野比卖神，又名野椎神，大山津见神和野椎神又生下了掌管土地、雾气、山谷的许多神。他们还生下了一位叫作“鸟之石楠船”的神，又叫天鸟船，是诸神乘坐的船；又生下了掌管食物的大宜都比卖。有的名字一长串，读起来就像是在点数天地间的万物：石头、门户、屋顶、海浪、水沫、雾气、山谷……世界就这样一样样有了自己的神灵。可是，在这一连串的诞生之后，等待着他们的却是一场灾祸。

*Having borne the lands, Izanagi and Izanami went on to bear deities, and the text records each name: Oho-koto-oshi-wo, Iha-tsuchi-biko, Iha-su-hime, Oho-to-hi-wake, Ame-no-fuki-wo, Oho-ya-biko, Kaza-mo-tsu-wake-no-oshi-wo; the great sea deity Oho-wata-tsu-mi; the river-mouth deities Haya-aki-tsu-hiko and Haya-aki-tsu-hime, who in turn bore deities of foam and of the water's surface; the wind deity Shina-tsu-hiko, the tree deity Kuku-no-chi, the great mountain deity Oho-yama-tsu-mi and the moor goddess Kaya-nu-hime, also called Nu-dzu-chi, who together bore deities of earth, mist and valleys. They bore the Bird's Rock-Camphor-Boat, also called the Heavenly Bird-Boat, and the food goddess Oho-ge-tsu-hime. Some names are very long, so that the list reads like a counting of everything in heaven and earth: rocks, doorways, roofs, waves, foam, mist, valleys. So, thing by thing, the world received its deities. But at the end of this long run of births a calamity was waiting.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第六节。</sub>

### 7. 火神之火 / The fire deity

最后，伊邪那美生下了火之迦具土神，也叫火之夜艺速男神、火之炫毘古神。因为生下了火神，她的阴部被烧伤，从此一病不起。她在病中呕吐，从呕吐物中生出了金山毘古神和金山毘卖神，也就是金属之神；从粪便中生出了波迩夜须毘古神和波迩夜须毘卖神，也就是黏土之神；从尿中生出了水神弥都波能卖神，以及和久产巢日神，和久产巢日神的女儿就是掌管食物的丰宇气毘卖神。即使在病痛之中，她的身体也还在不停地生出新的神来：金属、黏土、水和五谷，都是从这位垂死的母神身上来的。可是终于，伊邪那美因为生下火神而离开了人世——《古事记》用的说法是“神避”，也就是神的隐去。书中总结说，伊邪那岐与伊邪那美一起生下的岛屿共十四个，神共三十五位。创造了国土和万物之神的母亲，就这样因为生下火而死去，这是《古事记》里第一次出现死亡。

*Last of all Izanami bore the fire deity Kagutsuchi, also called Hi-no-yagi-haya-wo and Hi-no-kaga-biko. In bearing this child her private parts were burned, and she lay down sick. From her vomit were born Kana-yama-biko and Kana-yama-bime, deities of metal; from her dung, Hani-yasu-biko and Hani-yasu-bime, deities of clay; from her urine, the water goddess Mitsu-ha-no-me and Waku-musubi, whose daughter is Toyo-uke-bime, goddess of food. Even in sickness her body went on bringing forth deities: metal, clay, water and grain all came from the dying mother. But at last, because she had borne the fire deity, Izanami divinely retired — the Kojiki's word for her passing away. The text sums up that together the pair had borne fourteen islands and thirty-five deities. So the mother of the lands and their deities dies from giving birth to fire; it is the first death in the Kojiki.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第六节。</sub>

### 8. 哀哭与斩杀火神 / Mourning and the slaying of the fire deity

伊邪那岐悲痛地说：我亲爱的妹妹啊，我怎么能用你换来这一个孩子！他匍匐在伊邪那美的枕边和脚边痛哭，从他的眼泪里生出了一位女神，叫作泣泽女神，住在香山下的畝尾木本。伊邪那岐把伊邪那美安葬在出云国与伯伎国交界的比婆山上。接着，他拔出佩带的十拳剑，砍下了儿子迦具土神的头。剑尖上溅出的血落在岩石上，生出了石拆神、根拆神和石筒之男神；剑锷附近的血溅出来，生出了甕速日神、樋速日神和建御雷之男神，也就是后来降服出云的建御雷神；从剑柄上滴下、从指缝间漏出的血，又生出了暗淤加美神和暗御津羽神两位山谷之水的神。从迦具土神被砍杀的身体各处——头、胸、腹、阴部、左右手、左右脚——又生出了八位山神。伊邪那岐用来斩杀火神的这把剑，名叫天之尾羽张，又名伊都之尾羽张。

*Izanagi cried: 'Oh my beloved younger sister! That I should have exchanged you for this single child!' He crept weeping round her pillow and her feet, and from his tears was born the goddess Naki-sawa-me, who dwells at the foot of the trees on the hill of Unewo by Mount Kagu. He buried Izanami on Mount Hiba on the border of Izumo and Hahaki. Then he drew the ten-grasp sword at his side and cut off the head of his son Kagutsuchi. The blood on the point of the sword splashed on the rocks and became Iha-saku, Ne-saku and Iha-tsutsu-no-wo; the blood near the guard became Mika-haya-bi, Hi-haya-bi and Take-mika-dzu-chi-no-wo, the Takemikazuchi who will later bring Izumo to submission; the blood dripping from the hilt through his fingers became Kura-okami and Kura-mitsu-ha, deities of the valley waters. From Kagutsuchi's head, chest, belly, loins, hands and feet eight mountain deities were born. The sword was named Ame-no-wo-ha-bari, also Itsu-no-wo-ha-bari.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第七至八节。</sub>

### 9. 黄泉：不要看我 / Yomi: do not look at me

伊邪那岐思念妻子，追到了黄泉国。伊邪那美从宫殿紧闭的门里出来迎接他，伊邪那岐就隔着门对她说：我亲爱的妹妹啊，我和你一起造的国土还没有完成，请你回来吧！伊邪那美说：可惜你没有早一点来。我已经吃了黄泉灶火煮的食物——这句话的意思是，她已经属于黄泉了。不过，既然我亲爱的哥哥特意来了，我想回去。我要去和黄泉的神商量一下。在这期间，请你千万不要看我。她说完回到殿里，过了很久都没有出来。伊邪那岐等不下去了，他折下插在左边发髻上的多齿梳子的一根边齿，点起火来，走进殿里察看。只见伊邪那美的身上爬满了蛆虫，八位雷神盘踞在她身上：头上是大雷，胸口是火雷，腹部是黑雷，阴部是拆雷，左手是若雷，右手是土雷，左脚是鸣雷，右脚是伏雷。这就是后来故事里所说的“黄泉八雷神”。

*Wishing to see his wife again, Izanagi followed her to the Land of Yomi. She came out to meet him from the closed door of the palace. 'My beloved younger sister,' he said, 'the lands that you and I were making are not yet finished. Come back!' She answered: 'Alas that you did not come sooner! I have eaten of the food of the furnace of Yomi. Yet since my beloved elder brother has come, I wish to return; I will discuss it with the deities of Yomi. Do not look at me.' She went back in and stayed so long that he could not wait. He broke off an end tooth of the comb in the left bunch of his hair, lit it, and went in. Maggots were swarming over her, and eight thunder deities had been born on her body: Great-Thunder on her head, Fire-Thunder on her breast, Black-Thunder on her belly, Crack-Thunder below, Young-Thunder on her left hand, Earth-Thunder on her right, Sounding-Thunder on her left foot and Reclining-Thunder on her right.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第九节。</sub>

### 10. 黄泉丑女与三个桃子 / The Ugly Females of Yomi and the three peaches

伊邪那岐看见这副情景，吓得转身就逃。伊邪那美说：你让我蒙受了羞辱！她派黄泉丑女去追赶他。伊邪那岐取下头上的黑色发饰扔在身后，发饰立刻变成了葡萄，丑女们停下来捡葡萄吃；吃完了又追上来，他又折下右边发髻上的梳子扔出去，梳子变成了竹笋，丑女们又停下来拔竹笋吃。伊邪那美又派八位雷神，率领一千五百名黄泉的军队来追。伊邪那岐拔出十拳剑，一边在身后挥舞，一边逃跑。追到黄泉比良坂——黄泉与人间交界的山坡——的坡脚下时，他摘下长在那里的三个桃子，向追兵投去，追兵全都逃回去了。伊邪那岐对桃子说：你们帮助了我，以后苇原中国的人们遇到苦难的时候，你们也要像这样帮助他们。他给桃子取名意富加牟豆美命。

*Terrified at the sight, Izanagi turned and fled. 'You have put me to shame!' cried Izanami, and sent the Ugly Females of Yomi after him. He threw down his black head-dress, which turned into grapes, and while they stopped to eat he fled on; when they came after him again he broke the comb from the right bunch of his hair and threw it, and it turned into bamboo shoots, which they pulled up and ate. Then she sent the eight thunder deities with fifteen hundred warriors of Yomi. He drew his ten-grasp sword and fled brandishing it behind him. At the foot of the Even Pass of Yomi, the slope between Yomi and the living world, he took three peaches growing there and smote the pursuers with them, and they all fled back. He said to the peaches: 'As you have helped me, so help the people of the Central Land of Reed-Plains when they are in trouble,' and named them Oho-kamu-dzu-mi.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第九节。</sub>

### 11. 千引石前的诀别 / Farewell at the thousand-draught rock

最后，伊邪那美亲自追来了。伊邪那岐搬来一块要一千个人才拉得动的大石头——千引石，堵住了黄泉比良坂。两位神隔着石头面对面站着，说出了诀别的话，书中称之为“度事户”，一般理解为宣告断绝夫妻关系的话。伊邪那美说：我亲爱的哥哥，既然你这样做，我就每天把你国家的人勒死一千个。伊邪那岐回答：我亲爱的妹妹，如果你那样做，我就每天建起一千五百座产房。所以，世上每天必定有一千人死去，也必定有一千五百人出生。从此生与死被这块石头隔开，而活着的人总是比死去的多。伊邪那美从此被称为黄泉津大神，又因为她追上了伊邪那岐，也被叫作道敷大神。堵住黄泉坡的那块石头，被称为道反之大神，也叫塞坐黄泉户大神。书中说，所谓黄泉比良坂，就是现在出云国的伊赋夜坂。

*Last of all Izanami herself came in pursuit. Izanagi pulled up a rock that would take a thousand men to lift and blocked the Even Pass of Yomi with it. Standing face to face on either side of the rock, they spoke the words of parting, which the text calls the exchange of the door of words, usually understood as a declaration ending the marriage. Izanami said: 'My beloved elder brother, if you do this, I will strangle a thousand of the people of your land every day.' Izanagi answered: 'My beloved younger sister, if you do that, I will set up fifteen hundred birth-houses every day.' That is why a thousand people surely die every day and fifteen hundred are surely born: the rock divides life from death, and the living always outnumber the dead. Izanami was thenceforth called the Great Deity of Yomi, and also the Road-Reaching Great Deity because she had overtaken him. The rock is called the Great Deity of the Road-Turning-Back. The Even Pass of Yomi, the text says, is the pass of Ifuya in Izumo.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第九节。</sub>

### 12. 禊祓与三贵子 / Purification and the three noble children

伊邪那岐说：我去了一个丑恶污秽的国家，必须洗净我的身体。他来到筑紫日向的橘之小门的阿波岐原，在河口举行禊祓。他把手杖、腰带、袋子、衣服、裤子、帽子和左右手的手镯一件件扔掉，每扔下一件，就生出一位神，从手杖生出的是冲立船户神，一共生出了十二位神。接着他说：上游水太急，下游水太缓。就走进中游的水里洗身，先生出了由黄泉污秽而来的八十祸津日神和大祸津日神，又生出了纠正灾祸的神直毘神、大直毘神和伊豆能卖神，然后在水底、水中、水面洗涤，生出了三位绵津见神和三位筒之男神。书中说，三位绵津见神是阿昙连一族祭祀的祖神，三位筒之男神是墨江的三位大神。最后，他洗左眼时生出了天照大神，洗右眼时生出了月读命，洗鼻子时生出了建速须佐之男命。故事从这里接到下一部：三位尊贵的孩子即将各自领受父亲的命令。

*'I have been to a hideous and polluted land,' said Izanagi, 'so I must purify my body.' He came to the plain of Ahagi by the river-mouth of Tachibana in Himuka in Tsukushi and performed purification. As he threw down his staff, girdle, bag, garment, trousers, hat and the bracelets of each arm, a deity was born of each — from the staff, Tsuki-tatsu-funa-do — twelve in all. Then, saying that the upper waters were too swift and the lower too sluggish, he went down into the middle reach to wash. First were born the two deities of calamity, Ya-so-maga-tsu-bi and Oho-maga-tsu-bi, from the pollution of Yomi; then Kamu-nahobi, Oho-nahobi and Idzu-no-me to set the calamity right; then, as he washed at the bottom, in the middle and on the surface of the water, three Wata-tsu-mi deities and three Tsutsu-no-wo deities. The text adds that the three Wata-tsu-mi are the ancestral deities worshipped by the Adzumi clan, and the three Tsutsu-no-wo the three great deities of Suminoe. Last, as he washed his left eye, Amaterasu was born; his right eye, Tsukuyomi; his nose, Take-haya-Susanoo. The story passes here to the next part, where the three noble children receive their father's charge.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. I–X (kj008–kj017) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十节。</sub>

## 大宜都比卖 · Ōgetsuhime

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.japanese.iwato_orochi](../story.japanese.iwato_orochi.md)

> *Food goddess killed by Susanoo after his expulsion; silkworms, rice, millet, beans and barley grow from her body (Kojiki, Sect. XVII).*

**长篇故事 · 15 章**

目录：01 三位尊贵的孩子 · 02 哭干了青山的神 · 03 披甲的姐姐 · 04 天安河边的誓约 · 05 谁赢了誓约 · 06 高天原的胡闹 · 07 天地陷入长夜 · 08 河滩上的众神大会 · 09 挂满宝物的杨桐树 · 10 天宇受卖命的舞蹈 · 11 门缝里的光 · 12 放逐与五谷的起源 · 13 河上漂来的筷子 · 14 八坛烈酒与草薙剑 · 15 须贺的宫殿与八云之歌

### 1. 三位尊贵的孩子 / Three illustrious children

伊邪那岐从黄泉回来，在河里洗去污秽。他洗左眼时，生出了天照大神；洗右眼时，生出了月读；洗鼻子时，生出了建速须佐之男。伊邪那岐非常欢喜，说：我生了许多孩子，最后得到了三个尊贵的孩子。他摘下颈上的玉串，摇得叮当作响，交给天照大神，命她统治高天原；又命月读统治夜之国；命须佐之男统治大海。三位神各自领受了父亲的命令，去治理分给自己的地方——只有一位没有去。

*Returning from Yomi, Izanagi washed away its pollution in a river. As he washed his left eye, Amaterasu was born; as he washed his right eye, Tsukuyomi; as he washed his nose, Takehaya-Susanoo. Rejoicing that at the end of his begetting he had gained three illustrious children, Izanagi took off his necklace of jewels, shook it so that it jingled, and gave it to Amaterasu, charging her to rule the Plain of High Heaven. He charged Tsukuyomi with the dominion of the night, and Susanoo with the plain of the sea. Each went to rule as charged — all but one.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十一节。</sub>

### 2. 哭干了青山的神 / The god who wept the mountains dry

须佐之男不肯去治理大海。他一直哭，哭到胡子长了八拳那么长垂到胸前，还在哭。他哭得青山枯萎，河流和大海全都干涸。于是恶神的声音像五月的苍蝇一样到处嗡嗡作响，各种灾祸一齐发生。伊邪那岐问他：你为什么不治理我交给你的地方，却在这里哭个不停？须佐之男回答：我哭，是因为想去母亲所在的根之坚洲国。伊邪那岐大怒，说：既然如此，你就不许再住在这个国家！就把他赶走了。

*Susanoo would not rule the sea. He wept and howled until his beard, eight hands long, reached down over his breast. His weeping withered the green mountains and dried up all the rivers and seas, so that the voices of evil deities swarmed like summer flies and every kind of calamity arose. Izanagi asked why he wept instead of ruling the land entrusted to him. Susanoo answered that he wept because he wished to go to his mother's land, the Nether Distant Land. In great anger Izanagi said that he should not dwell in this land, and expelled him.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十二节。</sub>

### 3. 披甲的姐姐 / The sister in armour

须佐之男说：那么我先去向姐姐天照大神告别，然后再走。他向天上走去，山川都震动起来，大地都摇晃了。天照大神听到动静，大吃一惊，说：我弟弟上来，一定没安好心，他是想夺走我的国家。她立刻解开头发，绾成男子的发髻，在发髻上、发鬘上和左右手腕上都缠上长长的勾玉串，背上装着一千支箭的箭囊，胸前挂着装着五百支箭的箭囊，手臂上戴着护腕，挥舞着弓。她用力踏地，双腿陷到大腿那么深，把泥土像雪花一样踢散，威风凛凛地站着等他，大声问：你上来做什么？

*Susanoo said he would first take leave of his elder sister Amaterasu. As he went up to Heaven the mountains and rivers shook and every land quaked. Amaterasu, alarmed, said her brother could only be coming to take her land. She undid her hair and bound it up in the manner of a man, wound strings of curved jewels on her bunches of hair, her head-dress and both arms, slung on her back a quiver of a thousand arrows and at her side a quiver of five hundred, put on an elbow-pad and brandished her bow. She stamped till her legs sank into the hard earth to the thighs, kicked the soil about like rotten snow, and stood waiting to demand why he had come.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十三节。</sub>

### 4. 天安河边的誓约 / The oath by the Tranquil River

须佐之男说：我没有坏心。父亲问我为什么哭，我说想去母亲的国家，父亲就把我赶走了。我只是来向你告别的。天照大神问：怎么证明你的心是清白的？须佐之男说：我们各自立誓生孩子吧。两人隔着天安河站定。天照大神先拿过须佐之男佩带的十拳剑，把它折成三段，在天之真名井里涮得叮当作响，放进嘴里嚼碎，吹出的气雾中生出了三位女神。接着须佐之男拿过天照大神缠在头发和手臂上的勾玉串，同样在井里涮洗、嚼碎、吹气，从雾气中生出了五位男神。

*Susanoo replied that he had no evil intent: his father had expelled him and he had only come to say farewell. Asked how he could prove the purity of his heart, he proposed that each should swear and produce children. Standing on either side of the Tranquil River of Heaven, Amaterasu first took his ten-grasp sabre, broke it into three, rinsed the pieces jingling in the True-Pool-Well of Heaven, crunched them and blew out a misty breath, from which three goddesses were born. Then Susanoo took the strings of curved jewels from her hair and arms, rinsed, crunched and blew in the same way, and five male deities were born.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十三节。</sub>

### 5. 谁赢了誓约 / Who won the oath

天照大神宣布：后来生的五位男神，是从我的东西里生出来的，所以是我的孩子；先生的三位女神，是从你的东西里生出来的，所以是你的孩子。须佐之男却说：因为我的心是清白的，所以我生出了温柔的女孩子。这样说来，当然是我赢了。他自以为得胜，得意忘形起来。（这五位男神中的长子，后来成了天皇家的祖先；三位女神则成为宗像大社供奉的神。）

*Amaterasu declared that the five male deities, born from her jewels, were her children, and the three goddesses, born from his sabre, were his. But Susanoo said that because his heart was pure he had begotten gentle maidens, and so of course he had won. Puffed up with his victory, he grew reckless. (The eldest of the five male deities becomes, later in the Kojiki, the ancestor of the imperial line; the three goddesses are those worshipped at Munakata.)*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十四节。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：括号里关于皇室祖先与宗像三女神的说明见于《古事记》的同一段落及后文，属于文本自身的谱系说明。</sub>

### 6. 高天原的胡闹 / Riot in the Plain of High Heaven

仗着这场“胜利”，须佐之男在高天原胡作非为。他踏坏天照大神水田的田埂，填平灌溉的水沟，又在她品尝新谷的神殿里撒下粪便。天照大神却替他辩解：那像粪便的东西，是我弟弟喝醉了吐出来的；他填平水沟、踏坏田埂，是舍不得那些土地白白空着。可须佐之男的恶行不但没有停止，反而更加厉害。有一天，天照大神正坐在神圣的织殿里，让织女们为众神织造衣裳。须佐之男在织殿的屋顶上捅开一个洞，把一匹从尾巴倒着剥了皮的天之斑马扔了进去。织女吓得跳起来，被梭子刺中下身，就这样死了。

*On the strength of his victory Susanoo ran riot. He broke down the divisions of Amaterasu's rice fields, filled in the ditches, and strewed excrement in the palace where she tasted the first fruits. Amaterasu excused him: what looked like excrement was what her brother had vomited when drunk, and he had filled the ditches and broken the ridges only because he grudged the land lying idle. But his wickedness grew worse. While Amaterasu sat in her sacred weaving hall seeing to the weaving of the deities' garments, he broke a hole in the roof and flung down a heavenly piebald horse flayed backwards; a heavenly weaving maiden, startled, struck herself with her shuttle and died.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十五节。</sub>

### 7. 天地陷入长夜 / The world falls into night

天照大神看见这一切，感到害怕，就打开天之岩户——天上的岩石屋子的门——走了进去，把门牢牢关上，隐居起来。于是整个高天原一片黑暗，下面的苇原中国也一片黑暗，永远的黑夜笼罩了一切。在这片黑暗里，无数恶神的声音像五月的苍蝇一样到处嗡嗡作响，各种各样的灾祸接连发生。《古事记》对这场灾难只写了寥寥几句，却是神代故事的一个大转折：天照大神是照耀天地的女神，她一躲起来，天上和人间就没有了白天，众神的世界也失去了秩序。接下来的一整段，讲的都是众神如何同心协力，想办法把她请出来。

*Terrified at the sight, Amaterasu opened the door of the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling, went in, made it fast and hid herself. Then the whole Plain of High Heaven was dark, and the Central Land of Reed-Plains below was dark too: eternal night prevailed. In that darkness the voices of the myriad deities swarmed like summer flies, and every kind of calamity arose.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十六节。</sub>

### 8. 河滩上的众神大会 / The assembly in the river-bed

八百万位神聚集在天安河的河滩上开会商议，请高御产巢日神的儿子思金神想办法。依照他的计划，众神先把常世的长鸣鸟聚在一起，让它们一齐啼叫。又从天安河上游取来坚硬的岩石，从天上的金山取来铁，请来锻冶师天津麻罗，命伊斯许理度卖命造一面镜子，命玉祖命做一串长长的、有五百颗勾玉的玉串。他们又请天儿屋命和布刀玉命，从天香山上取来一头雄鹿完整的肩胛骨，用天香山上的桦树皮烧它，进行占卜。

*The eight hundred myriad deities assembled in a divine assembly in the bed of the Tranquil River of Heaven and bade Omoikane, child of Takami-musubi, think of a plan. They gathered the long-singing birds of eternal night and made them sing. They took hard rocks from the river-bed and iron from the Heavenly Metal-Mountains, called the smith Amatsumara, and charged Ishikoridome to make a mirror and Tamanoya to make a long string of five hundred curved jewels. They summoned Ame-no-Koyane and Futotama to pull out the whole shoulder-blade of a true stag of Mount Kagu and perform divination with cherry-bark from the same mountain.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十六节。</sub>

### 9. 挂满宝物的杨桐树 / The sakaki tree hung with treasures

众神把天香山上一棵长满枝叶的真杨桐树连根拔起，在上面的枝条上挂起那串五百颗勾玉，在中间的枝条上挂起那面八咫镜，在下面的枝条上挂起白色和青色的布帛。布刀玉命双手捧着这棵树，作为隆重的供品；天儿屋命念诵庄严的祝词。天手力男神则悄悄藏在岩户门边，等待时机。这些东西在后世都有了特别的意义：镜子和勾玉与后来从大蛇尾中得到的草薙剑，被看作代代相传的三件神圣宝物；注连绳、杨桐枝和祝词，至今仍是神社祭祀中常见的东西。不过在这个故事里，它们只有一个用处——把躲起来的女神请出来。

*They uprooted a five-hundred-branched true sakaki tree from Mount Kagu of Heaven. On its upper branches they hung the long string of five hundred curved jewels; on its middle branches the large mirror; on its lower branches pendant offerings of white and blue cloth. Futotama held these as grand offerings, Ame-no-Koyane recited a solemn liturgy, and Ame-no-Tajikarao stood hidden beside the door.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十六节。</sub>

### 10. 天宇受卖命的舞蹈 / The dance of Ame-no-Uzume

天宇受卖命用天香山的日影蔓束起衣袖，用真拆葛做成头饰，手里握着一束天香山的竹叶，在天之岩户前倒扣一只木桶，在上面用力踏脚，踏得咚咚作响。她跳着跳着，神灵附了体，露出了胸脯，把裙带一直推到了下身。整个高天原都震动起来，八百万位神一齐哈哈大笑。这是整个神话里最热闹、也最奇特的一幕：漆黑一片的天上，众神围着紧闭的岩户，看着一位女神在倒扣的木桶上又踏又跳，笑声震动了天地。后世把天宇受卖命尊为舞蹈与艺能之神，神乐的起源也常被追溯到这一段。

*Ame-no-Uzume bound up her sleeves with heavenly clubmoss from Mount Kagu, made a head-dress of spindle-tree, took in her hand a bundle of bamboo grass from Mount Kagu, and laid an upturned tub before the door of the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling, stamping on it until it resounded. Becoming divinely possessed, she bared her breasts and pushed her skirt-string down to her private parts. The Plain of High Heaven shook, and the eight hundred myriad deities laughed together.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十六节。</sub>

### 11. 门缝里的光 / Light through the door

天照大神觉得奇怪，把岩户的门稍稍打开一条缝，在里面问：我藏了起来，以为高天原会一片黑暗，苇原中国也会一片黑暗，为什么天宇受卖命还在跳舞，八百万位神都在大笑？天宇受卖命回答：因为这里来了一位比您更尊贵的神，所以我们高兴地跳舞欢笑。就在她说话的时候，天儿屋命和布刀玉命把镜子推到天照大神面前。天照大神看到镜中的光，越发惊奇，一点一点地走出门来想看个究竟。藏在门边的天手力男神一把握住她的手，把她拉了出来。布刀玉命立刻在她身后拉起一根注连绳，说：您不能再回到里面去了！天照大神一出来，高天原和苇原中国自然又恢复了光明。

*Amaterasu, wondering, opened the door a crack and asked why, when she had thought Heaven and the Central Land would be dark, Ame-no-Uzume danced and all the deities laughed. Uzume answered that they rejoiced because there was a deity more illustrious than she. While she spoke, Ame-no-Koyane and Futotama pushed the mirror forward to show it to Amaterasu, who, more and more astonished, came gradually out to gaze. Tajikarao, hidden beside the door, took her hand and drew her out, and Futotama stretched a bottom-tied rope behind her, saying she must not go back further in than this. As she came forth, Heaven and the Central Land were lit again.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十六节。</sub>

### 12. 放逐与五谷的起源 / Expulsion and the origin of grain

八百万位神一起商议，罚须佐之男交出一千桌赎罪的供品，剪掉他的胡子，拔掉他手指和脚趾的指甲，把他赶出了高天原。在路上，须佐之男向大宜都比卖讨要食物。大宜都比卖从鼻子、嘴巴和臀部取出各种美味，做成佳肴献给他。须佐之男偷偷看见了，以为她拿污秽的东西给他吃，就把她杀了。从被杀的女神身上长出了各种东西：头上生出蚕，两只眼睛里生出稻种，两只耳朵里生出粟，鼻子里生出小豆，阴部生出麦，臀部生出大豆。神产巢日神让人把这些收起来，当作种子。

*The eight hundred myriad deities took counsel together, fined Susanoo a thousand tables of offerings, cut off his beard and pulled out his finger- and toe-nails, and drove him out with a divine expulsion. Then he asked food of Ōgetsuhime, who took dainties from her nose, mouth and hindquarters and offered them to him. Watching, he thought she was offering him filth, and killed her. From her body things were born: silkworms from her head, rice-seeds from her eyes, millet from her ears, small beans from her nose, barley from her genitals and soy beans from her hindquarters. Kami-musubi had them taken and used as seeds.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十七节。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：《日本书纪》里杀死食物女神的是月读，对象叫保食神；本篇只讲《古事记》的版本。</sub>

### 13. 河上漂来的筷子 / Chopsticks on the river

须佐之男被赶出天界，降落到出云国肥河上游一个叫鸟发的地方。这时他看见河上漂下来一双筷子，心想上游一定有人住，就沿着河往上走。果然遇见一位老人和一位老妇人，两人中间夹着一位少女，正在哭泣。老人说：我是这里的国神大山津见神的儿子，名叫足名椎，妻子叫手名椎，女儿叫栉名田比卖。我们原本有八个女儿，可是高志的八岐大蛇每年都来，吃掉一个。现在又到了它来的时候，所以我们在哭。须佐之男问那条蛇长什么样。老人说：它的眼睛像红色的酸浆果，一个身子上长着八个头、八条尾巴，身上长满了苔藓、桧树和杉树，身长横跨八道山谷、八座山峰，看它的肚子，总是血淋淋地溃烂着。

*Expelled, Susanoo descended to Torikami at the head of the river Hi in Izumo. Seeing chopsticks floating down, he knew people lived upstream and went up the river, where he found an old man and an old woman weeping with a maiden between them. The old man said he was Ashinazuchi, son of the great mountain deity, his wife was Tenazuchi and their daughter Kushinadahime. They had had eight daughters, but the eight-forked serpent of Koshi came every year and devoured one, and now it was time for it to come again. Its eyes, he said, were like red winter-cherries; it had one body with eight heads and eight tails; moss, cypress and cryptomeria grew on its body; its length spanned eight valleys and eight hills; and its belly was always bloody and inflamed.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十八节。</sub>

### 14. 八坛烈酒与草薙剑 / Eight vats of liquor and the Kusanagi sword

须佐之男说：我是天照大神的弟弟，刚从天上下来。你们愿意把女儿嫁给我吗？老夫妇恭恭敬敬地答应了。须佐之男把少女变成一把密齿的梳子，插在自己的发髻上，然后吩咐老夫妇：酿造八次反复酿成的烈酒，再围一道篱笆，篱笆上开八个门，每个门口搭一个台子，每个台子上放一只酒槽，槽里都盛满烈酒。一切准备好后，八岐大蛇果然来了，它把八个头分别伸进八只酒槽里，大口喝酒，喝醉了就趴下睡着了。须佐之男拔出佩带的十拳剑，把大蛇砍成碎块，肥河里流的全成了血。他砍到大蛇中间的那条尾巴时，剑刃崩了个缺口。他觉得奇怪，用剑尖剖开尾巴一看，里面有一把锋利的大刀。他认为这是不同寻常的宝物，就把它献给了天照大神——这就是草薙剑。

*Susanoo revealed that he was the elder brother of Amaterasu, just descended from Heaven, and asked for the maiden; the old couple respectfully agreed. He turned her into a close-toothed comb and stuck it in his hair. Then he told them to brew eightfold-refined liquor, build a fence with eight gates, and at each gate set a platform with a vat full of the liquor. The serpent came as foretold, dipped a head into each vat and drank, then lay down drunk and slept. Susanoo drew his ten-grasp sabre and cut the serpent to pieces, so that the river Hi ran with blood. When he cut its middle tail, the edge of his sabre broke; wondering, he split the tail open with its point and found a great sword. Thinking it a strange thing, he presented it to Amaterasu — the Herb-Quelling sword, Kusanagi.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十八节。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“草薙”这个名字在《古事记》的这一段只作注明；名称由来的故事（日本武尊割草）在后文另有讲述。</sub>

### 15. 须贺的宫殿与八云之歌 / The palace of Suga and the song of eight clouds

之后，须佐之男在出云国寻找建造宫殿的地方。他来到须贺这个地方，说：我来到这里，心里清清爽爽的。就在那里建起了宫殿，所以那地方至今叫做须贺。他在那里建宫时，有云从地上升起，他便作了一首歌：八云立，出云八重垣，为了让妻子住进来，我筑起八重垣——啊，那八重垣！这被认为是日本最早的和歌。他把栉名田比卖的父亲足名椎叫来，任命他做宫殿的长官，并赐给他一个名字：稻田宫主须贺之八耳神。

*Then Susanoo looked for a place in Izumo to build a palace. Arriving at Suga he said that his heart was refreshed, and he built his palace there — which is why the place is still called Suga. As he built it, clouds rose up, and he made a song: Eight clouds arise. The eightfold fence of Izumo makes an eightfold fence for the spouses to retire within. Oh, that eightfold fence! He called Ashinazuchi and made him master of the palace, giving him the name Inada-no-Miyanushi-Suga-no-Yatsumimi.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sect. XI–XIX (kj018–kj026) · 《古事记》张伯伦译本第十九节。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“日本最早的和歌”是后世（如《古今和歌集》序）的说法，不是《古事记》本文的断言。</sub>

## 月读 · Tsukuyomi

### 1. 天浮桥上的长矛 / A spear from the Floating Bridge

*The heavenly deities command Izanagi and Izanami to make firm the drifting land and give them a jewelled spear. Standing on the Floating Bridge of Heaven, they stir the brine; when they lift the spear, the drops from its tip pile up into Onogoro Island.*

<sub>来源 / Source：The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain) · Sections III–X (kj010–kj017) · 张伯伦译本第三节（kj010）。</sub>

### 2. 火神出生 / The birth of the fire deity

*Joined together, the pair bring forth islands and deities. But when Izanami gives birth to the Deity-of-Fire she is burned, falls sick, and at length 'divinely retires' — she dies.*
