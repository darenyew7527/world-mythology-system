## 伏羲 · Fuxi

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.chinese.fuxi_eight_trigrams](../story.chinese.fuxi_eight_trigrams.md)

> 《系辞下》中的包牺氏（伏羲）：仰观天象、俯察地法，始作八卦，又结绳为网。

> *Baoxi (Fuxi) in Xi Ci II: the ancient ruler who observed heaven, earth and living things, first made the eight trigrams and knotted nets.*

### 1. 包牺氏治理天下 / When Baoxi ruled the world

《系辞下》讲古代圣人的发明，第一位就是包牺氏——后世通常写作伏羲。书里说，远古的时候，是他在治理天下。

*Xi Ci II lists the inventions of the ancient sages, and the first is Baoxi — usually written Fuxi in later texts. In remote antiquity, it says, he ruled all under heaven.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Book of Changes, Xi Ci II (Great Commentary, part 2) · Xi Ci II, ch. 2 · 《系辞下》第二章开头。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“包牺”即“伏羲”是传统注疏的读法。</sub>

### 2. 仰观天，俯察地 / Looking up, looking down

他抬头观察天上的日月星辰，低头观察大地的形势；又细看鸟兽身上的花纹，看各地适合生长什么。近处，他从自己的身体取法；远处，他从万物取法。

*He looked up at the images in the sky and down at the patterns of the earth; he studied the markings of birds and beasts and what each land was suited for. Near at hand he drew on his own body, and far off on all things.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Book of Changes, Xi Ci II (Great Commentary, part 2) · Xi Ci II, ch. 2 · 《系辞下》第二章。</sub>

### 3. 画出八卦 / The first eight trigrams

于是他开始画出八卦——八个由三条线组成的符号，用来通达神明的德性，把万物的情状分门别类。

*Thereupon he first made the eight trigrams — eight figures of three lines each — to communicate with spirit-like virtue and to sort the dispositions of the myriad things.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Book of Changes, Xi Ci II (Great Commentary, part 2) · Xi Ci II, ch. 2 · 《系辞下》第二章“于是始作八卦”。</sub>

### 4. 结绳为网 / Cords become nets

他还把绳子打结，做成罗网，人们用它打猎、捕鱼。《系辞》接下来再讲神农等后来的圣人，这里只讲伏羲这一段。

*He also knotted cords into nets, which people used to hunt and fish. The Xi Ci then moves on to later sages such as Shennong; this retelling covers only Baoxi's part.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Book of Changes, Xi Ci II (Great Commentary, part 2) · Xi Ci II, ch. 2 · 《系辞下》第二章“作结绳而为罔罟”。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：后续圣人的发明不在本故事范围内。</sub>

## 共工 · Gonggong

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.chinese.gonggong_buzhou](../story.chinese.gonggong_buzhou.md)

> *Figure who contends with Zhuanxu and strikes Mount Buzhou in the Huainanzi Tian Wen and Liezi Tang Wen witnesses; whether deity, clan head or rival ruler is foregrounded varies by source and is not resolved here.*

### 1. 共工与颛顼争为帝 / Gonggong contends with Zhuanxu

《淮南子·天文训》说，从前共工与颛顼争夺帝位。

*Huainanzi “Tian Wen” says that long ago Gonggong contended with Zhuanxu to become ruler.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Huainanzi, Tian Wen Xun · Huainanzi, Tian Wen Xun — passage opening 昔者共工 · 《淮南子·天文训》“昔者共工”段。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：颛顼在此作为传说中的君主登记，不作历史真实性判断。</sub>

### 2. 怒触不周之山 / Striking Mount Buzhou in anger

共工发怒撞向不周山，支撑天的柱子折断，系住大地的绳索断绝。

*In anger Gonggong strikes Mount Buzhou; the pillar holding up heaven breaks and the cords binding the earth snap.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Huainanzi, Tian Wen Xun · Huainanzi, Tian Wen Xun — passage opening 昔者共工 · 同段不周山、天柱与地维句组。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：不周山不映射为现代山名或坐标。</sub>

### 3. 一段独立的起源解释 / A standalone etiology

结果天向西北倾斜，日月星辰随之移动；地在东南不满，积水与尘埃都归向那里。这一段没有提到女娲，是《天文训》中独立的天地形势解释。

*As a result heaven tilts to the northwest and the sun, moon and stars shift with it; the earth falls short in the southeast, where waters and dust collect. The passage does not mention Nüwa and stands as an independent explanation of cosmic orientation in “Tian Wen”.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Huainanzi, Tian Wen Xun · Huainanzi, Tian Wen Xun — passage opening 昔者共工 · 《淮南子·天文训》共工段结尾。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：同书《览冥训》的女娲段与此段分属不同章节，不合成单一故事。</sub>

## 女娲 · Nüwa

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.chinese.nuwa_repairs_sky](../story.chinese.nuwa_repairs_sky.md)

### 1. 四极废，九州裂 / The four poles collapse

《淮南子·览冥训》以远古为背景：支撑天地的四极崩坏，九州开裂，天不能全覆、地不能全载，大火与洪水都无法止息，猛兽鸷鸟危害百姓。本段只按这一见证的顺序概述，不补入其他文献给出的起因。

*Huainanzi “Lan Ming” sets the scene in remote antiquity: the four poles holding up the world have failed and the nine provinces have split; heaven no longer covers everything and earth no longer bears everything, fires and floods cannot be stopped, and wild beasts and birds of prey attack the people. This section follows only this witness and adds no cause taken from other texts.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun · Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun — passage opening 往古之時 · 《淮南子·览冥训》“往古之时”段；Chinese Text Project 章节页。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：传世本用字存在异体；本站只发布独立概述，不复制整段原文。</sub>

### 2. 炼五色石，断鳌足 / Five-colored stones and the turtle's legs

于是女娲炼五色石来修补苍天，又截断巨鳌的足，用来重新立起四极。

*Nüwa then refines five-colored stones to patch the azure sky and cuts off the legs of a great turtle to set the four poles upright again.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun · Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun — passage opening 往古之時 · 同段女娲补天与立四极的句组。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：炼字与鳌字在不同版本中有异体，不影响本段概述。</sub>

### 3. 杀黑龙，积芦灰 / The black dragon and the reed ash

这一见证还写到女娲杀死黑龙以救济冀州，并堆积芦苇灰来堵住泛滥的洪水；随后天地重新安定，百姓得以存活。

*This witness adds that Nüwa kills a black dragon to relieve Jizhou and piles up reed ash to stop the flood waters; afterwards heaven and earth are set right and the people survive.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun · Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun — passage opening 往古之時 · 同段黑龙、冀州与芦灰句组。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：冀州在此是文本中的九州之一，不映射为现代行政区或坐标；《列子·汤问》对应段落未陈述这两项。</sub>

### 4. 这一段没有写共工 / No Gonggong in this passage

《览冥训》的女娲段落没有把天地崩坏归因于共工。《淮南子》确实在《天文训》另记共工怒触不周山，但那是另一章中独立的天地倾斜起源，本站不把两章拼接成因果链。

*The Lan Ming Nüwa passage does not attribute the collapse to Gonggong. The Huainanzi does narrate Gonggong striking Mount Buzhou, but in a separate passage in the “Tian Wen” chapter that explains the tilt of heaven and earth; the site does not splice the two chapters into a causal chain.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun · Huainanzi, Lan Ming Xun — passage opening 往古之時 · 《览冥训》女娲段与《天文训》共工段分别定位。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：把共工触山写成女娲补天起因的说法见于较晚文献，已作为独立接受层排队。</sub>

## 盘古 · Pangu

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.chinese.pangu_sanwu_liji](../story.chinese.pangu_sanwu_liji.md)

> 徐整《三五历纪》佚文中的盘古：生于如鸡子的混沌之中，天地分开时日长一丈。

> *Pangu as quoted from Xu Zheng's lost Sanwu liji: born in an egg-like chaos, he grows a zhang a day as heaven and earth separate.*

### 1. 混沌如鸡蛋 / Chaos like an egg

很久以前，天和地还没有分开，到处是一团混沌，样子像一个鸡蛋。盘古就生在这团混沌之中。

*Long ago heaven and earth were not yet apart; everything was one chaos shaped like a hen's egg, and Pangu was born inside it.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Yiwen leiju, juan 1 (Heaven, part 1) · juan 1, Heaven · 《艺文类聚》卷一引《三五历纪》；《太平御览》卷一引文字句略异。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：原书已佚，只能依靠类书引文。</sub>

### 2. 清者为天，浊者为地 / Clear above, murky below

过了一万八千年，天地开辟：轻而清的阳气上升成为天，重而浊的阴气下沉成为地。盘古就在天地之间，一天之中变化九次，比天更神，比地更圣。

*After 18,000 years heaven and earth opened: the light, clear yang rose to become heaven, the heavy, murky yin sank to become earth. Pangu stood between them, changing nine times a day, more divine than heaven and wiser than earth.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Yiwen leiju, juan 1 (Heaven, part 1) · juan 1, Heaven · 同一引文“阳清为天，阴浊为地”。</sub>

### 3. 一天长高一丈 / A zhang a day

从此天每天升高一丈，地每天加厚一丈，盘古也每天长高一丈。这样又过了一万八千年，天高到了极点，地深到了极点，盘古也长到了极点。在这之后，才有了三皇。

*From then on heaven rose a zhang each day, earth grew a zhang thicker, and Pangu grew a zhang taller. After another 18,000 years heaven was as high as it could be, earth as deep, and Pangu as tall. Only after that came the Three Sovereigns.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Yiwen leiju, juan 1 (Heaven, part 1) · juan 1, Heaven · 同一引文“天日高一丈……盘古日长一丈”。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“盘古垂死化身为日月山川”出自另一部书，本故事不合并。</sub>

> 《山海经·海内东经》的雷泽无名雷神；与后世雷公不自动合并。

> *Unnamed thunder spirit of Leize in the Classic of Mountains and Seas; not automatically merged with later Leigong.*

### 1. 雷泽中的神 / A spirit in Leize

《山海经·海内东经》的登记段落在雷泽描写一位无名雷神。

*The registered Hai Nei Dong Jing passage describes an unnamed thunder spirit in Leize.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Classic of Mountains and Seas, Hai Nei Dong Jing · Hai Nei Dong Jing · 《山海经·海内东经》。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“雷泽雷神”是数据库定位名，不是文本给出的专名。</sub>

### 2. 击腹发雷 / Thunder from the belly

该段把雷声与神灵击打腹部相连，系统把它保存为文本中的神话叙述。

*The passage connects thunder with the spirit striking its belly, and the system preserves this as a mythic statement in the text.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Classic of Mountains and Seas, Hai Nei Dong Jing · Hai Nei Dong Jing · 《山海经·海内东经》。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：不把神话陈述当作自然科学解释。</sub>

### 3. 与后世雷公分开 / Separate from later Leigong

因为文本没有给出后世雷公的专名与完整形象，系统不按“雷神”这个共通标签自动合并。

*Because the passage does not give the later proper name and full image of Leigong, the system does not merge the figures under the generic label thunder god.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Classic of Mountains and Seas, Hai Nei Dong Jing · Hai Nei Dong Jing · 身份区分研究备注。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：后世接受史将在独立版本中处理。</sub>

## 黄帝 · Yellow Emperor

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.chinese.huangdi_chiyou](../story.chinese.huangdi_chiyou.md)

> 《史记·五帝本纪》中少典之子轩辕，于涿鹿擒杀蚩尤并被尊为天子；本站不作历史真实性断言。

> *Xuanyuan, son of Shaodian, who in the Shiji fights Chiyou at Zhuolu and is honoured as Son of Heaven; no historical-reality claim is made.*

### 1. 神农氏衰落 / Shennong's line declines

黄帝是少典的儿子，姓公孙，名轩辕。他那个时代，神农氏的统治衰落了，诸侯互相攻打、欺压百姓，神农氏却无力征讨。轩辕于是操练军队，去讨伐不肯朝贡的诸侯。

*The Yellow Emperor was the son of Shaodian, surnamed Gongsun and named Xuanyuan. In his time the rule of Shennong had declined; the lords attacked one another and oppressed the people, and Shennong could not punish them. So Xuanyuan trained troops to subdue the lords who would not pay tribute.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Shiji, Annals of the Five Emperors · Wu di ben ji · 《五帝本纪》开篇。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：此前轩辕与炎帝在阪泉的三次交战本故事未展开。</sub>

### 2. 蚩尤作乱 / Chiyou rebels

诸侯之中，蚩尤最为凶暴，没有人能制服他。蚩尤起来作乱，不听从帝王的命令。

*Among the lords Chiyou was the most violent, and no one could subdue him. Chiyou rose in rebellion and would not obey the ruler's command.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Shiji, Annals of the Five Emperors · Wu di ben ji · 《五帝本纪》“蚩尤作乱，不用帝命”。</sub>

### 3. 涿鹿之野 / In the wilds of Zhuolu

轩辕征召各路诸侯的军队，在涿鹿的原野上与蚩尤交战，终于把蚩尤擒住并杀死。

*Xuanyuan called up the armies of the lords and fought Chiyou in the wilds of Zhuolu, and at last captured and killed him.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Shiji, Annals of the Five Emperors · Wu di ben ji · 《五帝本纪》“遂禽杀蚩尤”。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：涿鹿不在本站映射为现代坐标。</sub>

### 4. 尊为天子 / Honoured as Son of Heaven

诸侯们都尊奉轩辕为天子，取代了神农氏，这就是黄帝。

*The lords all honoured Xuanyuan as Son of Heaven in place of Shennong: this was the Yellow Emperor.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Shiji, Annals of the Five Emperors · Wu di ben ji · 《五帝本纪》“诸侯咸尊轩辕为天子”。</sub>
