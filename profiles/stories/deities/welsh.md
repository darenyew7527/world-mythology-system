### 1. 格林·库赫的猎犬 / The hounds at Glyn Cuch

达费德的王子皮威尔统治着七个坎特雷夫的土地。有一天，他在阿伯斯的宫殿里，忽然想去打猎，就来到了格林·库赫，那里是他最喜欢的狩猎场。他放出猎犬，吹响号角，追赶一头鹿，和同伴们走散了。他听见另一群猎犬的叫声，那群猎犬也在追赶一头雄鹿，在一片林间空地上，它们把雄鹿扑倒了。皮威尔看着这群猎犬，大吃一惊：他从没见过这样的猎犬，它们全身雪白，耳朵却是鲜红的，白得发亮，红得发亮。皮威尔赶走了那群陌生的猎犬，让自己的猎犬去吃那头鹿。这时，一个骑着高大灰马的人赶来了，他身穿灰褐色的猎装，脖子上挂着号角。那人说：王子，我认识你，可是我不向你问好，你太无礼了。我从没见过有人像你这样，赶走别人猎犬扑倒的鹿，让自己的猎犬去吃。皮威尔问他是谁。那人说：我是安农的国王阿拉文。皮威尔诚恳地道歉，问怎样才能赎罪，赢得他的友谊。阿拉文说：在我的国土旁边，有一个名叫哈夫甘的国王，他总是和我作对。你如果能替我除掉他，就能赢得我的友谊。

*Pwyll, prince of Dyfed, lord of seven cantrefs, was at Arberth when he wished to hunt and went to Glyn Cuch. Separated from his companions while following a stag, he heard another pack and saw it bring down a stag in a clearing — hounds such as he had never seen, of shining white with shining red ears. He drove them off and set his own hounds on the stag. A rider on a great grey horse came up, dressed in grey-brown hunting garb with a horn at his neck, and said he knew Pwyll but would not greet him, for he had never seen worse discourtesy than to drive off the hounds that had killed the stag and bait one's own upon it. Asked who he was, he said: 'Arawn, a crowned king in Annwn.' Pwyll asked how he might make amends and win his friendship. There was a king called Hafgan whose land bordered his, said Arawn, and who warred against him continually; by ridding him of Hafgan, Pwyll would win his friendship.*

### 2. 在彼世的一年 / A year in the Otherworld

阿拉文说：我会把你变成我的样子，让你代替我在安农做一年的国王，我的王国里没有一个人会认出你，连我的妻子也不会；而我会变成你的样子，替你统治达费德。一年后的今天，我们在这里相见。一年后，你要在浅滩上与哈夫甘决斗，你只能打他一下，他就会死；即使他求你再打第二下，你也绝不能答应，因为如果再打一下，他第二天就会像从前一样恢复过来。皮威尔答应了。阿拉文领着他来到安农的宫殿，那里有华丽的大厅和卧室，满是金银器皿和锦缎衣裳，人们都以为他就是国王，殷勤地侍候他。王后是皮威尔见过的最美丽的女人，她穿着金色的锦缎，与他谈笑，非常和蔼可亲。可是到了夜里，他们上床以后，皮威尔就转过身去，背对着她，一句话也不说，直到天亮。白天，他像国王一样和王后说笑、宴饮，到了夜里，又是一样。就这样，整整一年过去了，每一夜都是如此。皮威尔在安农打猎、宴饮、处理国事，享受着彼世的一切荣华，却始终没有辜负阿拉文的信任。

*Arawn said he would give Pwyll his own shape so that he could reign in Annwn for a year, and no one, not even Arawn's wife, would know him; Arawn would take Pwyll's shape and rule Dyfed. A year from that day they would meet there. At the end of the year Pwyll would meet Hafgan at the ford and must strike him only one blow, which would kill him; though Hafgan begged for a second, he must not give it, for with a second blow Hafgan would be as well as ever next day. Pwyll agreed. In Annwn's palace, with its halls and chambers full of gold vessels and brocaded robes, all took him for the king, and the queen, the fairest woman he had ever seen, in golden brocade, was kind and gracious to him by day. But each night in bed he turned his face to the wall and said not a word till morning, and so it was every night for a whole year; he hunted, feasted and governed in all the splendour of the Otherworld, yet never betrayed Arawn's trust.*

### 3. 浅滩上的一击 / One blow at the ford

约定的那一天到了，皮威尔带着安农的贵族们来到浅滩，哈夫甘也带着他的人马来了。一个骑士站出来宣布：各位贵族，请听好，这是两位国王之间的决斗，事关土地和统治权，其他人都不要插手，让他们两人一决胜负。两位国王驾着马，从浅滩的两边向中间冲去。第一次交锋，皮威尔一枪刺中了哈夫甘的盾牌中心，把盾牌劈成了两半，长矛穿透了他的铠甲，把哈夫甘从马背上掀了下去，一直摔出了一枪一臂的距离，哈夫甘受了致命的伤。哈夫甘说：你有什么权利要我的命？我从没冒犯过你，也不知道你为什么要杀我。可是既然你已经开始了，看在上天的份上，你就结束我的生命吧。皮威尔说：我可能会后悔做我已经做过的事。你去找别人杀你吧，我不会再打你了。哈夫甘对他的贵族们说：抬走我吧，我的末日到了，我再也不能保护你们了。皮威尔对哈夫甘的人说：请你们想一想，谁应该做我的臣民。于是，哈夫甘的贵族们都向皮威尔表示臣服，安农的两个王国从此合而为一，都归在阿拉文的名下。

*On the appointed day Pwyll went to the ford with the nobles of Annwn, and Hafgan came with his host. A knight proclaimed that this was a contest between two kings for land and rule, and all others should stand aside. The kings rode at each other from either side of the ford; at the first encounter Pwyll struck the centre of Hafgan's shield so that it split in two and the spear pierced his armour, and Hafgan was thrown a spear's and an arm's length over his horse's crupper, mortally wounded. 'What right have you to my death?' said Hafgan; 'I never wronged you. But since you have begun, for heaven's sake finish it.' 'I may repent of what I have done,' said Pwyll; 'let whoever will slay you; I will not strike again.' Hafgan bade his nobles carry him away, for his end had come. Pwyll asked Hafgan's men to consider whose subjects they should be, and they all did homage to him, and the two kingdoms of Annwn were united under Arawn's name.*

### 4. 安农之主 / Head of Annwn

一年期满，皮威尔来到格林·库赫，与阿拉文相见，两人各自恢复了原来的模样。阿拉文说：愿上天报答你对我的友谊，我已经听说了你为我做的一切。阿拉文回到安农，与贵族们谈话，发现他们都说他今年比往年更加慈爱、更加贤明，又得到了哈夫甘的国土。夜里，他与王后同床，和她说话、亲热，王后惊讶地说：你这一年来夜里从不和我说话，从不转过身来对着我，今夜怎么变了？阿拉文这才明白，皮威尔一整年都没有碰过他的妻子。他说：哎呀，我的朋友是多么忠实可靠啊。于是他把一切都告诉了王后，王后也说：你真有一个忠诚的朋友。皮威尔回到达费德，也问贵族们这一年他统治得怎么样，贵族们说：您从没有像今年这样公正、慷慨、贤明。皮威尔把真相告诉了他们，贵族们说：请您以后继续这样统治我们吧。从此，皮威尔和阿拉文结下了深厚的友谊，互相赠送马匹、猎犬、猎鹰和各种珍宝。因为他在安农做了一年国王，又把两个王国合在一起，人们不再叫他达费德的皮威尔，而叫他“安农之主”皮威尔。

*At the year's end Pwyll met Arawn at Glyn Cuch and each took his own shape again. Arawn returned to Annwn and found his nobles praising him for a year of greater kindness and wisdom than ever, and for winning Hafgan's land. That night, when he spoke lovingly to his queen, she wondered that he did so, for in a whole year he had not spoken to her or turned toward her at night. Then Arawn knew that Pwyll had kept faith, and said, 'How faithful a friend I have found,' and told her everything, and she agreed. In Dyfed Pwyll asked his nobles how he had ruled that year; never so justly, generously and wisely, they said, and when he told them the truth they asked him to go on ruling so. Pwyll and Arawn became firm friends and sent each other horses, hounds, hawks and treasures; and because Pwyll had ruled a year in Annwn and joined its kingdoms, he was called no longer Pwyll of Dyfed but Pwyll, Head of Annwn.*

### 5. 土丘上的白马女子 / The lady on the white horse

有一天，皮威尔在阿伯斯的王宫里举行宴会，宴罢之后，他走上了宫殿上方一座名叫戈尔塞斯·阿伯斯的土丘。人们说，任何一个贵族坐在这座土丘上，都会遇到两件事之一：要么被打伤，要么看见奇迹。皮威尔说：在这么多人中间，我不怕被打伤，倒很想看见奇迹。他在土丘上坐下来。不一会儿，他看见一位女子骑着一匹高大的纯白色骏马，身穿闪亮的金色锦缎，从大路上缓缓走来。皮威尔问身边的人：你们谁认识这位女子？没有人认识。他派一个人去追她，那人跑到大路上，女子从他身边经过，他拼命追赶，可是追得越快，女子离他越远，她的马却始终是那样不紧不慢地走着。第二天，皮威尔又派了一个骑着最快的马的人去追，结果还是一样，马跑得越快，她离得越远。第三天，皮威尔亲自骑马去追，他用尽全力，仍然追不上。最后他喊道：姑娘，看在你最爱的人的份上，请你停下来等我。女子说：我很乐意停下来，如果你早一点这样请求，你的马也就不用受这么多罪了。

*One day after a feast at Arberth, Pwyll went up the mound above the court called Gorsedd Arberth, of which it was said that any noble who sat on it would either receive wounds or see a wonder. He was not afraid of wounds among so many, he said, but would gladly see a wonder. As he sat, he saw a lady on a great pure-white horse, in a garment of shining gold brocade, coming slowly along the highway. No one knew her. He sent a man after her on foot, but the faster he ran, the further she drew ahead, though her horse kept the same slow pace. Next day he sent a rider on the swiftest horse, with the same result. The third day Pwyll rode after her himself and, failing with all his speed, called out: 'Maiden, for the sake of him you love best, stay for me.' 'I will stay gladly,' she said, 'and it would have been better for your horse if you had asked long ago.'*

### 6. 轻率的许诺 / The rash promise

女子揭开面纱，皮威尔觉得自己从没见过这样美丽的面容。她说：我是老赫菲德的女儿里安农，他们要把我嫁给一个我不愿意嫁的男人，因为我爱的是你。我来，就是想听你的回答。皮威尔说：在世上所有的女子中，我只愿意选择你。里安农说：那么一年以后的今天，在我父亲的宫殿里举行婚宴。一年后，皮威尔带着一百个骑士来到赫菲德的宫殿，婚宴非常丰盛。宴会正热闹时，一个身材高大、头发褐色、穿着锦缎的年轻人走进大厅，向皮威尔问候，说：我来是向你请求一个恩惠。皮威尔不假思索地说：无论你要求什么，只要我能做到，我都给你。里安农惊叫道：你为什么要这样回答！年轻人说：好，各位贵族都听见了。我要的是你今晚要娶的这位新娘，还有这场婚宴。皮威尔哑口无言。里安农说：你就沉默吧，从来没有人像你这样笨嘴拙舌的。这个人就是克卢德的儿子格瓦尔，我就是被许配给他的。可是一个贵族说出的话不能收回，皮威尔只好答应。里安农悄悄对他说：把我交给他，可是我决不会成为他的人。一年后，他会来这里举行婚宴，到时候你带着一百个骑士躲在果园里，再带上我给你的这只小口袋。

*She lifted her veil and he thought he had never seen a fairer face. 'I am Rhiannon, daughter of Hefeydd the Old,' she said, 'and they would give me to a husband against my will, because I love you; I have come for your answer.' Of all women in the world, said Pwyll, he would choose her. Then a year from that night, she said, there would be a wedding feast at her father's court. A year later Pwyll came with a hundred knights. In the midst of the feast a tall auburn-haired youth in brocade greeted Pwyll and asked a boon, and Pwyll said without thinking that whatever he asked, if it were in his power, he would have. 'Why did you answer so?' cried Rhiannon. 'Bear witness, nobles,' said the youth: 'I ask for the bride and the feast.' Pwyll was silent. 'Be silent as long as you will,' said Rhiannon; 'never was a man more slow-witted. This is Gwawl son of Clud, to whom they would give me.' A noble's word could not be taken back, so Pwyll had to agree; but Rhiannon whispered that she would never be Gwawl's, and that in a year, when Gwawl came for his feast, Pwyll should hide in the orchard with a hundred knights and bring the little bag she would give him.*

### 7. 口袋里的獾 / The badger in the bag

一年后，格瓦尔来赴他的婚宴。皮威尔穿上破烂的衣服，脚上套着笨重的旧鞋，扮成一个乞丐，带着里安农给他的小口袋走进大厅，向格瓦尔请求一个恩惠：只求你把我这只小口袋装满食物。格瓦尔说：这个要求不算过分，就给他装吧。仆人们往口袋里装食物，装了一盘又一盘，可是口袋怎么也装不满。格瓦尔问：你这口袋什么时候才能装满？皮威尔说：除非有一位拥有土地和财富的贵人站起来，用双脚把里面的食物踩实，说一声“装够了”，否则它永远也装不满。里安农对格瓦尔说：快去踩吧。格瓦尔站起身，把两只脚伸进了口袋里，皮威尔立刻把口袋往上一拉，盖过了格瓦尔的头，扎紧了袋口，然后吹响了号角。藏在果园里的骑士们冲进大厅，把格瓦尔的随从都抓了起来。每个骑士走进来，都用脚踢一下口袋，或者用棍子打一下，问：这里面是什么？其他人回答：是一只獾。这就是“口袋里的獾”游戏的来历。格瓦尔在口袋里求饶，赫菲德劝皮威尔饶了他，条件是格瓦尔放弃对里安农的一切要求，不再报复，并找来担保人。格瓦尔答应了。皮威尔和里安农终于成了婚，回到达费德，人民都欢迎他们。

*A year later Gwawl came to his feast. Pwyll, in ragged clothes and clumsy old shoes like a beggar, came into the hall with Rhiannon's little bag and asked Gwawl for a boon: only to fill the bag with food. Gwawl granted it, but dish after dish went in and the bag was no fuller. When Gwawl asked when it would be full, Pwyll said not until a nobleman with lands and riches stood up and trod down the food with both feet and said 'Enough has been put in.' Rhiannon urged Gwawl to do it; he put both feet in, and Pwyll pulled the bag up over his head, tied it and blew his horn. His knights rushed in from the orchard and seized Gwawl's men, and each who came in struck the bag with foot or staff and asked, 'What is here?' — 'A badger,' the others answered; and that was the first playing of Badger in the Bag. Gwawl begged for mercy; at Hefeydd's counsel Pwyll released him on condition that he give up all claim to Rhiannon and seek no vengeance, with sureties. So Pwyll and Rhiannon were married and went home to Dyfed, where the people welcomed them.*

### 8. 失踪的婴儿 / The vanished child

皮威尔和里安农幸福地统治着达费德，可是过了两年，他们还没有孩子。第三年，贵族们聚集在一起，劝皮威尔另娶一个能生育的妻子，免得王位没有继承人。皮威尔说：我们结婚的时间还不长，再等一年，如果到时还没有孩子，我就听你们的。就在那一年里，里安农生下了一个儿子。在孩子出生的那一夜，六个女人被派来守护母亲和婴儿。半夜里，她们都睡着了，里安农也睡着了。天快亮时，女人们醒来，发现孩子不见了，四处寻找都找不到。她们害怕受到惩罚，竟商量出了一个恶毒的主意：她们杀了一只小猎犬，把它的血涂在熟睡的里安农的脸上和手上，把骨头扔在她的床边，然后一口咬定，是里安农在夜里吃掉了自己的孩子，她们六个人怎么也拦不住她。里安农醒来，问孩子在哪里，女人们就这样诬告她。里安农说：你们不要撒谎了，我知道你们是害怕受罚，如果你们说出真相，我会保护你们。可是她们坚持自己的谎言。

*Pwyll and Rhiannon ruled Dyfed happily, but after two years they had no child, and in the third year the nobles urged Pwyll to take another wife who could bear him an heir. They had not been married long, said Pwyll; let them wait a year more. In that year Rhiannon bore a son. On the night of the birth six women were set to watch mother and child, but at midnight they and Rhiannon fell asleep, and toward dawn the women woke to find the boy gone. Fearing punishment, they killed a staghound pup, smeared its blood on the sleeping Rhiannon's face and hands, scattered the bones by her bed, and swore that she had devoured her son in the night and the six of them could not stop her. When Rhiannon woke and asked for her son, they accused her; she begged them not to lie, promising to protect them if they told the truth, but they held to their lie.*

### 9. 里安农的惩罚 / Rhiannon's penance

消息传开了，贵族们要求皮威尔休掉里安农，皮威尔说：他们要我休妻，理由是她不能生育；可是她已经生了孩子，我不会休掉她。如果她犯了罪，就让她受罚吧。里安农请来了教士和贤人，与其和那些女人争辩，不如接受惩罚。她被判处了这样的惩罚：在阿伯斯的宫门外，有一块上马石，她要在那里坐七年，每天对每一个来到宫门的陌生人讲述她犯下的罪行，并请求背着客人和外乡人进宫，如果他们愿意让她背的话。可是很少有人愿意让她背。这位曾经骑着白马、从彼世来到阿伯斯土丘上的女子，就这样每天坐在宫门边，向路过的人讲述一件她从没犯过的罪行。皮威尔没有抛弃她，可是他也无法为她洗清冤屈。里安农默默地忍受着这一切，她相信总有一天，真相会大白。故事在这里转向了另一个地方：在邻近的格温特，有一个名叫泰尔农的领主，他有一匹美丽的母马。

*The nobles demanded that Pwyll put her away. They had asked him to put her away for being childless, he said; she had borne a child, and he would not; if she had done wrong, let her do penance. Rhiannon, taking counsel with teachers and wise men, chose to accept penance rather than wrangle with the women. Her penance was to sit for seven years beside the horse-block outside the gate of Arberth, to tell every stranger who came the tale of her deed, and to offer to carry guests and strangers on her back into the court — though few would let her. The lady who had come from the Otherworld on a white horse to the mound of Arberth now sat each day at the gate telling of a crime she had not committed. Pwyll did not put her away, but he could not clear her; she bore it in silence, trusting that the truth would come out. Here the tale turns to Gwent nearby, where a lord called Teyrnon had a beautiful mare.*

### 10. 泰尔农的马驹 / Teyrnon's foal

格温特·伊斯·科伊德的领主泰尔农·特威尔夫·里安特有一匹母马，是他国土上最美的马。每年五月节的前夜，这匹母马都会生下一匹马驹，可是马驹生下来后，没有一个人知道它去了哪里，总是凭空消失。泰尔农对妻子说：今年我一定要弄个明白。五月节前夜，他把母马牵进马厩，自己全副武装地守在里面。天黑后，母马生下了一匹高大漂亮的马驹，马驹刚站起来，泰尔农就听见一阵巨大的声响，一只巨大的手爪从窗户里伸了进来，一把抓住了马驹的鬃毛。泰尔农拔出剑，向那只手臂砍去，把它从肘部砍断了，断臂和马驹一起留在了马厩里。外面传来一声惨叫。泰尔农冲出门去追，可是夜太黑了，什么也看不见。他想起门没有关，赶紧跑回来，看见门口放着一个裹在锦缎襁褓里的婴儿。他抱起孩子，看他是个强壮的男孩，就把他抱给妻子看。妻子说：我们没有孩子，就把他当作我们的儿子吧，我会对人说是我自己生的。他们给孩子取名为“金发的格利”，因为他头上的头发像金子一样黄。

*Teyrnon Twrf Liant, lord of Gwent Is Coed, had the fairest mare in his land; every May Eve she foaled, and no one knew what became of the foal. This year, he told his wife, he would find out. On May Eve he brought the mare into the stable and watched there armed. At nightfall she bore a large, handsome foal, and as it stood up he heard a great noise and a huge claw came through the window and seized the foal by the mane. Teyrnon drew his sword and struck off the arm at the elbow, so that it and the foal remained inside, and outside there was a scream. He rushed out but could see nothing in the dark; remembering the open door, he ran back and found at the threshold an infant boy wrapped in a mantle of satin. He took the strong child to his wife, who, being childless, said they would rear him as their own and she would say she had borne him. They named him Gwri Golden-hair, for his hair was yellow as gold.*

### 11. 长得像皮威尔的男孩 / The boy who looked like Pwyll

这个孩子长得飞快，一岁时就像三岁的孩子一样会走路，两岁时像六岁的孩子一样强壮，不到四岁，就要和马夫们讨价还价，要他们让他把马牵到河边去饮水。泰尔农的妻子说：我们那一夜得到的马驹在哪里？把它驯好，送给这孩子吧。泰尔农就把那匹马驹送给了男孩。就在这时，他们听说了里安农的事：她因为孩子失踪受到了惩罚。泰尔农为她感到难过，他仔细地看着这个男孩，越看越觉得他长得像皮威尔——他从前曾经在皮威尔手下做过侍从，熟悉王子的模样。男孩的面容和皮威尔一模一样。泰尔农想：留下别人的孩子是不对的，让那位贵妇人为了一件没有犯过的罪受苦更是不对。他把这个想法告诉了妻子，妻子虽然舍不得，也同意了，她说：我们会因此得到三样好处：让里安农摆脱惩罚，得到皮威尔的感谢，还有这孩子将来的报答——如果他是一个高尚的人，就会成为我们的养子。于是，泰尔农带着男孩，骑马向阿伯斯出发了。

*The boy grew wondrously: at one year he walked like a child of three, at two he was as big as a child of six, and before he was four he was bargaining with the grooms to let him lead the horses to water. Teyrnon's wife asked for the foal of that night to be broken in and given to the boy, and it was. Then they heard of Rhiannon's punishment for her lost son. Teyrnon, grieving for her, looked closely at the boy and saw that he was the image of Pwyll, whom he had once served and knew well. It was wrong, he thought, to keep another's son, and worse that the lady should suffer for a crime she had not done. His wife, though loath, agreed: they would gain three things — Rhiannon's release, Pwyll's thanks, and the boy's gratitude, for if he proved noble he would be their foster-son. So Teyrnon set out for Arberth with the boy.*

### 12. 普雷德里 / Pryderi

泰尔农带着男孩和两个骑士来到阿伯斯，看见里安农坐在宫门边的上马石旁。她站起来对他们说：大人们，请停下，我要把你们每个人背进宫去，这是我的惩罚，因为我杀死了自己的儿子。泰尔农说：夫人，我们谁也不会让你背。他们走进宫殿，皮威尔热情地款待了他们。宴会上，泰尔农讲述了母马、手爪和孩子的故事，然后对里安农说：夫人，这就是你的儿子，那些诬告你的人说了谎。大厅里所有的人一看见这个男孩，都认出他和皮威尔长得一模一样。里安农说：如果这是真的，我的忧虑就结束了。威尔士语里，“忧虑”这个词叫作“普雷德里”。贵族彭达兰·达费德说：您给您的儿子取了一个好名字，就叫他普雷德里吧。于是男孩被叫作普雷德里，是皮威尔安农之主的儿子。皮威尔要给泰尔农丰厚的报酬，泰尔农谢绝了。普雷德里被交给彭达兰·达费德抚养，长大后成了达费德最出色的青年。皮威尔去世后，普雷德里继承了达费德的七个坎特雷夫，又征服了更多的土地，娶了基格瓦为妻。第一支就这样结束了。

*Teyrnon came to Arberth with the boy and two knights and found Rhiannon by the horse-block. She rose and offered to carry each of them into the court, for that was her penance for slaying her son; none of them would let her. Pwyll welcomed them, and at the feast Teyrnon told the story of the mare, the claw and the child, and said to Rhiannon: 'Lady, here is your son; those who accused you lied.' Everyone in the hall saw that the boy was the image of Pwyll. 'If this is true,' said Rhiannon, 'I am delivered of my anxiety' — pryder, in Welsh. 'Well have you named your son,' said the nobleman Pendaran Dyfed: 'Pryderi.' So the boy was called Pryderi son of Pwyll, Head of Annwn. Teyrnon refused the rich rewards Pwyll offered. Pryderi was fostered by Pendaran Dyfed and grew to be the finest youth in Dyfed; after Pwyll's death he ruled the seven cantrefs, won more lands, and married Cigfa. So ends the First Branch.*
