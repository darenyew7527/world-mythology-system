## 尤弥尔 · Ymir

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.norse.ymir_world](../story.norse.ymir_world.md)

> 在金伦加鸿沟中形成的原初巨人；被奥丁兄弟杀死，世界由他的身体造成（《欺骗古鲁菲》第5–8章）。

> *Primordial giant formed in Ginnungagap; killed by Odin and his brothers, who make the world from his body (Gylfaginning 5–8).*

### 1. 冰与火之间 / Between ice and fire

世界还不存在时，只有一道叫金伦加的深渊。北方的寒霜与南方的热气在这里相遇，融化的水滴里生出了巨人尤弥尔。

*Before the world exists there is only the void of Ginnungagap. Where rime from the north meets heat from the south, the thawing drops bring forth the giant Ymir.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 5–8 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第5章。</sub>

### 2. 奥丁兄弟杀死尤弥尔 / Odin and his brothers kill Ymir

布尔的儿子们——奥丁、维利和维——杀死了巨人尤弥尔。

*The sons of Borr — Odin, Vili and Vé — kill the giant Ymir.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 5–8 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第7章。</sub>

### 3. 用身体造世界 / A world made from a body

他们用尤弥尔的身体造出世界：肉变成大地，血变成海洋和湖泊，骨头变成山岩，头骨被举起来成为天空。

*From Ymir's body they make the world: his flesh becomes the earth, his blood the sea and lakes, his bones the rocks, and his skull is raised up as the sky.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 5–8 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第8章。</sub>

## 尼奥尔德 · Njörðr

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.norse.vanir_family](../story.norse.vanir_family.md)

> 《欺骗古鲁菲》第23章中的华纳神：住在诺欧通，掌管风与海，是弗雷与芙蕾雅之父。

> *Vanir god in Gylfaginning 23: lives at Nóatún, rules wind and sea, father of Freyr and Freyja.*

### 1. 海风之神尼奥尔德 / Njörðr of wind and sea

尼奥尔德住在天上一个叫诺欧通的地方。他掌管风的运行，能平息海浪和火焰；出海航行和捕鱼的人都向他祈祷。他在华纳海姆长大，后来作为人质被送到阿萨神族。

*Njörðr lives in heaven at a place called Nóatún. He rules the motion of the wind and can calm sea and fire; those who sail or fish pray to him. He was raised in Vanaheim and later given to the Æsir as a hostage.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 23, 24 and 37 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第23章。</sub>

### 2. 弗雷与芙蕾雅 / Freyr and Freyja

尼奥尔德有一儿一女。儿子弗雷掌管雨水、阳光和大地的收成，人们向他祈求丰年与富足。女儿芙蕾雅住在弗尔克范格，每当她骑马出战，就分得一半战死者，另一半归奥丁；她乘坐的车由两只猫拉着。

*Njörðr has a son and a daughter. Freyr rules rain, sunshine and the produce of the earth, and people pray to him for good seasons and wealth. Freyja lives at Fólkvangr; whenever she rides to battle she takes half the slain, Odin the other half, and her chariot is drawn by two cats.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 23, 24 and 37 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第24章。</sub>

### 3. 弗雷交出宝剑 / Freyr gives away his sword

后来，弗雷爱上了巨人之女葛德。为了娶到她，他把自己的宝剑交给了别人作为代价——这把剑从此不在他手中。

*Later Freyr falls in love with Gerðr, a giant's daughter. To win her he gives away his sword as the price, and the sword is no longer his.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 23, 24 and 37 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第37章。</sub>

## 巴德尔 · Baldr

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.norse.frigg_baldr](../story.norse.frigg_baldr.md)

### 1. 知晓命运的女神 / The goddess who knows all fates

弗丽嘉是奥丁的妻子。斯诺里说，她知道一切人的命运，只是从不说出来。

*Frigg is Odin's wife. Snorri says she knows the fates of all, though she does not speak them aloud.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 20 and 49 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第20章。</sub>

### 2. 万物发誓 / Everything swears

巴德尔做了不祥的梦。弗丽嘉便去找火、水、铁和各种金属、石头、土地、树木、疾病、野兽、鸟和蛇，让它们都发誓不伤害巴德尔。只有长在英灵殿西边的一株小槲寄生，她觉得太小，没有让它发誓。

*Baldr has ominous dreams, so Frigg makes fire, water, iron and all metals, stones, earth, trees, sicknesses, beasts, birds and serpents swear not to harm him. Only a young mistletoe shoot she thinks too small to ask.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 20 and 49 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第49章。</sub>

### 3. 槲寄生 / The mistletoe

众神以为巴德尔刀枪不入，便拿东西扔他取乐。然而那株没有发誓的槲寄生被做成了投枪，巴德尔被它刺中而死。

*Believing Baldr unharmable, the gods amuse themselves by throwing things at him. But the mistletoe that never swore is made into a dart, and Baldr is struck down and dies.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 20 and 49 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第49章。</sub>

## 提尔 · Týr

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.norse.tyr_fenrir](../story.norse.tyr_fenrir.md)

> 众神中最勇敢的一位；束缚芬里尔时失去一只手（《欺骗古鲁菲》第25、34章）。

> *The boldest of the gods, who loses his hand when the wolf Fenrir is bound (Gylfaginning 25, 34).*

### 1. 最勇敢的神 / The boldest god

斯诺里说，提尔是众神中最勇敢、最无畏的一位，打仗时人们向他祈求胜利。他只有一只手。

*Snorri says Týr is the boldest and most valiant of the gods, invoked for victory in battle — and he has only one hand.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 25 and 34 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第25章。</sub>

### 2. 一只手作担保 / A hand as a pledge

众神想用一条魔法绳索捆住狼芬里尔。狼起了疑心，要求有人把手放进它嘴里，保证这不是诡计。众神面面相觑，只有提尔伸出了右手。

*The gods mean to bind the wolf Fenrir with a magic fetter. Suspicious, the wolf demands that someone put a hand in its mouth as a pledge of good faith. Only Týr holds out his right hand.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 25 and 34 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第34章。</sub>

### 3. 狼咬断了手 / The wolf bites

绳索越挣越紧，狼发现自己被骗了，便咬断了提尔的手。众神都笑了，只有提尔没有笑。

*The more the wolf struggles, the tighter the fetter grows. Realizing it has been tricked, Fenrir bites off Týr's hand — and all the gods laugh, except Týr.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Snorri Sturluson, Edda · Gylfaginning 25 and 34 · 《欺骗古鲁菲》第34章。</sub>
