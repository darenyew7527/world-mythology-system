## 俱利摩（龟形化身） · Kūrma

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.hindu.samudra_manthana_texts](../story.hindu.samudra_manthana_texts.md)

> *Tortoise form assumed by Viṣṇu to support Mount Mandara in the Bhāgavata Purāṇa witness; kept separate from the Mahābhārata tortoise king Akūpāra.*

### 1. 诸神与阿修罗暂时结盟 / A truce between gods and asuras

《薄伽梵往世书》第八篇第5–6章中，诸神按毗湿奴的建议与阿修罗暂时结盟，合力拔起曼陀罗山并运往大海，毗湿奴亲自出手相助。

*In Bhāgavata Purāṇa 8.5–8.6, on Viṣṇu's advice the gods make a temporary truce with the asuras; together they uproot Mount Mandara and carry it toward the ocean, with Viṣṇu himself stepping in to help.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Bhāgavatapurāṇa (GRETIL e-text) · Bhāgavatapurāṇa, Skandha 8, adhyāyas 5–9 · 《薄伽梵往世书》8.5–8.6。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：运山过程的细节只作概述，待逐颂校对。</sub>

### 2. 毗湿奴化身巨龟 / Viṣṇu takes the form of a tortoise

以婆苏吉为绳开始搅拌后，曼陀罗山沉入水中；毗湿奴化作巨龟潜到山下，用背托起大山。

*Once the churning begins with Vāsuki as the rope, Mount Mandara sinks into the water; Viṣṇu assumes the form of a giant tortoise, dives beneath it and raises the mountain on his back.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Bhāgavatapurāṇa (GRETIL e-text) · Bhāgavatapurāṇa, Skandha 8, adhyāyas 5–9 · 《薄伽梵往世书》8.7。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：与精校本的龟王阿库帕拉构成显式见证差异，不做身份等同。</sub>

### 3. 湿婆饮下毒药 / Śiva drinks the poison

搅拌最先产生可怕的毒药，向各方蔓延；众生求助于湿婆，湿婆把毒药饮下。

*The churning first produces a terrible poison that spreads in every direction; beings turn to Śiva, who drinks it.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Bhāgavatapurāṇa (GRETIL e-text) · Bhāgavatapurāṇa, Skandha 8, adhyāyas 5–9 · 《薄伽梵往世书》8.7。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：后续图像传统与虔信解释未在本批次逐句建模。</sub>

### 4. 檀文陀利与甘露 / Dhanvantari and the amṛta

其后又涌现多种宝物与存在，最后檀文陀利捧着盛满甘露的水罐出现。

*A series of treasures and beings then emerges, and finally Dhanvantari appears holding a vessel filled with the amṛta.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Bhāgavatapurāṇa (GRETIL e-text) · Bhāgavatapurāṇa, Skandha 8, adhyāyas 5–9 · 《薄伽梵往世书》8.8。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：此前出现之物的逐项清单尚未建模，不与精校本合并。</sub>

### 5. 摩希尼与罗睺 / Mohinī and Rāhu

阿修罗抢走甘露后，毗湿奴化身为摩希尼迷惑他们，并把甘露分给诸神。罗睺假扮天神混入其中饮下甘露，被日月指认后，毗湿奴以神盘斩下其头。

*When the asuras snatch the amṛta, Viṣṇu becomes Mohinī, captivates them and distributes the amṛta to the gods. Rāhu, disguised as a god, slips in and drinks; when the Sun and Moon point him out, Viṣṇu severs his head with the discus.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Bhāgavatapurāṇa (GRETIL e-text) · Bhāgavatapurāṇa, Skandha 8, adhyāyas 5–9 · 《薄伽梵往世书》8.8–8.9。</sub>

## 檀文陀利 · Dhanvantari

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.hindu.samudra_manthana_texts](../story.hindu.samudra_manthana_texts.md)

> *Divine figure who emerges from the churned ocean carrying the amṛta in both registered textual witnesses.*

### 1. 诸神求取甘露 / The gods seek the amṛta

精校本《初篇》1.15 中，诸神聚集在须弥山上商议如何得到甘露；那罗延（毗湿奴）建议诸神与阿修罗一起搅动大海。

*In the critical edition at Ādiparvan 1.15, the gods gather on Mount Meru to deliberate how to obtain the amṛta; Nārāyaṇa (Viṣṇu) advises that gods and asuras churn the ocean together.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mahābhārata, Ādiparvan (critical-edition e-text) · Mahābhārata CE, Ādiparvan 1.15–1.17 (Āstīkaparvan) · 精校本初篇 1.15。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：“那罗延”在此按既有毗湿奴实体连接，不扩展神学解释。</sub>

### 2. 曼陀罗山、婆苏吉与龟王 / Mandara, Vāsuki and the tortoise king

巨蛇阿难陀拔起曼陀罗山作为搅棒，龙王婆苏吉充当搅绳；山的底部由龟王阿库帕拉承托。这一见证没有说龟王是毗湿奴的化身。

*The serpent Ananta uproots Mount Mandara to serve as the churning rod and the nāga Vāsuki serves as the rope; the tortoise king Akūpāra bears the base of the mountain. This witness does not say that the tortoise king is a form of Viṣṇu.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mahābhārata, Ādiparvan (critical-edition e-text) · Mahābhārata CE, Ādiparvan 1.15–1.17 (Āstīkaparvan) · 精校本初篇 1.16。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：阿难陀拔山一项为中等置信度，待逐颂校对；阿难陀尚未单独建实体。</sub>

### 3. 檀文陀利捧着甘露出现 / Dhanvantari appears with the amṛta

搅拌中先后涌现多种存在与宝物，最后檀文陀利捧着盛满甘露的水罐出现。

*As the churning goes on, a series of beings and treasures emerges, and finally Dhanvantari appears holding a vessel filled with the amṛta.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mahābhārata, Ādiparvan (critical-edition e-text) · Mahābhārata CE, Ādiparvan 1.15–1.17 (Āstīkaparvan) · 精校本初篇 1.16。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：此前出现之物的逐项清单尚未建模，不从其他版本补齐。</sub>

### 4. 迷惑的女身与罗睺 / The deluding female form and Rāhu

阿修罗夺走甘露后，那罗延化作迷惑人的女子形象，把甘露取回交给诸神。罗睺假扮天神偷饮甘露，被日月揭发后，那罗延以神盘斩下其头；随后天神与阿修罗爆发大战。

*After the Dānavas seize the amṛta, Nārāyaṇa takes on a deluding female form and recovers it for the gods. Rāhu, disguised as a god, drinks some of it; when the Sun and Moon expose him, Nārāyaṇa cuts off his head with the discus, and a great battle between gods and Dānavas follows.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mahābhārata, Ādiparvan (critical-edition e-text) · Mahābhārata CE, Ādiparvan 1.15–1.17 (Āstīkaparvan) · 精校本初篇 1.16–1.17。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：专名实体“摩希尼”只连接《薄伽梵往世书》见证；此处保留“迷惑的女身”描述。</sub>

### 5. 精校本正文的范围 / Scope of the constituted critical text

在精校本 1.15–1.17 的正文里，没有湿婆吞下毒药的情节。这只说明本定位“未陈述”，不代表所有写本或地方传统都没有；校勘记录与附录尚待逐项核对。

*The constituted critical text at 1.15–1.17 does not include Śiva swallowing the poison. This records only that the locator does not state it; it does not mean that every manuscript or regional tradition lacks it, and the apparatus and appendices still have to be checked.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Mahābhārata, Ādiparvan (critical-edition e-text) · Mahābhārata CE, Ādiparvan 1.15–1.17 (Āstīkaparvan) · 精校本初篇 1.15–1.17 正文范围与 BORI 精校本说明。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：写本校勘与附录已作为独立研究队列排队。</sub>

### 5. 摩希尼与罗睺 / Mohinī and Rāhu

阿修罗抢走甘露后，毗湿奴化身为摩希尼迷惑他们，并把甘露分给诸神。罗睺假扮天神混入其中饮下甘露，被日月指认后，毗湿奴以神盘斩下其头。

*When the asuras snatch the amṛta, Viṣṇu becomes Mohinī, captivates them and distributes the amṛta to the gods. Rāhu, disguised as a god, slips in and drinks; when the Sun and Moon point him out, Viṣṇu severs his head with the discus.*
