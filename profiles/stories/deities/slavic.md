## 佩伦 · Perun

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.slavic.kyiv_idols](../story.slavic.kyiv_idols.md)

> 《往年纪事》所见东斯拉夫佩伦：条约誓言、980 年基辅神像与 988 年毁像分别登记；“全斯拉夫唯一主神”与命名神器不作无证断言。

> *Perun in East Slavic chronicle witnesses: treaty oaths, the 980 Kyiv idol and its 988 removal are separate claims; no unsupported universal pantheon or named weapon is asserted.*

### 1. 山岗上的神像 / Idols on the hill

《往年纪事》980 年条记载：弗拉基米尔开始在基辅独自为王，就在宫殿庭院外的山岗上立起一排神像。

*The Primary Chronicle's annal for 980 records that when Volodimer began to reign alone in Kyiv, he set up a row of idols on the hill outside the palace courtyard.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Tale of Bygone Years digital critical edition · annals 980 and 988 · 《往年纪事》980 年条。</sub>

### 2. 银头金须的佩伦 / Perun with a silver head

最前面的是佩伦：木头雕成，头是银的，胡须是金的。

*First stood Perun, carved of wood, with a silver head and a golden moustache.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Tale of Bygone Years digital critical edition · annals 980 and 988 · 《往年纪事》980 年条。</sub>

### 3. 名单最后的莫科什 / Mokosh at the end of the list

接下来是霍尔斯、达日博格、斯特里博格、西马尔格尔，名单的最后一位是莫科什。人们向这些神像献上祭品。编年史只记下了莫科什的名字，没有说她掌管什么。

*Then came Khors, Dazhbog, Stribog and Simargl, and last on the list, Mokosh. People brought offerings to the idols. The chronicle records only Mokosh's name, not what she governed.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Tale of Bygone Years digital critical edition · annals 980 and 988 · 《往年纪事》980 年条神名名单。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：关于莫科什职能的说法来自其他材料与现代研究，本故事不补写。</sub>

### 4. 988 年：神像倒下 / 988: the idols fall

八年后，弗拉基米尔受洗，下令推倒这些神像：有的砍碎，有的烧掉。佩伦被绑在马尾上拖下山，有人用棍子抽打，最后被扔进第聂伯河。

*Eight years later Volodimer was baptized and ordered the idols overthrown — some cut to pieces, others burned. Perun was tied to a horse's tail and dragged down the hill, beaten with sticks, and thrown into the Dnieper.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Tale of Bygone Years digital critical edition · annals 980 and 988 · 《往年纪事》988 年条。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：这是皈依一方的记载，不是对斯拉夫宗教的中立描述。</sub>
