## 哪吒 · Nezha

**有完整故事 / Full story**

完整故事档案：[story.chinese.nezha_wukong](../story.chinese.nezha_wukong.md)

> 《西游记》第四回中托塔李天王的三太子，变三头六臂迎战孙悟空（后世文学层）。

> *Third prince of Heavenly King Li in Journey to the West ch. 4, who takes three heads and six arms against Sun Wukong (later literary layer).*

### 1. 天兵来讨 / Heaven sends its army

孙悟空发现“弼马温”只是个看马的小官，一气之下回到花果山，自称“齐天大圣”。玉帝派托塔李天王和他的三太子哪吒，带领天兵前去捉拿。

*Discovering that his title merely made him a keeper of horses, Sun Wukong storms back to his mountain and calls himself Great Sage Equal to Heaven. The Jade Emperor sends Heavenly King Li, bearer of the pagoda, and his third son Nezha with the heavenly troops to seize him.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Journey to the West, chapter 4 · ch. 4 · 《西游记》第四回。</sub>

### 2. 三头六臂 / Three heads, six arms

哪吒大喝一声，变成三头六臂，六只手各拿一件兵器打来。悟空见了，也变成三头六臂，把金箍棒一晃变成三条，六只手拿着架住。

*Nezha gives a shout and becomes a figure with three heads and six arms, a weapon in every hand. Wukong does the same: he shakes his staff into three and parries with six hands.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Journey to the West, chapter 4 · ch. 4 · 《西游记》第四回。</sub>

### 3. 毫毛分身 / A hair becomes a double

两人斗得难分难解。悟空拔下一根毫毛，变成自己的模样继续迎敌，真身却绕到哪吒背后打了一棒。哪吒负痛败走，回去禀报父王：这猴子神通广大。

*Neither can win. Wukong plucks a hair and turns it into a double of himself to keep fighting, while his true body circles behind Nezha and strikes. Nezha flees in pain and reports to his father that the monkey's powers are great.*

<sub>来源 / Source：Journey to the West, chapter 4 · ch. 4 · 《西游记》第四回。</sub>

<sub>尚不确定 / Uncertain：这是小说情节，属于后世文学层。</sub>
