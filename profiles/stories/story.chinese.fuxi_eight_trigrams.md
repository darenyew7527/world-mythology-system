# 伏羲始作八卦 / Fuxi makes the eight trigrams

## 阅读边界

《周易·系辞下》说：古时包牺氏（伏羲）治理天下，他抬头观察天象，低头观察地理，看鸟兽身上的纹理和各地的物产，近取自身、远取万物，于是开始画出八卦；他又把绳子打结做成网，用来打猎捕鱼。

The Xi Ci II says that when Baoxi (Fuxi) ruled in antiquity, he looked up at heaven and down at the earth, studied the markings of birds and beasts and the land, drew on his own body and on distant things, and first made the eight trigrams; he also knotted cords into nets for hunting and fishing.

### 01 · 包牺氏治理天下 / When Baoxi ruled the world

《系辞下》讲古代圣人的发明，第一位就是包牺氏——后世通常写作伏羲。书里说，远古的时候，是他在治理天下。

Xi Ci II lists the inventions of the ancient sages, and the first is Baoxi — usually written Fuxi in later texts. In remote antiquity, it says, he ruled all under heaven.

> 证据说明：《系辞下》第二章开头。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.china.fuxi_baoxi`
> 未知／边界：“包牺”即“伏羲”是传统注疏的读法。

### 02 · 仰观天，俯察地 / Looking up, looking down

他抬头观察天上的日月星辰，低头观察大地的形势；又细看鸟兽身上的花纹，看各地适合生长什么。近处，他从自己的身体取法；远处，他从万物取法。

He looked up at the images in the sky and down at the patterns of the earth; he studied the markings of birds and beasts and what each land was suited for. Near at hand he drew on his own body, and far off on all things.

> 证据说明：《系辞下》第二章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.china.fuxi_observes`

### 03 · 画出八卦 / The first eight trigrams

于是他开始画出八卦——八个由三条线组成的符号，用来通达神明的德性，把万物的情状分门别类。

Thereupon he first made the eight trigrams — eight figures of three lines each — to communicate with spirit-like virtue and to sort the dispositions of the myriad things.

> 证据说明：《系辞下》第二章“于是始作八卦”。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.china.fuxi_bagua`

### 04 · 结绳为网 / Cords become nets

他还把绳子打结，做成罗网，人们用它打猎、捕鱼。《系辞》接下来再讲神农等后来的圣人，这里只讲伏羲这一段。

He also knotted cords into nets, which people used to hunt and fish. The Xi Ci then moves on to later sages such as Shennong; this retelling covers only Baoxi's part.

> 证据说明：《系辞下》第二章“作结绳而为罔罟”。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.china.fuxi_nets`
> 未知／边界：后续圣人的发明不在本故事范围内。
