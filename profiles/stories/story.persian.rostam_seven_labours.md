## 阅读边界

伊朗国王凯·卡乌斯听了一个魔鬼扮成的歌手赞美马赞德兰的歌，不顾扎尔的劝告，率军去征服这片魔鬼之地，结果被白魔用黑云和冰雹弄瞎了双眼，连同大军一起被俘。鲁斯塔姆骑着拉赫什，选择了一条近而险的路去营救国王，一路闯过七关：拉赫什杀死了扑来的狮子；一只公羊把渴得昏倒的他引到泉边；拉赫什三次唤醒他，与他一起杀死了恶龙；他识破了化作美女的女巫；他擒住乌拉德做向导；他斩下了阿尔章魔的头；最后他趁正午魔鬼们沉睡，闯进白魔的洞穴，杀死白魔，取出它的肝，用它的血让国王和勇士们重见光明。随后，马赞德兰国王在战斗中把自己变成一块岩石，被鲁斯塔姆扛回营中逼了出来。可是凯·卡乌斯并没有吸取教训：他又听了魔鬼的话，用四只饥饿的鹰拉着宝座想飞上天空，最后从空中跌进了森林，又一次需要鲁斯塔姆去把他找回来。

Kay Kāvus, shah of Iran, hears a demon disguised as a minstrel sing the praises of Māzandarān and, ignoring Zāl's warnings, marches to conquer that land of demons — only to be blinded with his whole army by the White Demon's dark cloud and hail and taken captive. Riding Rakhsh, Rostam takes the short and perilous road to rescue him and passes seven stages: Rakhsh kills a lion; a ram leads him, fainting with thirst, to a spring; Rakhsh wakes him three times and helps him kill a dragon; he unmasks a witch disguised as a fair woman; he captures Olād as his guide; he tears off the head of the demon Arzhang; and at last, at noon when the demons sleep, he enters the White Demon's cave, kills him and with the blood of his liver restores the sight of the shah and his warriors. The king of Māzandarān turns himself into a rock in battle and is carried back to camp by Rostam until he comes out. But Kay Kāvus learns nothing: tempted again by a demon, he tries to fly to heaven on a throne drawn by four hungry eagles, crashes into a forest, and once more must be found by Rostam.

### 01 · 魔鬼歌手 / The demon minstrel

凯·卡乌斯继承了伊朗的王位，坐在宝座上，看着满朝的勇士和堆积如山的财宝，以为天下再也没有人比他更强大。有一天，宫门口来了一个陌生的歌手，请求在国王面前献唱。他其实是一个魔鬼变的。他弹起琴，唱起了马赞德兰：那里四季鲜花盛开，夜莺在花园里歌唱，山上长满了郁金香，空气温暖而芬芳，城里堆满了金子和宝石，美丽的少女多得数不清。凯·卡乌斯听得入了迷，当即宣布：我要率军去征服马赞德兰，把那里的财富和美景都收归伊朗。满朝的勇士都惊呆了，他们知道马赞德兰是魔鬼居住的地方，以前的贤明君王贾姆希德和法里东都不敢去冒犯。他们不敢劝阻国王，就悄悄派人连夜赶到扎布尔斯坦，去请扎尔。

Kay Kāvus inherited the throne of Iran and, seated in state with his champions around him and treasure heaped high, thought no one in the world more mighty than he. One day a strange minstrel came to the palace gate asking to sing before the king. He was in truth a demon. He struck his lute and sang of Māzandarān: flowers bloom there in every season, nightingales sing in the gardens, tulips cover the hills, the air is warm and fragrant, the cities are heaped with gold and jewels, and its fair maidens are past counting. Kay Kāvus was enchanted and declared at once: 'I will march and conquer Māzandarān and add its wealth and beauty to Iran.' The champions were aghast, for they knew Māzandarān was the home of demons, which even the wise kings Jamshīd and Fereydūn had not dared to attack. Not daring to oppose the king, they secretly sent a messenger riding through the night to Zābulistān to fetch Zāl.

### 02 · 白魔的冰雹 / The White Demon's hail

扎尔赶到宫中，恳切地劝国王：马赞德兰是魔鬼的国度，他们会用魔法迷惑人，以前的国王都不敢去，你不要拿伊朗的勇士和自己的性命去冒险。凯·卡乌斯却说：我比贾姆希德和法里东更强大，我有更多的勇士和财宝，魔鬼又能把我怎样？你若是怕，就留在家里吧。扎尔无可奈何，只好告辞。凯·卡乌斯率领大军进入马赞德兰，烧杀抢掠，掳走了许多财宝和人口。马赞德兰的国王大为惊恐，派人去请魔鬼的首领白魔。白魔连夜召来一片乌云，天地间一片漆黑，接着降下了石头和冰雹，伊朗的军队在黑暗中乱成一团。等到天亮，凯·卡乌斯和他的勇士们全都瞎了眼睛，被魔鬼们抓住，关进了地牢。凯·卡乌斯在黑暗中后悔不已，偷偷派人给扎尔送信：我没有听你的话，如今落得这样的下场，快来救我吧。

Zāl hastened to court and earnestly urged the king: 'Māzandarān is the land of demons; they bewitch men with magic. No king before you has dared to go; do not risk the champions of Iran and your own life.' But Kay Kāvus said: 'I am mightier than Jamshīd and Fereydūn, with more champions and more treasure; what can demons do to me? If you are afraid, stay at home.' Zāl could do nothing but take his leave. Kay Kāvus led his army into Māzandarān, burning and plundering, carrying off treasure and captives. In terror the king of Māzandarān sent for the White Demon, chief of the demons. In the night the White Demon summoned a black cloud that plunged the world into darkness, then rained down stones and hail, and the Iranian army fell into confusion in the dark. When day came Kay Kāvus and all his champions were blind, and the demons seized them and cast them into a dungeon. In the darkness Kay Kāvus repented bitterly and secretly sent a message to Zāl: 'I did not listen to you, and this is my fate; come quickly and save me.'

### 03 · 两条路与狮子 / Two roads, and the lion

扎尔收到信，对儿子鲁斯塔姆说：国王落难了，只有你能救他。从这里到马赞德兰有两条路：一条长而安全，要走六个月；另一条短而凶险，只要两个星期，可路上有狮子、恶龙、女巫和魔鬼。鲁斯塔姆说：我选那条近路，愿神保佑我。他穿上虎皮做的战袍，带上祖父的铁杵，骑上拉赫什出发了，一天走两天的路程。第一天傍晚，他在一片芦苇荡边打了一头野驴，烤来吃了，然后摘下拉赫什的辔头，让它去吃草，自己躺在芦苇丛里睡着了。半夜，一头凶猛的狮子回到它的巢穴，看见熟睡的人和马，就向拉赫什扑过来。拉赫什像火一样跳起来，用前蹄猛击狮子的头，用牙齿咬住狮子的背，把它摔在地上踩死了。鲁斯塔姆醒来，看见死狮，却责备拉赫什说：谁让你去和狮子打？要是你被它咬死了，我怎么背着铁杵、穿着战袍走到马赞德兰去？下次遇到危险，先把我叫醒。

When Zāl received the letter he said to his son Rostam: 'The king is in peril, and only you can save him. Two roads lead to Māzandarān: one long and safe, six months' journey; the other short and perilous, only two weeks, but beset by lions, dragons, witches and demons.' 'I choose the short road,' said Rostam; 'may God protect me.' He put on his war-coat of tiger-skin, took his grandfather's mace, mounted Rakhsh and set out, covering two days' journey in each day. On the first evening he killed a wild ass by a reed-bed, roasted it and ate, then took off Rakhsh's bridle to let him graze and lay down to sleep among the reeds. At midnight a fierce lion came back to its lair, saw the sleeping man and horse, and sprang at Rakhsh. Rakhsh leapt up like fire, struck the lion's head with his forefeet, seized its back in his teeth, threw it down and trampled it to death. Waking to find the dead lion, Rostam scolded Rakhsh: 'Who told you to fight a lion? If it had killed you, how would I have walked to Māzandarān with my mace and war-coat? Next time danger comes, wake me first.'

### 04 · 沙漠中的公羊 / The ram in the desert

第二天，鲁斯塔姆走进了一片炎热的沙漠，烈日像火一样烤着大地，四周没有一滴水，也没有一片树荫。走了很久，人和马都渴得精疲力竭，拉赫什的脚步越来越慢，鲁斯塔姆也从马上摔了下来，躺在滚烫的沙子上，舌头干裂，眼看就要渴死了。他抬起头，向造物主祈祷：神啊，你知道我是为了去救国王才走上这条路的，我不怕任何敌人，可是我不愿意渴死在这荒野里，求你救救我吧。就在这时，一只肥壮的公羊从他身边走过。鲁斯塔姆想：这只羊一定是去喝水的。他挣扎着站起来，拿着剑跟在公羊后面，走了不远，果然看见了一眼清澈的泉水。他和拉赫什痛痛快快地喝足了水，洗净了身上的尘土。鲁斯塔姆没有伤害那只公羊，而是向天空祝福它，感谢造物主派它来救了自己的性命。

The next day Rostam entered a scorching desert; the sun burned the earth like fire, and there was not a drop of water nor a patch of shade. After a long march man and horse were spent with thirst; Rakhsh slowed, and Rostam fell from his back and lay on the burning sand, his tongue cracked, close to dying of thirst. He raised his head and prayed: 'O God, you know I took this road to save the king. I fear no enemy, but I would not die of thirst in this wilderness; save me.' At that moment a fat ram passed close by him. 'This ram must be going to drink,' thought Rostam. He struggled to his feet and followed it, sword in hand, and not far off he found a clear spring. He and Rakhsh drank their fill and washed off the dust. Rostam did not harm the ram, but blessed it towards heaven and thanked the Creator for sending it to save his life.

### 05 · 恶龙 / The dragon

那天夜里，鲁斯塔姆睡在泉水边，叮嘱拉赫什：不要再自己去和野兽打斗，有危险就叫醒我。半夜，一条巨大的恶龙从洞里爬了出来，它长八十腕尺，吐出的气像火一样，连大象都不敢靠近这片草地。拉赫什看见恶龙，连忙跑到鲁斯塔姆身边，用蹄子刨地、嘶叫，把他叫醒。鲁斯塔姆醒来四处张望，恶龙却用魔法隐去了身形，他什么也没看见，就骂拉赫什打扰他睡觉，又躺下了。恶龙又出现了，拉赫什又把他叫醒，恶龙又不见了，鲁斯塔姆大怒，说：你再这样无缘无故地吵我，我就砍下你的头，自己走到马赞德兰去。恶龙第三次出现，拉赫什不顾一切地又跑来叫醒他。这一次，造物主让鲁斯塔姆看见了恶龙。他拔出剑，和恶龙搏斗起来，恶龙太强大了，几乎要把他压倒。拉赫什冲上去，一口咬住恶龙的肩膀，把它的皮肉撕开，鲁斯塔姆趁机一剑砍下了恶龙的头。他向拉赫什道歉，感谢这匹忠诚的马救了他的命。

That night Rostam slept by the spring, telling Rakhsh not to fight beasts alone again but to wake him at any danger. At midnight a huge dragon crawled from its den, eighty cubits long, its breath like fire, so fearsome that not even elephants dared enter that meadow. Seeing it, Rakhsh ran to Rostam, pawing the ground and neighing to wake him. Rostam woke and looked around, but the dragon had hidden itself by magic, and he saw nothing; he cursed Rakhsh for disturbing his sleep and lay down again. The dragon came again, Rakhsh woke him again, and again it vanished. Rostam in fury said: 'Wake me again for nothing and I will cut off your head and walk to Māzandarān myself.' The dragon came a third time, and Rakhsh, heedless of the threat, ran again to wake him. This time the Creator let Rostam see the dragon. He drew his sword and fought it, but the dragon was so strong it nearly crushed him. Rakhsh rushed in, bit the dragon's shoulder and tore its flesh, and Rostam struck off its head. He begged Rakhsh's pardon and thanked the faithful horse for saving his life.

### 06 · 女巫 / The witch

第四天，鲁斯塔姆穿过一片美丽的山谷，来到一眼泉水边，只见树荫下铺着华丽的毯子，摆满了烤好的羊肉、面包、水果和一瓶美酒，旁边还放着一把琴。原来这是女巫们设下的筵席。鲁斯塔姆饿了，坐下来大吃大喝，拿起琴弹了起来，唱道：我鲁斯塔姆是个漂泊的人，一生没有安宁的日子，每天不是和狮子打，就是和恶龙斗，很少能见到这样的筵席和美丽的花园。歌声传开，一个女巫听见了，把自己变成一个美丽的少女，脸颊像春天的花朵，走到他的身边坐下。鲁斯塔姆很高兴，倒了一杯酒递给她，并说出了造物主的名字，感谢神赐给他美酒和佳人。可是女巫一听见神的名字，脸色就变了，她的美貌消失了，变回了一个又老又丑的黑脸巫婆。鲁斯塔姆看穿了她，抛出套索套住她的脖子，拔剑把她砍成了两半。

On the fourth day Rostam crossed a lovely valley to a spring, where beneath the trees rich carpets were spread with roast lamb, bread, fruit and a flask of wine, and a lute lay beside them. It was a feast laid by witches. Hungry, Rostam sat down, ate and drank, took up the lute and sang: 'I, Rostam, am a wanderer who never knows a day of rest; every day I fight lions or dragons, and seldom do I find such a feast and so fair a garden.' A witch heard the song, changed herself into a beautiful girl with cheeks like spring flowers, and came and sat beside him. Pleased, Rostam poured a cup of wine and gave it to her, naming the Creator and thanking God for the wine and the fair companion. But at the name of God the witch's face changed; her beauty vanished and she became again a black-faced, wrinkled old hag. Seeing through her, Rostam cast his lasso around her neck and cut her in two with his sword.

### 07 · 乌拉德 / Olād

第五天，鲁斯塔姆走进了一片没有日月、伸手不见五指的黑暗之地，他让拉赫什自己找路，走了很久，终于重见光明，来到一片绿油油的田野。他又累又困，就脱下被汗浸透的战袍，让拉赫什在田里吃庄稼，自己躺下睡着了。看守田地的人跑来，用棍子打他的脚，骂他放马糟蹋庄稼。鲁斯塔姆醒来，一言不发，抓住那人，一把拧下了他的两只耳朵。看田人捂着头跑去向当地的领主乌拉德哭诉。乌拉德带着一群武士赶来，要惩罚这个陌生人。鲁斯塔姆骑上拉赫什迎战，把乌拉德的武士杀得四散奔逃，又抛出套索，活捉了乌拉德。鲁斯塔姆对他说：如果你给我带路，带我找到白魔的洞穴和国王被关押的地方，等我打败了马赞德兰，我就让你做这片土地的国王；如果你骗我，我就要你的命。乌拉德答应了，从此做了鲁斯塔姆的向导。

On the fifth day Rostam entered a land of darkness without sun or moon, where he could not see his hand before him; he let Rakhsh find the way, and after a long time came out into the light, to green fields. Weary and sleepy, he took off his sweat-soaked war-coat, let Rakhsh graze in the crops and lay down to sleep. The keeper of the fields came running, struck his feet with a stick and cursed him for letting his horse spoil the crops. Rostam woke, said nothing, seized the man and wrenched off both his ears. Holding his head, the keeper ran to complain to the local lord, Olād. Olād came with a band of warriors to punish the stranger. Rostam rode out on Rakhsh, scattered Olād's men, and caught Olād alive with his lasso. 'If you guide me to the White Demon's cave and to where the king is held,' said Rostam, 'when I have conquered Māzandarān I will make you king of this land; if you deceive me, your life is forfeit.' Olād agreed, and became Rostam's guide.

### 08 · 阿尔章魔 / The demon Arzhang

乌拉德带着鲁斯塔姆向前走，告诉他：前面是魔鬼阿尔章的营地，他是马赞德兰魔鬼军队的首领之一，守着通往国王监牢的道路；过了那里，再往前是七座大山，白魔就住在山中的一个洞穴里，他身边有成千上万的魔鬼守卫。鲁斯塔姆把乌拉德绑在一棵树上，等到天亮，独自骑着拉赫什冲进了阿尔章的营地，发出一声雷鸣般的怒吼，震得魔鬼们心惊胆战。阿尔章听见吼声，从帐篷里冲出来迎战。鲁斯塔姆策马上前，伸手抓住阿尔章的头和耳朵，把他的头从身子上拧了下来，扔到魔鬼们中间。魔鬼们看见首领的头滚落在地，吓得魂飞魄散，四散逃命，许多魔鬼在逃跑中自相践踏而死。鲁斯塔姆杀散了阿尔章的军队，回到乌拉德身边，给他松了绑，让他继续带路，去找被关押的国王。

Olād led Rostam on and told him: 'Ahead is the camp of the demon Arzhang, one of the chiefs of the demon host of Māzandarān, who guards the road to the king's prison; beyond it lie seven great mountains, and in a cave among them lives the White Demon with thousands of demons on guard.' Rostam tied Olād to a tree, waited for dawn, then rode alone on Rakhsh into Arzhang's camp with a roar like thunder that shook the demons' hearts. Hearing it, Arzhang rushed out of his tent to fight. Rostam spurred forward, seized Arzhang by the head and ears, wrenched his head from his body and flung it among the demons. Seeing their chief's head roll on the ground, the demons fled in terror, many trampling one another to death as they ran. Having scattered Arzhang's host, Rostam went back to Olād, untied him, and bade him lead on to the imprisoned king.

### 09 · 白魔的洞穴 / The White Demon's cave

鲁斯塔姆找到了被关押的凯·卡乌斯和伊朗的勇士们，他们都瞎了眼睛，在黑暗中受苦。国王听见鲁斯塔姆的声音，悲喜交加，对他说：只有白魔心脏里的血滴进我们的眼睛，我们才能重见光明。鲁斯塔姆就让乌拉德带他去白魔的住处。他们越过七座大山，来到一个深不见底的洞穴前，洞口有许多魔鬼守卫。乌拉德说：等太阳升到最高、天气最热的时候再进去，那时魔鬼们都会睡觉。鲁斯塔姆等到正午，冲向洞口，杀死了守卫的魔鬼，走进了漆黑的洞中。洞里黑得像地狱一样，他擦了擦眼睛，才看见一个巨大的身影躺在那里，像一座山，脸是黑的，头发是白的，那就是白魔。鲁斯塔姆没有趁他睡着下手，大吼一声把他叫醒。白魔跳起来，拿起一块磨盘大的石头冲了过来。两人在洞中殊死搏斗，鲁斯塔姆一剑砍断了白魔的一条腿，白魔仍用一条腿扑上来和他扭打，鲜血和汗水流满了洞穴。最后，鲁斯塔姆把白魔举起来摔在地上，用匕首刺进他的胸膛，挖出了他的肝。

Rostam found Kay Kāvus and the champions of Iran in their prison, all blind and suffering in darkness. Hearing Rostam's voice, the king wept with joy and said: 'Only blood from the White Demon's heart dropped in our eyes can restore our sight.' So Rostam had Olād guide him to the White Demon's lair. They crossed the seven mountains to the mouth of a bottomless cave guarded by many demons. Olād said: 'Wait until the sun is highest and the heat greatest; then the demons sleep.' At noon Rostam rushed the entrance, killed the guards and went into the black cave. It was dark as hell; he rubbed his eyes and at last saw a huge form lying there like a mountain, its face black and its hair white: the White Demon. Rostam would not strike him sleeping, but woke him with a great shout. The White Demon sprang up, seized a stone like a millstone and charged. They fought for their lives in the cave; Rostam cut off one of the demon's legs, yet still it grappled him on one leg, and blood and sweat ran through the cave. At last Rostam lifted the White Demon, dashed him to the ground, plunged his dagger into his breast, and tore out his liver.

### 10 · 重见光明 / Sight restored

鲁斯塔姆带着白魔的肝走出洞穴，阳光照在他满是鲜血的身上，乌拉德看见他，吓得以为是魔鬼出来了，直到他开口说话才放下心来。鲁斯塔姆说：我已经杀死了白魔，打开了马赞德兰的大门，你的功劳我不会忘记。两人一起回到关押国王的地方。鲁斯塔姆把白魔肝中的血滴进凯·卡乌斯的眼睛里，国王的眼睛立刻亮了起来，又看见了阳光，看见了眼前的鲁斯塔姆。接着，鲁斯塔姆又把血滴进伊朗勇士们的眼睛里，所有的人都重见了光明。被囚禁的伊朗军队欢呼起来，他们拥抱鲁斯塔姆，赞美他是伊朗的救星，是勇士中的勇士。凯·卡乌斯坐在宝座上，命令摆设盛大的宴席，一连七天庆祝胜利，向造物主献上感恩的祷告。他对鲁斯塔姆说：没有你，就没有伊朗的国王。

Rostam came out of the cave with the White Demon's liver, the sunlight on his blood-soaked body; Olād, seeing him, took him for a demon and was terrified until he spoke. 'I have killed the White Demon and opened the gates of Māzandarān,' said Rostam; 'I will not forget your service.' Together they went back to where the king was held. Rostam dropped blood from the White Demon's liver into Kay Kāvus's eyes, and at once the king's eyes were bright again; he saw the sunlight and Rostam before him. Then Rostam dropped the blood into the eyes of the champions of Iran, and all of them saw again. The captive Iranian host cheered, embraced Rostam and praised him as the saviour of Iran, the champion of champions. Kay Kāvus took his throne and ordered a great feast to celebrate the victory for seven days, with prayers of thanks to the Creator. He said to Rostam: 'But for you, Iran would have no king.'

### 11 · 化为岩石的国王 / The king who became a rock

凯·卡乌斯恢复了力量，写信给马赞德兰的国王，命令他前来投降，交出财宝，做伊朗的属国。马赞德兰国王收到信，傲慢地拒绝了，召集了魔鬼和人组成的大军，带着战象前来迎战。两军在平原上激战了七天，不分胜负。第八天，鲁斯塔姆冲到马赞德兰国王面前，举起长矛刺去，眼看就要刺中，那位会魔法的国王忽然把自己变成了一块巨大的岩石，长矛刺在石头上，再也伤不了他。伊朗的勇士们都惊呆了，谁也搬不动这块岩石。鲁斯塔姆跳下马，一个人把岩石扛了起来，一直扛回伊朗的营地，扔在国王的帐篷前，对着岩石说：快出来，否则我就用铁锤把你砸成碎块，扔到火里烧掉！岩石里的国王害怕了，从石头里走了出来，变回了人形。凯·卡乌斯下令处死了这个作恶多端的魔法师。鲁斯塔姆履行了诺言，请国王封乌拉德为马赞德兰的统治者。伊朗的大军满载着战利品，凯旋回国。

Restored to strength, Kay Kāvus wrote to the king of Māzandarān commanding him to come and submit, surrender his treasure and become a vassal of Iran. The king of Māzandarān haughtily refused, gathered a host of demons and men with war-elephants, and came out to battle. For seven days the armies fought on the plain without either winning. On the eighth day Rostam reached the king of Māzandarān and thrust his spear at him, but just as it was about to strike, the sorcerer king turned himself into a huge rock; the spear struck stone and could not harm him. The Iranian champions were astonished, and none could move the rock. Rostam leapt down, lifted the rock alone, carried it all the way back to the Iranian camp, threw it down before the king's tent and said to it: 'Come out, or I will smash you to pieces with my hammer and throw you into the fire!' The king inside the rock was afraid, came out of the stone and took human shape again. Kay Kāvus had the wicked sorcerer put to death. Keeping his promise, Rostam asked the king to make Olād ruler of Māzandarān, and the army of Iran returned home laden with spoils.

### 12 · 飞天的宝座 / The flying throne

可是凯·卡乌斯并没有因为这次教训变得谨慎。后来，一个魔鬼又来迷惑他，对他说：你已经统治了整个大地，为什么不去统治天空呢？天上的日月星辰，难道不该属于你吗？凯·卡乌斯又动了心。他问智者们怎样才能飞上天空，最后想出一个办法：他让人从鹰巢里捉来四只小鹰，用最好的肉喂养长大，然后造了一个宝座，在宝座的四角各竖一根长矛，矛尖上挂着一块羊肉，再把四只饿了好几天的鹰绑在宝座的四角。鹰想吃矛尖上的肉，拼命往上飞，就把宝座带上了天空。凯·卡乌斯坐在宝座上，越飞越高，越过了云层。可是鹰飞累了，再也飞不动了，宝座从空中坠落下来，跌进了一片荒凉的森林。凯·卡乌斯侥幸没有摔死，却困在林中又饿又怕。鲁斯塔姆和勇士们四处寻找，终于找到了他。勇士们严厉地责备他：你一次又一次地听信魔鬼，像个疯子一样。凯·卡乌斯羞愧难当，从此闭门忏悔了许多日子。

But Kay Kāvus did not grow cautious from this lesson. Later a demon came again to tempt him: 'You rule the whole earth; why not rule the sky? Should not the sun, moon and stars be yours as well?' Again Kay Kāvus was tempted. He asked his wise men how to fly to the sky, and at last hit on a plan: he had four young eagles taken from their nests and reared on the choicest meat; then he had a throne made, set a long spear upright at each of its corners with a piece of mutton hanging from each spear-point, and bound four eagles that had been starved for days to its four corners. Straining for the meat above them, the eagles flew upward and carried the throne into the sky. Kay Kāvus rose higher and higher, above the clouds. But the eagles tired and could fly no more, and the throne fell from the sky into a desolate forest. Kay Kāvus escaped death but lay trapped in the forest, hungry and afraid. Rostam and the champions searched far and wide and at last found him. The champions rebuked him sharply: 'Again and again you listen to demons; you act like a madman.' Ashamed, Kay Kāvus shut himself away in penitence for many days.
