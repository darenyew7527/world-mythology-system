## 阅读边界

《史记·五帝本纪》说：神农氏衰落，诸侯互相攻伐，轩辕操练军队征讨不服者。蚩尤最为暴虐，作乱不听帝命。轩辕征召诸侯之兵，在涿鹿的原野擒杀蚩尤，诸侯于是尊他为天子，这就是黄帝。

The Shiji's Annals of the Five Emperors say that as Shennong's house declined and the lords fought one another, Xuanyuan trained troops. Chiyou, the most violent, rebelled against the ruler's command; Xuanyuan levied the lords' armies, captured and killed him in the wilds of Zhuolu, and was honoured as Son of Heaven — the Yellow Emperor.

### 01 · 神农氏衰落 / Shennong's line declines

黄帝是少典的儿子，姓公孙，名轩辕。他那个时代，神农氏的统治衰落了，诸侯互相攻打、欺压百姓，神农氏却无力征讨。轩辕于是操练军队，去讨伐不肯朝贡的诸侯。

The Yellow Emperor was the son of Shaodian, surnamed Gongsun and named Xuanyuan. In his time the rule of Shennong had declined; the lords attacked one another and oppressed the people, and Shennong could not punish them. So Xuanyuan trained troops to subdue the lords who would not pay tribute.

### 02 · 蚩尤作乱 / Chiyou rebels

诸侯之中，蚩尤最为凶暴，没有人能制服他。蚩尤起来作乱，不听从帝王的命令。

Among the lords Chiyou was the most violent, and no one could subdue him. Chiyou rose in rebellion and would not obey the ruler's command.

> 证据说明：《五帝本纪》“蚩尤作乱，不用帝命”。

### 03 · 涿鹿之野 / In the wilds of Zhuolu

轩辕征召各路诸侯的军队，在涿鹿的原野上与蚩尤交战，终于把蚩尤擒住并杀死。

Xuanyuan called up the armies of the lords and fought Chiyou in the wilds of Zhuolu, and at last captured and killed him.

### 04 · 尊为天子 / Honoured as Son of Heaven

诸侯们都尊奉轩辕为天子，取代了神农氏，这就是黄帝。

The lords all honoured Xuanyuan as Son of Heaven in place of Shennong: this was the Yellow Emperor.
