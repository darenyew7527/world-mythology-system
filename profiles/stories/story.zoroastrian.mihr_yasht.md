## 阅读边界

阿维斯塔第十颂《密赫尔颂》：阿胡拉·马兹达说，他创造密特拉时，使他与自己同样值得祭祀和祈祷；对密特拉说谎、背弃契约的人会给整个国家带来死亡；密特拉有千只耳朵、万只眼睛，在不朽的快马太阳之前最先越过哈拉山。

The Mihr Yasht, tenth hymn of the Avesta: Ahura Mazda says he made Mithra as worthy of sacrifice and prayer as himself; whoever lies to Mithra and breaks the contract brings death to the whole land; Mithra has a thousand ears and ten thousand eyes and is the first god to rise over Mount Hara, before the swift-horsed sun.

### 01 · 与造物主同样尊贵 / As worthy as his maker

颂歌一开头，阿胡拉·马兹达对查拉图斯特拉说：我创造广阔牧场之主密特拉时，使他与我自己一样，值得献祭、值得祈祷。

The hymn opens with Ahura Mazda telling Zarathustra: when I created Mithra, lord of wide pastures, I made him as worthy of sacrifice and as worthy of prayer as myself.

> 证据说明：Yt 10.1。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.zoro.mithra_created`

### 02 · 不可背弃契约 / Never break the contract

对密特拉说谎、背弃契约的恶人，会给整个国家带来死亡。所以契约绝不可背弃——不论是与信徒订立的，还是与不信者订立的。

The wretch who lies to Mithra, breaking the contract, brings death upon the whole land. So the contract must never be broken, whether made with one of the faithful or with an unbeliever.

> 证据说明：Yt 10.2。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.zoro.mithra_contract`
> 未知／边界：“契约”是译者对 miθra 一词的理解，学界另有讨论。

### 03 · 千耳万眼 / A thousand ears, ten thousand eyes

颂歌赞美密特拉说真话，是集会中的首领；他有一千只耳朵，一万只眼睛，什么都瞒不过他。

The hymn praises Mithra as truth-speaking, a chief in assemblies, with a thousand ears and ten thousand eyes — nothing escapes him.

> 证据说明：Yt 10.7。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.zoro.mithra_eyes`

### 04 · 越过哈拉山 / Over Mount Hara

每天清晨，他是天上诸神中第一个越过哈拉山的，比不朽的、驾着快马的太阳还要早。

Each morning he is the first of the heavenly gods to reach over Mount Hara, ahead of the undying, swift-horsed sun.

> 证据说明：Yt 10.13。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.zoro.mithra_hara`
