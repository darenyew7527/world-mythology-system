## 阅读边界

弗丽嘉是奥丁的妻子，知道一切命运。她的儿子巴德尔梦见自己有危险，她便让火、水、金属、石头、草木和野兽都发誓不伤害他——唯独漏掉了一株幼小的槲寄生。巴德尔最后正是被槲寄生杀死的。

Frigg, Odin's wife, knows all fates. When her son Baldr dreams of danger, she makes fire, water, metals, stones, plants and beasts swear not to harm him — all except a young shoot of mistletoe. In the end it is mistletoe that kills Baldr.

### 01 · 知晓命运的女神 / The goddess who knows all fates

弗丽嘉是奥丁的妻子。斯诺里说，她知道一切人的命运，只是从不说出来。

Frigg is Odin's wife. Snorri says she knows the fates of all, though she does not speak them aloud.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第20章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.frigg_wife`

### 02 · 万物发誓 / Everything swears

巴德尔做了不祥的梦。弗丽嘉便去找火、水、铁和各种金属、石头、土地、树木、疾病、野兽、鸟和蛇，让它们都发誓不伤害巴德尔。只有长在英灵殿西边的一株小槲寄生，她觉得太小，没有让它发誓。

Baldr has ominous dreams, so Frigg makes fire, water, iron and all metals, stones, earth, trees, sicknesses, beasts, birds and serpents swear not to harm him. Only a young mistletoe shoot she thinks too small to ask.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第49章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.frigg_oaths`

### 03 · 槲寄生 / The mistletoe

众神以为巴德尔刀枪不入，便拿东西扔他取乐。然而那株没有发誓的槲寄生被做成了投枪，巴德尔被它刺中而死。

Believing Baldr unharmable, the gods amuse themselves by throwing things at him. But the mistletoe that never swore is made into a dart, and Baldr is struck down and dies.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第49章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.baldr_death`
