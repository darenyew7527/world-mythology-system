## 阅读边界

海姆达尔住在彩虹桥边的希敏约格，替众神守着桥，防备山巨人。他睡得比鸟还少，夜里能看见一百里远，连草和羊毛生长的声音都听得见。他有一支加拉尔号角，吹响时所有世界都能听到。人们还说他是九位姐妹母亲的儿子。

Heimdallr lives at Himinbjörg beside the rainbow bridge Bifröst and guards it for the gods against the mountain-giants. He needs less sleep than a bird, sees a hundred leagues by night, and hears grass and wool growing. His horn Gjallarhorn can be heard in all worlds, and he is called the son of nine mothers who were sisters.

### 01 · 桥头的守望者 / Watchman at the bridge

海姆达尔是众神的守望者。他住在彩虹桥边一个叫希敏约格的地方，在天的尽头守着桥，防止山巨人闯进来。

Heimdallr is the watchman of the gods. He lives at Himinbjörg by Bifröst, at the edge of heaven, guarding the bridge against the mountain-giants.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第27章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.heimdall_watchman`

### 02 · 看得远，听得清 / Keen eyes and ears

他需要的睡眠比鸟还少，无论白天黑夜都能看见一百里远；他还能听见草在地上生长、羊毛在羊身上生长的声音。

He needs less sleep than a bird and sees a hundred leagues by day or night; he can hear grass growing in the ground and wool growing on sheep.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第27章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.heimdall_senses`

### 03 · 加拉尔号角与九位母亲 / Gjallarhorn and nine mothers

海姆达尔有一支号角，叫加拉尔号角，吹响时所有世界都能听见。斯诺里还说，他是九位母亲的儿子，而这九位母亲是姐妹。

Heimdallr has a horn called Gjallarhorn, whose blast is heard in all worlds. Snorri also says he is the son of nine mothers, who were sisters.

> 证据说明：《欺骗古鲁菲》第27章。
> Claim: `claim.v0310.norse.heimdall_horn`
