## 阅读边界

伊朗的勇士萨姆生了一个满头白发的儿子，他以为这是魔鬼的孩子，羞愧地把婴儿丢弃在厄尔布尔士山上。神鸟西摩格把孩子带回巢中抚养长大。多年后，萨姆在梦中受到责备，悔恨地上山寻子，西摩格把扎尔送下山，赠他一根羽毛，危急时焚烧就能召唤她。扎尔在喀布尔爱上了国王梅赫拉布的女儿鲁达贝，她在塔上放下长发让他攀援，他却用套索登上了塔楼。因为梅赫拉布是暴君佐哈克的后裔，萨姆和国王马努切赫尔都反对这门婚事；星象家却预言这对夫妇会生下一位伟大的英雄，王后辛多赫特亲自去说服萨姆，扎尔在宫廷里答出了智者们的谜语，婚事终于成就。鲁达贝怀了一个巨大的孩子，难产垂危，扎尔焚烧羽毛，西摩格教他剖开母亲的腹侧取出婴儿，这就是鲁斯塔姆。少年鲁斯塔姆一杵打死了发狂的白象，又从马群中套住了一匹玫瑰色斑纹的小马——拉赫什，从此人与马形影不离。

Sām, champion of Iran, has a son born with white hair; thinking him a demon's child, he exposes the baby in shame on Mount Alborz, where the great bird Simorgh raises him. Years later, rebuked in a dream, Sām repents and seeks his son; the Simorgh brings Zāl down and gives him a feather to burn when he needs her. In Kābul Zāl falls in love with Rūdābeh, daughter of King Mehrāb; she lets down her long hair from her tower for him to climb, but he climbs by his lasso. Because Mehrāb descends from the tyrant Zahhāk, Sām and King Manūchehr both oppose the match; but the astrologers foretell a great hero from it, Queen Sindokht goes herself to win Sām over, and Zāl answers the riddles of the king's wise men, so the marriage is made. Rūdābeh carries a giant child and is near death in labour; Zāl burns the feather, and the Simorgh teaches him to open her side and draw out the child: Rostam. As a boy Rostam kills a raging white elephant with one blow, and from the herds he lassos a rose-dappled colt, Rakhsh, who will never leave his side.

### 01 · 白发之子 / The white-haired son

在马努切赫尔国王统治伊朗的时候，扎布尔斯坦的领主萨姆是国王最英勇的勇士，他什么都不缺，只盼望有一个儿子。终于，他的妻子生下了一个男孩，孩子的脸像月亮一样美丽，身体健壮，可是满头的头发却像老人一样雪白。侍女们不敢告诉萨姆，过了好几天，才有一个侍女鼓起勇气去报喜。萨姆来看孩子，见他一头白发，心中又羞又怕，说：人们看见这个孩子，会说什么呢？他们会说这是魔鬼的孩子，会嘲笑我生了一个怪物。我怎么能把这样的儿子带到勇士们面前？他转过脸去，命令仆人把孩子抱走，丢到远离人烟的厄尔布尔士山上，让野兽去处置他。仆人们照办了，把这个刚出生的婴儿放在了荒凉的山顶上，任凭风吹日晒。《列王纪》是诗人菲尔多西在公元一千年前后写成的长篇史诗，讲述伊朗历代国王与英雄的事迹；萨姆、扎尔、鲁斯塔姆祖孙三代，是其中最有名的英雄家族，而这个家族的故事，就从一次抛弃开始。

When King Manūchehr ruled Iran, Sām, lord of Zābulistān, was the bravest of his champions; he lacked nothing except a son. At last his wife bore a boy, his face fair as the moon and his body strong, but his hair as white as an old man's. The women dared not tell Sām, and it was days before one of them found the courage to bring him the news. Sām came to see the child, and at the sight of the white hair he was filled with shame and fear. 'What will people say when they see this child? They will say he is a demon's child and mock me for fathering a monster. How can I bring such a son before the warriors?' He turned away and commanded his servants to carry the child off and leave him on Mount Alborz, far from all human dwellings, for the beasts to deal with. The servants obeyed and left the newborn on the bare summit, exposed to wind and sun. The Shahnameh, the Book of Kings, is the long epic the poet Ferdowsi completed around the year 1000, telling of the kings and heroes of Iran; Sām, Zāl and Rostam, grandfather, father and son, are its most famous family of heroes — and their story begins with an abandonment.

### 02 · 西摩格的巢 / The Simorgh's nest

厄尔布尔士山的高处，住着一只神鸟，名叫西摩格。它的巢筑在山巅，离星星很近，巢里有它嗷嗷待哺的雏鸟。西摩格飞出去为孩子们觅食，看见山坡上躺着一个婴儿，饿得哭泣，正在吮吸自己的手指。它俯冲下来，用爪子抓起婴儿，带回巢中，本想把他喂给雏鸟。可是当它把婴儿放在巢里，看着这个哭泣的孩子时，心中忽然生出了怜悯。它不仅没有伤害他，还把他和自己的孩子们一起抚养，给他最柔软的肉吃。就这样，扎尔在西摩格的巢里一天天长大，长成了一个高大英俊的青年。路过山下的商队常常抬头看见山上有一个人影，在神鸟的巢边走动，消息渐渐传遍了四方，说厄尔布尔士山上有一个由神鸟养大的年轻人。厄尔布尔士山在伊朗北部，是伊朗传说中最高、最神秘的山，西摩格就住在那里，被人们视为一只充满智慧的神鸟。

High on Mount Alborz lived a great bird called the Simorgh. Her nest was on the summit, close to the stars, and in it were her hungry young. Flying out to find food for them, she saw a baby lying on the slope, crying with hunger and sucking his fingers. She swooped down, caught him up in her claws and carried him to her nest, meaning to feed him to her young. But when she set the baby in the nest and looked at the weeping child, pity stirred in her heart. Far from harming him, she raised him with her own young, giving him the tenderest meat. So Zāl grew up day by day in the Simorgh's nest into a tall and handsome youth. Caravans passing below often looked up and saw a figure moving about beside the great bird's nest, and word spread far and wide that on Mount Alborz there lived a young man raised by the Simorgh. Mount Alborz in the north of Iran is the highest and most mysterious mountain of Iranian legend, and the Simorgh who lives there is held to be a bird of great wisdom.

### 03 · 萨姆的梦 / Sām's dream

有一天夜里，萨姆梦见一个骑着阿拉伯骏马的人从印度方向疾驰而来，告诉他：你的儿子还活着。萨姆醒来，把梦告诉了祭司们，问他们这是什么意思。祭司们说：连野兽和飞鸟都会照料自己的幼崽，你却抛弃了自己的儿子，只因为他有白头发。这是不义的，你应当去把他找回来。萨姆心中悔恨。第二天夜里，他又做了一个梦：一个英俊的青年举着旗帜，带着大军向他走来，一位贤者在旁边责备他说：你连自己的孩子都不要，又怎么配做勇士的首领？萨姆从梦中惊醒，再也坐不住了，带着随从赶到厄尔布尔士山下。他抬头望去，看见山顶上神鸟的巢，巢边站着一个青年。可是山峰陡峭，无路可上。萨姆跪在地上，向造物主祈祷，忏悔自己的罪过，求神让他找回儿子。一个曾经因为一头白发抛弃儿子的父亲，如今跪在山下，向造物主请求宽恕。

One night Sām dreamed that a rider on an Arab horse came galloping from the direction of India and told him: 'Your son is alive.' He woke and told the priests his dream, asking what it meant. They said: 'Even the beasts and birds care for their young, yet you cast off your own son only because his hair was white. It was unjust; you must go and find him.' Sām was filled with remorse. The next night he dreamed again: a handsome youth came towards him with a banner at the head of an army, and a wise man beside him rebuked Sām: 'You would not keep your own child; how are you fit to lead warriors?' Sām woke with a start and could rest no longer; with his followers he hurried to the foot of Mount Alborz. Looking up, he saw the great bird's nest on the summit and a youth standing beside it. But the peak was sheer, with no path to the top. Sām knelt and prayed to the Creator, confessing his sin and begging to be given back his son. A father who had cast off his son for his white hair now knelt at the foot of the mountain and begged the Creator's forgiveness.

### 04 · 扎尔回到人间 / Zāl returns to the world of men

西摩格看见山下的萨姆，知道他是来找儿子的。它对扎尔说：你的父亲来了，他是世上最伟大的勇士，你该回到他身边去，回到人间去了。扎尔流着泪说：难道你厌倦了我吗？你的巢就是我的家，你就是我的母亲。西摩格说：我并不是要赶你走，我只是想让你得到你应有的荣耀。我给你取名叫“达斯坦”。它从自己的胸前拔下一根羽毛，交给扎尔说：带着这根羽毛，如果有一天你遇到危难，就把它烧掉，我会立刻来到你身边。说完，它用翅膀托着扎尔飞下山峰，把他放在父亲面前。萨姆抱住儿子，哭着请求他原谅。他给儿子取名为扎尔，意思是“白发老人”。父子俩一同回到扎布尔斯坦，后来又到了马努切赫尔国王的宫廷，国王召来星象家为扎尔占卜，星象家说：这个年轻人将成为伊朗最伟大的勇士之一。

The Simorgh saw Sām below and knew he had come for his son. She said to Zāl: 'Your father has come, the greatest of warriors; you must go back to him and to the world of men.' Zāl wept: 'Are you weary of me? Your nest is my home and you are my mother.' The Simorgh said: 'I do not send you away; I want you to have the glory that is yours. I name you Dastān.' She plucked a feather from her breast and gave it to him: 'Keep this feather. If ever you are in danger, burn it, and I will be with you at once.' Then she bore Zāl down from the peak on her wings and set him before his father. Sām embraced his son and wept, begging his forgiveness, and named him Zāl, meaning the white-haired old one. Father and son returned to Zābulistān and later went to the court of King Manūchehr, who had his astrologers read the youth's stars; they said he would be among the greatest champions of Iran.

### 05 · 喀布尔的公主 / The princess of Kābul

萨姆奉国王之命出征，把扎布尔斯坦交给扎尔治理。扎尔四处巡游，来到了喀布尔。喀布尔的国王梅赫拉布出城迎接他，两人一见如故。梅赫拉布是一个英俊而有智慧的国王，可是他的祖先是那个肩上长着两条毒蛇、以人脑为食的暴君佐哈克。扎尔的随从中有人对他说：梅赫拉布有一个女儿，名叫鲁达贝，她比太阳还要美丽，头发像黑色的麝香一样垂到脚踝，身段像柏树一样挺拔。扎尔听了，心中就念念不忘。另一边，梅赫拉布回到宫中，在妻子辛多赫特和女儿面前不住地称赞扎尔，说他英俊、勇敢、谦和，只可惜头发是白的。鲁达贝听了，也悄悄爱上了这个从未见过面的年轻人。她把心事告诉了五个侍女，侍女们先是责备她，说他是被鸟养大的白发人，后来见她心意坚决，就答应帮她。

Sent by the king to war, Sām left Zābulistān in Zāl's charge. Zāl travelled through the land and came to Kābul. Mehrāb, king of Kābul, rode out to meet him, and the two took to each other at once. Mehrāb was a handsome and wise king, but his ancestor was Zahhāk, the tyrant with two serpents growing from his shoulders who fed them on men's brains. One of Zāl's company told him: 'Mehrāb has a daughter named Rūdābeh, fairer than the sun, her hair dark as musk falling to her ankles, her figure straight as a cypress.' From then on Zāl could think of nothing else. Meanwhile Mehrāb went home and praised Zāl again and again before his wife Sindokht and his daughter — handsome, brave and gentle, if only his hair were not white. Hearing it, Rūdābeh too secretly fell in love with the young man she had never seen. She confided in her five maidens; at first they scolded her — a white-haired man raised by a bird! — but seeing her resolved, they promised to help.

### 06 · 长发之绳 / The rope of hair

鲁达贝的侍女们装作去采玫瑰，来到扎尔营地附近的河边。扎尔正好在那里射猎，一箭射下一只水鸟，鸟落在侍女们身边，扎尔的仆人过来拾鸟，侍女们就和他攀谈起来，把公主的美貌和心意都告诉了他。扎尔送给她们许多礼物，约定在夜里与公主相见。到了夜里，扎尔来到鲁达贝的宫殿下面，公主站在高高的塔楼上，月光照在她的脸上。两人隔着塔楼说了许多话。鲁达贝说：英雄啊，你一路走来辛苦了，我把我的头发放下来给你，你抓着它爬上来吧。她解开发辫，把又长又黑的头发从塔上垂了下来。扎尔亲吻着她的头发，说：我怎么能让我心爱的人受这样的痛苦？他取出套索，抛上塔楼的垛口，攀着绳索爬了上去。两人相对而坐，倾诉衷情，彼此立下誓言：除了对方，谁也不嫁、谁也不娶。直到天快亮了，扎尔才依依不舍地离开。

Rūdābeh's maidens went out as if to gather roses by the river near Zāl's camp. Zāl happened to be hunting there and brought down a water-bird with an arrow; it fell near the maidens, and when his servant came to fetch it they fell into talk with him and told him of the princess's beauty and her heart. Zāl sent them back with many gifts and arranged to meet the princess at night. That night he came beneath Rūdābeh's palace, and she stood on her high tower with the moonlight on her face. They spoke long across the height. Rūdābeh said: 'Hero, you have come far and wearily; I will let down my hair to you; take hold of it and climb up.' She unbound her braids and let her long dark hair fall down from the tower. Zāl kissed her hair and said: 'How could I cause my beloved such pain?' He took his lasso, cast it over the battlements and climbed up by the rope. They sat together and opened their hearts, vowing that neither would marry anyone but the other, and only as dawn drew near did Zāl reluctantly leave.

### 07 · 父亲与国王的反对 / The opposition of father and king

扎尔召来祭司和贤者，把心事告诉了他们，他们都沉默不语，因为谁都知道梅赫拉布是佐哈克的后裔，伊朗的英雄怎能和暴君的子孙结亲？扎尔就写信给父亲萨姆，提醒他当年抛弃自己的事，请求父亲成全这门婚事。萨姆读了信，心中十分为难：一边是自己亏欠的儿子，一边是暴君的血统。他召来星象家，让他们观察星辰。星象家们算了很久，笑着回来说：萨姆啊，这是一桩吉祥的婚姻，扎尔和鲁达贝会生下一个力大无穷的英雄，他将为伊朗征服世界，扫除一切邪恶。萨姆听了，心中宽慰，答应去向国王求情。可是马努切赫尔国王听说了这件事，非常担心，怕佐哈克的血脉和萨姆的力量结合在一起，将来会危害伊朗。他命令萨姆率军去攻打喀布尔，铲除梅赫拉布一族。佐哈克是《列王纪》里最可怕的暴君，他统治了一千年，最后被英雄法里东推翻，被锁在德马万德山中，所以他的后代总被伊朗人提防。

Zāl summoned the priests and wise men and told them his heart, but they were silent, for all knew Mehrāb descended from Zahhāk; how could a hero of Iran marry into the tyrant's line? So Zāl wrote to his father, reminding him of how he had once cast him off and begging him to grant this marriage. Reading the letter, Sām was torn between the son he had wronged and the tyrant's blood. He called the astrologers to read the stars. After long reckoning they came back smiling: 'Sām, this is a blessed marriage. Zāl and Rūdābeh will have a son of matchless strength who will conquer the world for Iran and sweep away all evil.' Comforted, Sām promised to plead with the king. But King Manūchehr, hearing of it, was deeply troubled, fearing that Zahhāk's blood joined with Sām's might would one day threaten Iran, and he ordered Sām to march against Kābul and root out Mehrāb's house. Zahhāk is the most terrible tyrant of the Shahnameh, who ruled a thousand years before the hero Fereydūn overthrew him and chained him in Mount Damāvand; so the Iranians always mistrusted his descendants.

### 08 · 王后的出使 / The queen's embassy

消息传到喀布尔，梅赫拉布才知道女儿的事，他勃然大怒，拔出剑来要杀死鲁达贝，说她给全族带来了灭顶之灾。王后辛多赫特拦住了他，说：不要动手，让我去想办法。辛多赫特是一位聪明而勇敢的女子，她带着无数的礼物——金银、宝石、骏马、大象和奴隶——亲自去见正在进军的萨姆。她没有说出自己是谁，只说是喀布尔王派来的使者。萨姆收下礼物，耐心地听她说话。辛多赫特说：喀布尔的人们有什么罪过，要遭受战火？如果国王有罪，就惩罚国王；百姓是无辜的。最后她才说出自己就是鲁达贝的母亲。萨姆被她的智慧和勇气打动，握住她的手，发誓说：只要我活着，就不会让喀布尔受到伤害，扎尔和鲁达贝的婚事我一定会成全。萨姆派扎尔亲自去国王的宫廷求情。

When the news reached Kābul, Mehrāb learned for the first time of his daughter's love. In fury he drew his sword to kill Rūdābeh, saying she had brought ruin on the whole house. Queen Sindokht held him back: 'Do not strike; let me find a way.' Wise and brave, Sindokht went herself to meet the advancing Sām with countless gifts — gold, silver, jewels, horses, elephants and slaves. She did not say who she was, only that she came as the king of Kābul's envoy. Sām accepted the gifts and listened patiently. 'What crime have the people of Kābul committed to suffer war? If the king has sinned, punish the king; the people are innocent.' Only at the end did she reveal that she was Rūdābeh's mother. Moved by her wisdom and courage, Sām took her hand and swore: 'While I live, Kābul shall come to no harm, and I will bring about the marriage of Zāl and Rūdābeh.' He sent Zāl himself to plead at the king's court.

### 09 · 智者的谜语 / The riddles of the wise men

扎尔来到马努切赫尔的宫廷，国王让宫中的智者们用谜语来考验他。第一个智者问：有十二棵高大的柏树，每棵树上长着三十根枝条，这是什么？扎尔回答：这是一年的十二个月，每个月有三十天。第二个智者问：有两匹骏马，一匹黑，一匹白，它们彼此追逐，却永远追不上，这是什么？扎尔说：这是黑夜和白昼。第三个智者问：有三十个骑手，在国王面前经过，有时少了一个，再看又是三十个，这是什么？扎尔说：这是月亮的日子，有的月份少了一天。第四个智者问：有一座美丽的花园，一个人拿着锋利的镰刀走进去，不分青的黄的，把草全都割倒，这是什么？扎尔说：花园就是这个世界，割草的人就是死亡，他不分老少，把所有的人都带走。国王和满朝的人都赞叹扎尔的智慧。第二天，扎尔又在骑射和摔跤中显示了无人能比的本领。马努切赫尔国王终于答应了这门婚事。

At Manūchehr's court the king had his wise men test Zāl with riddles. The first asked: 'There are twelve tall cypresses, each with thirty branches; what are they?' Zāl answered: 'The twelve months of the year, each with thirty days.' The second asked: 'Two noble horses, one black and one white, chase each other and never overtake; what are they?' 'Night and day.' The third asked: 'Thirty horsemen pass before the king; sometimes one is missing, yet look again and there are thirty; what are they?' 'The days of the moon; some months lack a day.' The fourth asked: 'There is a lovely garden; a man enters with a sharp scythe and mows down all the grass, green and dry alike; what is it?' Zāl said: 'The garden is this world and the mower is death, who takes all, old and young alike.' The king and the whole court marvelled at his wisdom. The next day Zāl showed unmatched skill at riding, archery and wrestling, and King Manūchehr at last consented to the marriage.

### 10 · 婚礼 / The wedding

扎尔带着国王的许可，日夜兼程回到扎布尔斯坦，又赶往喀布尔。喀布尔城里张灯结彩，到处是音乐和欢笑，街道上撒满了麝香和玫瑰水，大象背上驮着鼓手，人们用金银和珠宝迎接新郎。萨姆也来了，辛多赫特领着他去看鲁达贝。萨姆一见这位美丽的儿媳，就满心欢喜，赞叹不已。扎尔和鲁达贝按照古老的礼仪结为夫妻，坐在同一张宝座上，接受众人的祝福。婚礼的庆典一连举行了许多天。之后，扎尔带着新娘回到扎布尔斯坦，萨姆把领地交给儿子治理，自己又出征去了。曾经被父亲抛弃在山上的白发婴儿，如今有了自己的家，也有了一位为他放下长发的妻子。星象家们预言的那位英雄，很快就要降生了。两家都拿出了最贵重的礼物，庆祝这桩来之不易的婚事；伊朗和喀布尔之间的战事，也因为这场婚礼而平息了。

With the king's consent Zāl rode day and night back to Zābulistān and on to Kābul. The city was hung with lamps and garlands and filled with music and laughter; the streets were strewn with musk and rosewater, drummers rode on elephants, and the people welcomed the bridegroom with gold, silver and jewels. Sām came too, and Sindokht led him to see Rūdābeh; at the sight of his lovely daughter-in-law he was filled with joy and admiration. Zāl and Rūdābeh were married by the ancient rites and sat together on one throne to receive the blessings of all. The feasting lasted many days. Then Zāl brought his bride home to Zābulistān, and Sām gave his son the rule of his lands and went off to war again. The white-haired baby once cast away on the mountain now had a home of his own and a wife who had let down her hair for him. The hero the astrologers had foretold would soon be born. Both houses brought out their costliest gifts to celebrate a marriage so hard won, and the threatened war between Iran and Kābul was laid to rest by the wedding.

### 11 · 难产与西摩格的羽毛 / The hard labour and the Simorgh's feather

不久，鲁达贝怀了孕。她的身体一天天沉重起来，腹中的孩子大得出奇，她日夜疼痛，脸色像枯萎的花一样苍白。到了生产的时候，孩子却怎么也生不下来，鲁达贝昏死过去，宫中一片哭声，人人都以为她要死了。扎尔想起了西摩格的话，取出那根羽毛，点起火来焚烧。天空立刻暗了下来，西摩格从云中飞落，问他为何如此悲伤。扎尔说了妻子的危难。西摩格说：不要哭，你的妻子会生下一个狮子般的儿子。去请一位手法娴熟的祭司来，先让鲁达贝喝下掺了药的酒，让她沉睡，不觉得痛，再剖开她的腹侧，把孩子取出来，然后把伤口缝好，把我给你的药草捣碎了和着牛奶敷上，再用我的羽毛轻轻抚过伤口，她就会痊愈。扎尔照着做了。孩子被取了出来，大得像一个满一岁的孩子。鲁达贝醒来，看见孩子，说：我终于解脱了！在波斯语里，“解脱”这个词的读音就像“鲁斯塔姆”，孩子就这样得了名字。

Soon Rūdābeh was with child. Her body grew heavier day by day, the child within her was wondrously large, she was in pain day and night, and her face grew pale as a withered flower. When her time came the child would not be born; Rūdābeh fainted, the palace filled with weeping, and all thought she would die. Zāl remembered the Simorgh's words, took out the feather and set it alight. At once the sky darkened and the Simorgh came down from the clouds and asked why he grieved. He told her of his wife's peril. The Simorgh said: 'Do not weep; your wife will bear a lion of a son. Fetch a skilled priest; first let Rūdābeh drink wine mixed with a drug so that she sleeps and feels no pain; then let him open her side and draw out the child, and sew up the wound; crush the herb I give you with milk and lay it on, and stroke the wound with my feather, and she will heal.' Zāl did all this. The child was drawn out, as big as a year-old infant. When Rūdābeh woke and saw him she cried: 'I am delivered!' — and because in Persian the word sounds like Rostam, so the child was named.

### 12 · 白象与拉赫什 / The white elephant and Rakhsh

鲁斯塔姆长得飞快，十个乳母都喂不饱他，断奶以后，他一个人吃的饭比五个大人还多。他长得像柏树一样高，力气像大象一样大。有一天夜里，鲁斯塔姆正在熟睡，忽然听见外面一片喧哗：国王的一头白象挣脱了锁链，发了疯，在城里横冲直撞，人们四散奔逃。鲁斯塔姆跳起来，抓起祖父萨姆留下的铁杵冲出门去，守门人怕他出事，拦着不让走，他一拳打倒守门人，砸开了门，迎着白象冲上去，一杵就把白象打倒在地。后来，鲁斯塔姆要为自己挑一匹马。人们把扎布尔和喀布尔的马群赶到他面前，他用手按在马背上，马就压得弯下腰来，没有一匹承受得住。最后，他看见一匹母马身后跟着一匹小马，毛色像玫瑰花瓣点缀在藏红花上，四肢有力，胸膛宽阔。牧马人说：这匹马叫拉赫什，许多人想要它，可谁也不敢碰它，它的母马会像狮子一样冲上来保护它。鲁斯塔姆抛出套索套住了小马，母马冲过来，被他一声大喝吓退。鲁斯塔姆问牧马人这匹马的价钱，牧马人说：如果你是鲁斯塔姆，就骑上它去保卫伊朗吧，它的价钱就是整个伊朗。从此，鲁斯塔姆和拉赫什再也没有分开过。

Rostam grew so fast that ten nurses could not satisfy him, and once weaned he ate more than five grown men. He grew tall as a cypress and strong as an elephant. One night as he slept he heard an uproar outside: the king's white elephant had broken its chains and run mad through the city, and people were fleeing. Rostam leapt up, seized the iron mace his grandfather Sām had left, and ran to the door; the gatekeepers, fearing for him, tried to stop him, but he knocked them down, broke the door open, charged the elephant and felled it with a single blow. Later Rostam wanted a horse of his own. The herds of Zābul and Kābul were driven before him, but when he pressed his hand on a horse's back it sank down under him; none could bear his weight. At last he saw a colt following a mare, its coat like rose-petals scattered on saffron, its legs strong and its chest broad. The herdsman said: 'This is Rakhsh. Many have wanted him, but no one dares touch him; his mother rushes to defend him like a lion.' Rostam cast his lasso over the colt; the mare charged, and he drove her back with a great shout. When he asked the price, the herdsman said: 'If you are Rostam, ride him and defend Iran; his price is the land of Iran.' From then on Rostam and Rakhsh were never parted.

## 编辑说明

依据齐默恩 1883 年的英文散文转述本《列王纪》中“扎尔”“扎尔与鲁达贝”“鲁斯塔姆”三节，按顺序讲述。齐默恩的本子是对菲尔多西长诗的节选与改写，并非完整译本，细节可能与菲尔多西原诗不同；人名改用现代通行的波斯语转写（齐默恩作 Saum、Rustem 等）。

> 本故事页是可持续扩张的阶段性阅读基线；只重述已连接见证，不把现代改编或推测写成古代事实。
