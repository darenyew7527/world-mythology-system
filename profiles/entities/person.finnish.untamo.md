## 概要

Brother of Kalervo who, after a run of petty quarrels, destroys Kalervo's people, fails three times to kill the infant Kullervo and sells him to Ilmarinen; his clan is later wiped out by Kullervo (Kalevala, Runes XXXI, XXXVI).
