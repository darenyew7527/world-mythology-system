## 概要

奥丁之妻，知晓一切命运；她让万物发誓不伤害巴德尔，唯独漏掉了槲寄生（《欺骗古鲁菲》第20、49章）。 / Odin's wife who knows all fates; she takes oaths from all things to protect Baldr, except the mistletoe (Gylfaginning 20, 49).
