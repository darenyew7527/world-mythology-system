## 概要

Son of Kalervo, born in captivity after his uncle Untamo destroys his kin; sold as a slave to Ilmarinen, he avenges the stone baked in his bread, unknowingly seduces his own sister, destroys Untamo's clan and dies on his own sword (Kalevala, Runes XXXI–XXXVI).
