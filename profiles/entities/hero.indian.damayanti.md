## 概要

Princess of Vidarbha who chooses Nala at her svayaṃvara by recognising him among four gods in his likeness, survives abandonment in the forest and devises the second svayaṃvara that brings him back (Mahābhārata, Vana Parva, Nalopākhyāna).
