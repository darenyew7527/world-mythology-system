## 概要

King of the Nishadhas, chosen by Damayantī over four gods; possessed by Kali, he loses his kingdom at dice, abandons his wife in the forest, serves King Ṛtuparṇa as the charioteer Bāhuka and regains wife and kingdom (Mahābhārata, Vana Parva, Nalopākhyāna).
