## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0310.canaan.athirat_consort_el` [VERIFIED / IN_TRADITION / 0.88] Penn Museum describes a Canaanite pantheon of El the creator and his consort Athirat (Asherah), with the storm god Baal and his sister Anat.
  - 来源：[Canaanite Religion](https://penn.museum/sites/canaan/CanaaniteReligion.html)；定位：Canaanite Religion — the pantheon

  - 来源：[Baˁlu Epic KTU 1.1–1.6 — corpus and tablet concordance](https://eupt.uni-goettingen.de/Korpus.html)；定位：KTU 1.4 II

  - 来源：[Baˁlu Epic KTU 1.1–1.6 — corpus and tablet concordance](https://eupt.uni-goettingen.de/Korpus.html)；定位：KTU 1.4 III
- `claim.v0360.baal_palace.10` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] The Ugaritic Baal Cycle, KTU 1.4 IV, says that Athirat rode a donkey saddled with silver and gold to El at the source of the rivers, and that El smiled, offered her food and wine and asked whether his love had stirred her.
  - 来源：[Baˁlu Epic KTU 1.1–1.6 — corpus and tablet concordance](https://eupt.uni-goettingen.de/Korpus.html)；定位：KTU 1.4 IV
