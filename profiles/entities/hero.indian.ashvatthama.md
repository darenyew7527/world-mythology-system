## 概要

Son of Droṇa who, after the war, raids the sleeping Pāṇḍava camp by night, kills Dhṛṣṭadyumna and Draupadī's sons, and is cursed by Kṛṣṇa (Mahābhārata, Drona and Sauptika Parvas).
