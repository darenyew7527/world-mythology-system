## 概要

Strongest of the giants, with a three-cornered heart of stone, a stone head, a stone shield and a whetstone for a weapon; after a horse race with Óðinn he boasts drunkenly in Ásgarðr and is slain by Thor at Grjótúnagarðar (Skáldskaparmál 17).
