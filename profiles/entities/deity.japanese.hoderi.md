## 概要

Eldest son of Ninigi, Umisachi-hiko, who demands back the fish-hook his brother lost and, subdued by the tide jewels, becomes his guard; named as ancestor of the Hayato of Ata (Kojiki, Sect. XXXVIII–XLI).
