## 概要

Daughter of Ōyamatsumi, also called Kamu-ata-tsu-hime, who marries Ninigi and, doubted by him, gives birth inside a burning doorless hall to Hoderi, Hosuseri and Hoori (Kojiki, Sect. XXXVII–XXXVIII).
