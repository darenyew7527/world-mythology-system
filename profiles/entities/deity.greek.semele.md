- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.01` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.4.2–3.4.3, says that Zeus loved Semele, daughter of Cadmus, and that, deceived by Hera, she asked Zeus, who had promised her anything, to come to her as he had come when he wooed Hera.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.4.2–3.4.3
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.02` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.4.3, says that Zeus came to Semele's chamber in a chariot with lightning and thunder and launched a thunderbolt, that Semele died of fright, and that Zeus snatched the six-month child from the fire and sewed it in his thigh.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.4.3
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.11` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.5.3, says that Dionysus, now honoured as a god, brought his mother up from Hades, named her Thyone and ascended with her to heaven.
