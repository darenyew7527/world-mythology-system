  - 来源：[Eleusis: The Great Mysteries](https://www.theacropolismuseum.gr/en/temporary-exhibitions/eleusis-great-mysteries)；定位：Eleusis: The Great Mysteries exhibition description

  - 来源：[Mystery Cults in the Greek and Roman World](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/mystery-cults-in-the-greek-and-roman-world)；定位：Great Eleusinia and mysteries
