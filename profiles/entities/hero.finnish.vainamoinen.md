## 概要

Ancient singer and sage of Kalevala, born old from Ilmatar after thirty summers in the womb; he sings Joukahainen into a swamp, seeks a bride in Pohjola, makes the first kantele and leads the theft of the Sampo (Kalevala, Runes I–L).
