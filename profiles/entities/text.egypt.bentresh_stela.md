## 概要

Hieroglyphic stela (Louvre C 284) telling how the god Khonsu-the-Provider was sent from Thebes to the land of Bakhtan to heal Bentresh, sister of the queen Neferure; set in the reign of Ramesses II but composed much later, perhaps in the Persian or Ptolemaic period.
