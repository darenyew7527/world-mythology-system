## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.03` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.4.3, says that Semele's sisters spread the report that she had lain with a mortal, and that Zeus at the proper time undid the stitches, gave birth to Dionysus and entrusted him to Hermes, who took him to Ino and Athamas to be reared as a girl.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.4.3
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.05` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.4.3, says that Zeus turned Dionysus into a kid to escape Hera and that Hermes took him to the nymphs at Nysa in Asia, whom Zeus later turned into the stars called the Hyades.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.4.3
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.06` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.5.1, says that Dionysus discovered the vine, was driven mad by Hera and roamed Egypt and Syria, was received by Proteus, and at Cybela in Phrygia was purified by Rhea and learned the rites of initiation.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.5.1
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.07` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.5.1, says that Lycurgus, king of the Edonians, insulted and expelled Dionysus, who fled into the sea to Thetis, and that Dionysus drove Lycurgus mad so that he killed his own son, and the Edonians had him destroyed by horses on Mount Pangaeum.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.5.1
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.08` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.5.2, says that Dionysus, having passed through Thrace and India and set up pillars, came to Thebes and forced the women to leave their homes and rave on Cithaeron, and that Pentheus, son of Agave and Echion, tried to stop the rites.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.5.2
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.09` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.5.2, says that Pentheus, spying on the Bacchic women on Cithaeron, was torn limb from limb by his mother Agave in her frenzy, and that Dionysus, having shown the Thebans he was a god, then drove the women of Argos mad.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.5.2
- `claim.v0360.lib_dionysus.10` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Apollodorus, Library 3.5.3, says that Tyrrhenian pirates hired to carry Dionysus from Icaria to Naxos tried to sell him in Asia, and that he turned the mast and oars into snakes and filled the ship with ivy and flutes, so that the pirates went mad, leapt into the sea and became dolphins.
  - 来源：[Pseudo-Apollodorus, Library](https://topostext.org/work/150)；定位：Library 3.5.3
