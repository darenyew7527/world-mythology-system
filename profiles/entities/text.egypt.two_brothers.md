## 概要

Late Egyptian tale preserved complete in Papyrus D'Orbiney (BM EA 10183): Bata, falsely accused by his elder brother Anubis's wife, flees to the Valley of the Pine, where the gods fashion him a wife; he dies and is restored, and becomes king through a cycle of transformations.
