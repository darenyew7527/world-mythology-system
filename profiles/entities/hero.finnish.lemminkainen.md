## 概要

Handsome, reckless hero of Kalevala, also called Ahti and Kaukomieli, who carries off Kyllikki, goes to Pohjola to woo, is killed at the river of Tuonela and restored by his mother (Kalevala, Runes XI–XV, XXVI–XXX, XXXIX–XLIII).
