## 概要

Harp made by Väinämöinen from the jawbone of a giant pike, its pegs from the pike's teeth and its strings from the hair of Hiisi's gelding; its music moves all creatures to tears, and it is lost overboard in Louhi's storm (Kalevala, Runes XL–XLIV).
