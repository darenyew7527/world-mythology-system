## 概要

《古事记》中的男性创世神：与伊邪那美造出淤能碁吕岛，追往黄泉，归来禊祓时生出天照大神、月读与须佐之男。 / Male creator deity of the Kojiki: with Izanami he forms Onogoro Island, follows her to Yomi, and on returning gives birth to Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo as he purifies himself.
