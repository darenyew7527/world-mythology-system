## 概要

UCL 数字埃及：太阳神阿图姆的第一个男性“子嗣”，名字与“干燥”相关；盖布与努特之父，并把天地分开。 / UCL Digital Egypt: first male offshoot of the sun-god Atum, linked with dryness; father of Geb and Nut and the one who holds earth and sky apart.
