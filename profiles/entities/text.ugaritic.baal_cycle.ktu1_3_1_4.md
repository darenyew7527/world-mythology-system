## 概要

Two tablets of the Ugaritic Baal Cycle telling how Anat and Athirat win El's consent for a palace for Baal, how Kothar-wa-Khasis builds it of silver and gold, and how Baal, after a debate over its window, thunders from it and challenges Mot.
