## 概要

Daughter of King Aśvapati of Madra, born by the boon of the goddess Sāvitrī, who chooses the exiled prince Satyavān knowing he will die within a year, follows Yama when he takes Satyavān's life, and wins it back by her steadfastness and wisdom (Mahābhārata, Vana Parva, Pativratā-māhātmya).
