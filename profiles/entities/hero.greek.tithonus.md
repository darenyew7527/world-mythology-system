## 概要

Trojan beloved of Eos; she asked Zeus to make him immortal but forgot to ask for ageless youth, so he grew old for ever and she shut him in a chamber where his voice flows on endlessly (Homeric Hymn 5, lines 218–238; Theogony 984).
