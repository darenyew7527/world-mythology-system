## 概要

Wonder-mill with a many-coloured lid forged by Ilmarinen from a swan's feather tip, a barren cow's milk, a barley grain and a summer ewe's wool; it grinds grain for eating, selling and storing, is locked in Pohjola's mountain, stolen and shattered at sea (Kalevala, Runes X, XXXIX–XLIII).
