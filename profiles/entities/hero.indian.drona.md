## 概要

Brahmin teacher of arms to both Kauravas and Pāṇḍavas and second commander of the Kaurava army, who lays down his weapons on hearing that Aśvatthāman is dead and is slain by Dhṛṣṭadyumna (Mahābhārata, passim).
