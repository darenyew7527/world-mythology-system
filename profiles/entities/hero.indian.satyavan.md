## 概要

Son of the blind, exiled King Dyumatsena of Śālva, raised in a forest hermitage; foretold to die a year after his marriage to Sāvitrī, he is restored to life when Yama yields to her (Mahābhārata, Vana Parva, Pativratā-māhātmya).
