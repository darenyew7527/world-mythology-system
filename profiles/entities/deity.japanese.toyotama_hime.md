## 概要

Daughter of the sea deity who marries Hoori, gives birth on the shore in a hut thatched with cormorant feathers and, seen in her true form as a great wani, returns to the sea (Kojiki, Sect. XL, XLII).

## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0350.kojiki_ninigi.10` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XL says Hoori spat a jewel into the vessel of Toyotama-bime's maid at the well, Toyotama-bime fell in love with him at sight, and the sea deity welcomed him on layered rugs and gave her to him in marriage.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XL, The Palace of the Ocean-Possessor (kj047)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_ninigi.12` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Sections XLII–XLIII say Toyotama-bime gave birth in an unfinished hut thatched with cormorant feathers, was seen by Hoori in her true form as an eight-fathom wani, and in shame closed the sea-boundary and returned to the sea, leaving her son Ugaya-fuki-aezu.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XLII–XLIII (kj049–kj050)
