## 概要

Grandsire of the Kurus, son of Gaṅgā, bound by his vow of celibacy and his loyalty to the Kuru throne; first commander of the Kaurava army, he falls on the tenth day onto a bed of arrows and chooses the hour of his death (Mahābhārata, passim).
