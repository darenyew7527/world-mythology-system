## 概要

Descendant of Susanoo in the Kojiki, also called Ōnamuji, Ashihara-shiko-wo, Yachihoko and Utsushi-kunitama; he heals the hare of Inaba, survives his brothers' plots and Susanoo's trials, makes the land with Sukuna-bikona and yields it to the heavenly line (Kojiki, Sect. XX–XXXII).

## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0350.kojiki_okuninushi.01` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XX lists Susanoo's descendants and gives Ōkuninushi's five names: Ōkuninushi, Ōnamuji, Ashihara-shiko-wo, Yachihoko and Utsushi-kunitama.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XX, The August Ancestors of the Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land (kj027)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_okuninushi.02` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XXI says Ōnamuji, carrying his brothers' bag, healed the hare of Inaba with fresh water and sedge pollen after the Eighty Deities had made its injuries worse, and the hare foretold that Princess Yakami would choose him.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXI, The White Hare of Inaba (kj028)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_okuninushi.04` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XXII says the Eighty Deities crushed Ōnamuji in a cleft tree, his mother restored him and sent him to Oho-ya-biko in Ki, who let him escape through the fork of a tree towards Susanoo's Nether-Distant-Land.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXII, Mount Tema (kj029)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_okuninushi.07` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XXIII says that when Susanoo set fire to the moor around Ōnamuji, a mouse showed him a hollow in the ground to shelter in and brought back the whizzing arrow, and he presented the arrow to Susanoo.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXIII, The Nether-Distant-Land (kj030)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_okuninushi.10` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Sections XXIV–XXV say that as Yachihoko he wooed Princess Nunakawa of Koshi with songs, and that when Suseri-bime was jealous they exchanged songs and pledged each other with a wine-cup.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXIV–XXV, The Wooing of the Deity-of-Eight-Thousand-Spears; The Jealousy (kj031–kj032)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_okuninushi.12` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XXVIII says that after Sukuna-bikona left, a deity came over the sea illuminating it and offered to make the land with Ōkuninushi if worshipped on the eastern mountain of Yamato, the deity of Mount Mimoro.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXVIII, The Deity of Mount Mimoro (kj035)
