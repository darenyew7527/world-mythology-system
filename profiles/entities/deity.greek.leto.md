## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0360.hym_apollo.01` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Homeric Hymn 3, lines 1–18, says that when Apollo enters Zeus's house the gods rise, and Leto alone stays seated, takes his bow and rejoices in having borne Apollo and Artemis, she in Ortygia and he in Delos.
  - 来源：[Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, tr. Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1914)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/348)；定位：Homeric Hymn 3, lines 1–18
- `claim.v0360.hym_apollo.02` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Homeric Hymn 3, lines 19–50, lists the many lands Leto visited while in labour with Apollo and says that none dared to receive him until she came to Delos.
  - 来源：[Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, tr. Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1914)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/348)；定位：Homeric Hymn 3, lines 19–50
- `claim.v0360.hym_apollo.03` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Homeric Hymn 3, lines 51–88, says that Delos agreed to receive Leto only after she swore by Earth, Heaven and the water of Styx that Apollo's altar and precinct would be there and that he would honour the island above all.
  - 来源：[Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, tr. Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1914)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/348)；定位：Homeric Hymn 3, lines 51–88
- `claim.v0360.hym_apollo.05` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Homeric Hymn 3, lines 115–126, says that Leto, clasping a palm tree and kneeling on the meadow, bore Apollo, whom the goddesses washed and swaddled and Themis fed with nectar and ambrosia.
  - 来源：[Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns, and Homerica, tr. Hugh G. Evelyn-White (1914)](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/348)；定位：Homeric Hymn 3, lines 115–126
