## 概要

Prince of the Yādavas and incarnation of Viṣṇu, cousin and counsellor of the Pāṇḍavas, who seeks peace as their envoy and drives Arjuna's chariot at Kurukṣetra, teaching him the Bhagavad Gītā (Mahābhārata, passim).

## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0350.mbh_kurukshetra.01` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Mahābhārata, Udyoga Parva (Bhagavat-yana Parva) says Kṛṣṇa went to Hastināpura as the Pāṇḍavas' envoy and asked for half the kingdom or even five villages, and that Duryodhana refused to yield even as much land as a needle's point.

- `claim.v0350.mbh_kurukshetra.12` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Mahābhārata, Sauptika Parva (Aishika Parva) says that when Aśvatthāman, overtaken, loosed the Brahmaśiras weapon and turned it on the Pāṇḍavas' unborn heirs, Kṛṣṇa cursed him to wander for three thousand years, and he gave up the jewel on his forehead.
  - 来源：[The Mahabharata of Krishna-Dwaipayana Vyasa, tr. Kisari Mohan Ganguli (1883–1896)](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/maha/index.htm)；定位：Sauptika Parva, Aishika Parva
