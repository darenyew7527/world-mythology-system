## 概要

Grandson of Amaterasu sent down from High Heaven to rule the Central Land; he descends on Takachiho in Himuka, marries Konohana-sakuya-hime and rejects her sister Iwanaga-hime (Kojiki, Sect. XXXIII–XXXVIII).

## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0350.kojiki_ninigi.01` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XXXIII says that when the land was reported pacified, Ame-no-oshi-ho-mimi proposed his newborn son Ninigi in his place, and Amaterasu and Takagi-no-kami commanded Ninigi to descend and rule the Land of Fresh Rice-Ears.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXXIII, The August Descent from Heaven of His Augustness the August Grandchild (kj040)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_ninigi.04` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Sections XXXIII–XXXIV say Ninigi pushed through the eight-fold clouds and descended on the peak of Kuji-furu-take of Takachiho in Himuka, called the place exceedingly good, and built his palace there.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXXIII–XXXIV (kj040–kj041)
