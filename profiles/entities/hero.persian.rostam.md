## 概要

Greatest champion of the Shahnameh, son of Zāl and Rūdābeh, born by the Simorgh's counsel; he rides Rakhsh, passes the seven stages to free Kay Kāvus and unknowingly kills his son Sohrāb (Shahnameh).
