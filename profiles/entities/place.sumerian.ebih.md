## 概要

Mountain range (usually identified with the Jebel Hamrin) that refused to bow to Inana and was destroyed by her in the Sumerian hymn Inana and Ebiḫ (ETCSL 1.3.2).

  - 来源：[Inana and Ebiḫ](https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/section1/tr132.htm)；定位：ETCSL 1.3.2, the destruction of Ebiḫ
