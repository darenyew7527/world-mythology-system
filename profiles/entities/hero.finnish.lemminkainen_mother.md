## 概要

Mother who warns Lemminkäinen against Pohjola, reads his death in the bleeding brush, rakes his body from the river of Tuonela and restores him to life (Kalevala, Runes XII, XV).

## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0350.kal_2.11` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kalevala Rune XV says that when the brush dripped blood Lemminkäinen's mother hurried to Pohjola, forced Louhi to tell where she had sent him, and after the trees, the road and the moon could not help, learned from the sun that he lay hewn in pieces in the river of Tuonela.
