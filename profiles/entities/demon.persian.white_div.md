# 白魔（迪弗·塞皮德） / White Demon

## 概要

Chief of the demons of Māzandarān who blinds and captures Kay Kāvus's army with dark cloud and hail and is killed by Rostam in his cave; his blood restores the captives' sight (Shahnameh).
