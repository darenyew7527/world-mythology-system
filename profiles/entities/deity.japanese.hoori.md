## 概要

Youngest son of Ninigi and Konohana-sakuya-hime, Yamasachi-hiko, who loses his brother's fish-hook, recovers it in the sea deity's palace, marries Toyotama-bime and subdues his brother with the tide jewels (Kojiki, Sect. XXXVIII–XLII).

## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0350.kojiki_ninigi.08` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XXXIX says Hoori persuaded his brother Hoderi to exchange their gear, lost the fish-hook at sea, and could not make his brother accept five hundred or a thousand hooks forged from his sword in its place.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XXXIX, The August Exchange of Luck (kj046)
- `claim.v0350.kojiki_ninigi.09` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Kojiki Section XL says the salt deity Shio-tsuchi set Hoori in a tightly woven bamboo boat and told him to follow the current to the sea deity's palace built like fish-scales and sit in the cassia tree by its well.
  - 来源：[The Kojiki: Records of Ancient Matters (tr. Basil Hall Chamberlain)](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kojiki.htm)；定位：Sect. XL, The Palace of the Ocean-Possessor (kj047)
