## 概要

Son of Kuntī by the sun god, raised by a charioteer, friend of Duryodhana and rival of Arjuna, who kills Ghaṭotkaca with Indra's dart and is slain by Arjuna when his chariot wheel sinks (Mahābhārata, passim).

## Claims 与证据

- `claim.v0350.mbh_kurukshetra.06` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Mahābhārata, Drona Parva (Ghatotkacha-vadha Parva) says that in the night battle Karṇa, begged by Duryodhana, spent the one-time dart from Indra that he had kept for Arjuna on Bhīma's rākṣasa son Ghaṭotkaca, and that Kṛṣṇa rejoiced that Arjuna was saved.
  - 来源：[The Mahabharata of Krishna-Dwaipayana Vyasa, tr. Kisari Mohan Ganguli (1883–1896)](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/maha/index.htm)；定位：Drona Parva, Ghatotkacha-vadha Parva
- `claim.v0350.mbh_kurukshetra.08` [VERIFIED / TEXT_SAYS / 0.90] Mahābhārata, Karna Parva says that on the seventeenth day Karṇa's chariot wheel sank into the earth through a brahmin's curse, that Kṛṣṇa answered his appeal to dharma by recalling Draupadī and Abhimanyu, and that Arjuna struck off his head.
  - 来源：[The Mahabharata of Krishna-Dwaipayana Vyasa, tr. Kisari Mohan Ganguli (1883–1896)](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/maha/index.htm)；定位：Karna Parva
